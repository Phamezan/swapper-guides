## Key tips

- Sidestep Q and give up CS to survive the lane. His poke pattern is the whole early game, so concede farm rather than health.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Survive first
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

- E-buffer his W stun. Fire E as he commits and the stun never completes, turning his engage into your return trade.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Stun buffer
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Pantheon.W]

- Time the R recast for after his shield ends. The shield blocks the execute pull, so wait it out and recast into the opening.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Execute timing
  abilities: [Urgot.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Read his empowered bar before trading. A charged bar means a stronger next ability, so give ground until it discharges on the wave.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Bar watch

- Reposition when he flips you. If his E turns you, face E back toward him so the return trade starts instantly.
  sources: [procatking]
  heading: Flip answer
  abilities: [Pantheon.E]

## Mid lane

- Ping his global R and push while he leaves. Every roam costs him a wave, so shove hard and punish the lost farm.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Roam punish
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Use E to get behind the shield when he raises it. The reposition beats the block and sets up knees from the safe side.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Shield answer
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

## Win condition

- Extend the game and outscale. Your 1v1 and teamfight both grow past his, so refuse early coinflips and win on items.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Outscale plan

- Execute only into shield downtime. Hold the recast until the block fades, and never spend the pull into a raised shield.
  sources: [goliath-urgot, procatking]
  heading: Shield discipline
