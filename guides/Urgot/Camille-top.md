## Key tips

- Fire E as she lands from Hookshot, never mid-air. The landing is the hittable moment; the airborne cast always misses the window.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: E timing
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Camille.E]

- Cast R during the E stun so she cannot dodge it. The chained control locks out her ultimate answer and guarantees the execute setup.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Execute chain
  abilities: [Urgot.R, Urgot.E, Camille.R]

- Kite her passive-shield auto and position W between poke and farm. Make her choose: contest the poke and concede farm, or farm into your knees.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Shield denial

## Overview

## Early lane

- Track her jungler every wave. Her gank setup is among the strongest top, so ward the lane and trade only with vision before committing.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Gank respect

- Keep trades short. She deals Q damage then leaves, so answer the Q at its edge and refuse the chase into her second rotation.
  sources: [raideru, goliath-urgot]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

## Mid lane

- Force her ultimate out before executing. Poke it free with E-stun combos, since she saves R to dodge the execute pull.
  sources: [raideru, goliath-urgot]
  heading: Bait the dodge
  abilities: [Camille.R, Urgot.E]

- Step inside her Q edge when she commits. She enters the inner ring to deny the edge, so hold the cast and W her at close range instead.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Inner answer
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

## Win condition

- Force teamfights over even 1v1s. Her scaling wins the isolated duel, while your knees decide grouped fights, so convert pressure into 5v5s.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Teamfight plan

- Punish her roams with plates and vision. She must leave to find odd fights, so take the tower gold and track every departure.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Roam punish
