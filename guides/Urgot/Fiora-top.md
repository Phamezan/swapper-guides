## Key tips

- Never E from max range while Riposte is ready. Bait the parry with a short E near her, or catch Q as she steps in for a vital.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Fiora.W]

- Trade autos after Q poke unless her wave is too large. The Q-plus-autos pattern is your safe exchange; a big wave turns it against you.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Safe exchange
  abilities: [Urgot.Q]

- Give her no free windows. She trades after your missed Q, spent passive shots, or an exposed vital, so Q with intent and keep vitals turned away.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Deny windows
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Meet her vital walk-ups with Q and autos. When a vital faces her and she steps in, answer immediately; back off once her wave grows too large to contest.
  sources: [goliath-urgot, justsad42-mf]
  heading: Vital answers
  abilities: [Urgot.Q]

- Do not E predictably while she holds Q and Riposte. She dashes into your E and parries the landing, so vary the timing or hold it until she commits elsewhere.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: E patience
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Fiora.Q, Fiora.W]

## Mid lane

- Press before her Ravenous turn. Bramble stalls your damage for a window, then she returns stronger, so force the lane while the stall is still buying time.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Spike race

- Respect her two-to-three-item parity. If even, she matches you in that window before you pull ahead again, so take duels from advantage, not from even.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Parity respect

## Win condition

- Wall her ultimate. Stand near a wall during R so the final vital stays denied and her full proc never completes.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Beat the duel
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- If she lands W, keep W firing. Your attack rate is not slowed by the parry, so the locked-on exchange still favors you.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Fire through

- Force teamfights over duels. Your 5v5 is much stronger than hers, so convert pressure into grouped fights instead of offering the isolated duel she wants.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Teamfight plan
