## Key tips

- Walk out of his E circle. The marked zone is the whole engage, so leave it on sight and re-enter only after it fades.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: E escape
  abilities: [Volibear.E]

- E-buffer his Q stun. Time E for the dash impact and the stun never lands, which also avoids the E he places on the landing spot.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Stun buffer
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Volibear.Q]

- Disengage after his W marks you. The second hit heals him, so break contact at the mark and deny the bonus damage and healing.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Mark discipline
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Poke him before he reaches you. He wants to avoid early poke, so zone with Q and knees rather than letting him walk into range free.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Early zone
  abilities: [Urgot.Q]

- Trade around his W cooldown. Engage into the spent W and leave at the mark, keeping every exchange on your terms.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: W windows
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

## Mid lane

- Save E and R control until he lands from his ultimate. He cannot be disabled mid-leap, so hold everything for the landing moment.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Landing answer
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Urgot.R, Volibear.R]

- Extend the game because he falls off hard. Every completed item widens your gap, so refuse coinflips and scale past him.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Outscale plan

## Win condition

- Respect the tower dive when low. His R disables the tower for him, so recall early or concede CS rather than dying under your own turret.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Dive respect
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

- Force fights after his falloff point. Once your items land, take every duel and convert the 1v1 edge into objectives.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Press the edge

## Itemization

- Expect Warden's Mail against your repeated damage. Trade with knees and E rather than autos into the armor, and keep the poke pattern going.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
