## Key tips

- Use E to cancel or buffer his E. Fire as he commits and the knockup never completes, turning his engage into your trade.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: E answer
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Ornn.E]

- Never stand in line with low-health minions. He fires Q through them to reach you, so offset from the wave's dying edge.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Positioning
  abilities: [Ornn.Q]

- Turn the lane at level 9 with Black Cleaver. The spike plus ranks flips every exchange, so survive to it and press after.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Press before his armor components land. He plays safely waiting for them, so the pre-component window is your only free pressure.
  sources: [eve0030]
  heading: Early window

- Respect Aery poke without conceding the wave. Take short contacts and answer with knees rather than brawling into brittle procs.
  sources: [eve0030]
  heading: Poke respect

## Mid lane

- E-buffer the knockups from his E and the second hit of R. Both telegraphs lose to a timed E, so hold it for the cast animations.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Buffer plan
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Ornn.E, Ornn.R]

- Keep firing into Frozen Heart. Its attack-speed slow does not touch your W rate, so his rush item buys him nothing against locked-on knees.
  items: [3110]
  sources: [eve0030]
  heading: Ignore Heart

## Win condition

- Duel after Black Cleaver completes. The armor-shred turn makes extended trades yours, so force them once the item is done.
  items: [3071]
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Cleaver duels

- Close before his teamfight value matters. Your edge is the 1v1 spike, so end the lane phase ahead rather than matching his utility scaling.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  heading: Close early

## Itemization

- Build Black Cleaver as the matchup spike. It answers his armor stacking and turns level 9 into your lane-winning window.
