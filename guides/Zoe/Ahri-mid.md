## Key tips

- She stays near minions so they block your E sleep bubble: splash or wait.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Zoe.E]

- She answers bubble with W: never throw it raw, pair it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Zoe.R]

- When you ult in, she ults and Charms your cast spot: you snap back to it, so vary.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ahri.E, Zoe.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She concedes pressure in levels 1-5: press before her answers come online.
  sources: [peng04]

- Your Exhaust and Ignite pickups keep her Charm defensive: show them.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- She Charms every portal forward: never portal predictably.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Zoe.R]

- She calls her jungler from 6 on: duels favour you, ganks favour her, so track it.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

## Win condition

- She wins through ganks and cooldown punishes, not duels: you find the 1v1 easy, so force it.
  sources: [shok-zoe, peng04]
