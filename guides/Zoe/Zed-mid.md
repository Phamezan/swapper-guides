## Overview

## Early lane

- He respects your passive autos early: press walk-ups.
  sources: [v222]

- He takes Cleanse or TP: the summoner is his safety net, so punish the window after use.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He times a triple Q on your R return spot: you always come back, so vary portals.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q, Zoe.R]

## Win condition

- Sleep landed plus return varied: with his Cleanse down and Qs dodged, his answers run out.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
