## Key tips

- Skill matchup, slightly hers: steal edges before she banks them.
  sources: [shok]

- She keeps creeps between to block Q and sleep bubble: the wave is her shield, so splash.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Zoe.Q, Zoe.E]

- If you waste bubble she plays very aggressive: the cooldown is her window, so hold it.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Zoe.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She respects your summoners and items: vary pickups.
  sources: [shok]

- She calls ganks: you are very gankable with weak waveclear, so ward.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Your all-ins end; her pressure does not: pick windows, never force.
  sources: [shok]

- After 6, if you ult forward she steps up and stuns your snap-back: vary the return.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Zoe.R]

## Win condition

- Snowballed plus returns kept: the Syndra whose edges never banked loses the skill check.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
