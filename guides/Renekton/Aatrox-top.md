## Key tips

- Bait a Q with spacing, then dash in during Q1 or Q2 for one quick combo and leave. The window closes the moment he chains into the extended sequence.
  sources: [xxshintaro]
  heading: Q1/Q2 window
  abilities: [Renekton.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Save the second dash for his Q3. Sidestep the first two casts and keep moving, then E out of the third rather than trading through its sweet spot.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: E for Q3
  abilities: [Renekton.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Track his R ({cd:266:R}). Disengage every extended fight while it is active unless your burst finishes him, because its healing reverses the trade.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: Respect R
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Secure level 2 with Q, then force the short E-plus-W stun trade before he can chain a full Q sequence. The level timing is your only clean engage angle.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: Level-2 force
  abilities: [Renekton.Q, Renekton.W, Aatrox.Q]

- Refuse the long trade before Ignite gives a realistic kill window. Brief fury trades are the whole lane; anything extended plays into his sustain.
  sources: [xxshintaro, godrekton]
  heading: Short only

- Leave his W the instant it lands. E out before he lines up Q damage behind it, and hold off trading until his passive-empowered attack is spent.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: Answer the pull
  abilities: [Aatrox.W, Renekton.E]

## Mid lane

- Shape the wave around the next goal. Hold it when you need safety or a gank setup, and build the slow push when the crash buys plates or a dive.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: Wave per goal

- Deny him the free proxy. Call the jungler to break it with you instead of answering alone, because he turns solo answers into dives.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: No solo proxy answer

## Win condition

- Force when he sits around half health. Renekton threatens the kill or the dive there, so judge the wave and the cooldowns before committing.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: Half-health force

- Open your R only with a clear way to stay on him. It extends the all-in and keeps pressure through the fight, but wastes itself if he simply walks away.
  sources: [godrekton]
  heading: R with a stick path
  abilities: [Renekton.R]
