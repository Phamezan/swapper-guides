## Key tips

- Full-health fights belong to you. Without a poke lead Jhin cannot win the 1v1, so start the fight before his bounces add up.
  sources: [omibro]

- His max-damage trade is Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) into the loaded fourth shot. Never stand in the bounce line once you are hurt.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Deadly Flourish is a follow-up for his support, not dry poke. When it misses, or his support has no crowd control ready, that is your window.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He works the wave like Miss Fortune, bouncing Q off dying minions onto you. Stand off the minion line so the bounce never reaches you.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- He loads the fourth shot on a minion and holds it to zone you off the wave. Trade while it is spent and give ground while it is held.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- He wants to crash the wave and base for Serrated Dirk. Match the shove when you can, and respect his poke once he returns with it.
  sources: [omibro]

- He plays this lane for priority and an early lead rather than kills, and rates it an easy one. Never hand him the free crash: answer every shove.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Mid lane

- He traps the objective side for vision and hovers instead of walking up blind. Clear the traps and never face-check when his position is unknown.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

- A landed Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) sets up a guaranteed first Curtain Call shot ({cd:202:R}). Keep moving and break vision the moment it connects.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Win condition

- Watch your health bar against his Dancing Grenade into loaded fourth shot, which takes about
  half your health. Reset once he lands it, and force the all-in while you are still full.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Slip Deadly Flourish with Supercharge ({cd:145:E}) once his support commits. Without the root he cannot hold you down for the combo.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Kai'Sa.E, Jhin.W]

- Left alone on a weak side he bleeds farm to divers like you. Call your jungler once you have the advantage and close the game off picks.
  sources: [omibro]

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on his first base is his spike. His Q and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so match it with your own component before trading.
  sources: [omibro]

- He funds his crit build by executing low targets with Collector and Deathfire Touch. Never linger at low health inside his W range after a lost fight.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.Q]
