## Key tips

- Vary Counter Strike timing every trade so Riposte cannot predict it. A telegraphed stun gets parried and stunned back, which loses the fight on the spot.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Stun timing
  abilities: [Jax.E, Fiora.W]
  patch: "26.21"

- Reset unfavorable vitals through vision before fighting. Give up bad positions and re-engage on a vital you can defend rather than dueling into her best angle.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Vital control
  abilities: [Jax.E]
  patch: "26.21"

- Never open a full fight first when her Riposte is up. The first engager usually loses, so show pressure and force her parry before committing your leap and stun.
  sources: [justsad42, ph45]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]
  heading: Engagement rule
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Leap in behind an active Counter Strike rather than jumping bare. She parries predictable leaps mid-animation, so the stun must already be running when you land on her.
  sources: [justsad42, ph45]
  heading: Leap setup
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.E, Fiora.W]
  patch: "26.21"

- Trade short Q auto combos and back off before she extends. Her extended trades beat yours, so chip and leave instead of standing in her vital line.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Short trades

## Mid lane

- Fight after her Riposte is spent, not into it. When she uses W on the wave or a gank, that missing parry is your commit window for the full combo.
  sources: [justsad42, ph45]
  heading: Parry downtime
  abilities: [Fiora.W]
  patch: "26.21"

- Take Ignite if she matches nothing, since the matchup swings hard on summoners. Without her Ignite to answer yours, your committed all-ins stick at low health.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Summoner edge

## Win condition

- Position near a wall when she commits her ult. The terrain cuts off her vital angles and limits the full-proc healing she needs to win the duel.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Ult answer
  abilities: [Jax.E, Fiora.R]
  patch: "26.21"

- Peel her off your backline rather than dueling her head-on late. If she saves Riposte for your stun, your team gets more from your counter-engage than from a solo all-in you lose.
  sources: [justsad42]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]
  heading: Teamfight role
