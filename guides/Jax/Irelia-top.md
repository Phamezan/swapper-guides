## Key tips

- Dodge her stun above all else. Never leap or trade into the setup; the landed stun starts everything she does.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Stun respect
  abilities: [Irelia.E]

- Track her passive stacks and Q resets. A fully stacked passive deals heavy damage, so count dashes and leave before the peak.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Stack tracking
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Hold Counter Strike until she commits. She sets up minions to bait E, so stun only the committed dash, never the feint.
  sources: [houcs, ph45]
  heading: E patience
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take short trades and reset. The matchup favors you, but extended fights let her stack for free, so hit inside E and step out.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Punish her dash-ins with E when no exit minion remains. If she dashes without a way back, the stun locks her in the exchange she cannot leave.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Trap the dash
  abilities: [Jax.E, Irelia.Q]

## Mid lane

- Count Q resets across the fight. Each dash refreshes on a new target, so track the minion wave as her second resource bar.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Reset counting
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Vary the stun because she baits it. She wants E spent before committing, so hold longer on some trades and release early on others.
  sources: [houcs, ph45]
  heading: Unreadable E
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Win condition

- Disengage at full stacks. When the passive peaks, give ground and re-engage as it falls instead of fighting the stacked duelist.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Peak respect

- Finish when her Q targets and stun are down. Without resets and setup, her commit has no damage and no exit.
  sources: [houcs, ph45]
  heading: Finish window
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Irelia.E]
