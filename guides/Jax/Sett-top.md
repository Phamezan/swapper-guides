## Key tips

- Block his Q with Counter Strike. His opener dies on the shield, so meet every Q step-in with E rather than trading through it.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Q answer
  abilities: [Jax.E, Sett.Q]

- Move behind or to the side as he casts W, then punish. Never stand in the true-damage center; the sidestep turns his kill tool into your window.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: W footwork
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Vary the stun because he times W for its expiry. Hold Counter Strike longer on some trades and release early on others so his Haymaker never lands clean.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Unreadable E
  abilities: [Jax.E, Sett.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Concede extended early brawls. He is bulky and hits hard before your timings come online, so take only E-answered shorts and give ground otherwise.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Early respect
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Keep trades short and reset. His autos win the long exchange, so hit inside E and step out instead of standing in his damage.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Short trades

## Mid lane

- Trade only while W is down. With W maxed, every cast is the exchange, so engage into the cooldown gap and leave before it returns.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: W windows
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Space the tower line after level 6. Never fight clumped near his tower, where R throws you under it for the kill.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Throw respect
  abilities: [Sett.R]

## Win condition

- All-in after W fires. The spent Haymaker is your extended-trade window, so commit once the true damage is gone.
  sources: [ph45, autolykus]
  heading: Punish window
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Force fights in open ground. Away from his tower the ultimate loses its setup, and your sustained dueling takes over.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Open ground
  abilities: [Sett.R]
