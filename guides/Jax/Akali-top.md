## Key tips

- Contest level 1. Walk up with E to block her empowered auto, or open Q-start for the early Grasp chunk; never concede the wave for free.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Level-1 contest
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Sidestep her E and disengage at shroud. Her dash commits her to a line you can leave, and the shroud hides nothing worth chasing into.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- Force her E before committing the stun. She saves it to answer Counter Strike, so threaten first and stun once the escape is gone.
  sources: [anguish, ph45]
  heading: Stun setup
  abilities: [Jax.E, Akali.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Match her farm against your E start. She takes Q to poke when you open E, so hold position and refuse the chase her poke invites.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: E-start answer
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- Hold Q when she opens Q-start. She takes E to escape your engage, so threaten the leap without spending it until her exit is gone.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Q-start answer
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Akali.E]

## Mid lane

- Hold the stun through all three level-6 dashes. She chains dashes to dodge Counter Strike, so wait for the last one before firing.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Post-6 patience
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Use the early ultimate to zone, not to chase. She backs off when you commit R early, so take the territory instead of diving after her.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Ult zoning
  abilities: [Jax.R]

## Win condition

- Win Grasp short-trade attrition. Repeated blocked-auto exchanges favor you, so stack small wins instead of forcing one big fight.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Attrition plan

- Finish when her E and shroud are down. Without the escape and the cover, her commit has no exit and your stun decides the duel.
  sources: [anguish, ph45]
  heading: Finish window
  abilities: [Akali.E, Jax.E]
