## Key tips

- Block Crippling Strike with Counter Strike and never let five stacks build. Answer his W with E, then leave before the passive maxes instead of extending into full stacks.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Stack denial
  abilities: [Jax.E, Darius.W]

- Manage the Decimate ring. Hold outside its outer edge by default; if you cannot leave the edge, leap onto him into the inner ring to deny the heal, the bleed stack, and the bonus damage.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Ring control
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Darius.Q]

- All-in after he misses Apprehend, but account for Ghost. A missed E is your window, yet he still runs you down once Counter Strike ends, so commit only when you can finish inside the window.
  sources: [ph45, federals1-darius]
  heading: Punish window
  abilities: [Darius.E, Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade only with Counter Strike available. His spacing sits at W outer edge beyond your W range, so close with the leap only when E is ready to block the W answer.
  sources: [federals1-darius, ph45]
  heading: Engage rule
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.E, Darius.W]

- Refuse his short-trade bait before you have E. He wants W-plus-Q contact without answering your stun, so give ground until E is up rather than conceding free stacks.
  sources: [federals1-darius, ph45]
  heading: Patience
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Mid lane

- Vary the stun because he predicts it. He times his W to land as the dodge ends, so hold Counter Strike longer on some trades and release early on others.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Unreadable E
  abilities: [Jax.E, Darius.W]

- Hold Ignite for his Decimate heal moment. Ignite limits his recovery, so apply it as he commits Q in the all-in rather than spending it on the opening trade.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Ignite timing
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

## Win condition

- Disengage when Counter Strike ends if his Ghost is up. He deliberately lets E finish before Ghosting, so step out at E expiry instead of offering the chase.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Respect Ghost
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- End fights before five stacks once Spear of Shojin is done. After that spike he looks for five stacks into the ultimate, so keep trades short and never offer the extended duel.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Post-Shojin rule
  abilities: [Darius.R]

## Itemization

- Take Flash and Ignite. Ignite answers his Decimate healing in all-ins, and the extra kill pressure fits a lane you are supposed to win.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]
