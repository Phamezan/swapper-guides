## Key tips

- Dodge his E and Q, never walk into both. The pull sets up everything he does, so sidestep the E first and the Q never gets its angle.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dodge rule
  abilities: [Jax.E, Mordekaiser.E, Mordekaiser.Q]
  patch: "26.21"

- Track his shield and commit only when it is down. Trading into a held shield wastes your burst and hands him the extended fight.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Shield respect
  patch: "26.21"

- Activate your ultimate after his to limit what the realm steals. His R first means fewer stolen resistances and a duel you can actually finish.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Ult order
  abilities: [Jax.R, Mordekaiser.R]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Bait his E before jumping in. He pushes off every bare leap, so show pressure until the pull is spent, then leap onto the cooldown gap.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: E bait
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Mordekaiser.E]

- Punish a missed Q at once. His all-in depends on the Q landing, so the whiff is your commit signal for the extended trade.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Punish Q miss
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

## Mid lane

- Re-engage after his E peel instead of accepting the reset. Each disengage costs you the stack-up, so stick back on once the pull is down.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Re-engage rule
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E]

- Keep the wave off his tower so his short-trade pattern never sets. His safe shorts need his turret nearby; mid-lane extended trades are yours.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Wave position

## Win condition

- Force short favorable trades before his level 6 spike. Once the realm is online on even terms, his duel math turns against you.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Pre-6 window
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.R]

- Build a lead before he goes even or ahead. His matchup eases from parity, so the early kill or plate is what keeps the duel yours.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Lead rule
