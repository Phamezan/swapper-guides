## Key tips

- Block her Q, especially the true-damage cast. Meet each Q step-in with Counter Strike rather than trading through the empowered damage.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Q answer
  abilities: [Jax.E, Camille.Q]

- Move toward her or Q onto her W. Closing the distance denies both the heal and the slow, turning her spacing tool into your engage.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: W denial
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Camille.W]

- Stun her E dash as it starts at a wall. The cancel stops the engage cold, so hold E for the hookshot sound rather than spending it early.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dash cancel
  abilities: [Jax.E, Camille.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep trades short. Her plan is Q damage then E away, so answer the Q and let her leave instead of chasing into the second rotation.
  sources: [ph45, raideru]
  heading: Trade length
  abilities: [Camille.Q, Camille.E]

- Bait her E before the jungler ganks. A spent hookshot means no escape, so threaten first and call the gank once it is down.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Gank setup
  abilities: [Camille.E]

## Mid lane

- Beware her R dodging your stun at level 6. Once her ultimate is up, delay the stun until she commits it elsewhere or you can guarantee the lock.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Ult respect
  abilities: [Jax.E, Camille.R]

- Vary the stun because she saves R for executes. An unreadable Counter Strike beats both the dodge and the all-in answer.
  sources: [raideru, ph45]
  heading: Unreadable E
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Win condition

- Start the run-down only when her R and E are down. She saves the ultimate to dodge and avoids Ghost chases, so commit once both mobility tools are spent.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Commit window
  abilities: [Camille.R, Camille.E]

- Punish after her ultimate expires. The spent R removes her dodge threat, opening the extended duel you win.
  sources: [ph45, raideru]
  heading: Post-ult punish
  abilities: [Camille.R]
