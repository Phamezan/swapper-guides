## Key tips

- Meet his engages with Counter Strike. E blocks both Q and W, so answer the dash with the shield instead of trading through the stun.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Engage answer
  abilities: [Jax.E, Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]

- Stay out of his E circle. The marked zone is the whole engage, so leave it on sight and re-enter only after it fades.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: E escape
  abilities: [Volibear.E]

- Let his W mark expire before re-engaging. Unless the kill is certain, break contact at the mark and deny the second-hit heal.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Mark discipline
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take short trades into his resistances. His bulk wins long exchanges, so hit inside E and step out before the return damage lands.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Threaten with a held E to zone him. He avoids committing into the shield, so the unspent Counter Strike holds him off the wave.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Shield threat
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Mid lane

- Stop relying on the tower after level 6. His ultimate dive disables it, so keep the lane in open ground instead of turtling low.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dive respect
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

- Re-engage as the mark expires. The expiry is your window back in, since his combo loses the heal threat without it.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Re-entry timing
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

## Win condition

- Win the extended duel after mark expiry. Without the second-hit threat, your sustained damage outlasts his combo pattern.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Duel plan
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

- Hold E for his committed all-in. The shield that answers the full Q-plus-W decides the late duel, so never spend it on poke.
  sources: [federals1-volibear, ph45]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Jax.E, Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]
