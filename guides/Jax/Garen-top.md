## Key tips

- Block his Q and never sit inside his spin. The silence starts his combo and the spin out-damages your autos, so answer the Q and exit the E.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Combo defense
  abilities: [Jax.E, Garen.Q, Garen.E]
  patch: "26.21"

- Respect the level 6 execute in every low-health trade. His ult range bends the whole exchange, so leave before you enter the health where it finishes you.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Execute respect
  abilities: [Garen.R]
  patch: "26.21"

- Re-trade to keep his passive down. His recovery needs quiet time, so short returns stop him healing back between your engages.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Regen denial
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Never leap bare into his spin start. He opens E and shields the jump, so activate Counter Strike before leaping or hold the jump entirely.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Leap setup
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.E, Garen.E]

- Vary the stun so his Q-as-stun-ends timing fails. He lands Q right after a predictable E, and an unreadable stun wastes his whole punish.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Stun variation
  abilities: [Jax.E, Garen.Q]

## Mid lane

- Save your ultimate for the committed all-in, not the short trades. He chips before engaging so your R has less to decide, so hold it for his real commit.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Ult patience
  abilities: [Jax.R]

- Follow his spin with your own engage instead of letting him disengage free. His pattern is E then out, so stick with the leap once his spin ends.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Chasing E
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Garen.E]

## Win condition

- Survive the level 6 through mid-game window and scale past him. His advantage lives in that stretch, while your late dueling matches and beats his.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Outscale plan

- Hold Counter Strike for his Q silence into ult at low health. When he Qs toward you in kill range, the stun on the silence window breaks his execute setup.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Execute answer
  abilities: [Jax.E, Garen.Q, Garen.R]
