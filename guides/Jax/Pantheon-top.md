## Key tips

- Survive the early lane on Lethal Tempo. His Q poke rules the opening levels, so farm through it and scale past him instead of forcing back.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Survive first

- Punish a wasted W when healthy. If he stuns nothing and you hold health, the spent shield-bypass is your kill window.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Punish window
  abilities: [Pantheon.W]

- Hold Counter Strike against stun-and-retreat. His pattern pokes, stuns, and leaves before E catches him, so never throw E at the retreat.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: E patience
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Farm under Q pressure without answering. Each answered poke extends his winning pattern, so take the CS and concede the trade.
  sources: [ph45, procatking]
  heading: Farm focus
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

- Jump without stunning into his held E. He keeps E ready to block the jump-stun, so vary the engage and never stun on pattern.
  sources: [procatking]
  heading: Engage variety
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Pantheon.E]

## Mid lane

- Fight at level 6 instead of matching roams. Your combat ultimate beats his map-movement R, so take the duel while he looks elsewhere.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Level-6 turn
  abilities: [Jax.R, Pantheon.R]

- Take Exhaust for committed all-ins. The summoner answers his burst window and turns his engage into your kill.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Summoner call

## Win condition

- All-in when his W and E are both down. Without the stun and the block, his kit has no answer to your sustained damage.
  sources: [ph45, procatking]
  heading: Combo window
  abilities: [Pantheon.W, Pantheon.E]

- Push when he roams with R. Every departure costs him a wave, so shove hard and convert the lost farm into plates.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Roam punish
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]
