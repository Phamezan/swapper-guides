## Key tips

- Your Q enables everything: a missed Q removes most of your threat, so hold it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]

- He never Rs while your Q is up: the held binding is your shield.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Lux.Q]

- He engages when you spend Q: the cooldown is his entire window, so vary it.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He dodges E at max range: change the angle, never the timing.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

- Your Q-E-R converts a single binding: fish, never force.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His Edge of Night blanks one Q: bait the shield before committing.
  items: [3814]
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Shield baited plus Q landed: the Zed whose Edge never blanks you never kills you.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
