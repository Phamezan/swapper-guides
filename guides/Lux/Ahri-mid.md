## Key tips

- She never stands with the wave: aim Qs where she must dodge to.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Her dash is your Q target: after one dash, bind her.
  sources: [pekinwoof]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Lux.Q]

- She dodges E then shoves: hold E for guaranteed hits.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She fights for the wave with autos: match autos, keep E threat.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- She answers E-catches by shoving: never throw E without a plan.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

## Mid lane

- After 6 she respects bind into combo: Q then R wins.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.Q, Lux.R]

- She shoves to pin you: clear fast, roam before forced to base.
  sources: [pekinwoof]

## Win condition

- Her misses are her windows: a Lux who never misses never loses.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]
