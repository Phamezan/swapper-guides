## Key tips

- You are his biggest counter. He must keep his distance from you, so take the space he gives and force the fights he is avoiding.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- He wins only short trades — you win every extended trade. Never let him hit and leave for free; chase every trade past his comfort point.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Jump angles decide the lane. Never jump straight at him — angle Rocket Jump ({cd:18:W}) diagonally so his reply misses you on the way in.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Tristana.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Force extended trades from the first waves. His whole plan is short trades precisely because standing to fight loses to you.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and respect it while held. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Walk up when he wastes Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) dry. Without the follow-up root he has no answer to your jump.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Mid lane

- Meet his objective groups and jump in. He plays for W-plus-R casts because he cannot duel you — deny him the range those casts need.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R, Tristana.W]

- Sweep the objective flanks for his traps and fog picks. Denied vision denies his casts, and he cannot set up what he cannot see.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Drag every fight long. Each extra second favors your extended-trade win over his short-trade plan, so kite forward rather than bursting and leaving.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Break vision and stay mobile once his root connects. The instant Curtain Call shot after it is guaranteed unless you are already moving.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Build to extend fights. Your win is the long duel he refuses, so spend for sustained damage rather than poke.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
