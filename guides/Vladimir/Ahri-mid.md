## Overview

## Early lane

- She takes early trade windows on your Q and pool cooldowns: vary both.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q, Vladimir.W]

- She plays the map before you scale: match priority, do not bleed plates.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- Match her post-6 shoves: lost priority is lost map.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- Never commit blind into a held Charm: force it with Q feints first.
  sources: [peng04, elite500]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Pool timed plus Q windows banked: the Ahri who never lands Charm never wins.
  sources: [elite500, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]
