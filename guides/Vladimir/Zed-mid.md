## Key tips

- He is favoured early: range and all-ins beat your sustain.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Do not stand for his Q follow-up auto: walk off after it lands.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- All-in his oversteps with pool ready: no shadows means no escape.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Vladimir.W, Zed.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Concede early farm for HP: your scaling outlasts his lane.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Track his jungler: his team plays around his R.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

## Mid lane

- Pool his R: the dodge turns his ult.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Vladimir.W, Zed.R]

- Save pool for his R, not his poke: W the ult, eat the Qs.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Vladimir.W]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: pool toward the exit when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Vladimir.W]

## Win condition

- Early survived plus pool held: the Zed who never snowballs never outscales you.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
