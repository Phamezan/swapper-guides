## Early lane

- Trade from max Q range: his walkup is your window.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.Q]

- Dodge Q, especially with shadows out: the double shuriken is the damage.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

## Mid lane

- He exits R behind you: W back the moment he appears.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R, LeBlanc.W]

- Hold your W return until his shadows are used: snap back to safety.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: W back to your pad when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, LeBlanc.W]

## Win condition

- Walkups punished plus W held: the Zed who never dives you never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
