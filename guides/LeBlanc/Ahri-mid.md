## Key tips

- Skill matchup: her Charm cancels your W, so the interaction is everything.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, LeBlanc.W]

- Trade around your W cooldown (about {cd:7:W}): no W means no trade.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

- W from side angles instead of straight in: the angle beats her Charm.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W, Ahri.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She neutralises leads well: bank small edges, do not force.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- She out-pushes and is hard to kill: match waves, not egos.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

## Mid lane

- Take Teleport and play for roams and scaling: the kill is optional.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Her Charm answers straight Ws: side-angle every engage.
  sources: [shokleblanc, peng04]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W, Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Angles plus roams: the Ahri who never catches you never wins.
  sources: [peng04, shokleblanc]
