## Key tips

- She wins the trades: never take the straight exchange.
  sources: [shok]

- Her E cancels your W: the stun breaks your combo.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, LeBlanc.W]

- When you dash in, she stuns then E-Qs back: expect the return.
  sources: [trisend3]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W, Syndra.E, Syndra.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade only when her E is down: no scatter means no answer.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

- She has gank setup: track the jungler before trading.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Get Mercs: they buy survival against ganks, not the lane.
  sources: [shok]

- W from angles she cannot cover: her E is single-target.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

## Win condition

- E-dodged plus ganks survived: the Syndra who never stuns you never wins.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
