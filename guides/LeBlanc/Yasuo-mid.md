## Key tips

- Hard matchup: mobility dodges your W and E, Wind Wall blocks Q and E.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- His passive shield acts like Bone Plating: pop it before trading.
  sources: [pumptm]

- After a Q hit, W into mimicked W: neither can be windwalled.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.Q, LeBlanc.W]
