## Overview

## Early lane

- Take Hail of Blades and E in early: he respects the HoB opener.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Ekko.E]

- Proc passive for free when he lets you: unanswered E-Qs stack up.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Ekko.E]

## Mid lane

- His W is the answer tool: engage when shadows are spent.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- R heals through his combo: save R for the turn, not the engage.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Ekko.R]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: E away from the exit when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Ekko.E]

## Win condition

- Procs landed plus R held: the Zed who never zones you never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
