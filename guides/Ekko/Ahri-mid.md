## Overview

## Early lane

- E in, wait out her W speed, then proc the passive: patience lands the third hit.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Ekko.E]

- Do not Q wave and face for free: she punishes Qs with push.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ekko.Q]

## Mid lane

- A connecting W stun means no fear of Charm: stun first, commit after.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Ekko.W]

- After 6, watch R-E forward plus Ignite: clone placement decides the all-in.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Ekko.R]

## Win condition

- E varied plus clone placed: the Ahri who never Charms you never wins.
  sources: [pengekko, v222]
