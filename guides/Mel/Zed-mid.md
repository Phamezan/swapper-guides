## Key tips

- W used means you die: hold reflect for his all-in.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Mel.W]

- He calls ganks since you cannot escape: ward and track.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He pokes from 3 and backs off: reflect the second cast, not the first.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- In all-ins he autos first to bait W, then Q-E: never reflect the auto.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He steps back after WEQ: hold Q until he lands.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- E down means his aggressive all-ins: W is your life, so track yours.
  sources: [v222]

## Win condition

- Panic W loses: hold reflect until Qs fly.
  sources: [v222]
