## Key tips

- She never Charms into your reflect: Charm comes only once your W is down (about {cd:800:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Mel.W]

- She ults in casting one spell per dash: reflect the last, not the first.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ahri.E, Mel.W]

- She throws Charm from behind a minion to force W first: hold W anyway.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Mel.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She dodges your missiles with W speed: lead the Qs.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Mel.Q, Mel.E]

- Her fox-fires cannot be dodged and proc Electrocute: short W trades are hers, so respect.
  sources: [evandabank]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

## Mid lane

- She punishes your weak waveclear: her Q spam takes priority, so match it.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- She answers instead of tanking minions: poke from range, never walk in.
  sources: [evandabank]

## Win condition

- She converts priority to map plays: pin her or lose the map.
  sources: [evandabank]

- She duels only at Charm-up angles: reflect down means no duel.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Mel.W]
