## Key tips

- He takes no early trades: concede the lane, win the all-in.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- After Voltaic he pokes: respect the spike.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- His Q curves from his right side: walk into it to make it miss.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Poke, do not commit early: his backwards combo punishes engages.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Track his W: shadow-down is your engage.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- His backwards WEQ answers your jump: bait it before committing.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Diana.E]

- He holds R to dodge yours: bait the R before committing yours.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Diana.R]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: E to the exit when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Diana.E]

## Win condition

- Engage baited plus R answered: the Zed who never holds answers never kills you.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
