## Key tips

- Dodge his E and answer with Fox-Fire timing and movement; his poke is the lane ({cd:112:E}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Viktor.E, Ahri.W]

- Walk up to trade in the window after he uses Q ({cd:112:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q]

- Do not force straight trades: he wins them at nearly every stage, so convert Charm into ganks instead.
  sources: [shokviktor]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Make him miss obvious Es: move with your W timing rather than standing still to cast.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Viktor.E, Ahri.W]

- After his Q, step in and trade while it recharges ({cd:112:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q]

## Mid lane

- Read his upgrade: Q first means a safe scaling lane, E first means he pushes and roams with his team, so match the roam.
  sources: [intaggar]

- Dodging your Charm is his top priority, so never throw it on a predictable rhythm.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Your kills come from Charm gank setup, not from winning trades: set up your jungler once vision lapses.
  sources: [shokviktor]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- When he pushes out to roam, answer with your own map movement instead of chasing the duel.
  sources: [intaggar]
