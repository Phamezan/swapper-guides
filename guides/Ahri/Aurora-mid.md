## Key tips

- Dodge her Q and E poke, especially while walking up to CS.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q, Aurora.E]

- Punish her cooldown windows and oversteps: step in only when Q or E is down.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q, Aurora.E]

- Whoever lands first wins: match her range and strike first.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not force trades in levels 1-5: her poke wins them, so wait for cooldown windows.
  sources: [peng04]

- Regain control by dodging at CS time: every dodged spell is a won trade.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q, Aurora.E]

## Mid lane

- The lane turns into a waveclear contest: shove and skirmish better.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Match her shove; never hand her free wave control.
  sources: [shok-ahri, auroraaddict]

## Win condition

- Win the skirmish game around shoves, not the 1v1.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Keep punishing cooldowns all game; her windows are your only openings.
  sources: [peng04]
