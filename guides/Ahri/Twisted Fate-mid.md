## Key tips

- Trade in the window after he uses Pick a Card ({cd:4:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.W]

- Time Charm onto his Gold Card walk-up so it lands at the same moment.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Twisted Fate.W]

- Sneak Qs onto him when he walks up to last-hit.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish his weak waveclear and trading: pressure the lane from the start.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Hit him with Q every time he steps to the minions.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- Take even 2v2 and 3v3 skirmishes: his kit wants numbers advantages, so fair fights favour you.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- After 6, look for big chunks: ulting in to trade health is fine from a good poke angle.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

- Keep pressure on him and the lane so he cannot roam freely, and answer when he ults away.
  sources: [shok-ahri, jammed]

## Win condition

- He baits Charm with fake steps and unlocked cards, so do not bite: hold for the real Gold lock.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Twisted Fate.W]

- Shove and move first: the mid-game axis is roam pressure, and your shove limits his.
  sources: [shok-ahri, jammed]
