## Key tips

- Stand on the side away from her ball.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- When she spends the ball, walk up and force her back.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- You hold the trading edge in levels 1-5 if you space around the ball well.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use autos to keep wave control so she gets no free harass time.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Step in on every spent ball while she cannot answer.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

## Mid lane

- From 6 on, one-shot the wave and play from fog and river.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- Your waveclear grows enough to neutralize the lane: shove and look elsewhere.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]

## Win condition

- Win through waveclear and roams, not duels.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]

- Ask for help when behind: this lane is won with jungle support as much as spacing.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
