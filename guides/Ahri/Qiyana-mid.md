  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Abuse the first two levels, then set the wave middle or pushing back to you for her level-3 spike.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Never let the wave sit in a bad position.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- From 6, roam and secure vision control with priority.
  sources: [peng04]

- Track her engage and answer with defensive Charm, not damage first.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Roam with prio: your map pressure beats her duel threat.
  sources: [peng04]

- Keep waves safe all game and deny her engage angles.
  sources: [peng04]
