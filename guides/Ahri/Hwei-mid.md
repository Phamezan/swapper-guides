## Key tips

- Dodge his E spells with W speed.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Hwei.E]

- Walk up and trade when his cooldowns are spent ({cd:910:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q]

- He is immobile: ganks and catches in fights are easy.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect his early poke: his max-range Q out-trades you while he last-hits.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q]

- Trade only into spent cooldowns, never into his full rotation.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- Set up ganks and catches in fights: his immobility is the window, not duels.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Do not take straight 1v1 poke trades; you win picked fights, not poke wars.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]

## Win condition

- Convert picks and jungle plays into map control.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Keep dodging his E all game: it stays his key spell.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Hwei.E]
