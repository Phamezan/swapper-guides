## Key tips

- Track which minions he can E to and stand where those angles cannot reach you.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E]

- Play skillshots around Wind Wall and go in when it is down ({cd:157:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- Do not duel him: you outperform him in teamfights, so play for those.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep the wave on your side and take care pushed up: he is strong in lane but very gankable.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Concede blind level-1 prio rather than losing XP: falling behind early hands him the easier lane.
  sources: [pengyasuo]

- After he dashes through a minion, that path is closed: he cannot E it again for a while.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E]

## Mid lane

- Call your jungler: his lane strength invites ganks, and he cannot answer them well.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Keep sidestepping his dash angles all game: the minion wave is his mobility.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E]

- Aim Charm where he sidesteps, not at the minion he hides behind: Q toward the blocker, then Charm the dodge.
  sources: [pengyasuo]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q, Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Win teamfights, not duels: avoid fair 1v1s all game.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Convert ganks when he pushes up; never give him the straight fight he wants.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Itemization

- First base into Lost Chapter plus boots: the spike plus the movement to space his dashes.
  sources: [pengyasuo]
  items: [3802, 1001]
