## Key tips

- Bait Playful Trickster: fake step in when minions are low, walk back, then play while it is down ({cd:105:E}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- If he commits an obvious Q, Charm him mid-travel: he cannot E while his Q is moving.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Fizz.Q]

- Abuse levels 1-2 hard, then let the wave come back to your side.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Bully the first two levels, then drop back and keep the wave in a safe position at all times.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]

- Never get caught far up the lane: he runs you down, especially if your Charm is already spent.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- After 6, respect his all-in and his gank setup instead of overextending.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Fizz.R]

- Expect him to E your Fox-Fire and trade through Bone Plating: keep trades to short procs.
  sources: [spaceturtlez]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Fizz.E]

## Win condition

- He outscales while you push by default: win before late game on early leads and E-down windows.
  sources: [spaceturtlez, peng04]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Close through E-down punishes: every baited E is your kill window.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]
