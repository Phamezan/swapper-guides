## Key tips

- Never stand with the wave: force Lux to choose between hitting you and clearing minions.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Do not dash forward carelessly: after one dash you are her Q target.
  sources: [pekinwoof]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Lux.Q]

- Dodge her E; if it lands, shove the wave over and harass while she waits on it.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Fight for the wave with autos from level 1; stand off-wave so she must pick a target.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- If her E catches you, answer by shoving over and harassing while she waits.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

## Mid lane

- After 6, getting caught by Q is very costly: respect the bind into full combo.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.Q, Lux.R]

- Shove hard to pin her: she cannot roam when forced to base over and over.
  sources: [pekinwoof]

## Win condition

- Convert her misses into picks: every dodged Q is your window.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]

- Respect her bind range all game: one Q decides every phase.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]
