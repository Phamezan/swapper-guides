## Key tips

- Stay near minions so they block her E sleep bubble.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Zoe.E]

- Answer her bubble with W; never Charm the instant she casts her portal.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Zoe.R]

- When she ults in, ult and Charm the spot she cast from: she snaps back to it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ahri.E, Zoe.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Survive levels 1-5: concede pressure and punish only spent cooldowns.
  sources: [peng04]

- If she holds Exhaust or Ignite, keep Charm defensive; she may force an all-in.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- Punish every portal forward: it is your free Charm and a won trade.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Zoe.R]

- Call your jungler from 6 on: duels favour her, ganks favour you.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

## Win condition

- Win through gank setups and cooldown punishes, not straight duels: she finds the 1v1 easy.
  sources: [shok-zoe, peng04]

- Keep minion cover all game: positioning decides the lane.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Zoe.E]
