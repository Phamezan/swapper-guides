## Key tips

- Dodge the cage with W speed ({cd:45:E}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Veigar.E]

- Push him from level 1: he must match your waveclear and cannot stop you.
  sources: [starzall, shok-ahri]

- Punish his last hits in levels 1-5; he is very weak early.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Step up as minions get low so he must choose between Q-ing you and last-hitting.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- Keep him pinned: he is hard to trade with but cannot hold the wave.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- After 6, shove and roam to snowball before he scales.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Coordinate dives with your jungler: from 6 on, a diver plus your Charm beats his cage.
  sources: [starzall]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Veigar.E]

## Win condition

- End before his scaling matters: his power only grows.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Keep pushing and moving: a pinned Veigar answers nothing.
  sources: [starzall, shok-ahri]
