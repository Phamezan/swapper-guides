## Key tips

- Dodge her stun first: everything she does starts from the Q into E combo ({cd:134:E}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Syndra.E]

- If the stun catches you anyway, buffer Charm or Orb through it so you trade damage back instead of taking the combo for free.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.Q]

- Do not force the duel. Syndra wins the 1v1 clearly; her real fear is your Charm setting up a gank, so play for your jungler.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Move before she lines up the stun: sidestep once the ball is placed, not after she fires.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Syndra.E]

- When stunned, answer with a buffered spell rather than waiting it out.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.Q]

## Mid lane

- Coordinate Charm ganks: she has to track and respect your jungler, so set up picks when her vision lapses.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Keep the same stun discipline as her combo scales: dodge first, buffer second.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Your win condition is a Charm pick with your team, not a solo kill on her.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Stay patient when even or behind: she punishes every forced fight.
  sources: [shok]
