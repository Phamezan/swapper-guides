## Key tips

- Track his cooldowns and punish each one: Q, W and E all open a window ({cd:101:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q, Xerath.W, Xerath.E]

- Use W speed bursts to dodge his skillshots.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- Fake an auto-attack approach to bait his spells, then walk in.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not stand with the wave: force him to choose between hitting you and clearing minions.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Bait first, trade second: every missed spell is your window.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- After 6, call your jungler and set up ganks on him.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- He stands behind minions to block Charm, so find angles around the wave instead of through it.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- After 6, your gank setups decide the lane more than poke trades.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Deny him free poke all game: off-wave positioning never stops mattering.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
