## Key tips

- Spacing is everything: do not let him stand in range to stack nails into E.
  sources: [penglocke]
  abilities: [Locke.Q, Locke.E]

- Level 1 is yours: W speed forces Electrocute trades he should refuse.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- Hold Charm until after his Soul Ignition dodge: he pops W to slip it as he engages.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Locke.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not get stacked into E: back off once the nails build up.
  sources: [penglocke]
  abilities: [Locke.Q, Locke.E]

- From level 2 to 5 he only fires Ritual Nails when you stand on his half of the lane, so manage where you stand.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

## Mid lane

- He heals back the damage he takes under Soul Ignition: Ignite him when he pops it.
  sources: [penglocke]
  abilities: [Locke.W]

- He keeps fighting after 6 and plays with his jungler, so bring your own help instead of dueling him alone.
  sources: [sephyrixx]

## Win condition

- His whole plan banks on owning the early game, so reaching mid game healthy is already winning it.
  sources: [sephyrixx]

- Do not trade Purgatory for Spirit Rush lightly: his recharge is shorter, which hands him a window after yours is spent ({cd:805:R}).
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Locke.R, Ahri.R]
