## Key tips

- Track his E above all: everything starts once it is down ({cd:245:E}).
  sources: [pengekko, v222]
  abilities: [Ekko.E]

- Punish the moment he Qs the wave: push up and give him no window to breathe.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ekko.Q]

- Bait his Q onto you so he cannot last-hit and clear at the same time.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ekko.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Auto the wave from level 1 and fight for priority; the XP lead sets up your level-6 jump.
  sources: [pengekko]

- Take the level-3 punish when his spells are down; never let him breathe.
  sources: [pengekko]

## Mid lane

- At 6, jump him when he does not expect it: ult forward into Charm.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ahri.E]

- Do not waste Charm showing off: open R-W-Q and save Charm for when it counts.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ahri.W, Ahri.Q]

## Win condition

- In teamfights, hold Charm for Ekko diving your backline: he never expects it.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- End him when he E's away: the skill-check ult forward wins it.
  sources: [pengekko]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ekko.E]

- Match his roams from mid game: never hand him free wave control.
  sources: [v222]

## Itemization

- Bramble Vest blunts his E-onto-face all-in, and Ignite cuts his healing in the 1v1.
  sources: [pengekko]
