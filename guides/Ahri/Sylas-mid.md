## Key tips

- Track Abscond ({cd:517:E}) and trade only while it is down; it is both his engage and his escape.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- When his second dash connects, do not panic: buffer Charm through it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Sylas.E]

- Bait out the dash before going aggressive, including under his tower.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Let him spend his E on the wave or on a trade, then answer with autos and Orb while it recharges ({cd:517:E}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Sylas.E, Ahri.Q]

- If the second dash lands on you, hold your nerve and Charm through it instead of burning everything to escape.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Sylas.E]

## Mid lane

- Trading ults is acceptable: once he steals Spirit Rush he cannot steal it again right away.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Sylas.R, Ahri.R]

- Keep forcing the dash out before committing Charm; never throw Charm into a Sylas who still holds E.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Sylas.E]

## Win condition

- Under his tower, bait the E first and only then play fully aggressive.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- Run the lane on his E windows: with no dash ready he has no engage, so press every window.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]
