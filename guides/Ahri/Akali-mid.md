## Key tips

- Hold Charm. She waits until your ult and Charm are both down before she all-ins, and she hides behind the wave and her shroud to dodge it.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.R]

- Punish her while Twilight Shroud is down ({cd:84:W}): with no shroud she cannot dodge, so walk up and play harder.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

- Take the early auto, Fox-Fire, auto Electrocute pattern whenever she offers it, and deny her the same.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Track her energy: her Q drains it fast, so extend trades once she has spent a couple of casts.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- Stack Manaflow Band early so you have the mana to survive the lane.
  sources: [peng04]

- Keep trades to the short Electrocute pattern and step out; extending against her early loses.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

## Mid lane

- When she goes in to proc her passive, expect her to answer with shroud speed to sidestep Charm: wait out the sidestep before throwing it.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Akali.W]

- She blocks Charm with the minion wave, so throw it when she steps clear of the wave.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Stay even and let scaling work: she rarely converts more than a small lead, and you get more annoying with every item.
  sources: [anguish]

## Itemization

- Build toward ability haste into Horizon Focus: the Akali main names it as the scaling point she dislikes on Ahri.
  sources: [anguish]
  items: [4628]
