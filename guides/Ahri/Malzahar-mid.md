## Key tips

- Never throw Charm into his spell shield: pop it first, then Charm.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Malzahar.P]

- Time Charm for the moment he starts casting his ult.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Malzahar.R]

- Bully him early: he has almost no trading tools, and you match his push.
  sources: [xblademojo, shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Pressure him from the first waves: your early kill threat forces him to respect you.
  sources: [xblademojo]

- Kill voidlings as soon as they spawn so his waveclear stays manageable.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Match his push and deny him free shoves.
  sources: [xblademojo]

## Mid lane

- After 6, only step forward when you know where the enemy jungler is: his ult plus a jungler ends you.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- He hides behind minions to block Charm, so time it against his shield or his cast, not into the wave.
  sources: [xblademojo, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Malzahar.P]

## Win condition

- Keep the pressure on through mid game: the one thing that makes the game easy for him is you respecting him, so do not.
  sources: [xblademojo]

- Start fights by popping the shield and then landing Charm.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Malzahar.P]
