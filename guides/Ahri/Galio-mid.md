## Key tips

- Poke him freely in the early levels; play around his Q while doing it ({cd:3:Q}).
  sources: [peng04, hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.Q]

- If your Charm is spent, watch for his all-in: E dash into W taunt.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Galio.E, Galio.W]

- Dodge with W speed: it answers his E dash.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Galio.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Bully him before the first base; after that he is hard to kill.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Keep Charm up while trading: without it he can engage on you.
  sources: [peng04, hexeria]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- The lane becomes a waveclear contest: shove and skirmish better.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Match his roams once he shoves: he plays with his jungler, so hand him no free side plays.
  sources: [hexeria]

## Win condition

- He believes he outscales and kills you later: close before that and keep pressuring.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Win through skirmishes and picks, not by sieging into him.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
