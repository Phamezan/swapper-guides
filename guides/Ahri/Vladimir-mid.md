  sources: [elite500, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Vladimir.W]

- Back off when his empowered Q is about to be ready, and trade around his Q windows ({cd:8:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]

- Pick trade windows carefully: bait him into fighting inside your minion wave.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take early trade windows on his Q and pool cooldowns.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q, Vladimir.W]

- Fight inside your wave so his trades cost him more minion damage.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- From 6, one-shot the wave and use the priority for vision control.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- On your first spike, shove and impact the map instead of sitting in duels with a scaler.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Win condition

- Play the map before he becomes a monster: your window is spikes and priority, not late duels.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Never commit Charm blind: bait or wait out the pool first.
  sources: [peng04, elite500]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Vladimir.W]
