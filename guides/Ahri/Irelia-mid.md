## Key tips

- Do not push unless you clear in one go: her sustain makes her nearly impossible to push out.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Hold Charm for defence until she commits; never throw it mid-dash.
  sources: [shok-ahri, acee1-irelia]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Her edge is Charm's long cooldown: every missed Charm is her window ({cd:103:E}).
  sources: [acee1-irelia]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Priority is not dying: give up CS if needed.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Expect her to dodge with Q resets; wait for the reset chain to end.
  sources: [acee1-irelia]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Mid lane

- Concede the 1v1 and play for teamfights: you handle her there even when behind.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Track Charm's cooldown above all: while it is down, give ground.
  sources: [acee1-irelia]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Win the game in teamfights, not the lane.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Stay healthy and relevant: the lane is about survival, the game about teamfights.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
