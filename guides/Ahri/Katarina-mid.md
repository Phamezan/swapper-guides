## Key tips

- Hold Charm for the moment she Shunpos to a dagger: that blink is the window to land it.
  sources: [peng04, acee1]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Katarina.E]

- Respect her level 2: her E-start all-in beats yours and can force Flash or a kill.
  sources: [acee1]
  abilities: [Katarina.E, Katarina.Q]

- After 6, save ult dashes to dodge her daggers and her ult: she holds Shunpo until your ult is down.
  sources: [acee1]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Katarina.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade while her Q is down ({cd:55:Q}) instead of into it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Katarina.Q]

- Give up ground at level 2: her E in, auto, Q, dagger grab, E again line beats your level 2.
  sources: [acee1]
  abilities: [Katarina.E, Katarina.Q]

- Keep the wave in a good spot so she cannot run you down.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- Expect her to dodge Charm sideways rather than backwards when she engages; lead her landing spot.
  sources: [acee1]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Katarina.E]

- When she shoves to roam, shove back and track her instead of following without ult.
  sources: [acee1]

## Win condition

- Deny her free roams: match her tempo, punish the lane she leaves, and answer with your own map plays.
  sources: [acee1]

- When behind, stay patient with Charm and play safe rather than forcing the duel.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]
