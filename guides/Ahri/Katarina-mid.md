## Key tips

- Hold Charm for the moment she Shunpos to a dagger: that blink is the window to land it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Katarina.E]

- Do not step on daggers, and hold Charm defensively.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Trade while her Q is down ({cd:55:Q}) instead of into it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Katarina.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep the wave in a good spot so she cannot run you down.
  sources: [peng04]

- Play the lane slowly; do not hand her snowball kills.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- After 6, keep her shoved in so she has no windows to roam.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- When behind, stay patient with Charm and play safe rather than forcing the duel.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Win through wave control and denied roams, not duels.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
