## Key tips

- Track his empowered third Q and stay out of its range while it is ready.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- When he ults onto you, Charm behind you: he always ends past you.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Yone.R]

- Hold Charm defensively unless he wastes his cooldowns.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect his windows: with Q3 or E ready, back off.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Give ground on his empowered Q, then trade back after.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Harass him hard at level 1 and abuse your range while slow-stacking the wave.
  sources: [sjorry]

- Take E at level 2 unless your spacing is perfect; it stops him getting on top of you.
  sources: [sjorry]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- After 6, do not get run down while pushed up; watch his gank setup.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Answer every ult onto you with behind-you Charm.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Yone.R]

## Win condition

- Stay safe post-6 and let him waste windows; your Charm answers his engage.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Turn every wasted window: an E or Q3 spent without a kill is your turn to trade.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.Q]
