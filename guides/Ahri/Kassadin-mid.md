## Key tips

- Bait his Q before going for a spell trade ({cd:38:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Kassadin.Q]

- Harass him every time he walks up to CS; bank gold, not just damage.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- After 6, turn priority into dragon, dives or a kill on him; never settle for even farming.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Bully hard from level 1: your burst and Charm pressure can ruin his lane.
  sources: [shok-ahri, xblademojo-kass]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Punish every CS walk-up and back out with more gold.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- Keep pushing and roaming: he always lacks waveclear, so snowball the map.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Hold Charm defensively for when he jumps on you.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Cash the lead before he outscales: without a big lead he beats you late.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Force plays on dragon and dives with priority; even lanes lose to him.
  sources: [peng04]
