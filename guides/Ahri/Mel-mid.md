## Key tips

- Never Charm into her reflect: Charm only once her W is down ({cd:800:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Mel.W]

- When ulting in, use one spell per dash and save Charm for after her W.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Ahri.E, Mel.W]

- Throw Charm from behind a minion to force her W out first.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Mel.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Dodge her Q missiles and her E with W speed; never walk in to trade while they are up.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Mel.Q, Mel.E]

- Your fox-fires cannot be dodged and proc Electrocute, so short W trades are free.
  sources: [evandabank]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

## Mid lane

- Punish her weak waveclear: spam Q on the wave and take priority.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- She pokes from longer range and wants you tanking her minions when you walk in, so hold your ground and answer.
  sources: [evandabank]

## Win condition

- You get lane priority: convert it to map plays while she is pinned.
  sources: [evandabank]

- Duel only at Charm-up angles: without her reflect down, do not force it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Mel.W]
