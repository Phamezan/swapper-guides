- Dodge her E with W speed: eating it starts her fear pressure ({cd:711:E}).
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Vex.E]

- Never dash in while her fear is up: every ult dash procs her passive into fear.
  sources: [shok-ahri, irdefender]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Vex.P]

- Poke with Q and punish wasted cooldowns; trading into her is hard.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play around her E in the early levels; W answers it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Vex.E, Ahri.W]

- Her Q poke outranges yours: concede range and answer cooldowns.
  sources: [irdefender, peng04]
  abilities: [Vex.Q]

## Mid lane

- From 6, secure vision and play out of fog.
  sources: [peng04]

- She holds fear for your commits: bait it out before going in.
  sources: [irdefender]
  abilities: [Vex.W]

## Win condition

- She answers committed dashes with fear plus shield: split your pressure, never go all-in blind.
  sources: [irdefender]
  abilities: [Ahri.R, Vex.W]

- Without Charm she feels no kill pressure: land it or leave.
  sources: [irdefender]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]
