## Key tips

- Trade while Distortion is down; her W recharge is far longer than your spells ({cd:7:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

- Charm her mid-dash to cancel it: landing that decides the lane.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, LeBlanc.W]

- Watch the flanks: she fears the cancel, so she dashes in from side angles instead of straight at you.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish obvious dashes: when she Ws in plainly, answer with Charm on the dash.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, LeBlanc.W]

- Take every W-down window to trade; while it recharges she cannot answer ({cd:7:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

## Mid lane

- After 6, punish her once she has dashed and spent another spell: she has no dash left for a while.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

- Push her in: she wants Teleport roams and scaling, so deny the roam with priority and track her.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

## Win condition

- Keep the wave cleared and answer her roams: you neutralise leads well and she rarely gets more than a small advantage.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Stay out of kill range and let your push and picks win: she is hard for you to kill, and that cuts both ways.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
