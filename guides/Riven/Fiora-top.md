## Key tips

- Draw out Riposte before committing Ki Burst or Wind Slash ({cd:114:W}). Its parry can deny your burst, and its return thrust slows attacks even without a stun.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Fiora.W, Riven.R]

- Hold Valor when you use your third Broken Wings cast on her. She can read that cast for Riposte, so dash sideways after it to leave its line.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Fiora.W]

- Hug a wall when Grand Challenge marks you. Denying access to one Vital makes it harder for Fiora to trigger the complete four-Vital heal.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

## Overview

- Vital positioning and Riposte decide the lane. Riven can threaten early burst, but a recovery kill makes Fiora's later dueling much harder to contain.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

## Early lane

- Play level 1 close to your minions and punish a missed Lunge. A miss gets no hit-based cooldown refund ({cd:114:Q}); check that she did not hit another unit.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

- Walk out of her vision to move your Vital to a worse spot for her. Do not offer a front Vital she can Lunge onto for free.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

- Freeze near your turret after letting her shove first. A long lane punishes Riposte waste, and your turret covers trades she tries to extend.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, all-in a chipped Fiora when Riposte is spent. If your third Broken Wings cast lands, follow promptly with Ki Burst rather than leaving a gap for her parry.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Fiora.W]

- Disengage if Riposte hits you and your burst is no longer lethal. Its attack-speed reduction weakens Runic Blade attacks even when the thrust only slows you.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.P, Fiora.W]

## Win condition

- Keep a lead safe rather than giving Fiora a recovery kill. Her Vital damage and Grand Challenge make later side-lane duels dangerous even after you win lane.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for early trading. Consider Executioner's Calling when Vital healing matters during fights, and Plated Steelcaps against her physical attacks; armor does not reduce Vital true damage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  items: [1055, 2003, 3123, 3047]
