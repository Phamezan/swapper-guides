## Key tips

- Avoid spending Runic Blade attacks into Spirit's Refuge. Use Ki Burst if you need to stall him, then resume attacks only after the blocking zone ends.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.P, Riven.W, Shen.W]

- Interrupt Stand United with Ki Burst or your third Broken Wings cast whenever he channels in reach, and chase him when he walks away to find an ultimate angle.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Shen.R]

- Track Shen's spirit blade before trading. If Twilight Assault misses its pull-through and Ki Barrier has expired, he has less protection for your next exchange.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Shen.P, Shen.Q]

## Overview

- Respect empowered Twilight Assault attacks and Spirit's Refuge. After level 6, make Stand United cost him waves or interrupt the channel.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Shen.Q, Shen.W, Shen.R]

## Early lane

- At level 1, draw Twilight Assault to trigger Ki Barrier and remove Bone Plating with a delayed third Broken Wings cast. Wait for those defenses to expire before committing to attacks.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Shen.P, Shen.Q]

- Do not let the returning spirit blade pass through you during Twilight Assault. That collision strengthens Shen's next attacks; if it happens, leave their range rather than fighting into them.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Shen.Q]

- Dodge Shadow Dash with a saved dash. If it connects, Ki Burst and leave when the taunt ends instead of staying beside empowered Twilight Assault attacks.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Shen.Q, Shen.E]

## Mid lane

- After level 6, keep a wave pushing into Shen before fights elsewhere when you can do so safely. Stand United then costs him minions and turret damage instead of giving him a free departure.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Shen.R]

- Avoid trading beneath Shen's turret with Shadow Dash ready. The taunt can force an attack on him and draw turret aggression even if you intended to leave after a short trade.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Shen.E]

## Win condition

- Once Shen has spent Spirit's Refuge and his empowered Twilight Assault attacks, you can extend an exchange if your health and spells allow it. Avoid letting him win through his initial burst, then leaving before your remaining damage lands.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Shen.Q, Shen.W]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for damage when Twilight Assault protection is spent. Item damage cannot make Runic Blade attacks connect through Spirit's Refuge, so preserve those attacks until it ends.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.P, Shen.Q, Shen.W]
  items: [1055, 2003]
