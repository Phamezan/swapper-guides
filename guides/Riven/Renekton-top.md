## Key tips

- Stay outside Ruthless Predator range when Renekton has at least 50 Fury. Its empowered hit destroys Valor's shield before dealing damage, so shielding in place is not protection.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Renekton.W]

- Control the wave to limit Renekton's safe attacks on minions. Denying that access slows his Fury generation before the next Ruthless Predator trade.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Renekton.W]

- Shield Cull the Meek with Valor and take Bone Plating in runes against Ruthless Predator. Surviving his first rotation is what creates your trade back.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Renekton.Q, Renekton.W]

## Overview

- Fury and Ruthless Predator decide whether you can approach. Preserve health, deny safe Fury generation and punish Slice and Dice recovery.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Renekton.W, Renekton.E]

## Early lane

- Cheese level 1 only before he gains experience and Fury, and only if he started Cull the Meek. If he started Ruthless Predator, respect the stun and farm.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Renekton.Q, Renekton.W]

- Take brief trades with a minion advantage, but do not treat the wave as a shield. Slice and Dice passes through units, and hitting a unit enables his second dash.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Renekton.E]

- Below half health Renekton generates Fury faster. Check his bar again before the next trade instead of assuming chip damage has made an empowered Ruthless Predator less likely.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Renekton.W]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, consider an Ignite all-in after Renekton wastes Ruthless Predator ({cd:58:W}) or Cull the Meek ({cd:58:Q}). Check his Fury, health and Dominus before committing; one spent spell is no guarantee.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Renekton.Q, Renekton.W, Renekton.R]

- Abuse Slice and Dice cooldown ({cd:58:E}) after he dashes in and out. Chase that window instead of trading while both dashes are ready.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Renekton.E]

## Win condition

- After a short trade, leave enough distance that Slice and Dice cannot immediately punish the exit. Do not spend every dash beside a Renekton who still has a second dash ready.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Renekton.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion with Bone Plating to survive early burst. Plated Steelcaps add armor against his physical damage; they do not stop empowered Ruthless Predator from breaking Valor's shield.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Renekton.W]
  items: [1055, 2003, 3047]

- Black Cleaver is an option into armor for health, haste and physical-damage shred. Death's Dance is a defensive first-item alternative when surviving Renekton's burst matters more than snowballing damage.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  items: [3071, 6333]
