## Key tips

- Freeze after pushing the first waves to bounce back. A frozen wave near your turret forces Kayle to walk up for every minion and gives you a long lane to run her down.
  sources: [saltyy]

- After level 6, threaten a committed all-in rather than trading your entire burst into Divine Judgment. Save damage or disengage during the invulnerability, then punish its cooldown.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Kayle.R]

- Build your lead before level 11. Kayle becomes ranged at level 6, then gains stronger attack pressure at level 11; do not treat the earlier ranged spike as harmless.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Overview

- Deny early farm with a bounced wave and freeze. Her ranged and later attack spikes make an even lane less attractive than an early experience lead.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Early lane

- At level 1, use a delayed third Broken Wings cast to remove Bone Plating before committing. Kayle can win sustained attack trades while your Q is down, so wait for the next rotation.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q]

- Threaten trades that force Celestial Blessing. Each cast spends mana on healing and movement speed; repeated defensive casts leave Kayle fewer resources for farming and escaping your next engage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Kayle.W]

- Shield her empowered Starfire Spellblade auto and Radiant Blast with Valor when you walk up. Do not give her free poke while you set the freeze.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Kayle.Q, Kayle.E]

## Mid lane

- Trade ultimates when doing so leaves you safe: at equal ability haste, Blade of the Exile ({cd:92:R}) returns before Divine Judgment ({cd:10:R}). Check her haste before relying on that gap.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Kayle.R]

- Ghost can replace Ignite if you need chase distance when Kayle pokes near your turret. Run her down along the long lane, while still saving damage for after Divine Judgment.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Kayle.R]

## Win condition

- Punish Kayle whenever she steps into reach of your freeze. If she stays back, deny the minions instead of needlessly pushing the wave toward the safety of her turret.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and a Health Potion to sustain through Radiant Blast and attack poke while maintaining a freeze. Preserving health lets you punish her walk-ups without giving away an extended attack trade.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Kayle.Q]
  items: [1054, 2003]
