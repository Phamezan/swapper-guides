## Key tips

- Dash through Ornn before Bellows Breath's final tick hits you. Avoiding that tick prevents its Brittle mark and the empowered attack that would consume it.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Ornn.W]

- Stay away from walls and the Volcanic Rupture pillar while Searing Charge is ready. A collision there gives Ornn a knockup that your shield cannot prevent.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Ornn.Q, Ornn.E]

- Save Ki Burst and the third Broken Wings knockup until Bellows Breath finishes. Ornn is unstoppable during the breath, so using crowd control then loses its interruption value.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Ornn.W]

## Overview

- Avoid Bellows Breath's final hit and Searing Charge terrain setups. Early pressure is valuable before his armor makes repeated kill attempts wasteful.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Ornn.W, Ornn.E]

## Early lane

- Pressure at level 1 if Ornn did not learn Bellows Breath, after removing Bone Plating. Do not assume a free all-in into a ready breath and Brittle attack.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Ornn.W]

- Remove Bone Plating, crash the third wave, recall, then freeze and bully every farm attempt. Away from his turret he must walk into your full rotation.
  sources: [saltyy]

- Use Valor to absorb damage from Ornn's Brittle attack if you cannot avoid it. The shield reduces damage taken, but does not prevent the mark from being consumed or its knockback.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.E]

## Mid lane

- Pressure Ornn before he purchases armor, including armor forged in lane with Living Forge. Once he is too durable, take farm instead of forcing repeated Brittle trades.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Ornn.P]

- Keep Ki Burst or the third Broken Wings cast for Ornn's Call of the Forge God recast. Interrupt him before he redirects the ram to deny the returning knockup.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Ornn.R]

## Win condition

- Trade immediately after Bellows Breath is spent ({cd:516:W}), provided Brittle is not still on you. Do not extend merely because the fire animation ended if he can still consume the mark.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Ornn.W]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for early pressure, or Doran's Shield if poke prevents trades. Black Cleaver supplies armor shred, health and haste once Ornn builds defenses; it does not prevent Brittle damage or knockups.
  sources: [saltyy]
  items: [1055, 1054, 2003, 3071]
