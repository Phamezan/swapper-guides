## Key tips

- Look to engage just after Jayce changes from hammer to cannon form. His transform must recover before he can return to Thundering Blow; the opening is brief, not the whole cannon stance.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Jayce.E]

- Before level 6, only last-hit when Valor or a Broken Wings cast is ready to dodge Shock Blast. Farming without a dash ready costs too much health.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Jayce.Q]

- At level 6, bait Thundering Blow before committing Blade of the Exile. Your new ultimate adds burst, while Jayce still relies on his existing hammer and cannon spells.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Jayce.E]

## Overview

- Preserve health against Shock Blast before level 6. Bait Thundering Blow or use the brief transform lockout to reach him with your ultimate available.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Jayce.Q, Jayce.E]

## Early lane

- Let him push into you and farm under your side of lane. A pushed Jayce is exposed to ganks and cannot poke you off every minion from range.
  sources: [saltyy]

- If he jumps on you with To the Skies!, Ki Burst him on landing and trade briefly, then leave before Thundering Blow knocks you into his cannon swap.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Jayce.Q, Jayce.E]

- Cheese at level 1 only when he lacks Phase Rush or facechecks brush. Otherwise respect cannon poke and preserve health for level 6.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Mid lane

- Respect Jayce's early lethality purchases, such as Serrated Dirk. Their armor penetration makes physical poke more punishing, so preserve health instead of assuming a level-6 all-in is automatic.
  sources: [saltyy]

- Build ability haste to make delayed Broken Wings trades more frequent. Re-engage when Thundering Blow is actually spent; changing form alone does not consume the knockback cooldown.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Jayce.E]

## Win condition

- During an all-in, hold the third Broken Wings cast while Jayce is in hammer form with Thundering Blow ready. He can cancel your knockup with his knockback.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Jayce.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and a Health Potion with Second Wind against cannon poke. Preserving health for level 6 gives more useful all-in chances than losing the lane to repeated Shock Blasts.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Jayce.Q]
  items: [1054, 2003]
