## Key tips

- Trade after Gwen spends Snip Snip! ({cd:887:Q}), especially a stacked cast on the wave. The window is brief because its cooldown falls with ranks.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q]

- Bait Hallowed Mist before the all-in. Gwen gains resistances inside it and is protected from enemies outside; wait out the mist rather than firing Wind Slash from outside it.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Gwen.W, Riven.R]

- Never stand still for Needlework. Keep moving with Broken Wings casts between autos so each needle wave misses part of its damage.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Gwen.R]

## Overview

- Use the early damage advantage before Gwen's first item. Snip Snip! and Hallowed Mist windows are safer than a full-health sustained duel.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q, Gwen.W]

## Early lane

- If Gwen approaches the wave at level 1 without Snip Snip! stacks, look for an early all-in. Once she has stacked the snips, bait that cast before committing.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q]

- When Snip Snip! is fully stacked, stay beyond Skip 'n Slash plus Snip Snip! reach. Bait the snips on a last-hit and dash sideways instead of absorbing the center.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q, Gwen.E]

- Against a Gwen with Ignite when you lack it, avoid gambling on a level-1 or level-2 all-in. Favor a short exchange and leave before her sustained damage takes over.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Mid lane

- If Gwen spends Skip 'n Slash and Snip Snip! and ends beside you, attack without wasting Broken Wings to close distance. Her dash can partially refund on an attack, so do not assume its full cooldown is available.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Gwen.Q, Gwen.E]

- Avoid a full-health all-in while even once Gwen has her first item. Chip her first; otherwise A Thousand Cuts healing and Needlework can extend the fight beyond your burst.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Gwen.P, Gwen.R]

## Win condition

- Respect Exhaust after Gwen reaches level 6. The damage reduction can stop your burst kill and give her time to hit successive Needlework casts; do not force a full-health all-in into both.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Gwen.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion to pressure Gwen before her first item. Delay Executioner's Calling until after your first completed damage item if healing warrants it; do not delay early burst just to rush Wounds.
  sources: [saltyy]
  items: [1055, 2003, 3123]
