## Key tips

- Never trade while his Frenzied Maul mark is on you. Its recast heals him and deals bonus damage, so leave until the mark expires.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

- Bait Thundering Smash by walking close, then dash away. Stunning Volibear during the charge resets its cooldown, so Ki Burst alone does not create a Q-down trading window.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Volibear.Q]

- Use the level-1 opening to pressure Volibear before his stronger levels 2 and 3. Back off as he gains access to Thundering Smash and Sky Splitter together.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Volibear.Q, Volibear.E]

## Overview

- Avoid Frenzied Maul recasts and Sky Splitter shield trades. A cancelled Thundering Smash charge is not a spent stun, so choose re-engages carefully.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Volibear.Q, Volibear.W, Volibear.E]

## Early lane

- Use only Ki Burst, an auto, then an instant dash out while Thundering Smash is ready. Longer casts let him stun you and layer Frenzied Maul on top.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]

- After Volibear actually spends Thundering Smash ({cd:106:Q}), use a fuller Broken Wings trade if you are unmarked. Do not confuse an interrupted charge with a spent stun.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Volibear.Q]

- Bait Sky Splitter ({cd:106:E}) with brief contact and leave its circle. Re-engage after any shield expires so Volibear does not receive both free damage and protection.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Volibear.E]

## Mid lane

- Around half health, look for Blade of the Exile burst while Sky Splitter or Thundering Smash is spent and you are unmarked. A Frenzied Maul recast can still overturn the attempt.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Volibear.Q, Volibear.W, Volibear.E]

- If you stay even, aim for your second-item dueling spike instead of forcing level-2 or level-3 kills. Avoid giving Frenzied Maul repeat heals just to create an early lead.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

## Win condition

- Use a dash or Flash to avoid Stormbringer's central landing damage when necessary. Escaping that burst can matter more than saving Flash for a later engage.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and a Health Potion to preserve health through early pressure. Executioner's Calling is optional against Frenzied Maul healing, but avoiding the marked recast is still better than accepting it with Wounds applied.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Volibear.W]
  items: [1054, 2003, 3123]
