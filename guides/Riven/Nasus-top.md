## Key tips

- Freeze to deny Siphoning Strike last-hits and delay Nasus's stacking. Repeatedly pushing into his turret instead gives him safer access to minions and Soul Eater healing.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Nasus.P, Nasus.Q]

- During Wither, rely more on ability damage and movement than an extended attack trade. The attack-speed reduction weakens your autos; disengage instead of standing beside repeated Siphoning Strikes.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q, Nasus.W]

- Treat level 6 and early armor as a reassessment point. A stack-starved Nasus remains punishable, but even farm and Fury of the Sands can remove the easy all-ins you had earlier.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Nasus.R]

## Overview

- Wave control matters more than aimless poke. Deny Siphoning Strike stacks early and reassess once Fury of the Sands and armor arrive.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q, Nasus.R]

## Early lane

- Assert level 1 by walking up and forcing Spirit Fire just to last-hit. Trading his mana for denied Siphoning Strike stacks wins you the lane slowly.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q, Nasus.E]

- Preserve a freeze by avoiding unnecessary attacks on minions. Aim Broken Wings away from the wave when trading, since its area damage can push Nasus back toward safe stacking.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q]

- Punish every walk-up for a stack with Ki Burst and a short rotation, then return to the freeze instead of chasing him under his turret.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W]

## Mid lane

- If you have already chunked Nasus, crash a wave for a coordinated early jungle dive. If he stays healthy, let the wave bounce and freeze instead of repeatedly pushing into Soul Eater sustain.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Nasus.P]

- Do not absorb Spirit Fire's initial hit while maintaining the freeze. Let its damage help push his wave toward you rather than standing in it to force a trade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Nasus.E]

## Win condition

- After level 6, draw out Fury of the Sands and disengage if you cannot burst Nasus. Re-engage after it ends; avoid trading substantial health that Soul Eater will let him recover.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Nasus.P, Nasus.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion to punish Siphoning Strike attempts. Consider Executioner's Calling when Soul Eater healing during combat matters, but brief Wounds will not cover every minion he attacks after you disengage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Nasus.P, Nasus.Q]
  items: [1055, 2003, 3123]
