## Key tips

- Never finish Broken Wings with its third cast while Apprehend is ready. A good Darius holds it to cancel that cast instead of using it to start the trade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Darius.E]

- Move inside Decimate or dash through it. The inner handle does far less and does not heal him like the blade edge does.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Leave before Hemorrhage reaches five stacks. Do not start another exchange with bleed stacks still on you; wait for them to expire before re-engaging.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Darius.P]

## Overview

- Darius wants a long lane and enough time to stack Hemorrhage. Keep exchanges short until his health and Apprehend cooldown permit a fast kill.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Darius.P, Darius.E]

## Early lane

- Consider a level-1 all-in if Darius starts Decimate and you can avoid its blade edge. This is a conditional opening, not permission to duel every Darius start or summoner setup.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Trade when he last-hits or misses Apprehend ({cd:122:E}): Ki Burst, auto, then Valor plus Broken Wings out before Hemorrhage builds.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Riven.E, Darius.P, Darius.E]

- Let him push and freeze near your turret when he has Ghost. A short lane near your turret denies the long run-down where Ghost and bleed kill you.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, look for a Blade of the Exile all-in after short trades lower him toward half health and Apprehend is spent. Armor purchases can push that kill threshold lower.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Darius.E]

- Before ability haste items, never spend every cast to engage far from safety. With everything down, Darius walks you down even after a good first trade.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Win condition

- If Darius has Ghost and Ignite, require a missed Apprehend before committing far from safety. Teleport replaces one combat summoner and reduces that run-down threat.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Darius.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for short trades. Ignite helps finish a chipped Darius and reduces Decimate healing if its blade connects during the all-in.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]
  items: [1055, 2003]
