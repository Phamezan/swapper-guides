## Key tips

- From level 3, bait Facebreaker before trading freely ({cd:875:E}). Spending the pull removes Sett's reliable early setup for a centered Haymaker.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.E]

- Dash sideways or through Sett as Haymaker releases. Its center deals true damage, so retreating straight along that center line wastes your escape.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- At a last-hit, use Ki Burst and one attack, then leave with Valor or Broken Wings available. Do not spend your whole rotation beside a ready Facebreaker.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Riven.E, Sett.E]

## Overview

- Facebreaker and Haymaker make careless sustained trades dangerous. Use short exchanges with an escape ready instead of letting his stun set up a centered Haymaker.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.E]

## Early lane

- At level 1, remove his Bone Plating before any real trade, and only all-in if he started Haymaker and wasted it. Otherwise wait for level 3.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- From level 3, bait Facebreaker at its edge, then trade with Ki Burst, an attack, and Valor plus Broken Wings away. Do not linger for Haymaker after forcing the pull.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Riven.E, Sett.W, Sett.E]

- Facebreaker only stuns if Sett catches enemies on both sides. Avoid a long fight with allied minions opposite you while his E and Haymaker are ready; that sets up a centered Haymaker.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.E]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, all-in near sixty percent health only while Haymaker is down ({cd:875:W}). If it is up, trade once to force it out, reset, then re-engage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Expect Sett to reposition Haymaker with Flash. Preserve a mobility spell rather than assuming your first sidestep has already avoided its center.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

## Win condition

- If Facebreaker pulls without stunning you, Ki Burst and disengage. If it stuns, wait out the control and prioritize escaping Haymaker over finishing your damage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Sett.W, Sett.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for short trades after Facebreaker is spent. Keep gold in damage for the later all-in; do not assume an early anti-heal purchase prevents Pit Grit regeneration between trades.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sett.P, Sett.E]
  items: [1055, 2003]
