## Key tips

- Disengage the moment Ground Slam lands. Its attack-speed slow ruins your auto weaving, so dash out and wait for the slow to fade before trading again.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Malphite.E]

- Absorb Seismic Shard with Valor and preserve health as Malphite spends mana. Look for a heavier trade once he lacks mana for Ground Slam ({cd:54:E}), rather than taking poke unshielded.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Malphite.Q, Malphite.E]

- Pressure Malphite before his armor purchases. Once Plated Steelcaps make kills unlikely, favor farming and roaming instead of spending every rotation trying to break his defenses.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]

## Overview

- Early pressure and mana preservation offer openings before armor stacks. Once Plated Steelcaps blunt your damage, farming and roaming are often more productive.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Early lane

- If Malphite starts Seismic Shard, punish after he spends it. If he holds Ground Slam, walk toward its range then step back to bait the cast before starting an attack trade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q, Malphite.E]

- From level 3, trade with an attack and Ki Burst, then Valor out before Ground Slam can punish your follow-up. Do not keep attacking while its attack-speed reduction is on you.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Riven.E, Malphite.E]

- Against Arcane Comet poke, use Doran's Shield and Second Wind to preserve health while he spends mana. Avoid letting each Seismic Shard force an unshielded last-hit attempt.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, look for burst after lowering Malphite toward sixty percent health, provided armor has not already made him too durable. Do not assume Wind Slash kills through Granite Shield.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Malphite.P, Riven.R]

- Bait Thunderclap by threatening a close trade, then retreat after Ki Burst and an attack. Avoid standing still for his empowered attacks while your mobility is spent.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Malphite.W]

## Win condition

- Keep Flash available for Unstoppable Force when committing at level 6. Valor can absorb damage, but its shield cannot prevent the knockup that gives Malphite time to finish you.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Malphite.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and a Health Potion against Seismic Shard poke. Build Black Cleaver into meaningful armor: its physical-damage shred, health and haste support repeated trades, but do not guarantee a kill on a tank build.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]
  items: [1054, 2003, 3071]

- Mercury's Treads are an option against a poke-heavy magic-damage build. Magic resist helps against Seismic Shard and Ground Slam; tenacity does not shorten Unstoppable Force's knockup.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q, Malphite.E, Malphite.R]
  items: [3111]
