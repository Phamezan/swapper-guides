## Key tips

- Trade while Ionian Fervor is low and check nearby low-health minions before engaging. If Bladesurge resets will let her finish stacking during your trade, leave instead of extending.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Irelia.P, Irelia.Q]

- Save Valor or a Broken Wings cast for Flawless Duet. If its stun lands, prioritize escaping once it ends instead of extending into Ionian Fervor attacks.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Irelia.P, Irelia.E]

- Hold Wind Slash until Irelia cannot Bladesurge out of its path. Use Ki Burst to control her position before firing rather than aiming while a low-health minion offers her an escape.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Irelia.Q, Riven.R]

## Overview

- Ionian Fervor stacks and nearby Bladesurge resets determine safe trades. Thin incoming waves and preserve a dash for Flawless Duet.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Irelia.P, Irelia.Q, Irelia.E]

## Early lane

- Pressure level 1 if Irelia starts Flawless Duet, but do not overstay until Ionian Fervor stacks. Denying her early access to the wave is safer than an extended stacked fight.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Irelia.P, Irelia.E]

- When Irelia uses Bladesurge to last-hit a low-health minion with few passive stacks, Ki Burst her on arrival. The stun delays her next move; it does not remove the kill-based Bladesurge reset.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Irelia.Q]

- Freeze on your side after the incoming wave thins. Do not fight inside a large enemy push: dying allied minions give Irelia Bladesurge resets and Ionian Fervor stacks.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Irelia.P, Irelia.Q]

## Mid lane

- Wait for Defiant Dance to end before using Wind Slash or your main burst. Irelia reduces incoming physical damage during the channel, including Riven's ultimate damage.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Irelia.W, Riven.R]

- During an all-in, aim Broken Wings through Irelia to change sides as she casts Vanguard's Edge. Do not run at her in a straight line with every dash already spent.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Irelia.R]

## Win condition

- Respect Blade of the Ruined King as Irelia's first-item spike. Easier Bladesurge last-hits and stronger attack trades mean you must reassess her available resets before dueling.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade for pressure or Doran's Shield against repeated poke, with a Health Potion. Plated Steelcaps reduce physical attack damage, but Ionian Fervor also adds magic damage, so the boots do not neutralize full stacks.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Irelia.P]
  items: [1055, 1054, 2003, 3047]
