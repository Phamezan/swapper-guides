## Key tips

- Cancel Decimating Smash with Ki Burst or the third Broken Wings cast, but save a dash afterward. An interrupted charge receives a reduced cooldown, so it can return sooner than {cd:14:Q}.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Sion.Q]

- Freeze near your turret and zone Sion from early minions. Forcing him to farm with Roar of the Slayer spends his mana while denying him easy attacks on the wave.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sion.E]

- Time kills around his death passive. Do not kill him where Glory in Death can farm the whole wave and undo your denial.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sion.P]

## Overview

- Interrupt Decimating Smash and deny early farm, while respecting its shortened recovery after interruption. Kill location matters because Glory in Death can recover a wave.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sion.P, Sion.Q]

## Early lane

- Trade hard at level 1 before Sion has accumulated much Soul Furnace health. Do not assume he learned its shield first; answer whichever spell he reveals.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sion.W]

- Crash the third wave, recall, then freeze near your turret and bully every walk-up. He must expose himself to Decimating Smash the wave or lose farm.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- If Roar of the Slayer hits you, leave Decimating Smash range immediately. The slow makes a charged hit easier to land, but does not guarantee it if you preserve a dash.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sion.Q, Sion.E]

## Mid lane

- Build a lead before Soul Furnace health and armor make Sion too durable. Once kills become unrealistic, keep your farm instead of forcing burst into his shield.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sion.W]

- Respect unwarded lane brush: Sion can charge Decimating Smash unseen. Approach with a dash available rather than walking into the release while farming.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

## Win condition

- When Soul Furnace is active, either break the shield with damage before it detonates or leave its blast range. Keep Valor for Decimating Smash rather than spending every dash on the shield.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Sion.Q, Sion.W]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for early denial. Black Cleaver adds health, haste and armor shred against his tank purchases; repeated physical hits apply it, while a fresh Soul Furnace shield still needs a deliberate response.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Sion.W]
  items: [1055, 2003, 3071]
