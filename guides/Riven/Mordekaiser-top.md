## Key tips

- Finish your short trade before Mordekaiser lands three attacks or abilities and activates Darkness Rise. Leave the aura instead of taking an extended exchange inside it.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.P]

- Keep Blade of the Exile available for a Realm of Death duel when possible. Do not fire Wind Slash during his transition into the realm; wait until you are inside and can hit him.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Mordekaiser.R]

- Trade where Obliterate will also hit a minion. Its isolated-hit bonus applies when it strikes only one enemy, so a shared hit deals less damage to you.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

## Overview

- Dodge Obliterate and keep trades below Darkness Rise activation. A charged Indestructible shield or an extended realm duel can absorb your burst.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.P, Mordekaiser.Q, Mordekaiser.W]

## Early lane

- If you do not get a clean level-1 opening, wait for level 3 rather than fighting into Darkness Rise. Dodge Obliterate and avoid letting attacks complete his three-hit activation.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.P, Mordekaiser.Q]

- Sidestep Obliterate with a step, then Broken Wings, then Valor if needed. Jump behind him with Broken Wings so Death's Grasp pulls air.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Mordekaiser.Q, Mordekaiser.E]

- Never all-in while Indestructible is fully charged. Trade once to force the shield and heal, leave, then commit before the bar refills.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.W]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, look for a Blade of the Exile all-in around half health after Indestructible is spent. A charged shield can absorb the burst you need to finish him.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Mordekaiser.W]

- If Mordekaiser uses Realm of Death without killing you, watch its recovery ({cd:82:R}). With the realm unavailable, he cannot isolate you on the next engage.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.R]

## Win condition

- After dodging Death's Grasp ({cd:82:E}), trade briefly and leave before Darkness Rise activates. The missed pull buys room to disengage; it does not turn off an aura that is already active.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.P, Mordekaiser.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for damage in brief trades before Darkness Rise activates. Boots are an optional early purchase if movement speed helps you sidestep Obliterate without spending Valor.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Mordekaiser.P, Mordekaiser.Q]
  items: [1055, 2003, 1001]
