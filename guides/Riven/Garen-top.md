## Key tips

- Use Valor as Decisive Strike reaches you, adding Ki Burst before contact if you can. The shield absorbs damage; it does not prevent the silence, so prepare your exit beforehand.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Riven.E, Garen.Q]

- Only all-in while Courage is down ({cd:86:W}). Its damage reduction eats Wind Slash, so bait it with one trade and burst on the next.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Garen.W, Riven.R]

- When Decisive Strike is down ({cd:86:Q}), take one Broken Wings rotation and leave. Avoid staying in melee range with every spell spent when his silence returns.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Garen.Q]

## Overview

- Shield Decisive Strike, kite Judgment and draw Courage before the all-in. Good disengages matter more than spending every cast in melee range.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Garen.W, Garen.E]

## Early lane

- Freeze near your turret from the start and punish his walk-ups. He must enter your rotation to farm, and a short lane protects you from Judgment chase.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Garen.E]

- When he runs at you with Decisive Strike, meet him with your third Broken Wings cast at its edge to knock him up before the silence lands.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Garen.Q]

- At level 1, bait Garen's learned spell with a delayed third Broken Wings cast. Re-engage on your next rotation only if he stays close while that spell is down.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q]

## Mid lane

- If Judgment is already spinning, kite away after shielding Decisive Strike. Minions do not split its damage; the nearest enemy takes extra damage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Garen.E]

- Respect Garen's Stridebreaker purchase. Its slow makes disengaging from Judgment harder, so preserve mobility instead of repeating the same close trades you took before the item.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Garen.E]

## Win condition

- Do not let Perseverance erase your lane pressure for free. Resume safe poke when your spells return, but avoid chasing into Decisive Strike and Judgment with no exit.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Garen.P, Garen.Q, Garen.E]

## Itemization

- Start Long Sword and Refillable Potion for damage and repeat lane sustain. Use the damage in short trades around Decisive Strike, while preserving potion charges for repeated lane returns.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Garen.Q]
  items: [1036, 2031]
