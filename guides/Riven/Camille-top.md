## Key tips

- Spend Adaptive Defenses before committing damage. Let her attack into Valor, wait for the shield to expire, and remove Bone Plating before the next trade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Camille.P]

- Interrupt Hookshot with Ki Burst or the third Broken Wings cast as the hook attaches and Camille moves toward the wall. Once both dash parts are spent, use its recovery window ({cd:164:E}) for your next trade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Camille.E]

- Stun her before Wind Slash. The Hextech Ultimatum can dodge Wind Slash, so Ki Burst first and only then fire it.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Camille.R, Riven.R]

## Overview

- Camille trades around Adaptive Defenses and Hookshot. Spending those defenses first opens a stronger level-6 all-in for Riven.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Camille.P, Camille.E]

## Early lane

- Play toward lane center while Hookshot is ready. Standing beside a wall gives Camille a shorter, harder-to-dodge Wall Dive into you.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Camille.E]

- Hold Valor for her engage and dodge Tactical Sweep with sideways Broken Wings casts. Eating the outer sweep heals her and slows you out of the trade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Camille.W]

- Respect Camille's Trinity Force spike. Its Spellblade proc strengthens Precision Protocol's delayed recast, so do not judge that attack by its pre-item damage.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, all-in near sixty to seventy percent health when Hookshot is down ({cd:164:E}). If it is up, she escapes or turns the trade with a wall stun.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Camille.E]

- Check Camille's summoner spells. Against Ignite and Teleport, interrupting Hookshot removes her main escape because she has no Flash to cover the failed dash.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Camille.E]

## Win condition

- Use Valor or Broken Wings to leave attack range before the delayed Precision Protocol recast. Mobility can deny that hit; its true damage is not reduced by armor.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Camille.Q]

## Itemization

- Start Long Sword and a Health Potion to put early gold into damage. Use that damage after Adaptive Defenses expires rather than trying to burst its fresh shield.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Camille.P]
  items: [1036, 2003]
