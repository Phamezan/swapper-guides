## Key tips

- Bait Counter Strike before committing to an all-in ({cd:24:E}). During the dodge, your Runic Blade attacks will not land; preserve mobility to leave before its stun.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.P, Jax.E]

- When Jax uses Leap Strike, shield the landing with Valor and use Ki Burst while moving away. Leave Counter Strike's area before it releases; shielding does not prevent its stun.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Riven.E, Jax.Q, Jax.E]

- Keep Valor ready whenever you walk up to farm against Leap Strike and Counter Strike. Spending it for a minion can leave you unable to escape his next jump.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Jax.Q, Jax.E]

## Overview

- Counter Strike is the main commitment check. Build early advantages from its recovery windows and keep an escape available for Leap Strike.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.E]

## Early lane

- At level 1, avoid committing while Counter Strike is ready. If he spends it without catching you, use your next Broken Wings rotation before his dodge returns.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Jax.E]

- If you both reach level 2 together after starting Broken Wings, take Valor second. You need the shield for his Leap Strike plus Empower trade, not more damage.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Jax.Q, Jax.W]

- From level 3, lead with Broken Wings, auto, Ki Burst, auto, then disengage. Stop there; extended autos let Relentless Assault stack against you.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Jax.P]

## Mid lane

- Delay Broken Wings recasts, use the third cast to draw Counter Strike, then Ki Burst and retreat. With Broken Wings already recovering, your next rotation can arrive before Counter Strike.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Jax.E]

- Disengage from an activated Grandmaster-at-Arms unless you can finish Jax. Its gained resistances blunt your physical burst; wait for that defense to end before spending Wind Slash.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Jax.R, Riven.R]

## Win condition

- Keep the wave near your turret and build an early advantage from Counter Strike windows. Jax becomes harder to duel as he completes items, so turn safe trades into denied farm.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion for early pressure, then consider Plated Steelcaps against physical attacks. Jax also deals magic damage with Empower, so armor is not permission to ignore Counter Strike.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Jax.W, Jax.E]
  items: [1055, 2003, 3047]
