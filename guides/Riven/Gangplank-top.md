## Key tips

- Shield Parrrley with Valor rather than taking repeated poke. When Valor is down ({cd:92:E}), concede a last-hit if collecting it would expose you to another free shot.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.E, Gangplank.Q]

- Auto his Powder Kegs whenever you trade near one. Killing a keg denies its damage and the speed he gains from detonating it.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Gangplank.E]

- At level 6, remove Bone Plating before committing Blade of the Exile. Your burst threat improves, but Cannon Barrage still damages and slows you during the chase.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.R, Gangplank.R]

## Overview

- Preserve health through Parrrley poke and contest Powder Kegs. Level 6 improves your burst threat, provided barrels and early armor do not stop your approach.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Gangplank.Q, Gangplank.E]

## Early lane

- Start Valor at level 1 with Doran's Shield if he pokes with Parrrley; if he starts Powder Keg instead, start Broken Wings and contest the keg before it arms.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Gangplank.Q, Gangplank.E]

- Avoid fighting beside brush you have not checked. Gangplank can hide a Powder Keg there and connect another keg to hit you before you reach him.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Gangplank.E]

- Track his available kegs under his health bar. Trade when the count is low and he must replace a keg before he can threaten a chain.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Mid lane

- A Sheen-first Gangplank can be vulnerable to level-6 burst before buying armor. Ki Burst him beside a nearly depleted Powder Keg, destroy the keg, then continue the all-in.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.W, Gangplank.E]

- If you chunk him and the wave pushes toward him, threaten a dive with your jungler rather than letting him farm safely under turret with oranges and kegs.
  sources: [saltyy]

## Win condition

- With ability haste, delay Broken Wings recasts and punish Gangplank when he steps too close. Spend fewer dashes reaching him so more casts and Runic Blade attacks hit him.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.P, Riven.Q]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and a Health Potion to preserve health against Parrrley. Plated Steelcaps can reduce attack damage and add armor against Powder Keg's physical damage, but do not replace destroying kegs.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Gangplank.Q, Gangplank.E]
  items: [1054, 2003, 3047]
