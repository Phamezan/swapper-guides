## Key tips

- Keep trades brief before Conqueror fully stacks and strengthens Aatrox's sustained fighting. Do not stay beside him after your mobility is spent.
  sources: [saltyy]

- Use Broken Wings to move behind the first two casts of The Darkin Blade. Reserve a Broken Wings cast or Valor for disengaging so its third sweet spot does not catch your exit.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Do not commit Blade of the Exile into a full-health Aatrox. First lower him toward half health so your burst can finish before repeated healing.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Riven.R]

## Overview

- Aatrox rewards missed dodges with healing. Preserve mobility for The Darkin Blade, then turn early cooldown gaps into a lead before armor blunts your burst.
  sources: [saltyy]
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

## Early lane

- Do not walk into The Darkin Blade range with every dash spent. Aatrox can aim its sweet spots freely when you have neither Broken Wings nor Valor to reposition.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Bait Infernal Chains and Umbral Dash before committing. After the third cast of The Darkin Blade, punish its recovery window ({cd:266:Q}); without Umbral Dash he cannot reposition the next sweet spot.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.W, Aatrox.E]

- Space out Broken Wings recasts rather than spending all three immediately. Keeping a dash available makes it harder for Aatrox to predict Valor with The Darkin Blade.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.E, Aatrox.Q]

## Mid lane

- Respect Aatrox after Serrated Dirk: preserve health and farm rather than assuming the trades you won before his purchase still work.
  sources: [saltyy]

- If he rushes Plated Steelcaps, prioritize farm and turret plates over forcing kills. His armor reduces the physical damage of your attacks and abilities.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]

## Win condition

- Get early push priority at level 1 while keeping close enough to move behind The Darkin Blade. Avoid casting Broken Wings in place inside a sweet spot.
  sources: [saltyy, xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Aatrox.Q]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and a Health Potion with Flash and Ignite for early pressure. Ignite reduces Aatrox's healing during the kill attempt.
  sources: [saltyy]
  items: [1055, 2003]

- Consider Executioner's Calling if repeated healing is preventing kills. Its Wounds apply while you deal physical damage; they do not permanently stop healing after you leave the trade.
  sources: [saltyy]
  items: [3123]
