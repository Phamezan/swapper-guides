## Key tips

- When Jax jumps onto you, use E to push him away and deny the stun follow-up. His whole engage depends on sticking after the leap, and the displacement breaks it.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Jump answer
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E, Jax.Q]

- Land Q when he commits; missing it makes the fight far worse. The all-in hinges on the Q hit, so only take committed trades with it ready to fire.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Q discipline
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

- Activate your ultimate after his, never before. His R up first limits what your realm steals, so let him commit his before taking him down.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Ult order
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.R, Jax.R]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- At level 1, farm with Q from range without pushing the wave. Contest nothing early; the matchup starts once your full pattern is online.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Level 1
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

- After level 2, do not walk up for farm if it exposes you to his trade. Give the cs rather than the health, then retake control with three abilities unlocked.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Level 2 respect

- Take short trades while the wave sits near your turret. The short window plus tower safety is your pattern; his extended stickiness is his.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Short trades

## Mid lane

- From level 4, all-in only with Q landing first. The committed fight is yours when Q connects and his when it misses, so check the hit before staying.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: All-in rule
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

- If he sticks to you for an extended early trade, pull him off with E. His damage needs contact time, and each peel resets his stack-up.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Peel rule
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E]

## Win condition

- Deny him short trades and you likely win the all-in. Force the committed fight on landed Q rather than trading his short pattern.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Fight selection
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

- If even or ahead after level 6, press the advantage. The matchup eases from that spot, so convert the realm window before he finds an item answer.
  sources: [dreadknight, ph45]
  heading: Post-6 press
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.R]
  patch: "26.21"
