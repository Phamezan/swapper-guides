## Key tips

- After level 3, W his Q-enhanced leap onto you, then E to slow him and line up both Q casts. Keep the exchange brief rather than staying beside his attack-speed buff.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Leap response
  abilities: [Rengar.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Do not spend every damage spell immediately after he lands. Battle Roar heals recent damage, so keep part of your burst until he has used W instead of giving him one large amount to recover.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Delay the burst
  abilities: [Rengar.W]

- Stand close to your minions when Rengar wants the first-level fight. Make a leap cost him wave damage instead of meeting his Q attacks alone beside the brush.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 wave cover
  abilities: [Rengar.P, Rengar.Q]

## Overview

- Respect his early Q damage and fight from a position where your wave can punish a jump. Your response needs both a fast shield and enough restraint to avoid handing all your burst back through W healing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Short retaliation
  abilities: [Rengar.Q, Rengar.W, Ambessa.W]

## Early lane

- Ward the lane brush on your way into lane when you need warning of his opening leap. Do not walk past an unchecked brush and rely on reacting only after he has already started his level-1 attack.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening brush vision
  abilities: [Rengar.P]

- End the exchange while you still have a retreat spell. His early cooldowns, attack speed, and sustain favor the extended fight, so do not follow another brush leap with your own spells already spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shorter cooldown respect
  abilities: [Rengar.P, Rengar.Q, Rengar.W]

## Mid lane

- If he is low, watch the minions he can jump to while escaping through brush. Aim R at a predictable landing when the catch is clear instead of following his jumps with every basic dash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Predict the exit jump
  abilities: [Rengar.P, Ambessa.R]

- With Executioner's Calling, land physical damage before his Battle Roar heal. Keep healing reduction active during the trade rather than waiting to hit him only after he has recovered the recent burst.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cut the recovery
  abilities: [Rengar.W]
  items: [3123]

## Win condition

- Before spending your final movement on a low-health Rengar, account for Thrill of the Hunt. His camouflage and movement can end the chase, so use a clear R catch rather than running blind after his escape.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate escape check
  abilities: [Rengar.R, Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade into Voltaic Cyclosword and buy Executioner's Calling to contest his healing. Plated Steelcaps helps against his repeated attacks when staying healthy in lane is the priority.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Burst and attack defense
  abilities: [Rengar.W]
  items: [1055, 6699, 3123, 3047]

- Against lethality Rengar, consider Death's Dance second or third to soften his physical burst. Keep the short-trade approach against bruiser builds rather than assuming the same item timing answers both styles.
  sources: [heywil]
