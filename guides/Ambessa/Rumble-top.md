## Key tips

- If Rumble starts Electro Harpoon, contest the first wave with Q while dodging his shots. Do not assume every Rumble takes E first; identify his learned spell before forcing priority.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 priority
  abilities: [Rumble.E, Ambessa.Q]

- From level 3, use W into Flamespitter damage, answer with Q, and slow him with E as you leave. Keep the exchange short instead of staying in his flame cone.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield the flame trade
  abilities: [Rumble.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.E]

- When his heat fills, E away from his empowered attacks. Overheat is a retreat signal even though he cannot freely cast more spells during it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Overheat exit
  abilities: [Rumble.P, Ambessa.E]

## Overview

- Rumble's heat state matters more than whether his last spell missed. Take brief spell trades, then create distance before his Overheat attacks can turn your retreat into a chase.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Heat controls the lane
  abilities: [Rumble.P]

## Early lane

- Before the second level-up, check how much heat Rumble has generated. Play beside your wave and give space if he can Overheat rather than letting his level-2 attacks catch you away from minion support.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-2 heat check
  abilities: [Rumble.P]

- Use an opening wave lead to reach level 3 first. That timing gives you W, E, and Q for a full short exchange instead of contesting him with only part of your kit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Race the third level
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

## Mid lane

- Do not stand close with your spells down when Rumble has Flash and Overheat available. If you lack Flash or Exhaust, keep enough distance that his instant approach cannot start an auto chase.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Flash plus heat
  abilities: [Rumble.P]

- After he resets, leave a low-health wave rather than overstaying for a few last hits. The Equalizer ({cd:68:R}) can catch your exit even when he is not close enough to start with an auto.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Do not linger after recall
  abilities: [Rumble.R]

## Win condition

- Use R to leave The Equalizer when its burning line cuts off your retreat. Save that option for the actual trap rather than spending your ultimate on a marginal trade and being stranded in the flames.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate escape
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Rumble.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield with Second Wind and Shield Bash, then build Eclipse. The start softens harpoon and flame poke; Eclipse adds a shield to your brief level-3-and-later trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Sustain into shields
  abilities: [Ambessa.W]
  items: [1054, 6692]

- Hexdrinker into Maw of Malmortius is useful when Rumble's magic damage is stopping you from collecting waves. Flash keeps your escape safer; Ignite is the riskier option for finishing an early lead.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Magic defense
  abilities: [Rumble.Q, Rumble.R]
