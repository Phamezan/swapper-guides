## Key tips

- Sidestep Decimating Smash every time. Dash sideways as the axe rises instead of backing in a straight line, and punish the spent smash with a full combo.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Smash sidestep
  abilities: [Sion.Q, Ambessa.Q]

- Land sweet-spot Q hits as your main damage. Against his health stacking, the outer-edge Q damage is the tool that actually melts him, so space every cast for the edge.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Sweet spots
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Sion.P]

- Save a dash for his death. When you kill him, Glory in Death keeps him hitting, so keep one dash to leave his passive range instead of trading with the corpse.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Death dash
  abilities: [Sion.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Watch bushes and fog constantly. Sion players charge Decimating Smash from brush where you cannot see the cast, so ward lane brush and never face-check it on his timing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Brush watch
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- Expect early smash releases from good Sions. If he drops the axe early to clip you, shorten your trades and keep dashing sideways rather than holding for the full charge.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early release
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- If he slows you with Roar of the Slayer right before dying, wait out the slow before finishing him. Killing him while slowed leaves you in reach of his death passive autos.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Slow wait
  abilities: [Sion.E, Sion.P]

## Mid lane

- Once he stacks enough health, stop forcing the duel. A Sion too tanky to kill still loses fights when you ignore him and R his backline, so take plates and rotate.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ignore and rotate
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- In fights it can be your job to kill him. Sweet-spot Q damage melts health stackers faster than most of your team, so peel him off your carries with repeated edge hits.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tank duty
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]

## Win condition

- Win by never eating a charged smash. Without Decimating Smash knockups he cannot start a trade, and your dash uptime keeps every later fight on your angle.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Smash denial
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- Convert health leads into his team. Suppress a carry with R while he lumbers toward the fight, and finish teamfights before his passive and second health bar matter.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Carry picks
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Sion.P]

## Itemization

- Rush Serpent's Fang when he leans on Soul Furnace shields. Cutting the shield in every trade keeps your sweet-spot damage sticking instead of bouncing off his W.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695]
  abilities: [Sion.W]

- Build Eclipse into Black Cleaver against health stacking. Cleaver shred stacks fast through your multi-hit combos, and Eclipse keeps your trades winning as his health grows.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 3071]

- Start Doran's Shield or Blade with Teleport and Flash. Shield plus Second Wind survives his poke if you struggle with smash timings; Blade funds early kills when you dodge cleanly.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054, 1055]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]
