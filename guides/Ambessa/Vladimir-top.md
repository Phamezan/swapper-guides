## Key tips

- Trade after his empowered Transfusion, never into it. His crimson Q chunks and heals him; once it fades his early damage and healing are both weak, so strike then.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Crimson window
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q]

- Shield Tides of Blood on reaction. W blocks his charged E burst, and your E-empowered-auto-Q combo abuses his short range whenever he steps up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tide shield
  abilities: [Vladimir.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Burst after Sanguine Pool, before his ultimate pops. Pool spent means no escape; kill him inside Hemoplague's fuse instead of letting it heal him back.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pool punish
  abilities: [Vladimir.W, Vladimir.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Make the lane volatile on purpose. His early sustain is weak, so repeated short combos deny the free scaling farm he wants, but respect his Q-Ignite burst trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Volatile lane
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q]

- Learn how he pools in the first trades. If he panics Pool early, bait it and all-in on cooldown; if he holds it, save burst and farm your spike.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pool read
  abilities: [Vladimir.W]

- Abuse his short range. He must walk close to farm and poke, so E in with an empowered auto and Q out whenever he oversteps your casters.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Range abuse
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Vladimir.Q]

## Mid lane

- He outscales bruiser builds, so lethality evens the game. Burst him or his carries before his teamfight healing takes over long fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lethality scale
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- One mistake feeds his snowball. A death here lets him bully the rest of lane, so keep trades disciplined even while playing aggressively.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No donations
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q]

## Win condition

- Win by ending his scaling clock early. Kills and priority before his items make his late-game healing arrive too late to save his team.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Clock win
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- In fights, burst him after Pool or ignore him for carries. R a carry while he waits out his rotation, and never let Hemoplague heal through a long fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight burst
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Vladimir.W, Vladimir.R]

## Itemization

- Rush Voltaic Cyclosword on the lethality line. Burst plus slow sticks you to a short-range mage whose escape is one Pool per fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6699]
  abilities: [Vladimir.W]

- Add Hexdrinker or Maw of Malmortius if his burst starts winning. The magic shield survives his full rotation while your trades keep pressuring.
