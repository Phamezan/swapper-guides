## Key tips

- Memorize these three answers. If he only leaps with Q, W away. If he starts Counter Strike on top of you, E out. If he leaps with Counter Strike active, answer with W into Q, or W into E when Q is spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Three answers
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- Poke with Q only while both W and E stay ready. Aggression without both answers up is how a winning lane gets stunned and run down.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Poke discipline
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- Use R to deny the Counter Strike stun. Its unstoppable blink lets you damage him through the sequence instead of eating the stun at the end of it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: R unstoppable
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Kite backward with E whenever he leaps without Counter Strike. A leap answered cleanly leaves him in your wave with no way to stick.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Leap kite
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Ambessa.E, Jax.E]

- Shield his Counter Strike with W when E is unavailable, then leave before the stun pops. Half-answers still lose the trade, so commit to the full exit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield answer
  abilities: [Jax.E, Ambessa.W]

- Expect the lane to sharpen after level 3 once he holds both Leap Strike and Counter Strike together. Judge every trade from that point by whether both of your answers are ready, not by how the first two levels went.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Post-3 trades
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.E]

## Mid lane

- All-in only while Counter Strike ({cd:24:E}) is down. That single cooldown is his entire defense against your burst sequence.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: All-in window
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Keep answering his engage the same way even when ahead: W away from a leap alone, E out when Counter Strike starts on top of you, and W into Q when he leaps with Counter Strike active. One stunned combo from behind lets him stack Relentless Assault and stat-check the rest of the fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No autopilot
  abilities: [Jax.P, Jax.E]

## Win condition

- Win by never giving him a stunned target. Without the stun he cannot stack attack speed on you, and your poke plus short trades grind him out of lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stun denial
  abilities: [Jax.E, Jax.P]

- Convert Counter Strike downtime into plates, wave crashes or R picks. His leap alone cannot answer your ultimate engage once his dodge is spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Downtime plays
  abilities: [Jax.E, Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first on the bruiser line even while running Electrocute. The rune wins the lane trades, but you need bruiser durability to survive the one Jax combo you eventually misanswer.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]

- Start Doran's Blade with Flash and Ignite. Early kill pressure before his scaling stacks matter is how this lane stays playable.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]

- Take Bone Plating and Shield Bash as your Resolve secondary runes. Bone Plating blunts his leap burst, and Shield Bash pays you back every time W answers his engage.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Jax.Q]
