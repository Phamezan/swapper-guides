## Key tips

- Do not feed Tongue Lash to a low-health Tahm Kench. Dodge or use minion cover rather than assuming W shielding the damage also prevents his champion-hit healing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Low-health Tongue Lash
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Always retain one dash for Abyssal Dive. E can slow his auto chase, but spending every movement to poke leaves you trapped when the W landing circle appears, including from fog of war.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Keep a dive dodge
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W, Ambessa.E]

- Back away before repeated hits build An Acquired Taste near his turret. Do not let him Devour you and reposition the fight under his tower just to land one more Q.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Devour near his tower
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.P, Tahm Kench.R, Ambessa.Q]

## Overview

- Use Q sweet spots to wear down his health while denying repeated attacks. His apparent tankiness is not permission to stand beside him: autos build the stacks that unlock Devour.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Space the health tank
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Tahm Kench.P, Tahm Kench.R]

## Early lane

- Farm behind your minions so they intercept Tongue Lash ({cd:223:Q}). Keep your spacing when he walks around the wave; do not concede free Grasp attacks to maintain a last-hit position.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Minion screen
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.Q]

- Hit your Q sweet spots and W the damage you cannot avoid while circling back out. Short spell trades exploit his health build without entering the extended auto fight he wants.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Spaced damage
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W]

## Mid lane

- If Abyssal Dive begins while he is within your R line, interrupt him with Public Execution before he disappears. Your R can also buffer an incoming stun, but it is not a cleanse after you are already disabled.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate interruption
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W, Tahm Kench.Q, Ambessa.R]

- When he invests in Heartsteel, keep playing through Q sweet spots rather than matching his autos. His health-heavy purchase gives your percentage-health damage a clearer target as your own items arrive.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Heartsteel transition
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]
  items: [3084]

## Win condition

- Before committing to a carry in a teamfight, account for Tahm Kench's allied Devour. He can remove your target and return them with a shield, so do not assume the first low-health carry is an immediate kill.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Devour peel check
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and choose Eclipse for shielded bruiser exchanges or Voltaic Cyclosword for burst. Both approaches still depend on landing Q at the right range instead of trading autos.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First item choice
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]
  items: [1055, 6692, 6699]

- Consider Serpent's Fang when Tahm Kench has other shielders alongside him or you intend to attack the backline he protects. The anti-shield effect is useful against allied Devour shielding as well as his own Thick Skin.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shielding answer
