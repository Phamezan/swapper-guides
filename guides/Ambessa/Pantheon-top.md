## Key tips

- Sidestep Comet Spear above everything. Dodge or bait his Q, especially the thrown version, and punish its cooldown while his Mortal Will stacks are spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Spear dodge
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q, Pantheon.P]

- Burst before Aegis Assault. Land your damage before his shield faces you, because comboing into his E block feeds him a free winning trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield race
  abilities: [Pantheon.E, Ambessa.W]

- Answer Shield Vault with your W. React or predict his stun jump with your shield, then trade back before his spear returns.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Vault answer
  abilities: [Pantheon.W, Ambessa.W, Pantheon.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect his level 1 and short spear cooldowns. He can run you down if you overextend early, so trade only at your spacing and top off health with potions.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early respect
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

- Mind his execute spear. Empowered Comet Spear hits harder on low targets, so recall or potion before sitting in its lethal range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Execute spear
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q, Pantheon.P]

- After level 6, ult him when his shield is spent. R onto him while Aegis Assault is down, or punish his cooldowns with your ultimate engage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Six punish
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Pantheon.E]

## Mid lane

- Track Grand Starfall ({cd:80:R}) after every death or reset. He returns to lane or another lane aiming his ultimate at someone, so ping and respect the sky.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Starfall track
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Outscale his bullying. Your duels and teamfights grow past his, so stop forcing lane kills and take fights where your R picks his carries.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Scale past
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Win by making spears whiff. Without Comet Spear hits he cannot stack pressure, and your scaling burst decides later fights on your engage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Whiff scaling
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q, Ambessa.R]

- Punish his roams with plates and priority. When Grand Starfall takes him away, shove and take turret gold while he gambles on another lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Roam punish
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first on the Conqueror line. It sustains trades through his spear poke and shields the one Vault combo that lands.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q, Pantheon.W]

- Start Doran's Shield with Teleport and Flash. Shield survives spear spam, and Teleport recovers the lane after his early all-in pressure.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054]
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

- Add armor penetration as he builds bruiser. Without shred your mid-game bursts stop threatening him, so buy it before more raw damage.
  sources: [heywil]
