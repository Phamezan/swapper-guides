## Key tips

- Kite backward through level 1 and avoid a heavy opening trade. Losing most of your health immediately gives Yi a level-2 dive opportunity before your defensive kit is available.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Healthy first level

- Track his Double Strike attacks before walking up for Q poke. If the extra attack is ready, expect him to use that moment to begin the trade rather than treating it as an ordinary auto.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Double Strike check
  abilities: [Master Yi.P, Ambessa.Q]

- R can interrupt Meditate, but do not throw it while Alpha Strike can make Yi untargetable. Bait his Q first when possible so your main crowd control does not disappear into his dodge.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate versus Q
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Master Yi.W, Master Yi.Q]

## Overview

- Treat this as an off-meta top-lane duel with real early kill pressure. Preserve enough health to reach level 3, then use short shielded trades instead of racing his autos through an extended fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Survive the opening
  abilities: [Ambessa.W]

## Early lane

- From level 3, W his damage and use your dashes to leave after a brief exchange. Wait for the full basic kit rather than forcing the same trade while you still lack a defensive spell.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Third-level defense
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Use light Q poke without spending both W and E to travel forward. Keep those tools for getting back out so a forward trade cannot become a long chase and another dive setup.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Keep the retreat spells
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

## Mid lane

- Hold the main burst while Yi channels Meditate ({cd:11:W}). He can use its damage reduction to erase your rotation; punish an early W spent as an attack reset instead.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Meditate restraint
  abilities: [Master Yi.W]

- Respect Highlander from level 6 and avoid a long all-in with your spells spent. His movement and attack speed let him run you down; prioritize dash distance instead of expecting E's slow to stop him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-6 chase
  abilities: [Master Yi.R, Ambessa.E]

## Win condition

- Favor coordinated teamfights over the later side-lane duel. Use your team's crowd control to focus Yi before a takedown grants him the resets that let him keep chasing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stop the resets
  abilities: [Master Yi.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and build Voltaic Cyclosword with Electrocute. The damage is for a clean opening after his defenses are spent, not for racing Highlander attacks.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Short-trade burst
  abilities: [Master Yi.R]
  items: [1055, 6699]

- Take Shield Bash and Bone Plating with Flash and Teleport. Bone Plating helps the opening contact, while Flash gives an exit when an aggressive dash would otherwise let Yi stay in attack range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Safe lane setup
  abilities: [Ambessa.W]
