## Key tips

- Never engage in a fist fight with Sett. Kite outward, hit him with Q at range, and make him walk through your spacing to reach you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No fist fights
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Sett.Q]

- If Facebreaker grabs you, W immediately and E out of auto range. Lingering after the pull eats his full punch sequence for free.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pull escape
  abilities: [Sett.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- When he misses Facebreaker ({cd:875:E}), start a short trade and hold your W for his Haymaker answer. Your shield negates the return punch he relies on to flip losing trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Miss punish
  abilities: [Sett.E, Sett.W, Ambessa.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat the lane as reactive. Your wins come from his mistakes, so hold cooldowns and punish rather than forcing trades into his pull range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Reactive lane
  abilities: [Sett.E]

- He should never reach you without Facebreaker or Flash. If he is walking at you in auto range, your spacing already failed; reset range with a dash before trading.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Range reset
  abilities: [Sett.E]

- Avoid the Facebreaker stun into Haymaker combo at all costs. Getting stunned inside his grit punch is the one sequence that loses an otherwise controlled lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Combo denial
  abilities: [Sett.E, Sett.W]

## Mid lane

- Play carefully after level 6. The Show Stopper ({cd:875:R}) gives him a second way to stick to you, so keep a dash reserved whenever his ultimate is up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Post-6 care
  abilities: [Sett.R]

- Answer his ultimate with your own: R behind yourself as he carries you, or time the blink to dodge the carry entirely, then kite out while his engage tools are spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult answer
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Sett.R]

## Win condition

- Outscale him through fights he cannot start. Without a landed pull he spectates while your R chooses the target, so group on your ultimate and deny him flanks onto you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight scaling
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Sett.E]

- Respect him even when he is behind. Like Darius, a behind Sett still one-shots with a full-grit Haymaker and peels you off his carries with his ultimate, so never autopilot fights around him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Behind respect
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.R]

## Itemization

- Eclipse first on the Conqueror line. Longer kited trades stack Conqueror while Eclipse shield covers the one pull combo you fail to space, unlike a pure burst build that dies to it.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Sett.E]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash. Blade supports Q-spacing trades from level 1, and Teleport insures against the single Facebreaker combo that would otherwise end your lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Sett.E]

- Bone Plating plus Shield Bash is the source secondary. It softens his pull-in burst and pays you back every time W answers Haymaker.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Sett.W]
