## Key tips

- Dodge the returning Nevermove before worrying about Vision of Empire. Avoiding E removes the easy setup that lets him place W on a trapped target.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Root into eye
  abilities: [Swain.E, Swain.W]

- W his Death's Hand, then use E and Q to trade around him and move back out. Repeat brief exchanges instead of offering your health to his repeated close-range Q casts.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield the close burst
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Swain.Q]

- After level 6, keep mobility available unless he is low enough for a real kill attempt. Do not spend your last dash entering range and leave yourself stuck beside Demonic Ascension.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Save an exit at six
  abilities: [Swain.R]

## Overview

- Swain's lane is manageable while you can leave after your spells. His level-6 drain and a Frozen Heart rush change the commitment threshold, so do not turn every favorable poke into an all-in.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Short trades before drain
  abilities: [Swain.R]
  items: [3110]

## Early lane

- Use R before Nevermove or Vision of Empire lands when you need to cover the incoming control with your unstoppable cast. Choose whether the landing gives you an engage or an exit before committing it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate through control
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Swain.E, Swain.W]

- If you brought Ignite, apply it for the low-health level-6 kill attempt before his drain recovers the damage. Enter with your full burst available instead of spending healing reduction on a poke trade you cannot finish.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Healing-cut finish
  abilities: [Swain.R]

## Mid lane

- If he rushes Frozen Heart, stop treating him as the same solo-kill target. Preserve farm and shorten attempts rather than forcing damage through the armor because the earlier lane felt easy.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Frozen Heart reassessment
  items: [3110]

- Leave Demonic Ascension's champion-drain range when your burst fails. The transformation can continue while he drains champions, so standing nearby waiting for your next rotation helps keep his ultimate active.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: End the drain
  abilities: [Swain.R]

## Win condition

- Pressure Swain in the side lane once your damage and healing reduction can win a short fight. If Frozen Heart or repeated drain still prevents a finish, clear the wave rather than extending the duel for another rotation.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Side-lane threshold
  abilities: [Swain.R]
  items: [3110]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield with Second Wind and Shield Bash. Choose Eclipse for shielded bruiser trades or Voltaic Cyclosword for burst when he has not built early armor.
