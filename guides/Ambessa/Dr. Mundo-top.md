## Key tips

- Slow-push the opening waves, crash for a short recall, and freeze the returning wave. Make Mundo walk forward to farm instead of letting him collect safely beside his turret.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bounce into a freeze

- Dash out before Heart Zapper detonates. Do not give his W a champion hit that returns more of the damage he stored during your trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Leave the detonation
  abilities: [Dr. Mundo.W]

- When Mundo walks up for an auto or Blunt Force Trauma, E into him and line up both Q sweet spots. His decision to farm in melee range is your opening to chase.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Punish melee farming
  abilities: [Dr. Mundo.E, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

## Overview

- Build an early wave advantage rather than trading autos into his first health purchases. E into Q sweet spots punishes his farming, while denying plates delays the health and sustain that make him difficult to remove.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wave and healing control
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

## Early lane

- Dodge Infected Bonesaw ({cd:36:Q}) and make him spend it on last hits. On a contested cannon, W can absorb his cleaver while you stand in its path; punish him if he walks closer afterward.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cleaver economy
  abilities: [Dr. Mundo.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Watch the low-health minion in front of Mundo. W the corpse he sends through the wave with E rather than letting Blunt Force Trauma poke you down before your next trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Minion swat
  abilities: [Dr. Mundo.E, Ambessa.W]

## Mid lane

- After his first recall with Giant's Belts, stop taking extended trades. Keep using E to set up Q sweet spots rather than trying to win a sustained auto fight into his new health.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Health purchase respect
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]
  items: [1011]

- Protect your plates before minute 14 to slow his Warmog's Armor timing. If he later commits to a side lane during a major teamfight, weigh the fight against the tower rather than automatically following him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Delay his sustain
  items: [3083]

## Win condition

- Use R pressure to draw Maximum Dosage ({cd:36:R}), then reassess the dive after his healing ends. Forcing his ultimate is progress; do not treat its activation as permission to finish him immediately.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Force the ultimate first
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Dr. Mundo.R]

## Itemization

- Choose Eclipse into his Heartsteel health build for shielded trades. Against a health-and-AD build with little armor, Voltaic Cyclosword supports the burst approach; neither choice excuses missed Q sweet spots.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First item by defenses
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]
  items: [6692, 3084, 6699]

- Ignite covers early healing when you want lane pressure. After your first completed item, consider Mortal Reminder for physical-damage Grievous Wounds and armor penetration against his growing sustain.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Healing cut timing
  abilities: [Dr. Mundo.R]
  items: [3033]

- Start Doran's Blade when you can dodge cleavers reliably; choose Doran's Shield if Q poke is forcing bad recalls before the first wave bounce.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Safer start
