## Key tips

- Kite backwards with abilities, never auto-trade. When he spins onto you, W and E away at once; standing to fight his Fury autos loses at every stage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Backwards kite
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Tryndamere.P]

- Read his keystone before trading. Grasp means short poke autos to outsustain you, Hail of Blades means a spin plus three fast autos to react to, and Lethal Tempo means never extend.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Rune read
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E, Tryndamere.P]

- Zone him when his Fury is empty. Without Fury he loses crit chance and sustain, so step up and deny minions in that window instead of respecting him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fury zoning
  abilities: [Tryndamere.P, Tryndamere.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Wave control decides this lane. Freeze on him and you dictate trades; let waves push to you badly and he dives with Undying Rage and snowballs.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R]

- If he freezes on you, consider proxying. Breaking his freeze behind his turret beats walking into his spin range and donating a dive setup.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Freeze break

- Respect Ghost and Ignite run-downs. His chase kills from full health when your dashes are spent, so never trade deep without an exit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Chase respect
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E]

## Mid lane

- After his first item, expect heavy trades into dives. He heals back with sustain, then dives with ultimate, so keep your health and dashes topped.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dive watch
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R, Tryndamere.Q]

- Push waves before objectives, then Teleport back. He will tax side turrets while you group, so clear first and answer his split with numbers.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wave first

## Win condition

- Win by never giving the first death. One kill turns him into a permanent side-lane stat-check; a starved Tryndamere only annoys turrets your team can defend.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Death denial
  abilities: [Tryndamere.P]

- In fights he matters least. Suppress his carries with R while he spins at your frontline, and end fights before he cleans up low targets.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight value
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Tryndamere.E]

## Itemization

- Rush Death's Dance, starting with two Cloth Armors. Its armor and bleed-out passive make his all-ins survivable and his trades finally losable for him.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6333, 1029]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E]

- Add Plated Steelcaps against his auto damage. Tabis blunt the Fury crit autos that win his extended trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3047]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.P]

- Start Doran's Blade with Flash and Exhaust. Blade funds early Fury zoning, and Exhaust blunts the Ghost all-in his ultimate otherwise guarantees.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R]
