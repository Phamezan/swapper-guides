## Key tips

- W as Nimbus Strike reaches you, then E backward immediately. Shielding his E-Q burst and slowing his follow-up denies the attack-speed window he wants to spend on you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield the entry
  abilities: [Wukong.E, Wukong.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- Let Stone Skin stacks expire between trades. Reengaging while his passive armor and regeneration are still built up makes your next spell rotation much less effective.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wait out armor
  abilities: [Wukong.P]

- Do not throw R at Wukong while Warrior Trickster ({cd:62:W}) is ready. Make him spend the clone first so your main engage is aimed at the real champion.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Hold the ultimate
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Wukong.W]

## Overview

- His E into Q requires a quick W answer. Keep trades tied to your available spells because his passive armor and repeated attacks reward every extra moment you stay beside him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fast defensive response
  abilities: [Wukong.E, Wukong.Q, Wukong.P, Ambessa.W]

## Early lane

- During your retreat, hold Q if he still has Warrior Trickster available. Aim both Q casts after he uses W rather than dumping damage into the clone as he disengages.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Q after the clone
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Wukong.W]

- Keep moving away while trading so he has to use Crushing Blow at reach instead of first landing an auto and resetting it with Q. That spacing removes an extra attack from his exchange.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Deny the reset auto
  abilities: [Wukong.Q]

## Mid lane

- At level 6, respect Cyclone and Ignite before dashing into him. The knockup can interrupt your dashes; do not assume a late movement command will get you out once the spin reaches you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-6 restraint
  abilities: [Wukong.R]

- Against Grasp Q poke, use W as he steps in for Crushing Blow and answer with E if he follows using Nimbus Strike. This version of the lane calls for punishing his poke rather than starting an extended fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Grasp adjustment
  abilities: [Wukong.Q, Wukong.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

## Win condition

- Remove Bone Plating with a light Q hit, then wait for its protection to end before spending your full burst. Do not combine his defensive rune and stacked Stone Skin into one expensive trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bone Plating window
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Wukong.P]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and build Eclipse with Conqueror, Shield Bash, and Bone Plating. The shield helps answer his E-Q entry while your own Bone Plating reduces the opening burst.
  sources: [heywil]

- Teleport and Ignite keep his lane trades punishable, but respect his own Ignite when judging an all-in. Avoid relying on sustain to survive his level-6 burst through healing reduction.
