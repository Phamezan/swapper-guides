## Key tips

- Prioritize dodging Mourning Mist ({cd:83:E}). Do not spend your last dash moving toward Yorick when the mark can turn that approach into a losing trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mist first
  abilities: [Yorick.E]

- Dash out of Dark Procession's forming area before the wall closes. Once trapped, do not rely on another short passive dash to solve the cage; keep Flash for a reliable emergency exit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Move before the cage
  abilities: [Yorick.W, Ambessa.P]

- Use W as Yorick or his summons damage you, then hit him with the empowered return strike. Casting W too early wastes the damage-triggered increase you need in the close trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield real damage
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Yorick.P]

## Overview

- The lane is much easier while Yorick cannot pin you in Dark Procession. Contest the opening wave, then keep an exit available whenever you approach his E and W threat.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cage discipline
  abilities: [Yorick.E, Yorick.W]

## Early lane

- Play for level-1 priority with Q spacing. Hit Yorick from outside his Last Rites attack reach instead of exchanging autos to push the first wave.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening priority
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Yorick.Q]

- If Dark Procession catches you and Flash is unavailable, attack the destructible wall to open a way out. Recognize the cage as a separate target rather than continuing to attack Yorick inside it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Break a trapped cage
  abilities: [Yorick.W]

## Mid lane

- When fed, look for R onto Yorick after his Flash is spent. Commit the burst while he cannot Flash your engage rather than giving his bruiser build a long fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lead into a pick
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Once Yorick has his bruiser items, retreat after spending Q and E even if you won the opening lane. His sustained damage can win the rematch while those spells are unavailable; do not stay in melee range trading autos.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Respect the bruiser rematch

## Win condition

- Keep the route out of his turret clear before pressuring him there. Without Flash, give up a close-range trade under his tower rather than risk Dark Procession trapping you in turret range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Turret approach
  abilities: [Yorick.W]

## Itemization

- Choose Doran's Blade if you can consistently dodge Mourning Mist; Doran's Shield is the safer alternative when his poke is denying farm. The aggressive start only pays if E does not keep marking you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Starting confidence
  abilities: [Yorick.E]
  items: [1055, 1054]

- Build Eclipse for shielded exchanges and keep Flash to escape Dark Procession or dodge Mourning Mist. Ignite adds kill pressure, but it leaves less room for a failed cage dodge.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First item and summoners
  abilities: [Yorick.W, Yorick.E]
