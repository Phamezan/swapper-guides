## Key tips

- Dodge or shield Shadow Dash before anything else. Dash sideways or tank the taunt with W, because the taunt into empowered autos is his only real kill threat.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Taunt answer
  abilities: [Shen.E, Ambessa.W]

- Never trade into his Twilight Assault autos. When his blade passes through you and his autos glow, leave; those empowered hits win every extended trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Empowered refusal
  abilities: [Shen.Q]

- Burst short and leave. Quick Electrocute-style combos that end before he drags his blade through you are how you win a lane you can never fist-fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Short bursts
  abilities: [Shen.Q, Shen.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat it as a farm lane you slowly win. He has no all-in without jungle help, so take clean bursts, keep your health high, and recall rather than dying to a gank at low health.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Farm control

- If he takes Ignite, play safer early. Ignite Shen is trading real kill pressure for his late map play, so survive it and punish him with your Teleport advantage on resets.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ignite read

- Punish health-over-armor starts. If he builds health before armor, your combos keep winning; armor first is the signal to shorten trades and play for plates.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Build read

## Mid lane

- Every Stand United ({cd:98:R}) is free plates. The moment he ults away, hard-shove and take turret gold with Demolish while he saves someone across the map.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult plates
  abilities: [Shen.R]

- Abuse his weak waveclear to move first. Crash the wave, then proxy or dive mid while he is stuck clearing under his turret without his ultimate.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First move

## Win condition

- Win the map trade: his ultimate saves one teammate, your pressure takes plates and turrets. Keep shoving before objectives so he must choose between his turret and his team.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Map trade
  abilities: [Shen.R]

- In fights, R his carries through his protection. Serpent's Fang value grows when he shields allies with Stand United and items, so burst the target his shield cannot save in time.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield burst
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Shen.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first on the Conqueror line. Longer trades stack Conqueror while Eclipse shield covers the one taunt combo you fail to dodge.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Shen.E]

- Add Serpent's Fang when his team stacks shields beyond him. It cuts his Ki Barrier and ally shields alike, and it becomes mandatory with other shielders on his team.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695]
  abilities: [Shen.P]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash, and take Demolish plus Bone Plating in Resolve. Blade funds early bursts, Teleport matches his map play, and Demolish converts every ultimate he spends into turret gold.
  sources: [heywil]
