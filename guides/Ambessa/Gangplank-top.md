## Key tips

- Win the barrel timing duel. Your longer auto reach lets you last-hit Powder Keg barrels before his Parrrley or auto chains them, and a barrel you pop denies his whole trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Barrel duel
  abilities: [Gangplank.E, Gangplank.Q, Ambessa.P]

- Punish Remove Scurvy with R. When he oranges out of pressure, his escape is spent, so R onto him before it returns and force the kill or his Flash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Orange punish
  abilities: [Gangplank.W, Ambessa.R]

- Trade only while Trial by Fire is down. His passive true-damage auto wins any trade it joins, so wait for the burning blade to fade before stepping in.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Passive window
  abilities: [Gangplank.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect his level 1 completely. Trial by Fire plus early barrel placement can throw the lane before it starts, so concede the first push and start fighting from level 3.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 respect
  abilities: [Gangplank.P, Gangplank.E]

- Play faster from level 3 onward. Short Electrocute combos punish him between barrels, but leave before a fresh barrel slows you and speeds him up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-3 start
  abilities: [Gangplank.E]

- If he takes Ignite, respect the run-down. One barrel slow into passive autos plus Ignite kills from health totals that look safe against Teleport Gangplanks.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ignite respect
  abilities: [Gangplank.E, Gangplank.P]

## Mid lane

- Match your build to his. Against full-crit Gangplank, lethality burst kills him before barrel chains matter; against bruiser Gangplank, build bruiser and match his longer fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Build match

- Press a lead hard. A squishy Gangplank behind on gold cannot farm safely into your R threat, so crash waves and deny him the safe scaling he wants.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lead press
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Starve his scaling by winning barrels and forcing fights on your R. He needs quiet farm to reach his late-game barrel damage, and every orange-forced R trade steals that time.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Time theft
  abilities: [Gangplank.W, Ambessa.R]

- In teamfights, R a carry before he chains barrels into your team. His Cannon Barrage ({cd:41:R}) zones grouped fights, so start the fight on your target instead of walking through his zone.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight start
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Gangplank.R]

## Itemization

- Open Eclipse against bruiser Gangplank or Voltaic Cyclosword against crit Gangplank. Eclipse sustains the longer barrel trades; Voltaic burst deletes the squishy build before it chains barrels.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]
  abilities: [Gangplank.E]

- Start Doran's Shield with Flash and Ignite. Shield survives Parrrley poke while you learn his barrel timings, and Ignite gives the early kill pressure his orange otherwise denies.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054]
  abilities: [Gangplank.Q, Gangplank.W]

- Take Shield Bash and Second Wind as your Resolve secondary runes. Shield Bash pays off every W block, and Second Wind patches the Parrrley poke between your trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Gangplank.Q]
