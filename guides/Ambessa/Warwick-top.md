## Key tips

- Only attempt the all-in when Primal Howl ({cd:19:E}) and Barrier are down and your burst can finish him. A half-health Warwick with either defense ready can reverse an apparently winning fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Defensive gate
  abilities: [Warwick.E]

- When Warwick launches Infinite Duress from far away, react with R to deny the suppression during your unstoppable cast. Keep watching his approach rather than casting after he has already pinned you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Answer the leap
  abilities: [Warwick.R, Ambessa.R]

- If he reaches you, W his damage and E away immediately. Do not remain beside him for extra autos; your shield and slow are there to end his access to you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Contact escape
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

## Overview

- Warwick sitting near half health is normal, not proof that he is ready to die. Treat Primal Howl and Barrier as part of his remaining health before committing your burst.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Health is deceptive
  abilities: [Warwick.E]

## Early lane

- Use Q at its outer reach and leave before Warwick can turn the exchange into repeated attacks. Keep the lane trade to damage he cannot freely heal back by hitting you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: One-sided poke
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Warwick.P]

- Time W for Jaws of the Beast rather than only for his first auto. Good Warwick players can follow your movement with held Q, so a dash alone is not a dependable answer to his bite.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Predict the bite
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Warwick.Q]

## Mid lane

- Once he has Blade of the Ruined King, favor teamfights over side-lane duels. An even Warwick can still win the sustained 1v1 despite being less effective when your team can focus him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Blade side lane
  items: [3153]

- After you collect safely and deny his lane kills, watch his route out of top. Ping his roams and jungle invades so your safe lane does not become a lead for him elsewhere.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Warn the invade

## Win condition

- Preserve farm and use Flash defensively rather than gambling on an early solo kill. An unfed Warwick is easier to handle in grouped fights, where your team can stop him before he keeps attacking and healing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Deny the snowball
  abilities: [Warwick.P]

## Itemization

- Buy Executioner's Calling early. Its physical-damage Grievous Wounds matters against his Q and passive healing even when you also brought Ignite.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early healing cut
  abilities: [Warwick.Q, Warwick.P]
  items: [3123]

- Start Doran's Blade into Eclipse with Conqueror, Shield Bash, and Bone Plating. Keep Teleport and Flash for the safe lane; Eclipse's shield helps a short trade but does not let you outlast his repeated attacks.
