## Key tips

- From level 3, poke with Q while staying outside Thundering Smash reach. His Q stun sets up Sky Splitter; retreat before he reaches you and W the lightning damage if escape is too late.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Deny the stun setup
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W, Volibear.Q, Volibear.E]

- Do not reengage while Frenzied Maul has you marked. Wait for the mark to disappear rather than giving him a stronger second W and its heal; the cast cooldown is {cd:106:W}.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wait out the mark
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

- If he dives with Stormbringer, use your own R to buy time during the turret disable. This is an emergency stall, so recall before low health makes his next dive easy.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dive stall
  abilities: [Volibear.R, Ambessa.R]

## Overview

- Let his passive push the wave into you when you cannot contest. Tank Volibear becomes easier to handle with items, while an AP bruiser build remains a reason to clear side waves and join fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Collect and scale
  abilities: [Volibear.P]

## Early lane

- If he starts E, take only a light Q trade before the first waves meet and his passive stacks. Dodge Sky Splitter with your Q dash rather than standing in the circle to contest an auto.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening before stacks
  abilities: [Volibear.E, Volibear.P, Ambessa.Q]

- When your opening Q is on cooldown, give up priority and collect his crash. Do not turn the wave contest into an auto battle that lets his passive and repeated W casts take over.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Concede the crash
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Volibear.P, Volibear.W]

## Mid lane

- Against AP bruiser Volibear, push the side wave before objectives and group. Do not assume a lane lead lets you beat the sustained damage of that build in a long duel.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Build-dependent side lane

- When his passive clears the wave, check whether he walks into your jungle or toward mid. Warn those lanes immediately instead of treating every free incoming wave as a harmless farming trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Track his shove exits
  abilities: [Volibear.P]

## Win condition

- Against tank Volibear, preserve even farm until your items let you take stronger Q trades. Keep the fights brief even then; scaling past his tank build does not make repeated marked W hits acceptable.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tank build transition
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Volibear.W]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and build Eclipse for AD, haste, and a shield in your short exchanges. Conqueror with Shield Bash and Revitalize supports this setup without requiring you to stay for his second W.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shielded trades
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Volibear.W]
  items: [1054, 6692]

- Keep Flash for safety and add Executioner's Calling when his repeat W healing becomes a problem. Apply physical damage before his healing trade rather than expecting your W shield to prevent his recovery.
  sources: [heywil]
