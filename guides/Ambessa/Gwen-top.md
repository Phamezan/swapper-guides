## Key tips

- Shield Snip Snip on reaction every time. W as her scissors stack and snip, then combo immediately; blocking the center damage flips every trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Snip shield
  abilities: [Gwen.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Snowball before level 6. Kill her or build a big lead before Needlework arrives, because after 6 her ult slows and burst make your escapes cost dashes you need.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pre-6 snowball
  abilities: [Gwen.R]

- Respect Hallowed Mist completely. Inside her W she gains resistances and you cannot target her from outside, so leave and re-engage when the mist fades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mist respect
  abilities: [Gwen.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Fight her early and often. Her early game is where your Electrocute combos out-trade her sustain, so press before her E all-in and stacks grow.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early press
  abilities: [Gwen.E]

- Avoid extended trades at any point. Her passive percent-health autos win long fights, so land the shielded burst and dash out before she stacks on you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No long trades
  abilities: [Gwen.P]

- Track her scissors stacks. A fully stacked Snip Snip is her trade threat; when stacks fall, step in and force the combo she cannot answer.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stack watch
  abilities: [Gwen.Q]

## Mid lane

- Never let her split for free. She takes turrets and scales with gold faster than you, so match her wave, then move to fights while she is stuck clearing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Split match

- Lethality punishes her better than bruiser when you dodge the mist. Burst her outside Hallowed Mist; if she mists well every time, bruiser durability keeps you alive longer.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Burst choice
  abilities: [Gwen.W]

## Win condition

- Win lane early, win fights late. Your lead must become R picks on her team before her scaling turns the side lane into a tax you cannot pay.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lead conversion
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Kill her before the mist saves her. Full burst from outside Hallowed Mist, or hold damage until it fades; half combos inside the mist just feed her sustain.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mist timing
  abilities: [Gwen.W, Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Open Eclipse against her standard build or Voltaic Cyclosword to burst her. Eclipse shields the snip trades; Voltaic deletes her before percent-health autos stack.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q]

- Add Maw of Malmortius later if her magic damage still threatens you. It will not save a misplayed mist trade, but it wins the close bursts after level 6.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3156]
  abilities: [Gwen.R]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Ignite. Blade funds the pre-6 snowball, and Ignite cuts her early sustain during the trades you must win.
