## Key tips

- After a few levels, engage when Noxious Blast misses or W its impact to start your response. Do not let the short base cooldown ({cd:69:Q}) turn your punish into a stationary extended trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Missed Q entry
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Track Miasma ({cd:69:W}) before committing forward. If she throws it at your feet, dash before it lands; once grounded, use your basic spells without their passive movement rather than repeatedly trying to dash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ground cooldown
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.W, Ambessa.P]

- After R connects, use Q and move sideways or through her, then turn away before Petrifying Gaze lands. Avoid standing directly in front of her after your suppression finishes.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Post-R facing
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Ambessa.Q, Cassiopeia.R]

## Overview

- Play the first levels patiently, then use missed Q casts as openings. Your basic abilities still work without their passive dashes inside Miasma, but the loss of movement makes a long poisoned trade dangerous.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Grounding is manageable
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.Q, Cassiopeia.W, Ambessa.P]

## Early lane

- Use brushes and Doran's Shield to preserve health through the opening levels. Do not assume she started Twin Fang; a level-1 Noxious Blast start can punish an exposed first-wave approach.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening sustain
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.Q, Cassiopeia.E]
  items: [1054]

- If Noxious Blast hits, leave Twin Fang range instead of extending the punish. Twin Fang deals extra damage and heals against poisoned targets, so the trade changes sharply once Q poisons you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Poisoned retreat
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.Q, Cassiopeia.E]

## Mid lane

- During an all-in, vary your dashes across her position instead of repeatedly landing straight in front. Make her retarget both Noxious Blast and Petrifying Gaze rather than offering the same landing angle each time.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Unpredictable approach
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.Q, Cassiopeia.R]

- If you anticipate Petrifying Gaze, cast R before it lands to cover the impact with your unstoppable window. This requires a prediction; R cannot free you after the stun has already landed.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Predictive ultimate
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.R, Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- On the assassin build, disengage when a vital Q misses instead of forcing the rest of the all-in. Leaving with remaining mobility is better than waiting beside her for poisoned Twin Fangs while your damage is unavailable.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Failed burst exit
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Cassiopeia.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield with Second Wind and Shield Bash. This supports the patient opening while W provides a shielded response when you step into Noxious Blast range.
