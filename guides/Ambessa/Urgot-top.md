## Key tips

- Bait or dodge Disdain, then trade freely. His E flip is his only engage; sidestep it and his spent cooldown is your window for full combos.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Flip bait
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

- Do not burst through his flip shield early. When Disdain gives him a shield, wait it out instead of feeding damage into it, then punish the cooldown.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield wait
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

- Kite around his shotgun knees. Circle him so the same leg cannons stay spent, because walking straight lines lets every Echoing Flames passive blast you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Knee kite
  abilities: [Urgot.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Against good Urgots, never greed an auto in your combo. E in and instantly Q away, because the extra auto is the moment his flip catches you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No greed
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Urgot.E]

- Shield Corrosive Charge on reaction. W blocks his Q poke and the slow mark his Purge wants, keeping his shotgun chase from starting.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Charge shield
  abilities: [Urgot.Q, Ambessa.W, Urgot.W]

- If you misplay, R through his flip. Your unstoppable blink bypasses the Disdain stun, turning his one engage into your escape or counter-kill.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Blink save
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Urgot.E]

## Mid lane

- Respect his execute math forever. Fear Beyond Death ({cd:6:R}) plus Ignite kills from around seventy percent against careless targets, so track both before trading low.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Execute math
  abilities: [Urgot.R]

- Beware the flip-Flash surprise. Good Urgots extend Disdain with Flash into ultimate; keep your own Flash or R ready when his sum is up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Flip flash
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Urgot.R]

## Win condition

- Win the flip economy. Every dodged Disdain is a free combo, and without flips he cannot hold you in Purge or shotgun range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Flip economy
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Urgot.W]

- Outscale into team picks. Suppress carries with R while he walks at fights, and force him to execute frontliners instead of your team.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Team picks
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Urgot.R]

## Itemization

- Rush Serpent's Fang when his flip shield and team shields matter. Cutting Disdain's shield restores your trade math in every punish window.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695]
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

- Start Doran's Shield with Flash and Teleport. Shield survives Corrosive Charge poke, and Teleport recovers you after the one flip combo that lands.

  abilities: [Urgot.Q]

- Build Eclipse after your shield answer. It sustains the longer Purge trades while your flip-bait combos keep winning the lane.
