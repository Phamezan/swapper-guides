## Key tips

- Play around his Fury bar before anything else. Empowered Ruthless Predator destroys shields, so your W loses its usual value exactly when his bar is high.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fury first
  abilities: [Renekton.W, Ambessa.W]

- Outspace him at level 1 with Q. Take W second to answer a Slice and Dice start, but expect good Renektons to hold W specifically to counter your shield.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening plan
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W, Renekton.E, Renekton.W]

- When he dashes forward near half Fury, dash away with Q immediately. He must then walk back on a spent dash or burn Fury chasing, and only then is W safe to block and punish with.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dash discipline
  abilities: [Renekton.E, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Without empowered W, you win trades. The lane flips entirely on whether his next Predator is empowered, so trade on his low-Fury windows only.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Low-Fury trades
  abilities: [Renekton.W]

- Remember Dominus ({cd:58:R}) refills his Fury instantly. A low bar stops being safety the moment his ultimate is available, so recheck before every trade after level 6.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult Fury
  abilities: [Renekton.R]

- Most Renekton picks are counterpick attempts rather than mains. Punish sloppy dash-ins and wasted Fury calmly instead of respecting every Renekton like an expert one.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Punish tourists
  abilities: [Renekton.E]

## Mid lane

- Accept a lost lane against a truly skilled Renekton. Play safe, protect your health, and move your value into teamfights where your R pick outvalues his lane bullying.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Safe pivot
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Use your scaling. He falls off while your ultimate picks and mobility grow in fights, so every quiet minute after lane closes his window and opens yours.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Outscale plan
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Survive the empowered-W cycles without losing your health bar. A Renekton who never lands a shield-breaking stun on you has no kill pattern left.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Survive cycles
  abilities: [Renekton.W, Ambessa.W]

- Turn mid-game fights with R on his backline. He must walk into fights after his early dash pressure fades, while you choose the target and the moment.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight picks
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Eclipse first on the Conqueror bruiser line. Extended low-Fury trades are your only winning pattern, and Eclipse plus Conqueror rewards staying in those fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Renekton.W]

- An early Bramble Vest is a strong cheap buy here. It punishes his auto-heavy trades and trims his Cull the Meek healing while you survive lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3076]
  abilities: [Renekton.Q]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash. Blade supports the level-1 spacing duel, and Teleport is insurance against the one empowered combo that chunks you out of lane.
