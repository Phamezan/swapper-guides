## Key tips

- Do not only space her with Q. Play surprisingly aggressive with W: when she jumps on you, shielding her engage and hitting back is how trades flip.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Counter W
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Riven.Q]

- Always keep a way out. If your full combo does not kill her, her shorter cooldowns finish you, so all-in only with lethal confirmed.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Exit rule
  abilities: [Riven.Q]

- Trade after her Valor shield fades or bait it out first. Electrocute burst through a spent shield is your cleanest short-trade window.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield window
  abilities: [Riven.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect levels 1 and 2 and her level-up timers. You start winning from level 3, so give early waves rather than gambling the lane before your kit is complete.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early respect
  abilities: [Riven.Q]

- Win by countering, not by engaging. Your best trades start with her Broken Wings spent into your W, not with your dashes spent into her stun.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Counter plan
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Ambessa.W, Riven.W]

- Do not let her proxy. The moment she spends abilities on the wave behind you, send R at her and punish the cooldown-free target.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Proxy punish
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Mid lane

- Fear her Eclipse spike in extended fights. Even in a learned matchup her first-item all-in bursts through sloppy spacing, so keep trades short around it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Eclipse spike
  items: [6692]

- Use Exhaust value through her ultimate combo. It stalls her burst long enough for your cooldowns to return and turns her all-in into your turn.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Exhaust turn
  abilities: [Riven.R]

## Win condition

- Protect an early lead ruthlessly. Ahead, your counter-trades bully her every engage, while an even lane leaves her scaling and cooldown edge live.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lead protection

- Force fights where her proxy and side-lane cooldown games do not matter. Your R suppression starts the fight she must answer without a wave to play through.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight forcing
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Eclipse first mirrors her spike with shield and burst for counter-trades, and Death's Dance follows to survive the one full combo you fail to space.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6333]
  abilities: [Riven.R]

- Serpent's Fang is the situational slot when her Valor shields and Eclipse procs keep eating your burst. Cutting shields restores your short-trade lethal math.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695]
  abilities: [Riven.E]

- Start Doran's Blade with Flash and Exhaust. Blade funds the level-3 counter-trade plan, and Exhaust is taken specifically to blunt her ultimate all-in rather than for lane poke.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Riven.R]
