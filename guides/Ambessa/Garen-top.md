## Key tips

- Always have W ready for his Decisive Strike. Shielding the silence hit is the whole matchup; without W up, stay out of its reach entirely.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Silence shield
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Garen.Q]

- Read his level 1 from his keystone. Conqueror means respect Judgment spin at level 1 and match carefully with W, while Phase Rush points to a Decisive Strike start you can space with Q.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 read
  abilities: [Garen.E, Garen.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.Q]

- Answer his Q into E combo with W then instant E out. Cancelling your W dash with E after the cast beats the silence, because a dash already cast is not stopped by it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Combo escape
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Garen.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Never let him spin on you unanswered when W is down. Judgment ({cd:86:E}) without a shield trade is free damage he repeats all lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Spin respect
  abilities: [Garen.E, Ambessa.W]

- Engage only into spent cooldowns: his W shield down, or both Q and E down. Trading into Courage ({cd:86:W}) wastes your burst on his damage reduction window.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Engage check
  abilities: [Garen.W, Garen.Q, Garen.E]

- You are generally stronger in lane, so press the health bar while respecting the one combo. Small wins stack because his passive sustain needs time out of combat to work.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lane press
  abilities: [Garen.P]

## Mid lane

- Commit to the bruiser build to keep beating him past lane. Eclipse into Death's Dance style durability keeps your trades winning when pure burst stops finishing him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bruiser commit
  items: [6692, 6333]

- Track his R in teamfights. A silenced Ambessa caught near his execute range dies before dashing, so enter fights after Decisive Strike and Demacian Justice ({cd:86:R}) have targets that are not you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Execute watch
  abilities: [Garen.R, Garen.Q]

## Win condition

- Win the W-for-Q exchange every cycle. If your shield always eats his silence engage, he has no way to start a trade you lose.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cycle control
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Garen.Q]

- Carry the lane lead into fights on your R. Suppress backline targets he cannot reach without Flash, and force him to run through your team to matter.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Backline access
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first. Its burst proc plus shield fits the shield-for-silence trade, where your W eats his Decisive Strike and Eclipse procs as you hit back, and it scales into the bruiser items this matchup demands later.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Garen.Q]

- Add Death's Dance and an armor-penetration slot as he stacks health and armor from Courage. Without penetration your mid-game trades bounce off his passive resistances.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6333, 6694]
  abilities: [Garen.W]

- Start Doran's Blade with Flash and Teleport. You are the stronger laner, so Blade funds the press, and Teleport protects the lead if one silence trade goes wrong.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
