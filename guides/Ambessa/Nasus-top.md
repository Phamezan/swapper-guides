## Key tips

- Deny stacks without shoving him to safety. Punish every Siphoning Strike walk-up with a short combo, but keep the wave on your side so he must overextend for gold.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stack denial
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Hold dashes for Fury of the Sands. After level 6 his ultimate plus Wither runs you down, so kite the duration out and re-engage when it fades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult kite
  abilities: [Nasus.R, Nasus.W]

- Track the enemy jungler constantly. One death here hands him plates, stacks, and a turret, and the lane may never be winnable again.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Jungler track
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play aggressive from level 1. He should not farm for free; every minion he takes without eating your combo is a stack you donated.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early bully
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Respect Wither gank setups. His slow guarantees jungle arrival, so never trade deep without vision and a dash reserved for the escape.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wither respect
  abilities: [Nasus.W]

- Adapt to poke Nasus starts. If he maxes Spirit Fire with Aery-style poke, Doran's Shield and shorter trades survive the chip he trades for stacks.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Poke adapt
  abilities: [Nasus.E]

## Mid lane

- If you fall behind, peel for your carry. A stacked Nasus walks at your backline, and your R suppression plus slows protect the damage that kills him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Peel duty
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Nasus.Q]

- Keep pressuring before his stacks spike. Crash waves and force fights while his ultimate is down, because quiet farming minutes are his win condition ticking.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pressure clock
  abilities: [Nasus.R]

## Win condition

- Win by starving the stack clock. A Nasus behind on Siphoning Strike stacks cannot duel or threaten carries, and your team closes before he recovers.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Starve clock
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- End fights before he becomes the raid boss. R his carries early and take objectives on your timing instead of letting him scale into grouped fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early fights
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Open Eclipse against his standard build or Voltaic Cyclosword to burst him. Eclipse wins the longer Wither trades; Voltaic punishes him before armor and stacks land.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]
  abilities: [Nasus.W]

- Add Black Cleaver against his armor and health. Fast shred from your combos keeps your damage relevant while his stacks grow.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3071]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Start Doran's Shield against poke starts or Blade to bully stack starts, with Flash and Teleport. Match the start to his first ability, and keep Teleport for the reset after his ultimate chase.
