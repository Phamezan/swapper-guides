## Key tips

- Dodge Path Maker above everything. His charged W is most of his damage and his stun; dash it sideways, but not so late that the cast cancels your dash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Charge dodge
  abilities: [K'Sante.W]

- Set up every Q with E first. Your E slow guarantees the sweet spot, and trading without it lets him walk out of your damage and answer freely.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Slow setup
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, K'Sante.W]

- Escape All Out with your own ultimate. When he ults you through a wall, R straight back out instead of dueling his transformed damage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult escape
  abilities: [K'Sante.R, Ambessa.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Accept losing priority to good K'Santes. His early trades are strong, so farm cleanly and take heavy trades only when Path Maker or his dash is spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Priority accept
  abilities: [K'Sante.W, K'Sante.E]

- If he kidnaps you anyway, save abilities to escape. Getting carried toward his turret is survivable if your dashes are up; spending them first is not.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Kidnap escape
  abilities: [K'Sante.R]

- Shield his Ntofo Strikes third hit. The knockup cast is his trade starter, so W as the empowered strike comes or dash before it lands.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Strike shield
  abilities: [K'Sante.Q, Ambessa.W]

## Mid lane

- Scale to Black Cleaver and take over trades. Once Cleaver lands your combos shred his armor stacking and the lane flips in your favor.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cleaver flip
  items: [3071]

- Beware his turret kidnaps forever. Even ahead, one Path Maker into All Out under his turret kills you, so crash waves from range and never linger at his wall.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Turret respect
  abilities: [K'Sante.W, K'Sante.R]

## Win condition

- Win by making Path Maker whiff. Without his charged stun he cannot start the kidnap combo, and your Cleaver-scaled trades grind him out.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Whiff grind
  abilities: [K'Sante.W]

- In fights, R carries past his peel. He wants to body-block and kidnap you; blink past him onto his backline and let your team handle the tank.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Past the tank
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, K'Sante.R]

## Itemization

- Rush Black Cleaver from a Long Sword and Refillable Potion start. Cleaver is the whole matchup spike against his armor, so reach it before chasing other damage.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3071, 1036, 2031]
  abilities: [K'Sante.W]

- Build Eclipse after Cleaver for shielded trades. It covers the one charged combo you fail to dodge while Cleaver keeps your damage climbing.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [K'Sante.W]

- Take Teleport and Flash, or double mobility sums to dodge Path Maker. Flash is the reliable answer to his charged stun and turret kidnaps.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [K'Sante.W, K'Sante.R]
