## Key tips

- Use W against Bramble Smash when he steps up to trade. Absorb the Q damage rather than allowing repeated poke to erase the health you need for wave priority.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield the poke
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Maokai.Q]

- Before pushing beyond your safe retreat, account for Flash into Twisted Advance. His targeted root ({cd:57:W}) is a gank setup you cannot solve by merely sidestepping.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Flash root respect
  abilities: [Maokai.W]

- Prefer pushing and taking useful movement windows over holding a long freeze. Denying a few minions does less to stop his crowd control than getting yourself to the next fight first.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Push over freeze

## Overview

- Maokai can absorb repeated pressure and still provide crowd control without much gold. Get ahead through waves and nearby skirmishes instead of spending the lane trying to kill through every Sap Magic heal.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Priority over solo kills
  abilities: [Maokai.P]

## Early lane

- If you want to punish Maokai directly, do it in the early levels before his defensive purchases. Do not expect the same solo-kill opportunity after he has reset and can sustain through your trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early damage window
  abilities: [Maokai.P]

- Check his purchases before walking through Sapling Toss poke. A tank lane does not require the same respect for E damage as an AP build; do not carry that relaxed approach into magic-damage purchases.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: AP sapling adjustment
  abilities: [Maokai.E]

## Mid lane

- With safe priority and jungle information, proxy the wave to create time for an invade or gank. Cover the wave first so your movement produces an advantage beyond another low-value trade into his sustain.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Proxy into movement

- When Nature's Grasp is available, watch the route behind and beside your pushed wave. Do not let his advancing roots cut off your escape while you are busy hitting the next minion.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate gank angle
  abilities: [Maokai.R]

## Win condition

- Use the first move from your pushed wave to support your jungler when Maokai starts a nearby skirmish. Arriving early gives you a chance to answer his gank setup before his crowd control creates a numbers advantage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Contest the setup
  abilities: [Maokai.W, Maokai.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield and build Eclipse for safe farming and shielded trades. Conqueror, Shield Bash, and Revitalize support the bruiser route while you keep the lane stable.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Neutral lane start
  abilities: [Ambessa.W]
  items: [1054, 6692]

- Take Teleport and Ignite when you want to join nearby fights and threaten early healing trades. Ignite helps against Sap Magic, but do not let its availability tempt you to spend an entire wave chasing a sustained tank.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Summoners for movement
  abilities: [Maokai.P]
