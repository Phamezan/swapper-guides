## Key tips

- Dodge Volcanic Rupture and leave its pillar range at once. His fissure sets up the Searing Charge knockup, so the pillar appearing means dash sideways immediately.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fissure dodge
  abilities: [Ornn.Q, Ornn.E]

- Cancel Call of the Forge God return with your R. Blink as his ram comes back to deny the second knockup, or use R to dodge his Searing Charge stun instead.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ram cancel
  abilities: [Ornn.R, Ambessa.R, Ornn.E]

- Time W into his Brittle proc. Shield the Bellows Breath moment when he would auto to pop Brittle, because that proc is most of his trade damage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Brittle shield
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ornn.W, Ornn.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play aggressive in levels 1 to 3. Tanks are weakest before components, and Ornn crafting in lane does not save him from your early Electrocute trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early press

- Do not expect solo kills to win this game. Shove, proxy, and roam mid or with your jungler, because your value against Ornn is map pressure, not his health bar.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Roam value
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Respect his all-in when your cooldowns are down. Without dashes you eat the full fissure, charge, and Brittle combo, so never linger in his range after trading.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cooldown respect
  abilities: [Ornn.Q, Ornn.E]

## Mid lane

- Keep moving first. Crash waves and arrive at skirmishes before him; he wants grouped front-to-back fights where Call of the Forge God ({cd:516:R}) decides everything.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Move first
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Ornn.R]

- If you built an early lead, Eclipse keeps you threatening. Its shield and damage let you keep trading into his armor instead of becoming a farm-only lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lead item
  items: [6692]

## Win condition

- Win through his team, not through him. Suppress carries with R while he walks between fights, and take objectives during his ultimate downtime.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Team pivot
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Ornn.R]

- Scale your picks against his upgrades. His team grows stronger late, so close advantages with repeated R picks before his forged items stack up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pick clock
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Rush Voltaic Cyclosword on the lethality line, or Eclipse if you took an early lead. Voltaic burst threatens his team; Eclipse sustains the longer armor trades against him.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6699, 6692]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash. Blade funds the early trades before his components, and Teleport matches his lane crafting without losing waves.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]

- Add Black Cleaver as armor grows. Your multi-hit combos stack its shred instantly, restoring damage his tank build otherwise erases.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3071]
  abilities: [Ornn.P]
