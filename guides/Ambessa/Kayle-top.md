## Key tips

- Bully her before level 6 without spending every dash. E in, empowered auto, Q out on repeat, but keep one dash back because Lethal Tempo plus her passive can run you down.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bully combo
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Kayle.P]

- Bait Divine Judgment, then re-engage with R. Burst her until she ults herself, leave, and R back in the moment her invulnerability ends.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult bait
  abilities: [Kayle.R, Ambessa.R]

- Reaction-test her ultimate. R her suddenly and fire Q immediately; if she fails to ult in time, your burst plus Electrocute lands before she can answer, and if she does ult, dash out and come back the moment it ends.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Reaction test
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Ambessa.Q, Kayle.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Deny farm, do not donate kills. She should leave lane behind on gold even when she plays perfectly, so zone her from the wave without dashing under her turret.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Farm denial
  abilities: [Kayle.P]

- After level 6, trade after she spends Q or W. Radiant Blast slow and Celestial Blessing speed are her escape tools, so combo only when one is freshly spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cooldown trades
  abilities: [Kayle.Q, Kayle.W]

- Respect her Starfire Spellblade execute. It hits harder the lower you are, so top off with potions before trading instead of sitting at half health.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Execute respect
  abilities: [Kayle.E]

## Mid lane

- Hold priority and move first. Push her in and win skirmishes with your team while she is stuck farming for her ascension under turret.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First move

- Stop her turret scaling. Shove waves with a teammate before objectives so she cannot free-farm side lanes while you group, and punish her when she steps up alone.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Turret denial
  abilities: [Kayle.R]

## Win condition

- Win before her late game arrives. Your mid-game R picks and skirmish power must close the game while she still needs items and levels to carry.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Close early
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- In fights, delete her before Divine Judgment saves the dive. Burst her first or bait the ultimate early, because a saved Kayle ult turns your pick into her teamfight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult first
  abilities: [Kayle.R, Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Rush Voltaic Cyclosword on the lethality line. Its slow keeps you on a champion whose escape is movement speed, and burst ends her before Lethal Tempo stacks.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6699]
  abilities: [Kayle.W, Kayle.P]

- Start Doran's Blade, or Long Sword with Refillable Potion to rush your spike. Blade funds the early bully trades; the long-sword start reaches Voltaic sooner when you farm cleanly.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055, 1036]
  abilities: [Kayle.P]

- Take Flash and Ignite. Ignite pressures the early levels she must survive, and Flash covers the one Lethal Tempo chase you misjudge.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Kayle.P]
