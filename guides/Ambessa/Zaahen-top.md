## Key tips

- Keep a lateral dash or sidestep for Dreaded Return ({cd:904:W}) when leaving a trade. Walking straight back through his stab lets the pull undo the distance you just created.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dodge the return pull
  abilities: [Zaahen.W]

- At level 1, use Q reach to space outside his attacks. Aim to take no more than one Q-empowered attack rather than remaining close enough for both casts and ordinary autos.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Limit Q contact
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Zaahen.Q]

- When Zaahen commits Grim Deliverance, aim R at his landing and disengage after your suppression ends. Use his committed landing to create an exit instead of staying for the renewed melee fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimate landing exit
  abilities: [Zaahen.R, Ambessa.R]

## Overview

- Keep every trade short enough to leave before Zaahen sticks to you. Dreaded Return is the critical exit check, while later haste makes an old spacing advantage less reliable.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pull decides the exchange
  abilities: [Zaahen.W, Zaahen.E]

## Early lane

- Use W to absorb the Q hit you cannot space away from. The shield lets you answer a brief contact; it does not make standing beside him for the next Q cast safe.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield his Q
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Zaahen.Q]

- When ahead, zone him away from minions before he can use The Darkin Glaive to recover health. Do not hand him free wave access between your short trades as the lane continues.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Deny wave healing
  abilities: [Zaahen.Q]

## Mid lane

- After his first item, reassess Aureate Rush availability before trading forward. Haste can shorten the gap between his dashes; use {cd:904:E} as the base cooldown rather than assuming the first dash leaves a long escape window.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First-item chase
  abilities: [Zaahen.E]

- End the exchange before your short trade becomes a full-Determination fight. His passive adds attack damage and enables a revive at full stacks, directly undermining a kill attempt based only on his visible health.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Determination ceiling
  abilities: [Zaahen.P]

## Win condition

- Keep track of the second Darkin Glaive cast when choosing your exit. It knocks up, so do not wait beside him for the recast and expect a late dash to complete your retreat.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Second Q knockup
  abilities: [Zaahen.Q]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and build Eclipse for AD, haste, and a shield in the brief Q exchanges. Electrocute rewards ending the trade rather than matching Zaahen's passive stacks.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Short-trade build
  abilities: [Zaahen.P, Ambessa.W]
  items: [1055, 6692]

- Take Shield Bash and Bone Plating with Flash and Ignite for the aggressive setup. Bone Plating cushions his first contact, while Flash remains an exit when a missed W dodge would otherwise let him stay in melee range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening protection
  abilities: [Zaahen.W, Ambessa.W]
