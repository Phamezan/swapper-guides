## Key tips

- Dodge Flawless Duet or lose. Always hold at least one dash for her stun blades, because the stun into stacked autos is her whole kill threat.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stun dodge
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Ambessa.E]

- Never spend more than one dash to engage. She dashes to your wave faster than you reposition, so the second dash you spend forward is the escape you no longer have.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: One-dash rule
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- When she ults, R behind yourself to leave Vanguard's Edge. Escaping the blade wall denies her free Bladesurge resets and the slow that lets her stack on you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult escape
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Ambessa.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Expect her to take wave priority. Contest only around large waves of your own, and punish her on waves bouncing back to you where she must overextend to stack her passive.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wave terms
  abilities: [Irelia.P]

- Track what she can dash to. Low-health minions are her roads; stand away from dying casters so her Bladesurge chain cannot reach you for free.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dash roads
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Respect her level-up timers. A sudden level edge means full stacks and an all-in, so back off the wave in the seconds before she levels.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Timer respect
  abilities: [Irelia.P]

## Mid lane

- Respect her Blade of the Ruined King spike. Her first item lets her stat-check even when behind, so shorten trades and keep the Flawless Duet dodge perfect around it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Blade spike
  items: [3153]

- Even ahead, never autopilot. One landed Flawless Duet in the side lane still kills you, so keep playing for grouped picks where your R starts fights she cannot stack in.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No autopilot
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Win by making Flawless Duet whiff all game. Without the stun she cannot hold you in her passive autos, and your short trades plus R picks decide fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Whiff economy
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Ambessa.R]

- After she Bladesurges onto you, strike back with Q2 behind you. Hitting her on the landing side of her dash is the counter-hit that keeps her honest.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Landing punish
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Ambessa.Q]

## Itemization

- Choose Eclipse for bruiser trades or Voltaic Cyclosword for burst. Eclipse survives her stacked autos longer; Voltaic deletes her before stacks matter when you play hit-and-run.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]
  abilities: [Irelia.P]

- Start Doran's Blade with Flash and Ignite for the aggressive line, killing her in levels 1 to 3 before her stacks rule the lane. Teleport instead is the safe scaling choice.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]

- Take Shield Bash and Bone Plating in Resolve. Bone Plating blunts her first stacked burst, and Shield Bash pays off every W block while you wait out her passive.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Irelia.P]
