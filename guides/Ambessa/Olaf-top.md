## Key tips

- Kite axes or die. Dodge Undertow and never stand near the landed axe, because picking it up refunds his chase and he can run you down from full health.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Axe kite
  abilities: [Olaf.Q]

- Shield burst, then leave instantly. W into him to block Undertow plus Reckless Swing damage, Q straight back out, and let your E slow keep him off you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Block and leave
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Olaf.Q, Olaf.E, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.E]

- Never take extended trades. His sustain, attack speed at low health, and raw stats win every long fight, so repeat short combos until he is low enough to finish.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No long fights
  abilities: [Olaf.P, Olaf.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Before level 6, kite with E slows and Q pokes. Keep cooldowns up before stepping forward, because one axe hit without dashes ready is a death.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pre-6 kite
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Olaf.Q]

- Respect his low-health turn. Vicious Strikes shield and healing flip all-ins you thought were lethal, so confirm the kill before committing everything.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Low-health turn
  abilities: [Olaf.W]

- If you fall behind, play safe without tilting. He snowballs through mistakes, but you outscale him; quiet farming minutes close his window.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Safe scaling
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Mid lane

- During Ragnarok ({cd:2:R}), only kite. He is immune to your slows and suppression peel while it lasts, so spend dashes surviving and fight when it fades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ragnarok kite
  abilities: [Olaf.R, Ambessa.E]

- Read his build before dueling. Ravenous Hydra sustain demands anti-heal thinking, Stridebreaker slows demand dash discipline, and Blade of the Ruined King demands shorter trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Build read
  items: [3074, 6631, 3153]

## Win condition

- Win by never letting him touch you. Axe dodges and E slows starve his whole kit, and your scaling teamfight picks outvalue his run-at-you fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Touch denial
  abilities: [Olaf.Q, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.R]

- Punish mistakes even when he is behind. Like other run-down bruisers he kills careless targets at any score, so keep the kite perfect until fights group.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mistake punish
  abilities: [Olaf.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first on the Phase Rush line. Phase Rush plus E slows is your kite engine, and Eclipse shields the one axe combo that reaches you.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q, Ambessa.E]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash. Blade funds early pokes, Flash is mandatory to escape axe chases, and Teleport recovers the lane after his ultimate.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q, Olaf.R]

- Take Phase Rush with Inspiration secondary runes. The speed burst after your short combo is what keeps his axes and Ragnarok from ever connecting.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q, Olaf.R]
