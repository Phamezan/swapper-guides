## Key tips

- When Kennen walks too close, E into range, auto, and use both Q casts while moving back out. Leave after Electrocute rather than staying to trade into more marks.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Punish an overstep
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- If you already carry a Mark of the Storm, move out before Electrical Surge can add another. Do not let his W turn a small poke hit into the start of a stun trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Marked retreat
  abilities: [Kennen.P, Kennen.W]

- In grouped fights, catch Kennen before he starts Slicing Maelstrom or commit to killing his backline first. Do not spend the opening slowly trading into him while his ultimate reaches your carries.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight entry decision
  abilities: [Kennen.R, Ambessa.R]

## Overview

- Whittle Kennen down without collecting free marks, then threaten R at level 6. Winning lane does not remove the danger of his ultimate reaching your backline at the next objective.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lane versus teamfight
  abilities: [Kennen.P, Kennen.R, Ambessa.R]

## Early lane

- Dodge Thundering Shuriken or W its impact when a dodge is unavailable. Keep his Q cooldown ({cd:85:Q}) in mind before stepping out for your next poke.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shuriken answer
  abilities: [Kennen.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Watch his W-empowered attack and avoid giving it a free hit. Electrocute and Ignite make that extra marked auto more dangerous than his ordinary last-hit harassment.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Empowered auto respect
  abilities: [Kennen.W]

## Mid lane

- If he stays outside E reach, use W or a Q dash to close only far enough to land your short trade. Do not spend all your mobility reaching him and leave no route back out.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bridge the spacing gap
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.E]

- After short trades have lowered him, use level-6 R to start the kill attempt. Your new engage range is the reward for keeping enough health through his early poke.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-6 finish
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Avoid forcing a marginal turret dive just because Kennen is squishy. Slicing Maelstrom adds damage and marks in close range, making his turret much more dangerous than an ordinary ranged champion's.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dive restraint
  abilities: [Kennen.R, Kennen.P]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield with Second Wind and Shield Bash. These cover his ranged harassment while your W creates the shielded return hit for a short Electrocute trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Poke sustain
  abilities: [Ambessa.W]
  items: [1054]

- Build Voltaic Cyclosword for burst into his low defenses. If both enemy top-side threats deal heavy magic damage, Hexdrinker into Maw of Malmortius gives a safer route through his level-6 pressure.
