## Key tips

- Dodge Test of Spirit or lose the trade. Her E pull is the whole lane; sidestep it, and punish its long cooldown with your full combo.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pull dodge
  abilities: [Illaoi.E]

- Keep cooldowns to kite her ultimate. When Leap of Faith lands, leave the tentacle zone with dashes held for exactly this, and re-engage when it fades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult kite
  abilities: [Illaoi.R]

- Deny free Harsh Lesson poke. Her W leap lets tentacles slam you from range, so space its reach and punish her when she spends it on minions.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lesson spacing
  abilities: [Illaoi.W, Illaoi.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Never eat Tentacle Smash in lane. Her Q is slow and telegraphed; sidestep it and trade while both her pull and smash are spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Smash sidestep
  abilities: [Illaoi.Q, Illaoi.E]

- Clear tentacles when safe. Walls near your turret grow her damage; kill lone tentacles between waves so her ultimate zone stays small.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tentacle clear
  abilities: [Illaoi.P]

- If her pull hits anyway, leave at once. Do not trade into the spirit; run out, break the tether, and accept the vessel pressure over a lost fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tether break
  abilities: [Illaoi.E]

## Mid lane

- After engaging with R, keep dashing around her. Test of Spirit can still be cast mid-fight, so circle her between combos instead of standing still to auto.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Circle fight
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Illaoi.E]

- Against tank Illaoi, build bruiser and stop side-laning into her. Push with priority and win teamfights and skirmishes where her tentacles are not set up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight pivot
  abilities: [Illaoi.P]

## Win condition

- Win fights she is not set up for. Without pre-placed tentacles her ultimate threatens little, and your R starts picks she cannot answer.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Setup denial
  abilities: [Illaoi.P, Ambessa.R]

- Respect her ultimate answer to yours. She can cast Leap of Faith to become unstoppable through your suppression engage, so bait it before committing R.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult answer
  abilities: [Illaoi.R, Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Open Eclipse against bruiser or tank Illaoi, or Voltaic Cyclosword to burst squishier builds. Eclipse survives tentacle trades; Voltaic ends her before tentacles stack.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]
  abilities: [Illaoi.P]

- Start Doran's Blade with Flash and Ignite. Blade funds early pull-punish combos, and Ignite cuts the healing her spirit and tentacles return.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Illaoi.E]

- Add Executioner's Calling when her sustain starts winning trades. Cutting Tentacle Smash and spirit healing keeps your short trades sticking.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3123]
  abilities: [Illaoi.Q, Illaoi.E]
