## Key tips

- W the moment Briar jumps onto you, then E and Q backward into your minion wave. Make Blood Frenzy chase you through allied minions instead of fighting her in empty space.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Jump response
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Punish after Blood Frenzy ends and before the next W ({cd:233:W}). Do not waste that opening waiting until she can restart the attack-speed chase.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Frenzy downtime
  abilities: [Briar.W]

- Use R to avoid or punish Chilling Scream after she commits the channel. Do not stand between her and a nearby wall waiting for the fully charged knockback.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Scream punishment
  abilities: [Briar.E, Ambessa.R]

## Overview

- Do not match Briar's frenzied autos head-on. Use the wave behind you, then trade back while Blood Frenzy and Chilling Scream are unavailable rather than while her defensive channel is active.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Punish the downtime
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.E]

## Early lane

- If Briar starts Head Rush with Press the Attack, a W start with Electrocute and Shield Bash lets you answer the opening burst. Respect her learned Q instead of assuming she must begin with frenzy.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 Q check
  abilities: [Briar.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Use your health potion early enough to enter the next trade healthy. Snack Attack deals more damage based on your missing health, so delaying the potion until her next bite makes the chase more dangerous.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Potion before danger
  abilities: [Briar.W]

## Mid lane

- Do not dump your remaining burst into a charging Chilling Scream. The channel reduces damage and heals Briar; move out and spend your damage after that protection ends.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Channel damage reduction
  abilities: [Briar.E]

- Keep your ordinary dashes available to leave Chilling Scream's line before release. When R is unavailable, move across the scream rather than backing toward the wall it can slam you into.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wall-safe movement
  abilities: [Briar.E]

## Win condition

- If Briar left lane without a lead, pressure her in teamfights before Certain Death connects. She is more vulnerable without the additional defenses and sustain from a successful ultimate.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight without her R stats
  abilities: [Briar.R]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and build Eclipse. The extra short-trade shield supports the jump response while you use E and Q to leave her attack range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shielded bruiser start
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]
  items: [1055, 6692]

- Take Electrocute, Shield Bash, and Bone Plating with Flash and Ignite. This setup pays for a brief retaliatory trade and protects her opening contact instead of asking you to stay through Blood Frenzy.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening runes
  abilities: [Briar.W, Ambessa.W]
