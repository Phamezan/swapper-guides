## Key tips

- Whoever engages first loses. Never jump onto Darius without a reason; a wasted engage costs you Flash or your life.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First-move rule

- Space him at level 1 with Q at max reach. Land two Q casts without eating a single auto before you think about walking closer.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 spacing
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]

- Cap his autos with your passive-empowered attack spacing. He should get at most one auto, or only a W hit, before you are out of reach again.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Auto cap
  abilities: [Ambessa.P, Darius.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- If he casts Decimate, Q away from the blade arc. If he pulls you with Apprehend ({cd:122:E}), you can win the moment: W into him, Q back out, and keep E as the final escape with its slow.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cast answers
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Darius.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.E]

- Beware a Phase Rush level-1 run-down. Give the first wave respect if he sprints at you, since early bleed stacks plus the speed proc can chase you out of lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Rush respect
  abilities: [Darius.P]

- Play losing lane gracefully if he gets ahead. Not dying matters more than cs here; ping your team off fights near him and give waves you cannot safely touch.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lose gracefully

## Mid lane

- Pivot to the lethality scaling plan when lane is lost. Your game becomes R access onto his bot lane, not dueling a fed Darius in the side lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Backline pivot
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Only revisit the 1v1 on the bruiser build once your items land. Eclipse plus durability lets you survive five stacks long enough for your shorter cooldowns to win the rematch.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bruiser rematch
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Darius.P]

## Win condition

- Starve his bleed. Every trade where he ends below five stacks is a trade your burst already won, so disengage before Hemorrhage maxes and Noxian Guillotine ({cd:122:R}) threatens.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stack starvation
  abilities: [Darius.P, Darius.R]

- Win through his team, not through him. Suppress a carry with R and let your team collapse while Darius walks at the fight without a dash to close distance.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Team pivot
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Choose the first item by his build. Eclipse bruiser answers his standard bruiser setup in longer fights, while Voltaic Cyclosword burst is the pick into a Phase Rush build that cannot afford to be bursted first.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]

- Doran's Blade is the confident start for solo-kill pressure; Doran's Shield is the safe start when you expect to lose early trades. Match the start to how you plan to play level 1.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055, 1054]

- Take Electrocute with Flash and Ignite plus Bone Plating. Burst trades are your only winning pattern, Ignite cuts his Decimate healing, and Bone Plating blunts the pull combo.
  sources: [heywil]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]
