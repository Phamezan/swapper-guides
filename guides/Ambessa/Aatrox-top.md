## Key tips

- Sidestep the first Darkin Blade cast, dash past or around him to avoid the second sweet spot, and save a Q dash to kite away from the third cast.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Blade spacing
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Ambessa.Q]

- Track Umbral Dash ({cd:266:E}) above every other cooldown. When he spends it forward without landing the combo, that is your window to go in.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dash clock
  abilities: [Aatrox.E]

- Never dash straight into him while his blade casts can cancel you. Either predict the cast and go first, or react the instant a cast starts before committing W.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dash safety
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Aatrox.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Expect the Q1 plus E poke pattern. Step sideways or Q backward as the dash comes forward, then punish him while both pieces are spent.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Poke answer
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.E, Ambessa.Q]

- If he misspaces a cast, answer with E into Q and a W trade, then leave sideways or backward depending on whether his dash is still up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Misspace punish
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Stay close enough to invade his space rather than eating blade tips at max range. Fighting on top of him shrinks the sweet spots you must dodge.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Inside range
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

## Mid lane

- Respect the post-6 all-in, and play tighter again at level 9 when his blade cooldown ({cd:266:Q}) drops sharply. Keep your own cooldowns up before trading into World Ender.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ender respect
  abilities: [Aatrox.R, Aatrox.Q]

- Keep baiting blade casts without getting clipped. An Aatrox who whiffs casts and holds the third one too long has handed you the next trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cast baiting
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

## Win condition

- Win the dash economy: he repositions once per rotation while your Q, E and passive dashes keep resetting your angle. Longer trades favor the player still holding a dash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dash economy
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.E, Aatrox.E]

- If a dash gets cancelled anyway, spend the next one escaping Infernal Chains sideways instead of re-engaging. Surviving his chain pull keeps the fight on your terms.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Chain escape
  abilities: [Aatrox.W]

## Itemization

- Rush Voltaic Cyclosword on the lethality line. Its energized slow sticks you to a champion whose whole defense is spacing his blade casts, and your dashes charge it constantly.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6699]

- Buy early boots and upgrade them fast in this matchup. The extra move speed lets you walk out of Infernal Chains sideways before the pull triggers, without spending a dash.
  sources: [heywil]

  abilities: [Aatrox.W]

- Take Electrocute with Flash and Ignite per the source setup. Short dodge-and-burst trades are how you win here, and Ignite covers his combat healing during World Ender.
