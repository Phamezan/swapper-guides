## Key tips

- When he starts Bountiful Harvest on minions without tethering you, take a short Electrocute trade. Do not walk into a fresh drain simply because he is standing still near the wave.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wave-only drain
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.W]

- R him during Bountiful Harvest to interrupt the channel. This denies the completed-channel cooldown refund, leaving a longer punish window based on {cd:9:W}.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Interrupt the refund
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Fiddlesticks.W]

- Use R predictively when Terrify is about to hit so the fear arrives during your unstoppable cast. This is a timing answer, not a way to cast R after you are already feared.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fear timing
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Fiddlesticks.Q]

## Overview

- Respect his early drain instead of forcing constant contact. Later, your ability to interrupt W helps the duel, but vision still decides whether Crowstorm overturns a teamfight.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Drain and vision
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.W, Fiddlesticks.R]

## Early lane

- Do not continue a low-health trade into the end of Bountiful Harvest. Its final hit deals missing-health damage, so what looks like a small remaining drain can still finish you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Low-health drain danger
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.W]

- When short trades have lowered him, apply Ignite before committing the all-in. Leave room for his fear to push you away rather than relying on one uninterrupted melee chase through his healing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ignite finish
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.Q, Fiddlesticks.W]

## Mid lane

- From level 6, keep vision on the brushes and approaches beside the wave before pushing. Crowstorm ({cd:9:R}) makes an unseen flank far more dangerous than another ordinary lane poke.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-6 fog check
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.R]

- Ping when he leaves lane and warn the direction of his possible gank. A neutral lane is not a win if he turns the next unseen Crowstorm into kills on your teammates.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Missing-lane warning
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.R]

## Win condition

- When he is pushing without a protected retreat, bring your jungler into the lane rather than forcing another solo drain trade. Coordinate the catch so fear and healing do not simply reset the attempt.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Gank the exposed lane
  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.Q, Fiddlesticks.W]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and build Voltaic Cyclosword with Electrocute. Your damage is for the short window around a wave-only drain or interrupted W, not for standing in Bountiful Harvest.
  sources: [heywil]

  abilities: [Fiddlesticks.W]
  items: [1055, 6699]

- Build Maw of Malmortius if his magic damage is preventing you from safely farming or contesting. It helps reach the later-game matchup without gambling on another low-health drain fight.
