## Key tips

- Shield or sidestep Boomerang Throw and trade back. Block his Q with W, then E in with an empowered auto and Q out before he kites away.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Boomerang answer
  abilities: [Gnar.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Never spend dashes forward without an exit. A good Gnar saves Hop to chase your engage, so engaging dashless turns your trade into his run-down.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dash discipline
  abilities: [Gnar.E, Ambessa.E]

- Respect Mega Gnar near walls completely. Dodge his smashes and stuns, and never stand between him and terrain when his ultimate can pin you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mega respect
  abilities: [Gnar.R, Gnar.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Accept the farm matchup. If he plays well neither of you dies, so take Electrocute pokes, finish him when low, and scale instead of forcing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Farm accept
  abilities: [Gnar.Q]

- Time W into his Hyper proc. When two rings sit on you, shield the third-hit burst or leave before his passive speed lets him chase.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Proc timing
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Gnar.W]

- Hit your Q2 or do not trade. Your second Q is the damage that makes pokes stick; careful aim matters more here than combo speed.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Q2 aim
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]

## Mid lane

- Play much safer after his Trinity Force. His kiting and damage jump at the spike, but you outscale him later, so survive the item window.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Trinity respect
  items: [3078]

- Punish tiny mistakes hard. One overstep in Mini form eats your full combo; the same patience protects you, because his punishment for your overstep is just as fast.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mistake punish
  abilities: [Gnar.E]

## Win condition

- Win by outscaling his lane. Patience and clean pokes bring you to fights where your R picks outvalue his form-dependent engage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Scale win
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Force fights on Mini Gnar timing. When Mega fades he cannot teamfight, so start with R before his Rage rebuilds.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Form timing
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Gnar.P]

## Itemization

- Rush Voltaic Cyclosword on the lethality line. Its slow ruins his kite spacing, and your constant dashes keep it charged for every poke.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6699]
  abilities: [Gnar.E]

- Start Doran's Shield with Teleport and Flash. Shield survives boomerang poke while you farm to your spike, and Teleport covers a Mega Gnar trade gone wrong.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054]
  abilities: [Gnar.Q]

- Take Shield Bash and Second Wind in Resolve. Shield Bash rewards blocked boomerangs, and Second Wind patches his poke between your trades.
