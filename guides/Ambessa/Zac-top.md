## Key tips

- Step or dash onto Zac's dropped chunks before he collects them. Denying those pickups prevents part of his repeat-trade healing without requiring you to chase him for another full exchange.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Deny the blobs
  abilities: [Zac.P]

- If Zac hits a minion with Stretching Strikes, move out before he attacks you as the second target. The initial skillshot aimed away from you can still become the setup for pulling both targets together.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Minion Q warning
  abilities: [Zac.Q]

- Before triggering Cell Division, check whether you will have enough damage left for the bloblets. Do not exhaust your spells for the first health bar and then die beside him after he recombines.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Revive budget
  abilities: [Zac.P]

## Overview

- His first defensive recall makes a solo kill harder, so farm cleanly and deny the healing you can reach. Do not spend every ability triggering Cell Division unless you can also finish its bloblets safely.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Sustain before kills
  abilities: [Zac.P]

## Early lane

- Dodge Stretching Strikes' initial line when it is aimed directly at you. If you cannot avoid the hit, W the damage rather than expecting the shield to prevent its crowd control.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First Q dodge
  abilities: [Zac.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Your best direct kill opportunity is before his first defensive purchases. Take a clean early trade when available, but do not keep forcing the same finish after his first recall has made his sustain harder to overcome.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early kill window
  abilities: [Zac.P]

## Mid lane

- Check fog near his turret before walking forward to pressure it. Zac can prepare Elastic Slingshot out of sight and turn your approach into a turret-side ambush.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Hidden slingshot
  abilities: [Zac.E]

- At close range, W an Elastic Slingshot landing you cannot dash away from. Absorbing the damage is the fallback; the shield does not stop the knockup from connecting.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Close landing shield
  abilities: [Zac.E, Ambessa.W]

## Win condition

- Keep landing Q sweet spots against his health build instead of spending the whole fight chasing chunks. Your percentage-health damage is the pressure that makes denying a nearby blob worthwhile.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Sweet-spot damage
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Zac.P]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade and build Eclipse with Conqueror, Shield Bash, and Revitalize. This supports sustained lane stability and shielded responses without making a post-recall solo kill mandatory.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Bruiser setup
  abilities: [Ambessa.W]
  items: [1055, 6692]

- Buy Executioner's Calling to limit blob healing and consider Mortal Reminder later for healing reduction with armor penetration. Ignite can help an early finish, but it does not replace saving damage for Cell Division.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Healing reduction
  abilities: [Zac.P]
