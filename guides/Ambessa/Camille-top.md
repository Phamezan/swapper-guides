## Key tips

- Never stand in the outer half of Tactical Sweep. Answer it with W into an empowered auto and E away before she follows up.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Sweep answer
  abilities: [Camille.W, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- Hit without being hit back. Kite her Precision Protocol autos with E and save Q to disengage or dodge Hookshot instead of starting trades with it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: One-sided trades
  abilities: [Camille.Q, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Hold R as a Hookshot answer. Cast it as she launches off the wall, or pre-aim it at the anchor point she first dashes toward, to buffer through the engage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: R buffer
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Camille.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Levels 1 to 3 are volatile and can decide the lane. Fight for the middle of the lane and take clean short trades rather than conceding the wave for free.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Opening levels

- Use the E auto into Q exit pattern so she does not get even a single answering auto. Every free auto she lands feeds her passive shield and turns the trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Clean exit
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Camille.P]

- Later in lane, Q into Q poke is safe chip. It pressures her health bar without giving her the extended auto fight she wants.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Q poke
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]

## Mid lane

- Respect the Trinity Force spike. If you are even when she completes it, her burst and all-in threat jump, so stop forcing trades you were winning earlier.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Trinity spike
  items: [3078]

- Keep refusing extended fights after level 6. Her Hextech Ultimatum ({cd:164:R}) locks you into exactly the long duel your kit loses, so trade short and leave before it comes down.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ultimatum refusal
  abilities: [Camille.R]

## Win condition

- Bank a lead before her first full item, then protect it. The lane is decided early; an even game at Trinity Force hands her the all-in timing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early bank
  items: [3078]

- Scale with your R ranks in later fights. Your suppression pick on a carry decides teamfights while she must find a clean Ultimatum target, so group on your ultimate cooldown.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pick fights
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Open with Serpent's Fang. It cuts through her Adaptive Defenses shield in every short trade, and it keeps paying off if she later adds Sterak's Gage or her team stacks shields.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695]
  abilities: [Camille.P]

- Take Teleport and Ignite with Doran's Blade. Ignite pressures the early volatile levels where the lane is decided, while Teleport covers you if a Hookshot trade goes wrong.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Camille.E]

- If her team leans on shielding beyond Camille, keep Serpent's Fang as a permanent slot rather than a lane-only buy. Its value grows with every shield she and her allies stack.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695]
