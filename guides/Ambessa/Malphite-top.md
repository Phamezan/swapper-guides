## Key tips

- Shield his poke on cooldown. W his Seismic Shard as it flies, or shield Ground Slam and Thunderclap if he maxes those, so his mana buys him nothing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Poke shield
  abilities: [Malphite.Q, Ambessa.W, Malphite.E, Malphite.W]

- Do not take extended fights once armor lands. After his first armor buy your autos lose the stat race, so burst short and leave his Ground Slam attack-speed slow.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Armor refusal
  abilities: [Malphite.E]

- Play around his Granite Shield and mana. Trade when his rock shield is freshly broken and his mana runs low, because without either he is a mage with no spells.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Rock timing
  abilities: [Malphite.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take the free farm and priority. He cannot stop your push early, so crash waves and be first to skirmishes while he clears under turret.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Free priority

- Beware the turret dive bait. If you chase him under his turret he can clear the wave and let tower shots delete you, so dive only with your minions alive.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Turret bait

- Against AP Malphite, keep killing him. The AP build stays squishy, so punish every Shard trade with full combos instead of respecting armor that never comes.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: AP punish
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]

## Mid lane

- After your first item, extended fights return. Lower cooldowns and sustain let you trade through his armor again, so retest the duel once Eclipse lands.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Item retest
  items: [6692]

- Your game is starting fights, not dueling him. R a carry to open skirmishes before Unstoppable Force ({cd:54:R}) picks your team, and force him to ult defensively.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Start first
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Malphite.R]

## Win condition

- Win by out-moving him. He brings one teamfight ultimate; you bring repeated R picks and lane priority, so keep the map moving faster than his cooldown.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tempo win
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Malphite.R]

- Outscale his duel. Later, penetration plus sustain beats his armor stack, while his value stays locked to grouped ultimates your mobility can dodge or pre-empt.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Late duel
  abilities: [Malphite.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first on the Conqueror line. Its shield and sustain carry the short trades, and later items reopen extended fights his armor tried to close.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Malphite.E]

- Add Black Cleaver or Serylda's Grudge as armor stacks. Without penetration your mid-game combos bounce off, so buy shred before chasing more damage.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3071, 6694]

- Start Doran's Shield with Flash and Teleport. Shield plus Second Wind erases Shard poke, and Teleport protects your priority if one Ground Slam trade goes wrong.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q, Malphite.E]
