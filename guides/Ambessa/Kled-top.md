## Key tips

- Dodge Bear Trap on a Rope, including when Kled uses Jousting to deliver it. Do not keep trading while the tether can pull you back into his attacks.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Trap dodge
  abilities: [Kled.Q, Kled.E]

- Make mounted Kled spend Violent Tendencies before engaging. Its fourth attack carries the large damage hit, so do not volunteer to take the end of his W while trying to finish a short trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wait for four attacks
  abilities: [Kled.W]

- Save your kill burst for dismounted Kled with W unavailable ({cd:240:W}). Entering while Violent Tendencies is ready gives him fast courage gains and a chance to remount through your damage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dismounted burst gate
  abilities: [Kled.W, Kled.P]

## Overview

- Treat dismount as a reset in your decision, not as the final part of one chase. Recover your cooldowns, check Violent Tendencies, and only then decide whether you can burst him before courage restores Skaarl.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Two health states
  abilities: [Kled.P, Kled.W]

## Early lane

- At level 3, do not assume Kled has wasted W just because you have not seen it trigger. He can delay learning Violent Tendencies and put the point in when you finally commit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Unlearned W trap
  abilities: [Kled.W]

- Keep early exchanges brief when your jungler is elsewhere. Losing health to mounted Kled gives his strong tower dive and Ignite a much easier target, even if you never intended to all-in him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Weakside health

## Mid lane

- If he casts Q and E before dismounting, do not chase straight after Skaarl leaves. The remaining Bear Trap tether can still pull you back while dismounted Kled builds courage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Tether across dismount
  abilities: [Kled.Q, Kled.E, Kled.P]

- Keep a dash available during the dismounted kill attempt. Pocket Pistol knocks Kled backward and restores courage, so do not use your last movement just to reach his initial position.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pistol recoil
  abilities: [Kled.Q]

## Win condition

- If Kled remounts, end the pursuit rather than fighting the restored health and refreshed mounted Q and E. The burst window has closed; retreat until your own cooldowns let you start a new exchange.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Remount reset
  abilities: [Kled.P, Kled.Q, Kled.E]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Blade with Shield Bash and Bone Plating. Bone Plating helps against his opening attacks, but you still need to avoid the fourth Violent Tendencies hit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Survive the entry
  abilities: [Kled.W, Ambessa.W]
  items: [1055]

- Choose Eclipse for shielded short trades or Voltaic Cyclosword for a sharper burst finish on dismounted Kled. Keep Flash and Teleport when you want a safer lane instead of gambling on an Ignite remount race.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: First-item route
  abilities: [Kled.P]
