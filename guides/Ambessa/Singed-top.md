## Key tips

- Walk to lane with your level-1 minions and make his first proxy attempt awkward. If he gets past you, collecting the wave under turret is better than abandoning it for an uncertain chase.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Escort the first wave

- W before Fling lands to absorb its damage, then use E to catch and slow him after you land. Check where the throw will put you before deciding that following him is safe.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fling answer
  abilities: [Singed.E, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- Dash away from Mega Adhesive's landing area before it grounds you. Do not spend all your movement approaching Singed and leave him an easy W placement on your retreat.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Move before the ground
  abilities: [Singed.W]

## Overview

- Pressure Singed without following him on a long route through Poison Trail. Proxy catches are worthwhile when your wave is covered and your jungler can help, not when they cost you the lane's farm.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wave before chase
  abilities: [Singed.Q]

## Early lane

- If Mega Adhesive catches you, you can still cast your basic damage and shield abilities without taking a passive dash. Keep using Q, W, or E as appropriate instead of treating the grounding field as a silence.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Grounded spell casts
  abilities: [Singed.W, Ambessa.Q, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.E]

- Poke with Q and kite back while your other abilities recover. Do not run after Singed through Poison Trail with no spells available to damage him or escape a Fling.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: No cooldown chase
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Singed.Q, Singed.E]

## Mid lane

- When Singed proxies, coordinate with your jungler and use R to pin him before he executes. Only take the catch while your incoming wave is covered, so a failed chase does not pay him in lost farm.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Proxy catch
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Avoid repeatedly conceding the shove when you can safely clear. Priority limits the time Singed has to slip behind your next wave and establish another proxy.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Keep wave priority

## Win condition

- Ping Singed's exits from lane and look to countergank when your wave is handled. If he keeps proxying instead, counter-proxying can preserve your income without following him through poison.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Answer his roams
  abilities: [Singed.Q]

## Itemization

- Choose Doran's Shield if Poison Trail chip is wearing you down, or Doran's Blade if you can keep short trades favorable. Take Electrocute so a brief Fling response pays off without a long chase.
  sources: [heywil]

- Build Eclipse for a shielded bruiser exchange or Voltaic Cyclosword for burst when a coordinated R catch is realistic. Neither purchase makes chasing a full-health Singed through poison a good use of your wave.
