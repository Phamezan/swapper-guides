## Key tips

- Reserve a dash for Abduct, the chain cast after his initial E movement. Missing that second E is the opening you need to keep his trade from locking you into the rest of his damage.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dodge the second E
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- Use W for Kingslayer's damage after avoiding his chain. Do not spend your shield on a harmless approach and leave his point-blank W hit unanswered.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shield Kingslayer
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Sylas.W]

- When low-health Sylas is ready to Kingslayer, Ignite before he lands it. Applying healing reduction after the heal has already resolved wastes the timing that makes the finish reliable.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ignite before the heal
  abilities: [Sylas.W]

## Overview

- Identify whether Sylas is investing in W-E trades or Q waveclear. Your response changes with his ranks, and a favorable first exchange does not mean you can immediately repeat it while your cooldowns are longer.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Read the skill order
  abilities: [Sylas.W, Sylas.E, Sylas.Q]

## Early lane

- Do not commit your important Q hit before checking whether he still has Abscond. His first E can dodge your damage even when you correctly save movement for his second E.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Account for his first dash
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q, Sylas.E]

- After dodging Abduct, keep moving out of Chain Lash's delayed intersection explosion. Avoid winning the chain dodge only to stand still on the damage he left underneath you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Leave the Q explosion
  abilities: [Sylas.E, Sylas.Q]

## Mid lane

- As his W and E ranks and haste rise, separate your burst rotations with real disengages. Do not keep trading autos while your spells recover if his next Kingslayer and Abduct are already returning.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Repeated-cast pressure
  abilities: [Sylas.W, Sylas.E]

- If he puts early ranks into Chain Lash to clear safely, play for wave pressure instead of pretending he has the same W-max threat. Reassess his trading strength from the spell he is actually leveling.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Q-max lane
  abilities: [Sylas.Q, Sylas.W]

## Win condition

- Keep your minion wave beside you when he jumps in. Make the short exchange happen with allied minion damage rather than following him into open lane for another free Kingslayer.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight with the wave
  abilities: [Sylas.W]

## Itemization

- Use Doran's Shield for safer repeated trades or Doran's Blade when you can punish his misses. Eclipse supports shielded exchanges; Voltaic Cyclosword suits the burst route when you can finish before another Kingslayer.
