## Key tips

- Survive Soul Unbound, then win. His E plus Lethal Tempo stat-checks any extended trade, so keep dashes to leave his spirit form and fight when it snaps back.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Spirit survival
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.P]

- Never spend every cooldown while his E is ready. If he chases with spirit form up and yours are down, he runs you down; always hold an exit.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Exit held
  abilities: [Yone.E]

- Punish his Spirit Cleave cooldown. His W shield and damage are his short-trade tool, so combo when it is freshly spent and sidestep his Q thrusts between autos.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Cleave punish
  abilities: [Yone.W, Yone.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take quick trades and get out. One short Electrocute combo, then leave before Lethal Tempo stacks turn his autos into the winning trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Quick trades
  abilities: [Yone.P]

- Sidestep Mortal Steel thrusts. His stacked Q dash is his engage, so step sideways as the wind gathers and punish the missed dash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Thrust sidestep
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Consider Exhaust for his all-ins. It blunts the Soul Unbound chase the same way it blunts other dash duelists, buying time for your cooldowns to return.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Exhaust option
  abilities: [Yone.E]

## Mid lane

- Even an item behind, he still wins your mistakes. One overstayed trade into his E lets him stat-check you, so keep the two-rotation rule: burst, leave, burst again.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Two rotations
  abilities: [Yone.E]

- Respect his Death's Dance if he buys it. The bleed-out durability ruins one-shot math, so recalculate lethal before committing R to him.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Dance respect
  items: [6333]

## Win condition

- Win by kiting his spirit, then one-shotting late. Survive Soul Unbound, win two short rotations, and by late game delete him or his carries before Lethal Tempo matters.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Kite then delete
  abilities: [Yone.E, Ambessa.R]

- In fights, R past his Fate Sealed. Suppress a carry while he must aim his ultimate at your team, and force him to chase your mobility.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight picks
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Yone.R]

## Itemization

- Open Serpent's Fang when his team stacks shields or he builds Shieldbow. Cutting Spirit Cleave and item shields restores your burst math; otherwise Eclipse or Voltaic leads.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695, 6692, 6699]
  abilities: [Yone.W]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash. Blade funds early short trades, and Teleport protects you after the one spirit chase that goes wrong.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1055]
  abilities: [Yone.E]
