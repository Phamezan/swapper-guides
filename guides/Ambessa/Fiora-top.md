## Key tips

- Keep W ready as counteroffense. When she lunges with Q, shield the Lunge and retaliate with a full combo instead of running first.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lunge answer
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Ambessa.W]

- Kite around her passive Vitals with your dashes and keep the fight inside your wave. Minion aggro plus denied Vitals is how her trade math collapses.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Vital denial
  abilities: [Fiora.P]

- Block the last Grand Challenge Vital by dashing to a wall. Hugging terrain makes one Vital unreachable and denies her healing zone.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wall block
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- If you play aggressive at level 1, do not dash in, and you must land Q2. Dashing into Fiora lets her run you down exactly like dashing into Riven.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 rule
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]

- Playing passive until level 3 is a valid fallback. From level 3 your full kit starts winning the shield-and-dash trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-3 start

- Abuse her weak early waveclear for tempo. Crash waves she cannot thin quickly, and punish her for stepping to the wave without respecting your casters.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Wave tempo

## Mid lane

- Slow down hard once she completes Ravenous Hydra. She sustains off the wave between trades, so short winning trades stop sticking unless you commit fully.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Hydra respect
  items: [3074]

- Side-lane dueling her late is a losing default. She outscales the 1v1, so take your lead into grouped fights where your R suppression decides targets faster.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Leave the duel
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Snowball the lane before her sustain item lands. Early priority, plates and a health lead are the currency that buys your mid-game teamfight plan.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early snowball

- Win fights, not the split. Suppress a carry with R and force her to teamfight on your timing instead of scaling alone in a side lane.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fight plan
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Fiora.R]

## Itemization

- Delay anti-heal until it matters: after your first item, or once her Ravenous Hydra lands. Executioner's Calling bought too early slows your own spike without a sustain target to cut.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3123, 3074]

- Match the start to her keystone: Doran's Shield into Grasp or Press the Attack poke, Doran's Blade into Conqueror duelists. The wrong start gives away the level-1 priority fight.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054, 1055]

- Choose Eclipse for bruiser duels or Voltaic Cyclosword for burst trades. Either way the first item must land before her sustain turns trades into a healing race you lose.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692, 6699]
