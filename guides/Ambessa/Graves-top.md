## Key tips

- Keep minions between you and Graves so they absorb his shotgun attacks. Step around the wave only when your trade is ready instead of giving him free direct shots while farming.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Minion screen
  abilities: [Graves.P]

- Leave the return explosion of End of the Line, and avoid standing beside terrain that makes it detonate sooner. A dash timed for an open-lane Q may be too late when he fires into a nearby wall.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Q away from walls
  abilities: [Graves.Q]

- Prefer an all-in when his Quickdraw armor stacks have expired. Do not treat the base E cooldown ({cd:104:E}) as proof the stacks are gone; watch the active defenses before committing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Empty armor window
  abilities: [Graves.E]

## Overview

- Treat Graves as a burst-heavy duelist rather than a fragile ranged target. His armor stacks and build choices change the kill threshold, and even a gank does not make an extended melee fight safe.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Respect the ranged bruiser
  abilities: [Graves.E]

## Early lane

- If Graves starts Quickdraw with Flash and Ignite, respect the early chase. Preserve minion cover rather than contesting an exposed auto trade into his E start.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Level-1 restraint
  abilities: [Graves.E, Graves.P]

- W an incoming shotgun attack, return an auto, and Q backward. Finish the short exchange without remaining beside him for another round of direct shots.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Shielded exit
  abilities: [Graves.P, Ambessa.W, Ambessa.Q]

## Mid lane

- Keep Quickdraw available in your damage estimate when lining up Sundering Slam. If Graves still has E, avoid committing Q2 to an easy straight-line target he can dash away from.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Q2 aim check
  abilities: [Graves.E, Ambessa.Q]

- If he follows your retreat, E him to slow the chase and line up Q2. Let his forward movement bring him into your damage rather than using every dash to chase his Quickdraw.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Chase reversal
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Graves.E]

## Win condition

- After your first spell rotation in a 1v2, leave direct shotgun range while your cooldowns recover. Graves can still burst one of you down; extra allies do not remove the need for short trades.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Do not overstay a gank
  abilities: [Graves.P]

## Itemization

- Start Doran's Shield with Second Wind and Shield Bash, then build Eclipse. The sustain helps you keep farming behind minions, while the first-item shield supports the brief W-auto-Q response.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Start for poke
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Ambessa.Q]
  items: [1054, 6692]

- Use Black Cleaver on the bruiser route or Serylda's Grudge on the penetration route when you need side-lane damage through his armor. Check whether he chose bruiser, crit, or Bloodthirster before judging his durability.
