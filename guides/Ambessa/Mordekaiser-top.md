## Key tips

- Never, ever get hit by Death's Grasp. Played cleanly you always win trades because he cannot start his stat-check without the pull landing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Pull denial
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E]

- Take short trades that end before Darkness Rise spins up. Three hits is his whole fight plan, so your combo must be leaving before the aura and move speed activate.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Passive denial
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.P]

- Flash his Death's Grasp without hesitating when low or after level 6 with cooldowns up. Eating the pull costs a heavy chunk or a death inside his ultimate, which is always worse than the Flash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Flash the pull
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E, Mordekaiser.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use the bread-and-butter counter combo: W into him through his Obliterate, empowered auto, E to the side, then Q twice out while still dodging his pull.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Counter combo
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Mordekaiser.Q, Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q]

- Play reactive before level 6 like the Darius plan. If he starts with Death's Grasp and misses, kite out and punish; do not walk into his Q range to start trades yourself.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Reactive start
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E, Mordekaiser.Q]

- A single mistake can still kill you in an otherwise winning lane. Treat every pull cast as lethal and keep a dash reserved for it at all health totals.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: One-mistake rule
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E]

## Mid lane

- Try to dodge and cancel Realm of Death ({cd:82:R}) with your own R blink. It is difficult, but a cancelled ultimate leaves him without the stat steal his duel depends on.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Ult cancel
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Mordekaiser.R]

- Only all-in him below roughly a quarter health while his cooldowns are up. Above that line his ultimate plus Indestructible shield still wins the duel you started.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: All-in line
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.R, Mordekaiser.W]

## Win condition

- Win by making Death's Grasp whiff all game. Without pulls he never reaches you, and your short trades plus R picks decide every fight on your timing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Whiff economy
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.E, Ambessa.R]

- Force grouped fights over side-lane duels. His ultimate isolates one target, so fight where your team collapse answers the Realm of Death instead of feeding him isolated stats.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Group answer
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.R]

## Itemization

- Serpent's Fang is great when his team allows it, cutting his Indestructible shield in every trade; Maw of Malmortius is the fallback when you are struggling or facing double AP on top side.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6695, 3156]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.W]

- Doran's Shield is the safe start for surviving his early Q poke, with Blade as the confident alternative. Match the start to how cleanly you trust your pull dodging.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [1054, 1055]
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

- Take Electrocute with Flash and Teleport. Burst trades end before his passive spins up, and Teleport protects you after the one pull that eventually lands.
