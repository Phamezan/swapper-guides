## Key tips

- Shield his hammer combo, dodge his cannon shots. Block melee To the Skies burst with W, and sidestep or W his ranged Shock Blast so neither form pokes you for free.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Form answers
  abilities: [Ambessa.W, Jayce.Q]

- Get your damage out before Thundering Blow. His hammer E knocks you away and cancels dashes, so burst first and keep a dash for after the knockback.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Knockback race
  abilities: [Jayce.E, Ambessa.W]

- All-in only his ranged form. Right after he transforms to cannon he is most vulnerable for a few seconds, with no knockback ready to peel you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Form window
  abilities: [Jayce.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play defensively and let him push. He must snowball to matter, while you outscale hard, so trade only when he forces a trade onto you.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Safe scaling
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Use the simple repeat combo: E in, empowered auto, Q out. It procs your burst before his knockback and never leaves you standing in his hammer range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Repeat combo
  abilities: [Ambessa.E, Ambessa.Q, Jayce.E]

- Beware fog-of-war poke. Shock Blast casts from brush chunk you before you see him, so ward lane brush and respect cannon range when he disappears.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Fog respect
  abilities: [Jayce.Q]

## Mid lane

- Chilling until level 6 is a winning line. At 6 your R gives you the all-in his kit cannot peel if you predict Thundering Blow and blink through it.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Six timing
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Jayce.E]

- Never overdash forward without lethal. If your combo leaves him alive, his shorter cooldowns and chase run you down, so confirm the kill before spending everything.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Lethal check
  abilities: [Jayce.E]

## Win condition

- Win by outscaling his lane kingdom. Even trades crown you later, because lethality Ambessa one-shots the carries his poke only softens.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Scale crown
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Force fights where his spacing breaks. R through his knockback onto his team denies his peel, and grouped fights punish a champion built to duel at range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Peel denial
  abilities: [Ambessa.R, Jayce.E]

## Itemization

- Rush Voltaic Cyclosword on the lethality line. Its slow sticks you to a champion whose whole defense is knockback spacing, and your dashes charge it constantly.
