## Key tips

- Dodge Rupture or lose the matchup. Predict and dash the knockup circle every time; it is the cast his whole combo and kill threat hangs on.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Rupture dodge
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q]

- Do not hold W into his silence. Feral Scream silences you before your shield answers, so dash first and W only outside his cone.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Silence respect
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.W, Ambessa.W]

- Keep trades short after every dodge. Land your combo and leave before Vorpal Spikes autos and his next Rupture cycle turn the trade.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q, Cho'Gath.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hold priority and pressure from level 1. You dictate the wave while he waits on Rupture, so crash, poke, and make him farm under turret.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Early pressure
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q]

- Fear AP Cho'Gath specifically. Ability-power builds with Hail of Blades burst through sloppy spacing, so only trade after Rupture is spent and respect his silence range.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: AP fear
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q, Cho'Gath.W]

- One mistake still kills you. A landed Rupture into silence, spikes, and Feast ({cd:31:R}) deletes even ahead Ambessas, so never trade without a dash.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Mistake price
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q, Cho'Gath.W, Cho'Gath.R]

## Mid lane

- In teamfights, expect his silence on you. Enter after Feral Scream is spent or flank his carries, because a silenced Ambessa gets bursted before dashing.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Silence entry
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.W, Ambessa.R]

- A great Cho'Gath scales into a shutdown tank. If he reaches that point, stop hunting him and R his carries while he lumbers between fights.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Carry pivot
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Win by making Rupture whiff all game. Without knockups he cannot start fights or protect carries, and your priority controls every objective setup.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Whiff control
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q]

- Close before his stacks peak. Feast stacks make him unkillable late, so convert early priority into turrets and picks on his team.
  sources: [heywil]
  heading: Stack clock
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.R, Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Build Eclipse first against tank Cho'Gath. Its shield and sustain win the short trades while you wait out his cooldown cycles.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [6692]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q]

- Rush Black Cleaver if he buys armor first. If he greedily stacks health with no armor, Eclipse damage already suffices and Cleaver can wait.
  sources: [heywil]
  items: [3071]

- Start Doran's Blade with Teleport and Flash. Blade funds early priority trades, and Teleport recovers you after the one Rupture that eventually lands.
