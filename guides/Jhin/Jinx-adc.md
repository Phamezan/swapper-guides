## Key tips

- This is an easy lane that she outscales, so the lead has to come early. Falling even into the late game is losing.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Never let her pin you under your tower. Push back and contest every shove, or the lane snowballs away from you.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Take short trades only. Extended fights give her time to set Flame Chompers ({cd:222:E}) under you.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jinx.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Contest the shove from the first wave. Whoever holds the push decides who gets to trade and who has to catch.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Bounce Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) off dying minions and hold the loaded fourth shot to zone her. Grenade first, then the shot, is your max-damage trade.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Crash the wave and base for Serrated Dirk. That first base is your biggest spike, so plan recalls around it.
  sources: [omibro]

## Mid lane

- Trap the objective side for vision and clear waves with grenades and Deadly Flourish so she cannot pin you in and siege.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E, Jhin.Q, Jhin.W]

- Convert priority into moves before her scaling lands. Every dragon or roam you take first is worth more than another wave traded evenly.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Win condition

- Turn the early lead into objectives. A lead that never becomes a dragon or a tower is just farm she will eventually match.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Hold Deadly Flourish for support crowd control and fire Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) the instant it lands. Dry ults from fog waste your best teamfight tool.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on the first base is the spike. Your grenade and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so crash, base, and return before trading hard.
  sources: [omibro]
