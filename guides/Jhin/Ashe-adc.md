## Key tips

- Her range advantage is the whole problem. Trade only very briefly, grenade plus the fourth shot and then leave, and mostly avoid fighting.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Play around her Volley ({cd:22:W}) cooldown. Wait for it to be down and for your support to find an engage.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Ashe.W]

- Rush Swiftness boots for slow resist, and take Fleet Footwork with Cleanse. The setup exists to blunt her permaslow game.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Make every trade a hit and run. Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) into the loaded fourth shot, then walk out before the slows stack.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Your engage window is Volley down plus support crowd control. Without both, keep farming and concede the push.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Ashe.W]

## Mid lane

- Match her permaslow with movement. Swiftness boots plus Fleet Footwork keep you out of the range where her autos run you down.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Hold Cleanse for Enchanted Crystal Arrow ({cd:22:R}), not for slows. The arrow is the only thing that truly locks you down.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Ashe.R]

## Win condition

- Fight from range after your support lands something. Hold Deadly Flourish for the follow-up and fire Curtain Call the instant it connects.
  sources: [yunfang, omibro]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Never chase through slows. If the trade extends past your burst, reset and wait for the next Volley-down window instead of bleeding out.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Itemization

- Swiftness boots first is the whole item plan for this lane. Everything else follows the standard crit build; the boots are what keep you playable.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
