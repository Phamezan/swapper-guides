## Key tips

- He wins trades, so this is a skill matchup your support decides. Never take the straight fight without them.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- With Nami beside him this is the hardest lane in the game. Do not duel at all: play for teamfights and objectives with Deadly Flourish and Curtain Call instead.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Track his dash ({cd:236:E}) above everything else. His engage, his escape, and his trade pattern all hang on it.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Lucian.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Refuse straight trades — his burst wins them. Fight only off your support's crowd control, then leave.
  sources: [antiwis, omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

- Against the Nami pairing, preserve health over CS. Giving a few minions beats dying with the wave pushing toward you.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Group for objectives instead of catching side waves into him. Your W plus R decides fights; your autos do not.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Engage only while his dash is down. The moment it comes back, the duel flips to him again.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Lucian.E]

## Win condition

- Win through casts, not duels. Hover the objective, land W off your frontline, and fire the ult the instant it connects.
  sources: [yunfang, omibro]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Trap the objective side for vision so his dash cannot find a blind angle. If his position is unknown, do not walk up.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Itemization

- Do not build to match his dueling. Your win condition is teamfight casts, so spend for the fight, not for a 1v1 you refuse anyway.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
