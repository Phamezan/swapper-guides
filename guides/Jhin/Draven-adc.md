## Key tips

- He is a personal permaban for a reason: respect the axes and never take the straight fight.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- He is useless once crowd controlled. Wait for your support's engage instead of trading into his axe range alone.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Race him to level 2 with your support. The first engage usually decides who holds the wave for the next minutes.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Give the first waves rather than eating axes for them. Contesting a Draven early loses more health than the minions are worth.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Bounce Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) off dying minions from outside his auto range. Never step into axe range just to last-hit aggressively.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

## Mid lane

- Trap the objective side for vision and hover instead of walking up blind. If his position is unknown, walking in means dying.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

- Never be the isolated target he runs down. Rotate with the team and let him come into your crowd control, not the reverse.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Win condition

- Fight from range after your support lands something. Hold Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) for the follow-up and fire Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) the instant it connects.
  sources: [yunfang, omibro]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Keep every trade to poke first, then commit. His all-in wins from full health, so only fight once the bounces have landed.
  sources: [omibro, antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on the first base is the spike. Your grenade and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so crash, base, and return before trading hard.
  sources: [omibro]
