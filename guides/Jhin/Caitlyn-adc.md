## Key tips

- This lane is even but you outscale her hard. A 0/0/0 lane is a win, so do not force fights to manufacture a lead.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- She is useless once crowd controlled. Wait for your support's engage instead of trading into her range alone.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Her 90 Caliber Net ({cd:51:E}) is her disengage. Trade hard when it is down and give ground while she holds it.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play for priority rather than kills. Match her shove so she never banks a free crash, and be content going even.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Race her to level 2 with your support. The first engage usually decides who holds the wave for the next minutes.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- She keeps her net ready before trading, so bait it with movement before committing your own engage.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E]

## Mid lane

- With the lane even and your scaling harder, spend shoves on objectives and teamfights instead of dueling her in her range.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Track her net in skirmishes. When she has no disengage, your root into Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) is the fight starter.
  sources: [rowzkit, omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R, Caitlyn.E]

## Win condition

- Take the game long. Every even minute favors you, so protect the 0/0/0 line instead of gambling it on low-percentage plays.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- In fights, hold Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) for support crowd control and fire the ult the instant it lands for the guaranteed first shot.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- There is no item race to win here. Even gold is winning because of your scaling, so base on clean crashes instead of forcing buys.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
