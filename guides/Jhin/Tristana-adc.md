## Key tips

- She is your biggest counter. Keep your distance at all times and concede the phases you cannot contest.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Never stand where she can jump on you. Hang back and angle yourself diagonally from her, never straight in front.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Never 1v1 her. She wins every extended trade, so your fights must be short or supported.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Give ground early. Her jump plus bomb all-in beats anything you can answer, so farm what is free and protect your health.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Tristana.W, Tristana.E]

- Bounce Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) from diagonal max range. Never step into jump range just to last-hit aggressively.
  sources: [yunfang, omibro]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q, Tristana.W]

## Mid lane

- Trap the objective side for vision and hover instead of walking up blind. If her position is unknown, walking in means dying.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

- Keep diagonal angles through every rotation. Never group where her jump can reach your backline in one hop.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Tristana.W]

## Win condition

- Poke first, then fight. Her engage only works from full health, so chip her with bounces before every objective.
  sources: [omibro, antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Punish the committed jump. When she lands in, root her with Deadly Flourish and turn the fight while she has no escape.
  sources: [yunfang, omibro]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Tristana.W]

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on the first base is the spike. Your grenade and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so crash, base, and return before trading hard.
  items: [3134]
  sources: [omibro]
