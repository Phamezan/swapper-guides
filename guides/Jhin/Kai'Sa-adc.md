## Key tips

- Never take her full-health 1v1. Without a poke lead you lose it every time, so only fight her from an advantage.
  sources: [omibro]

- Bounce Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) off dying minions and hold the loaded fourth shot to zone her. Grenade first, then the shot, is your max-damage trade.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Play this lane for priority and an early lead rather than kills. Answer every shove and never hand her the free crash.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- She wins the straight fight, so make her bleed before it starts. Constant grenade bounces plus the held shot build a slow push she cannot walk into.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Every cast resets the fourth-shot hold, so spend E to keep it loaded almost indefinitely and pressure her off the wave.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q, Jhin.E]

- Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) is a follow-up for your support, not dry poke. Hold it until their CC lands; a wasted W drains mana and invites her all-in.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

- Crash the wave and base for Serrated Dirk. That first base is your biggest spike, so plan recalls around it instead of forcing fights before it.
  sources: [omibro]

- Respect level 2 against engage supports. Do not auto into their range near your tower; her support only needs one opening.
  sources: [omibro]

## Mid lane

- Trap the objective side and the chokes for vision. Hover instead of walking up blind: if her position is unknown, walking in means dying.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

- A landed Deadly Flourish sets up a guaranteed first Curtain Call shot ({cd:202:R}) when fired instantly. Fish with W, then commit the ult at once.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Win condition

- Your pattern is poke first, then fight. But do not refuse every fight: walking up to kill her from half health is correct when the poke has landed.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.Q]

- Fund the crit build with executes. Deathfire Touch plus Collector lets W or Q steal kills and saves a bullet for the next target.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.Q]

- Left alone on a weak side you bleed farm to divers like her. Ask the jungler bot when she is the island matchup; your W sets up kills for both of you.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

- Late, the loaded fourth shot plus Curtain Call outright kills a squishy from around 80% health. No tanks or blockers means your ult is a free kill.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on the first base is the spike. Your grenade and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so crash, base, and return before trading hard.
  sources: [omibro]

- Collector plus Deathfire Touch is the execute package. The extra health threshold lets you save bullets and keep stealing gold toward full crit.
  sources: [omibro]
