## Key tips

- Bully her in lane. Your range and grenade bounces control the push, and she cannot answer your shove early.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Never 1v1 her. She wins every extended trade, so your fights must be short or supported.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Scaling is roughly even between you, so the lane decides the game. A lead now matters more than it does in scaling mismatches.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Bounce Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) off dying minions and hold the loaded fourth shot to zone her. Grenade first, then the shot, is your max-damage trade.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Crash the wave and base for Serrated Dirk. That first base is your biggest spike, so plan recalls around it.
  items: [3134]
  sources: [omibro]

- Hold Deadly Flourish for your support's crowd control and never dry-fire it. A wasted W invites the extended trade you always lose.
  sources: [omibro, yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Mid lane

- Trap the objective side for vision and hover instead of walking up blind. If her position is unknown, walking in means dying.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

- Answer her shove with the team, never alone in a side lane. She runs down lone targets, so rotate as a group.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Win condition

- Fight from range after your support lands something. Hold Deadly Flourish for the follow-up and fire Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) the instant it connects.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Keep every fight short. The moment a trade extends past your burst, disengage instead of chasing into her stacking damage.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on the first base is the spike. Your grenade and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so crash, base, and return before trading hard.
  items: [3134]
  sources: [omibro]
