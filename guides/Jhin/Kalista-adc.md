## Key tips

- She wins the straight duel, so refuse it. A scoreless lane is your victory: 0/0/0 at fifteen minutes counts as winning this matchup.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Play through your jungler instead of through trades. Your snare into Curtain Call gives your jungler a strong setup to convert, which is how a conceded lane turns back around.
  sources: [omibro]

- Read her support before anything else. With a strong engage partner she is suffocating, but she cannot close gaps on her own, so without one her threat collapses.
  sources: [omibro]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade only in short fourth-shot bursts and keep Dancing Grenade ({cd:202:Q}) cycling off dying minions. Short in-and-out damage is your whole offense; anything extended belongs to her.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Give the push early and play under your tower against her engage supports. Contesting the shove walks you into the all-in you cannot win.
  sources: [omibro]

- Do not walk up to auto the wave at all. You give up last hits, never experience: hold the tower line and let every wave arrive to you.
  sources: [omibro]

## Mid lane

- Carry the even scoreline past fifteen and keep refusing duels. Your teamfight casting outscales her need to snowball the lane.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Show for nothing until her support shows first. Every threat she poses arrives through her engage partner, so a visible support is your permission slip to step up.
  sources: [omibro]

## Win condition

- Convert through numbers: ping your jungler into the pressured wave and stack Deadly Flourish into Curtain Call the moment she commits. Your setup, not your damage, kills her.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Close the game even or better and let the bigger fight decide it. She must win lane to matter, so if the lane ends tied her window has already closed.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Itemization

- Buy survivability first and sit on tier-one boots. You never walk up to auto against her engage support, so early damage and movement stats buy nothing: live, stay even, spike later.
  sources: [omibro]
