## Key tips

- This is the best matchup on the board. Crush him from level 1 and never give him a free wave.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Deny his minions and experience, stay aggressive, and do not let him hit level 2 first. The whole lane is decided in the first minutes.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- He outscales if the game drags, so a lead that never becomes an objective is just farm he will eventually match. Close early.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Zone him off the wave with the loaded fourth shot from the first minions. He cannot answer your range, so hold the push and make him choose between farm and health.
  sources: [omibro, yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Crash the wave and base for Serrated Dirk. That first base is your biggest spike, so plan recalls around it.
  items: [3134]
  sources: [omibro]

## Mid lane

- Keep shoving him in and never hand him free waves. Every caster wave he clears uncontested is stacks banked for later.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Trap the objective side for vision and hover instead of walking up blind. If his position is unknown, walking in means dying.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Turn the early lead into dragons and towers. Sitting on a farm lead while he stacks is how this matchup is thrown.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Fight from range after your support lands something. Hold Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) for the follow-up and fire Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) the instant it connects.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Serrated Dirk on the first base is the spike. Your grenade and fourth-shot trades jump in damage, so crash, base, and return before trading hard.
  items: [3134]
  sources: [omibro]
