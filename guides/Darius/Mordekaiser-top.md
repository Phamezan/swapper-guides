## Key tips

- Start fighting at level 2 or 3, never the level-1 coinflip. His early pattern is readable once abilities are down; level 1 is the only window where variance beats you.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Opening level

- Pull then ult before his W comes up. If he holds W for your R, bait it first: E into R denies him the block window entirely.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Beat the shield
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.R, Mordekaiser.W]

- Win the item race to Shojin before his Rylai's. At even farm he spikes first on the cheaper item, so the lane is a clock you must beat.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Item clock

## Overview

## Early lane

- Press the first three minutes while he concedes. Take free health with Q edge and zone him off last-hits instead of handing him uncontested farm.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Early press
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Ghost-dodge his Q and E rather than eating chains. Save Ghost for his pull combo, and step out of every Q cone instead of trading inside it.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Dodge pattern
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q, Mordekaiser.E]

## Mid lane

- Respect his level 4–5 Ignite all-in when healthy. Track Ignite before committing; if it is up and he is full, refuse the extended fight.
  sources: [dreadknight]
  heading: Ignite respect

- Avoid his Qs once Rylai's lands. The slow field makes every later Q stick, so stop standing in pull range at even items.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Post-spike rule
  abilities: [Mordekaiser.Q]

## Win condition

- Reach five stacks and ult before he realms you. His stat steal guts your damage inside the realm, so finish the setup outside it.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Pre-realm finish
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- If he realms someone else, punish immediately. A quick combo into R, even before five stacks, turns his engage into your reset opening.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Teamfight punish
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.R]

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Shield into Pickaxe and Boots, then Spear of Shojin, Boots of Swiftness, Stridebreaker, and Kaenic Rookern for the stack-and-chase package.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
