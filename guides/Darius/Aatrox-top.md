## Key tips

- Space around Q and never eat his full combo. Give ground during each cast sequence and step back in only once the blades land.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Spacing rule
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Take short E plus auto plus W plus Q trades once Executioner's Calling lands. His sustain is mostly combat healing, so the anti-heal window is when your trade wins.
  items: [3123]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Anti-heal window
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.W, Darius.Q]

- Press level 1 and threaten Ghost. He respects both, so contest the opening wave instead of conceding it for free.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Level-1 press

## Overview

## Early lane

- Never dash forward into his pull range blindly. He holds E for your step-in, so approach on foot and force him to spend Qs defensively.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Patient approach
  abilities: [Darius.E]

- Do not time your pull on his second Q. His E repositions mid-sequence and evades exactly that timing, so hold E until the dash is spent.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Hold pull
  abilities: [Darius.E]

## Mid lane

- Respect the level-6 burst, especially with Ignite. His all-in turn arrives with R, so track both summoner and ultimate before trading below full health.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Burst respect

- Slow him with Stridebreaker to force E defensively. Once the slow sticks, his reposition goes to survival instead of combos, and your window opens.
  items: [6631]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stridebreaker use

## Win condition

- Build five stacks and finish with R once Shojin completes. After that spike your stacked trades outscale his healing, so stack patiently and close with the reset.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Shojin turn
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- Favor side lanes over grouped fights. The 1v1 lets you control spacing and stacks, while a grouped fight gives him multi-target combo angles.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side-lane plan

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror with Celerity, opening Doran's Blade into Plated Steelcaps and Executioner's Calling, then Stridebreaker and Spear of Shojin.
  items: [1055, 3047, 3123, 6631, 3161]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
