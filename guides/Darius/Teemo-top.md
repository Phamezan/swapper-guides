## Key tips

- Never fight him while low. He wins the melee if Darius starts below roughly 90% health, so concede last-hits until Second Wind tops Darius back up rather than forcing a bad trade.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Health gate
  patch: "26.20"

- Buy Negatron Cloak and Mercury's Treads before walking up. His poke only matters while Darius lacks magic resist, and Treads cut the slow that lets him run Darius down after a short trade.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: First buys
  patch: "26.20"

- Hold Apprehend until he steps past the minion line. He wants to poke from beyond pull range and disengage with his W movespeed into seeded shrooms, so a wasted pull leaves Darius blinded in the open with nothing to show for it.
  sources: [trickybam]
  heading: Pull discipline
  abilities: [Darius.E, Teemo.W]
  patch: "26.17"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Check the turret-side bush before leashing or walking to lane at level 1. His standard cheese is waiting there for a free blind-plus-autos opener that ruins the health gate for the whole lane.
  sources: [temoonlyifok]
  heading: Level 1

- After a pull lands, expect blind plus a step inside Q. Open with W crippling immediately after Apprehend lands, so he cannot walk into the Q dead zone for free while Darius is blinded.
  sources: [temoonlyifok]
  heading: Pull answer
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q, Darius.E, Teemo.Q]

- Do not rely on a single slow to stick to him. He commonly runs Swifties precisely to blunt Decimate slows, so plan the all-in around the Stridebreaker active rather than a foot chase.
  sources: [temoonlyifok, federals1-darius]
  heading: Stick tools
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Darius.E]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Make him spend his burst movespeed before Apprehend. Whether it is his W sprint or a rune proc, the pull only sticks once that speed is gone, and Stridebreaker's slow creates a far more reliable window than a raw walk-up.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Movespeed first
  abilities: [Darius.E, Teemo.W]
  patch: "26.20"

- Take only short W-plus-Q contacts while his blind is up. His whole counter is blind-then-run into shrooms, so deal damage in brief Crippling-plus-Decimate touches and reset instead of chasing through the blind.
  sources: [trickybam, federals1-darius]
  heading: Short contacts
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q, Teemo.Q]
  patch: "26.17"

## Win condition

- Sweep with a red trinket after level 6 and never walk a shroomed choke at low stacks. His shrooms decide where fights happen, and one blind walk through them turns a won side lane into a throw.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Shroom sweep
  abilities: [Teemo.R]
  patch: "26.20"

- Prefer side pressure when his shroom coverage makes grouped routes unsafe. He cannot cover every lane entrance at once, so push the side he has not seeded and force him to answer without his minefield.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side-lane plan
  abilities: [Teemo.R]
  patch: "26.20"

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror with Second Wind, opening Doran's Shield into Negatron Cloak and Mercury's Treads, then Stridebreaker and Kaenic Rookern as the core package that lets Darius walk up at all.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  patch: "26.20"

- Finish Kaenic Rookern once teamfights start. The shield plus magic resist keeps a stray shroom or blind-plus-focus from ending Darius before the frontline fight begins.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  patch: "26.20"
