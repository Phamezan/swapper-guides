## Key tips

- Vary pull and ult timing to bait Riposte. Never give her a predictable E: threaten, hold, and cast only when the parry guesses wrong.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Bait the parry
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.R, Fiora.W]

- After she Qs in, answer W plus Q. The contact either forces her parry early or loses her the trade on the spot.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Answer the dash
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q, Fiora.Q]

- Zone her off the wave at levels 1–2. She concedes everything before level 3, so take the farm gap while it is free.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Early zone

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not chase her max-range Q pokes at level 1. Let the unanswerable pokes expire and hold position instead of donating health for nothing.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Poke patience
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

- Commit only after she dashes in. Her mobility dodges dry Qs, so trade on her entry rather than throwing Qs at her spacing.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: No dry Qs
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Fiora.Q]

## Mid lane

- Time Stridebreaker against her Q dash. Active as she commits so the slow tracks her, then hold Q edge while it lasts.
  items: [6631]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stridebreaker tracking

- Press before her first item lands. Her reliable edge arrives with it against a careful pilot, so force the lane while she is still item-gated.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Pre-item window

## Win condition

- Wall her ultimate. Stand with a wall at your back so one vital stays denied and her full proc plus healing zone never completes.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Beat the duel
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- Take side lanes only with spacing and items. After three or four items your spaced 1v1 holds, but a chaotic grouped fight lets her reach all four vitals.
  sources: [federals1-darius, justsad42]
  heading: Side-lane rule

- Hold Ghost as the answer to her disengage. She fears the held summoner more than the spent one, so threaten with it and cast only once she commits.
  sources: [justsad42, federals1-darius]
  heading: Ghost threat

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Shield into Bramble Vest or Executioner's Calling and Plated Steelcaps, then Stridebreaker, Spear of Shojin, and Sterak's Gage.
  items: [1054, 3076, 3123, 3047, 6631, 3161, 3053]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
