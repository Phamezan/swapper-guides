## Key tips

- Save Apprehend to interrupt Hookshot and never spend it first. Holding E keeps her engage honest; spending it gives her a free dash with no answer ready.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Hold E
  abilities: [Darius.E, Camille.E]

- Never initiate with E. Her ultimate dodges your Q and traps you for the gank, so let her commit before answering.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: No blind pulls
  abilities: [Darius.E, Camille.R]

- Keep trades short. Her plan is Q damage then E away, so answer at Q edge and refuse the chase she wants.
  sources: [federals1-darius, raideru]
  heading: Trade length
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Camille.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect level 1 if she holds Flash plus Ignite with Q. Concede the cheese angle rather than donating first blood to the burst window.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 respect

- Freeze and make her choose. A frozen wave forces her to pick farm or roam, so hold it and ping every departure.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Freeze plan

## Mid lane

- Space her true-damage second Q. Never stand at its edge for free; step out of the empowered cast and re-engage after it lands.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Dodge Q2
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

- When she steps inside your Q, hold the cast and W her instead. She enters the inner ring to deny the edge, so punish the entry with the slow rather than whiffing.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Inner-ring answer
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Darius.W]

## Win condition

- Punish her backline dives after R. Once the ultimate is down, turn on her team fight instead of letting her reset onto a carry.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Dive punish
  abilities: [Camille.R]

- Ghost only when her R and E are down. She saves the ultimate to dodge and avoids Ghost chases, so start the run-down once both mobility tools are spent.
  sources: [raideru, federals1-darius]
  heading: Ghost timing

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Blade through Cloth Armor and Phage into Plated Steelcaps, then Stridebreaker, Spear of Shojin, and Sterak's Gage.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
