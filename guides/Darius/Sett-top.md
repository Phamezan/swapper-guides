## Key tips

- Never trade at his auto range. Open with W into Q from your longer W reach, so he must spend his pull to answer or concede the poke exchange.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Spacing opener
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q, Sett.E]

- Build two passive stacks before he reaches auto range. Once both sides finish Stridebreaker, spacing and active timing decide the duel, so enter it stacked, not flat.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stack first
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Darius.W]

- Never spend the ultimate into a held Haymaker. Sett players chip you first and time W for your ult, so bait it out with Q and W trades and only commit R after W is gone.
  sources: [autolykus, federals1-darius]
  heading: Ult timing
  abilities: [Darius.R, Sett.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Levels 1–3, position outside his auto range and let W plus Q do the work. Each cast forces a choice on him: spend his pull early or take free damage.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Levels 1–3
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q, Sett.E]

- Once his pull is down, he has no clean way to start an all-in. Hold your pull for his re-engage and keep the trade at Q edge instead of walking into his autos.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Punish the spent pull
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.Q, Sett.E]

## Mid lane

- Reach five Hemorrhage stacks through short W and Q contacts before committing. His side wants to chip you and answer the all-in, so do not extend a flat fight at full health into his second rotation.
  sources: [federals1-darius, autolykus]
  heading: Stack discipline
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q, Darius.P]

- Time your Stridebreaker active against his. With the item done on both sides, the duel turns on who slows whom first: active as he steps in, then stay at Q edge while it lasts.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stridebreaker timing

## Win condition

- Keep his pull from starting a clean all-in. When he drags you in, space back toward Q edge instead of standing still and brawling at his auto range.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Deny the engage
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.Q, Sett.E]

- Dodge the true-damage part of Haymaker ({cd:875:W}). Never stand still directly in front of him while W is available; move out before it fires, then re-engage while it is down.
  sources: [federals1-darius, autolykus]
  heading: Beat Haymaker
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Prefer side-lane pressure over grouped fights. His ultimate can isolate you in front of his team, while a side lane gives you room to space his pull and chase him down once it is down.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side-lane plan
  abilities: [Sett.R]

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror with Bone Plating and Overgrowth, opening Doran's Blade into Plated Steelcaps, then Stridebreaker and Sterak's Gage as the core duel package.
  sources: [federals1-darius]

- If he rushes Blade of the Ruined King, start Bramble Vest and finish Thornmail before Sterak's, so his extended trades stop outpacing your stacks.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
