## Key tips

- Make him spend both dashes to reach you before turning to fight. A Renekton with no E left cannot answer the extended bleed trade.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Burn both dashes
  abilities: [Renekton.E, Darius.Q]

- Without a wave built for the all-in, refuse the trade. His short fury trades win every even exchange, so concede and reset rather than bleed out.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Wave-gated fights

- Draw out his empowered W before relying on Sterak's. It breaks the shield, so bait the stun first and commit only after it is spent ({cd:58:W}).
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Bait empowered W
  abilities: [Renekton.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- At level 1, W-punish him if he opens Q. If he opens E, match with your own E to catch his second dash, or play the slow Q game and let him come to you.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 answer
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.E, Renekton.Q, Renekton.E]

- The level-1 W plus Doran's Blade cheese works on him, but only on your terms. Set the wave first so the trade happens where you win it.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Set cheese
  abilities: [Darius.W]

- Respect his early damage and Q range through the first levels. Track the enemy jungler too, because his gank setup punishes every overextended freeze break.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Respect and track
  abilities: [Renekton.Q]

## Mid lane

- Respect level 6 above everything. The extra health and fury turn every half-commit into his kill window, so trade only with a way out until the spike passes.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-6 rule
  abilities: [Renekton.R]

- Build five Hemorrhage stacks before spending Guillotine late. Grouped fights hand you the stacks, while chasing him solo into an empowered stun gives him the turn.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stack before ult
  abilities: [Darius.R, Renekton.W]

## Win condition

- You outscale him, so stop forcing the side 1v1 late. Short trades stay unsafe while his empowered stun is up; group for resets instead.
  sources: [federals1-darius, paul231]
  heading: Group to close
  abilities: [Renekton.W]

- Close through items, not ego. Stack patiently, ult at five, and let Sterak's carry the turn once his empowered W is gone.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Patient close
  abilities: [Darius.R]

## Itemization

- If Renekton rushes Blade of the Ruined King, build Thornmail second and Sterak's third. The rush beats your default curve, so answer the item before returning to damage.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: BotRK answer
