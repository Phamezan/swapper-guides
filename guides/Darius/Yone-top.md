## Key tips

- Never open with a blind Decimate. His Soul Unbound slips the cast for free, so pull first and land Q on the caught target.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: No blind Q
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.Q, Yone.E]

- Contest level 1 only while his Q3 is nowhere near ready ({cd:777:Q}). A stored Q3 lets him reverse the fight or leave for free, so concede the first wave rather than force it.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 condition
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Finish at five Hemorrhage stacks once Spear of Shojin is done. Ult before he snaps back to his E mark, because the recast erases the execute window.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Shojin finish
  abilities: [Darius.R, Yone.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hold Ghost while his Q3 is available. He disengages or turns on you the moment you commit into it, so spend Ghost only after the knock-up is gone.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Ghost discipline
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Refuse short trades under his tower. His minions add the damage your axe cannot match there, so hold your side, thin the wave, and invite him out.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: Your side only

## Mid lane

- Win mid game through spacing, not volume. Every Q he dodges is a lost exchange, so trade only the E-first combo and walk out before he stacks Momentum.
  sources: [federals1-darius, paul231]
  heading: E-first only
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.Q]

- Do not chase his E mark after a short trade. The recast returns him for free, so bank the damage dealt and reset instead of following into his Q3.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: No mark chase
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.Q]

## Win condition

- Take side duels only after Randuin's plus Sterak's. Before that pairing his Q3 reverses every all-in, so group for resets rather than forcing the 1v1.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Item-gated duels

- Re-engage while your bleed is still ticking on him. The stacked ticks turn his Q3 return into a losing trade, which is why he must clear stacks before coming back.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: Bleed re-engage
  abilities: [Yone.Q]
