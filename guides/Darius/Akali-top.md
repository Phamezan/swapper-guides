## Key tips

- Take short auto plus W plus Q trades, then disengage before Titanic Hydra. The burst-and-out pattern denies her extended combo while your item turn approaches.
  items: [3748]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Trade pattern
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q]

- Pull her E-dash and never throw E into shroud. Her dash commits her to a line; your pull answers it, while shroud eats blind casts.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Darius.E, Akali.E]

- Track three stacks: she shrouds to drop them. Re-engage as the shroud fades instead of letting the reset go free.
  sources: [anguish, federals1-darius]
  heading: Stack tracking

## Overview

## Early lane

- When she steps inside your Q, answer with W and autos. She enters the ring to deny the edge and heal, so punish the entry instead of chasing the whiffed cast.
  sources: [anguish, federals1-darius]
  heading: Inner-ring answer
  abilities: [Darius.W, Darius.Q]

- Punish her Q and E cooldowns with short trades. Step in while either is down and leave before the full sequence returns.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Cooldown abuse
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Akali.E]

## Mid lane

- Finish Negatron into Titanic for the repeatable three-stack trade. The auto reset lands stacks even through shroud, so the trade no longer needs vision.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Item turn

- Disengage with Stormraider speed after trades. Take the burst and leave before her return combo, resetting on your terms.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Trade exits

## Win condition

- Press side-lane towers instead of hunting teamfight catches. You rarely catch her grouped without assured resets, so convert pressure where she must answer.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side-lane plan

- Reset when chunked. Her all-in line on a low target is real, so only commit above it and give the freeze rather than the kill.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Health discipline

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Shield into Giant's Belt and Negatron Cloak, then Titanic Hydra, Boots of Swiftness, Kaenic Rookern, and Hullbreaker for the trade-and-shove package.
  items: [1054, 1011, 1057, 3748, 3009, 2504, 3181]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
