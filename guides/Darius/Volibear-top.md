## Key tips

- Respect levels 1–2 and the bleed stacks. He fears your early zone, so press with spacing but never donate the all-in he wants.
  sources: [federals1-volibear, federals1-darius]
  heading: Early respect

- Pull and trade at Q edge; never stand and brawl to the death. His Lethal Tempo wins static slugfests, so Q and step out instead of trading inside his autos.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Edge trading
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Build five stacks and spend R once Shojin completes. The stacked reset is your kill condition, so stack patiently and finish with the dunk.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Shojin turn
  abilities: [Darius.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish level 1 without overextending the deny. Take the free health and zone, but do not chase past the wave for XP he will reclaim anyway.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 punish

- When he steps inside Q, W him instead of whiffing the edge. The inner-ring entry denies your heal, so answer with the slow and autos.
  sources: [federals1-volibear, federals1-darius]
  heading: Inner answer
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Darius.W]

## Mid lane

- Respect level 3. His Q plus marked-W sustained trade is his strongest window, so refuse extended contact until your item answer lands.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-3 respect
  abilities: [Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]

- Vary pull timing because he times R against it. Never pull on pattern; hold E until his R answer cannot cover the cast.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Pull discipline
  abilities: [Darius.E, Volibear.R]

## Win condition

- Race Shojin plus Stridebreaker into his Rod plus Navori two items. Your spike beats his at even farm, so force the issue in that window.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Spike race

- Take teamfights from strength. If he catches up to full build his value rises, so convert your mid-game edge before parity returns.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Teamfight timing

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror with Transcendence and Axiom Arcanist, opening Doran's Blade into Mercury's Treads and Shojin, with Executioner's Calling, Stridebreaker, and Sterak's Gage.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
