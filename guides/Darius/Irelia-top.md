## Key tips

- Deny her XP at level 1 before her passive stacks. Stand between her and the wave early; an unstacked Irelia cannot contest your zone.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 deny

- Dodge her E and never chase through minions. Sidestep the stun line instead of walking it straight, and let chained Q dashes expire without an answer.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stun discipline
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Irelia.Q]

- Rush Bramble Vest plus Plated Steelcaps before her Blade of the Ruined King. That window answers her rush; without it, her completed item turns every all-in.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Armor timing

## Overview

## Early lane

- Give her no punish window at levels 4–5. She starts winning fights there off your missed Qs, so cast Q only when she cannot answer and concede the brief peak instead of forcing into it.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Respect the peak
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Hold Q edge while she repositions. Each dash is a guess at your cast point, so delay Q until she commits to a minion rather than throwing it at her first dash.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Delayed Q
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Irelia.Q]

## Mid lane

- Never walk into her R after level 6. The slow sets up her stun, so step out of the blades and re-engage only once the slow field expires.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Post-6 respect
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.E]

- Answer Blade of the Ruined King with short trades. Each missed dodge costs more once her item is done, so freeze and punish cooldowns instead of brawling.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Post-item rule

## Win condition

- Stack to five and ult once Spear of Shojin is done. After that spike your stacked trades outscale her burst, so build patiently and finish with the reset.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Shojin turn
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- Prefer side lanes over grouped fights. A side lane gives room to space her dashes and stack freely, while a grouped fight lets her chain Q through your team onto you.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side-lane plan
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Blade into Bramble Vest and Plated Steelcaps against her rush, then Stridebreaker into Spear of Shojin and Sterak's Gage.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
