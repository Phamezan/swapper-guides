## Key tips

- Cheater-recall on wave 3 or 4 for Cull, then freeze on your side. The frozen wave forces him to overextend into pull range or concede farm.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Freeze plan

- Trade less before Stridebreaker, then stick with its slow. The item is the lane's hinge: before it, concede the mobility game; after it, he cannot walk away from stacked trades.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stridebreaker hinge

- Manage Bone Plating around his burst windows. When Plating is down at levels 1–2, refuse his E-auto-Q; with Plating up, you can contest the same trade.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Plating windows
  abilities: [Garen.E, Garen.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish his attempts to break the freeze. If he walks up to crash the frozen wave, meet him with the pull rather than letting him reset for free.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Punish breaks
  abilities: [Darius.E]

- Do not walk into the lane brush at level 1. Meet the wave instead, take short contact, and back out before he can extend an auto-Q-auto into a longer duel.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Level 1
  abilities: [Garen.Q]

## Mid lane

- Follow his speed rune with your own and rotate first. Clear side waves quickly and move, since he covers the map faster later and punishes late rotations.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Rotation race

- Run him down the long lane when the wave sits on your side. With Ghost up and distance behind him, his short-trade pattern cannot save him; without Ghost, hold the freeze instead of diving.
  sources: [yshuro13, federals1-darius]
  heading: Long-lane chase

## Win condition

- Respect the level-6 execute range. Around 65% health with his Ignite up and no pull available, his full burst can finish you; reset or hold summoners before crossing that line.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Execute respect
  abilities: [Garen.R]

- At rank-3 ultimate, stay above his execute threshold and follow an ally's engage. Enter after the fight starts rather than opening it, so his burst lands on someone else first.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Teamfight entry
  abilities: [Garen.R]

## Itemization

- Take Stormraider's Surge with Cash Back and the movement-speed shard, opening Doran's Blade into Cull and Boots of Swiftness, then Stridebreaker, Spear of Shojin, and Sterak's Gage.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
