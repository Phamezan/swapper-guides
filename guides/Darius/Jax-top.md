## Key tips

- Never open with Apprehend and refuse short trades. Space at W outer edge, beyond his W range, and let him come to you instead of offering the brawl he wants.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Spacing rule
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.W, Jax.W]

- Reserve Apprehend to interrupt Counter Strike or his leap. Holding E keeps his stun honest; spending it first gives him a free engage with no answer ready.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Hold E
  abilities: [Darius.E, Jax.E, Jax.Q]

- Let Counter Strike finish before Ghosting. Chasing into an active E wastes the summoner, so start the run-down at E expiry and build to five stacks into the ultimate at Spear of Shojin.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Ghost timing
  abilities: [Darius.R, Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish from Q edge without committing. He closes only when E is ready to block the W answer, so meet each approach with Q and step out instead of trading inside his stun.
  sources: [federals1-darius, ph45]
  heading: Q edge
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Jax.E]

- Respect his Ignite window. He takes Ignite to limit your Decimate healing, so refuse committed all-ins while it is up and fight once it is down.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]
  heading: Ignite respect

## Mid lane

- Predict the E reactivation and time W to land as the dodge ends. Watch his rhythm across trades: the W that lands right after Counter Strike expires is what turns the exchange.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Beat the dodge
  abilities: [Darius.W, Jax.E]

- Answer his leap-disjoint with Q plus Flash. When he times the leap to avoid Decimate, add Flash to the Q cast so the heal and stacks still land.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Q Flash
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Jax.Q]

## Win condition

- Ghost only after E ends and keep the chase in open ground. His plan is to block W and reset the ring, so do not start the run-down until the stun threat is gone.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Run-down
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Prefer side lanes over teamfighting into his E. A side lane gives room to space his autos and stack to five, while a grouped fight lets him stun you inside his team.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side-lane plan
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror with Transcendence, opening Doran's Blade through Ruby Crystals into Boots, then Spear of Shojin into Stridebreaker and Sterak's Gage for the stack-and-chase package.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
