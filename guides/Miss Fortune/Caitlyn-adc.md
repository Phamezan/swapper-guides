- Never duel her range. She pokes you down from outside your reply, so win through Double Up ({cd:21:Q}) bounce angles from far away, not through trades.
  sources: [yunfang-mf, rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Slow her with Make It Rain ({cd:21:E}) and take only short trades with your support. Extended fights and solo trades belong to her.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E]

- Track her 90 Caliber Net ({cd:51:E}) and Flash. She keeps disengage ready before trading, so engage only when both are down.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Be patient and farm with Q bounces off the wave while she holds her net. Forcing plays into her ready disengage is how this lane is lost.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Punish when her net is down. That is the only window your short trades win, so call your support the moment she spends it.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E]

- Position outside her auto range when going for last hits. Every free poke she lands without an answer is the lane slipping toward her.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"

## Mid lane

- Group skirmishes around your support, where waves meet and bounce angles appear. Alone in a side lane you have no setup and she picks you apart.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Hold your slow for her overextensions. When she steps up to poke, land Make It Rain and take the short support trade before she disengages.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E]

## Win condition

- Take only short supported trades in fights, never the long poke war. Her range wins every extended exchange, so force quick engages and leave.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"

- Collapse her range with numbers. She dies once locked down without her net, so bring your support or jungler to the fight she wants to take alone.
  sources: [rowzkit, yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E]

## Itemization

- Your gold comes from patient Q-farm, not kills. Base on clean crashes and keep your support's tempo instead of forcing buys for a duel you cannot win yet.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
