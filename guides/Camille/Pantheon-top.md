## Key tips

- Open trades with Q, never with an auto. Your passive shield lands before his spear does, so the first damage exchange starts in your favor.
  sources: [wizbe]
  heading: Q first
  abilities: [Camille.Q, Pantheon.Q]

- Fire Q2 early when he holds E for it. He saves the shield for your true damage, so a banked proc is a blocked proc: release it before he sets.
  sources: [wizbe, procatking]
  heading: Q2 mindgame
  abilities: [Camille.Q, Pantheon.E]

- Refuse direct trades before your first item. He wins them all, so farm with W, keep health high, and duel only after the spike lands.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Pre-item patience

## Overview

## Early lane

- Sweep where his W landing still pays. He jumps inside your W to blunt it, so place the zone on his exit rather than his entry.
  sources: [procatking]
  heading: Inside answer
  abilities: [Camille.W, Pantheon.W]

- Hold the charged Q2 as a zoning threat. He refuses it outright, so keep it glowing while he decides, and release it the moment he commits to the trade.
  sources: [procatking, wizbe]
  heading: Charged zone
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

## Mid lane

- Hold R for his stun and E to interrupt his engage. The pair beats every committed jump he owns, so keep both answers off cooldown when he postures.
  sources: [wizbe]
  heading: Stun answer
  abilities: [Camille.R, Camille.E, Pantheon.W]

- Ping the moment he leaves lane past level 6. His R turns other lanes while you answer with plates, so call it early and shove.
  sources: [wizbe]
  heading: Roam ping

## Win condition

- Take past the first item the duels you refused early. The lane flips at the spike, so punish the windows his spent cooldowns leave.
  sources: [raideru, wizbe]
  heading: Item turn

- Wait out empowered E instead of feeding it Q2. Its duration eats the proc outright, so hold the kick until the shield drops.
  sources: [wizbe]
  heading: Shield wait
  abilities: [Pantheon.E, Camille.Q]
