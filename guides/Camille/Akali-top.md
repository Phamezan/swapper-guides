## Key tips

- Open every exchange with poke near walls after her Q is spent, and refuse to chase into shroud. Long shroud fights are where she wins; short wall trades are where you do.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Trading rule
  abilities: [Camille.W]

- Bank a health lead before committing. Chunk her with shielded shorts first, and take the all-in only while you are up on health.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Health-lead rule

- Hold Hookshot and Hextech Ultimatum for spent mobility. She dodges your E with her own E and holds E2 and R2 for your R landing, so engage only after those answers are gone.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Answer discipline
  abilities: [Camille.E, Camille.R, Akali.E, Akali.R]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Levels 1 to 3, poke when she steps for farm and trade behind your passive shield, but never chase extended E lines. Her Q downtime is the window; her up-Q near a wall is her turn, not yours.
  sources: [raideru, anguish]
  heading: Levels 1–3

- Time engages for right after her Q. She fights in Q, passive auto, Q bursts, so stepping in on her spent Q leaves her without the pattern while your shield and second Q are ready.
  sources: [anguish, raideru]
  heading: Q timing
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Camille.Q]

## Mid lane

- Keep fights near walls for Hookshot angles. She wants open ground where your E finds nothing, so pull skirmishes toward terrain and punish her drift into the open.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Positioning

- Zone her approach with the outer ring of your W. She refuses to stand in it, which funnels her Q, passive, Q pattern into the line your E is already holding.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Wave and zone
  abilities: [Camille.W, Camille.E]
  patch: "26.20"

## Win condition

- Commit the ultimatum only from a health lead with her E2 and R2 tracked as spent. She has no answer left at that point, and the Grasp plus cookies scaling she relies on never arrives.
  sources: [anguish, raideru]
  heading: Closing rule
  abilities: [Camille.R]
