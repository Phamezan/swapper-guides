## Key tips

- Start Q and fight the level-1 brush. The first levels are your strongest, so bank the lead before his range and sustain matter.
  sources: [sharphandjoe]
  heading: Level-1 brush
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

- Start every trade after he spends Q. Cancel autos to bait the cast when he aims at your last-hits, then go while the cooldowns are dark.
  sources: [sharphandjoe, raideru]
  heading: Q clock
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

- Threaten with a held R to starve his rotation. He withholds Q3 while your ultimate is up, so the menace of the dodge punishes the forced cast that follows.
  sources: [lilpicky, sharphandjoe]
  heading: Held R
  abilities: [Camille.R, Aatrox.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Never trade autos into his ready passive. Space the Q zone and wait the proc out instead of donating health for stacks you cannot win.
  sources: [sharphandjoe]
  heading: Passive respect
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

- Take the opening trade when he spends E early. Without the dash his Q sequence cannot track you, so punish the wasted mobility at once.
  sources: [sharphandjoe]
  heading: E opening
  abilities: [Camille.E, Aatrox.E]

## Mid lane

- Chip with shorts past level 6 and hold R to dodge one Q in the all-in. The dodge turns his committed rotation into your kill window.
  sources: [sharphandjoe, raideru]
  heading: Post-6 chip
  abilities: [Camille.R, Aatrox.Q]

- E out the instant his W pull catches you. Staying gifts him the chain his Q3 ends, so exit first and re-engage on your terms.
  sources: [sharphandjoe]
  heading: W exit
  abilities: [Camille.E, Aatrox.W]

## Win condition

- Never Hookshot onto a spot where his Q sweet spot waits. Pick landings outside his drawn zones, since a predicted entry is a free knock-up for him.
  sources: [sharphandjoe]
  heading: Landing rule
  abilities: [Camille.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Spend E as an exit, not an entry, in the deciding fights. He watches your Hookshot to time Q3, so keep it holstered until you need the escape.
  sources: [sharphandjoe, lilpicky]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Camille.E]
