## Key tips

- Vary Hookshot timing every trade to bait her Riposte. A predictable E gets parried and stunned, which ends the fight before your second Q lands.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Hookshot timing
  abilities: [Camille.E, Fiora.W]

- Trade only with your passive shield up and deny her free vitals. Shieldless autos into her let her proc and kite for nothing.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Shield rule

- Never ult into a held Riposte on a gank. She saves W for your R landing, so commit the ultimatum only after her parry is gone.
  sources: [justsad42, raideru]
  heading: Ult discipline
  abilities: [Camille.R, Fiora.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Fight near walls for Hookshot angles and keep her in the open off them. Her game is open-ground vitals, so pull every skirmish toward terrain she cannot ignore.
  sources: [justsad42, raideru]
  heading: Terrain rule
  abilities: [Camille.E]

- Sweep at max range where she must Q or eat the outer hit. If she burns Q to exit, her disengage is gone and your E window opens.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Sweep setup
  abilities: [Camille.W, Fiora.Q]

## Mid lane

- Hold your second Q until her kite options are spent. She walks away with vital and ult move speed after Q1, so proc Q2 only once she has committed position.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Q2 patience
  abilities: [Camille.Q, Fiora.R]

- Bait mid-lane Hookshots without committing, then re-engage. She answers the first E with positioning, so the second cast after her cooldowns is the real engage.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: E baiting
  abilities: [Camille.E]

## Win condition

- Commit from a health lead with her Flash and Riposte tracked as spent. Her wall-bait setup needs both, and without them she cannot punish your engage.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Closing rule
  abilities: [Camille.E, Fiora.W]

- Split away from her rather than dueling even. She controls the 1v1 once Riposte timing settles, so carry fights with roams and picks instead.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Macro answer
