## Key tips

- Spin in right after she autos a minion, before the hit can trigger her shield. The trade starts while her passive is still dark, which is the only fair opening she gives.
  sources: [chaddouk]
  heading: Auto bait
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E]

- Keep exchanges short around her Q. Standing through repeated procs loses to the true-damage conversion, so hit and leave on every cycle.
  sources: [chaddouk]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

- Spend your spin to force or follow her Hookshot, never before it. Her E cooldown runs longer than yours, and a held Hookshot escapes both Ghost and your ultimate.
  sources: [chaddouk, raideru]
  heading: E for E
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E, Camille.E, Tryndamere.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect her level 1 and answer with short trades only. Neither side wins the opener by committing, so poke without overextending into her shield window.
  sources: [chaddouk, raideru]
  heading: Level-1 respect

- Check her summoners and runes before first contact. Her setups change the level-1 math completely, so scout the loadout before picking the opener.
  sources: [chaddouk]
  heading: Summoner scout

## Mid lane

- Spin through her W instead of eating it, inward or out. Respect its range and slow for the whole lane, since the outer edge sets up everything she does next.
  sources: [chaddouk]
  heading: W dodge
  abilities: [Tryndamere.E, Camille.W]

- Bait the Hookshot with a re-engage threat before committing Ghost and R past level 6. She holds E to disengage exactly that combination, so force it out first.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Ghost bait
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R, Camille.E]

## Win condition

- Take the direct duel from even or ahead, and never donate free empowered Q hits on the way in. Your sustained damage holds the 1v1 once the approach is clean.
  sources: [chaddouk]
  heading: Even duel
  abilities: [Camille.Q]

- Ping the instant she leaves lane and check the sides. She roams even from behind, so answer the disappearance before her deficit becomes your team's problem.
  sources: [chaddouk]
  heading: Roam ping
