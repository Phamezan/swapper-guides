## Key tips

- Open with a faked Q to draw Riposte, then commit the real combo after her W is gone. A parried Q hands her the stun that decides the duel.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Q bait
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Fiora.W]

- Track her vitals and move away from the marked side, using a wall to deny easy angles. Every free proc is the extended trade she wants.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Vital denial

- Silence before ulting, and never telegraph Q into R. Apply Q's silence right before R while varying the timing, since a predictable Q or R gets parried.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Ult setup
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Garen.R, Fiora.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use Q to clear her Riposte slow and attack-speed debuff, then spin through it. The slow never reduces your E damage, so keep spinning once Q is back up.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Slowed spins
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Garen.E, Fiora.W]

- Accept a small Q poke if minion aggro keeps the wave near your side. Conceding position for perfect health loses the wave, while a held wave denies her run-down angles.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Wave over health
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

## Mid lane

- Cancel E early sometimes to surprise her with Q. The shortened spin breaks her parry timing and lands the silence she was holding W against.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: E cancel
  abilities: [Garen.E, Garen.Q, Fiora.W]

- Answer her melee commits with E instead of chasing max-range pokes. She wants you to overextend into her vitals; punish the commits she actually makes.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Punish commits
  abilities: [Garen.E]

## Win condition

- Favor fights when both champions are already around half health over fresh full-health duels. The short window favors your burst combo before her sustained damage takes over.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Half-health fights

- Never chase her split alone even with a movement-speed rune. Account for her chase and split-push threat by answering with numbers instead of solo runs.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Split answer
