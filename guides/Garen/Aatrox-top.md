## Key tips

- Sidestep the first Q laterally and leave the second Q's outer zone. Stepping forward beats some casts, and your Q carries you out of his W pull when the zone closes.
  sources: [yshuro13, yshuro13-web]
  heading: Both Q casts
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.W]

- Own the first three levels while tracking his cooldowns. That window plus spacing is the whole early game, so press it before his range comes online.
  sources: [yshuro13-web, yshuro13]
  heading: Levels 1–3

- Hold Courage for his commit, never for the wave. He times poke around your W downtime, so a spent shield is his invitation to open up.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: W discipline
  abilities: [Garen.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Expect Q on every approach to a last hit. Concede the creep whenever the trade costs more than the gold, since his aim point is your farming pattern.
  sources: [yshuro13-web, yshuro13]
  heading: Last-hit tax

- Use Q to leave the center of his W, not to engage. The pull zone is where his combo starts, so save the silence-break for exits.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Q as exit
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Aatrox.W]

## Mid lane

- Take Conqueror only to build an early lead with its fighting power. When the damage will not open kills, run Stormraider's Surge and save Q to clear slows instead.
  sources: [federals1-garen]
  heading: Setup pick

- Leave completely until Perseverance restarts. He tags you to hold your healing down, so a half-reset just invites the next Q chain.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Full resets
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Garen.P]

## Win condition

- Let an even lane stay even. You scale slightly better from parity, so stop forcing duels and let the map tilt instead.
  sources: [yshuro13-web, yshuro13]
  heading: Even wins

- Call the jungler only for certain kills. His Q spread turns a hesitant dive into his double kill, so bring numbers or do not come at all.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Gank care
