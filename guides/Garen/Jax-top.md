## Key tips

- Start E and use W when he jumps in or when his stun lands. Decide which part of the trade to blunt, since the shield covers only one burst window.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Trade defense
  abilities: [Garen.E, Garen.W, Jax.Q, Jax.E]

- Never auto-attack into his Counter Strike. Its return damage turns your spin trade into his winning exchange.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: E respect
  abilities: [Garen.E, Jax.E]

- Shape every trade to disengage after your E instead of staying for his return damage. His full combo after your spin ends is where he wins.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Disengage rule
  abilities: [Garen.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Time Q as his stun ends so its damage lands right after, accepting the risk. The tight window punishes his E cooldown, but a mistimed Q wastes the silence.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Q timing
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Jax.E]

- Re-hit him before his passive regenerates. His recovery depends on quiet time, so short re-engages keep him from healing back to full.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Regen denial
  patch: "26.21"

## Mid lane

- From level 6, take short trades before attempting an all-in. Chipping first keeps his ultimate from deciding the finish at full health.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Post-6 pattern

- Threaten the execute once he is low, since he must respect it. His whole late trade bends around your ult range, which opens the all-in you set up with shorts.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Execute threat
  abilities: [Garen.R]
  patch: "26.21"

## Win condition

- Close the lane in the level 6 through mid-game window. You hold the advantage there, while he scales to match you late.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Timing window

- Track his scaling and end before it lands. An even lane that drags on becomes his lane, so convert the mid-game lead into objectives.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Closing rule
