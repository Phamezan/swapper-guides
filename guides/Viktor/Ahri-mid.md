## Overview

## Early lane

- Throw non-obvious Es: she dodges telegraphed casts with W.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- Keep the wave pushed: her threat is Charm into ganks, not the raw 1v1.
  sources: [shokviktor]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- After 6, track her jungler: gank setup is her whole comeback.
  sources: [shokviktor]

- Keep forcing trades: your stage never ends.
  sources: [shokviktor]

## Win condition

- Trades forced plus ganks denied: the Ahri who never sets up never wins.
  sources: [peng04, shokviktor]
