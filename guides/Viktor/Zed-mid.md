## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep him at E range: your poke is free, his engage is not.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- Track his W cooldown: shadow-down is your poke window.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- Field his shadow engage: W under yourself when he commits.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Viktor.W, Zed.W]

- Punish spent shadows: no W means a free E.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: pre-aim W behind yourself when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Viktor.W]

## Win condition

- Range held plus field ready: the Zed who never reaches you never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
