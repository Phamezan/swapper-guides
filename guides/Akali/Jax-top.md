## Key tips

- Against his E start, take Q and farm with poke. Never auto into the raised shield; ranged Qs gather the wave while it is up.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: E-start answer
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Jax.E]

- Against his Q start, take E to escape. His leap commits him, so hold the dash exit until he jumps.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Q-start answer
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Akali.E]

- Save E for his stun and nothing else. Spending it on poke leaves you with no answer when Counter Strike opens.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Akali.E, Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take only short contacts into Grasp. His short-trade pattern wins long exchanges, so Q-poke and leave instead of standing in autos.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Short contacts
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- Re-engage as shroud fades. He disengages at the cover, so the reopening as it expires catches him without an answer ready.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Shroud timing

## Mid lane

- Spread the three level-6 dashes. He holds the stun for the last dash, so never chain all three into an engage; keep one to exit.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dash discipline

- Back off when he ults early. The committed R zones you out, so concede the territory and return once it expires.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Ult respect
  abilities: [Jax.R]

## Win condition

- All-in when Q poke has chunked him and E is held. The poke-then-commit pattern needs both conditions: his health down, your escape up.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Commit rule
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Akali.E]

- Reposition inside shroud because he clicks through it. His targeting aid follows your last position, so never sit still under the cover.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Shroud movement
