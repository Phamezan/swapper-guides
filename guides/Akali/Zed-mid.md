## Key tips

- Poke when he walks up to CS: his farm attempts are your Qs.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- All-in only after his W is down: no shadow means no escape.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Do not walk up early: survive to Voltaic, then poke for kills.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Dodge Q, especially with shadows out: the double shuriken is the damage.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- He Sweepers for your shroud: reposition after stealth, do not linger.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

## Mid lane

- He exits R behind you: E1 there is near-guaranteed.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Akali.E]

- Hold E2 until his shadows are used: recast into safety, not into damage.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- Pre-aim shroud behind you when he Rs: his exit is your escape.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Akali.W]

## Win condition

- Zhonya's plus W-down all-ins: the Zed who never combos first never kills.
  sources: [anguish]
