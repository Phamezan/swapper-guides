## Key tips

- Walk or dash inside the edge of his Q. The outer blade deals more damage and heals him, so the safe spot is right next to him.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Inside Decimate
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Darius.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Keep every trade short. His full sequence out-damages yours, so land Q, proc the passive, and leave before he extends.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Shorts only
  abilities: [Akali.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Change the flip timing and angle every trade. He dodges E on read, so a telegraphed E is a dodged E followed by his pull.
  sources: [anguish, federals1-darius]
  heading: E variation
  abilities: [Akali.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Meet the first wave with the wave, not behind it. His level-1 deny only works if you arrive late, so hold minion contact from the start.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 contact

- Drop his stacks in shroud at three or more. The reset dodges both the bleed damage and the all-in that follows a full stack count.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Stack reset
  abilities: [Akali.W]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Punish Q and E cooldowns with short entries. His threat vanishes between casts, so step in exactly when both axes are down.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Cooldown punish
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Darius.Q, Darius.E]
  patch: "26.20"

- Never overchase past level 6 into his R slow setup. Keep E or the second R cast ready to exit the moment he turns instead of finishing the chase.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: R-slow respect
  abilities: [Akali.E, Akali.R, Darius.R]

## Win condition

- All-in only after shorts have chunked him. The full combo finishes a wounded target, it never starts a fresh duel.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Chunked close
  abilities: [Akali.R]
  patch: "26.20"

- Answer his side lane only from a chunk. His spaced 1v1 outlasts yours at full health, so meet his shove after poke, never before it.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Side answer
