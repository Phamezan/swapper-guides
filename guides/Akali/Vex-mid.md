- Your dashes refill her passive: every E feeds her fear.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- Dodge spells, especially fear on red passive: marked means death.
  sources: [anguish]

- When stacked, her W cancels dashes: never E into a stacked Vex.
  sources: [anguish]
