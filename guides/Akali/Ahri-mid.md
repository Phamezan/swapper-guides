## Key tips

- Do not let her proc Electrocute with auto-W-auto early: spacing beats the rune.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- Weave in and out of your wave: bait Q, dodge Charm.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q, Ahri.E]

- Never all-in while she holds E or R: held answers beat your combo.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- After shroud, use its MS to sidestep E while going for passive: the chase is angled.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.W, Ahri.E]

- Track her energy: Q is expensive, so an empty Ahri cannot answer.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- Your shroud runs a long cooldown ({cd:84:W}): spend it on kills, not farm.
  sources: [peng04]

- When shroud is down she pushes forward: bait the push, then turn.
  sources: [peng04]

## Win condition

- Shroud cycling plus energy starvation: the Ahri who never lands Charm never wins.
  sources: [anguish, peng04]
