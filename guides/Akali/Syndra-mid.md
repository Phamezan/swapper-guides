## Overview

## Early lane

- Bait wave pushes by standing in the wave: her spells hit minions, not you.
  sources: [anguish]

- First Strike scaling page: banks compound while she cannot shove you out.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Drop shroud to freeze her: she gives up CS and leaves when you vanish.
  sources: [trisend3]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

- She escapes if you blow everything: hold a cooldown for the chase.
  sources: [anguish]

## Win condition

- Pre-6 bank plus shroud pressure: the Syndra who never shoved you out never scales past you.
  sources: [shok, anguish]
