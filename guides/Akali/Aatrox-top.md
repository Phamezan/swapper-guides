## Key tips

- Hunt nothing before level 6. Use E movement to avoid Q and kite around shroud until the wave sits near your tower, where your all-in starts.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Pre-6 patience
  abilities: [Akali.E, Akali.W, Aatrox.Q]

- Trade hard the moment his third Q is spent. His early cooldowns are long, so the finished rotation is your whole window to go in.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Q3 rule
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Zone him by holding shroud, and expect his engage the instant it fades. He refuses to commit while shroud or E is up, so the threat of the zone is pressure by itself.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Shroud threat
  abilities: [Akali.W, Akali.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Meet each Q cast with E movement. Sidestepping the sweet spot beats blocking it, so move first and trade second.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: E dodges
  abilities: [Akali.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Ride passive movement speed through his whole sequence. The lane is dodging empowered edges with feet plus E, never standing still to out-trade them.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Feet first
  abilities: [Akali.E, Aatrox.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Keep the wave near your tower, then all-in when he steps forward. The short lane makes his extended Q chains whiff while your combo reaches.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Tower wave

- Kite around shroud instead of standing for repeated Q hits. The zone forces him to choose between eating passive autos and giving ground.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Shroud kite
  abilities: [Akali.W, Aatrox.Q]

## Win condition

- All-in when he extends toward your tower. His kill pressure dies past the midpoint while yours starts, so let him overreach first.
  sources: [paul231]
  heading: Extended punish
  abilities: [Akali.R]

- Throw E only when his movement locks inside a Q cast. A free E is the trigger he plays around, while a forced one starts your all-in.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Committed E
  abilities: [Akali.E, Aatrox.Q]
