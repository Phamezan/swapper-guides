## Key tips

- Never take auto-auto-Q trades early. Hit Q plus a passive auto, then leave before his Q move speed lets him turn the extended fight and stack grit.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Trading rule
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Sett.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Bait Facebreaker at max range and shroud the moment he steps in for it. Without E he cannot flip you, and his whole extended-trade plan falls apart.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Beating Facebreaker
  abilities: [Akali.W, Sett.E]
  patch: "26.20"

- Hold E or R for Haymaker and only commit when his W or E is gone. If he burns either on the wave, that missing cooldown is your all-in window.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Haymaker discipline
  abilities: [Akali.E, Akali.R, Sett.W]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Levels 1 to 3, poke with Q and proc the passive auto, then step out immediately. A second auto lets him stick with Q move speed and build the grit you are trying to deny.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Levels 1–3
  abilities: [Akali.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Expect Grasp and W max with defensive itemization. He wants repeated bruising trades, so give him single Q procs instead and keep E or R ready because Haymaker cycles back fast.
  sources: [autolykus, anguish]
  heading: His setup
  abilities: [Sett.W]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Kite every extended fight with passive move speed, Q and E. The moment Facebreaker is available again, break contact at max range or shroud it rather than standing in flip range.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Kiting pattern
  abilities: [Akali.W, Akali.E, Sett.E]
  patch: "26.20"

- Watch his cooldown use on the wave. When Haymaker or Facebreaker hits minions, engage at once since he has no answer left for your full rotation.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Punish wave use
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.E]
  patch: "26.20"

## Win condition

- Take fights only with shroud plus E or R ready. With that combination up you dictate the trade, and without it you give ground, because a straight stat-check into his cooldowns is the one way he turns the lane.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Fight selection
  abilities: [Akali.W, Akali.R]
