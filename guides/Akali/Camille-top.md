## Key tips

- Trade Q, passive auto, Q, then leave. When her passive shield blocks magic resist, finish with autos, and when it blocks armor, finish with spells.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Trading pattern
  abilities: [Akali.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Dodge Hookshot with your E, using shroud instead if you react late, and never stand in the outer ring of her W. Both of her engage tools miss a moving Akali who holds an answer.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Beating her engage
  abilities: [Akali.E, Akali.W, Camille.E, Camille.W]
  patch: "26.20"

- Poke her down before any all-in and hold E2 and R2 until she lands from her R. Committing early lets her dodge the whole rotation with untargetability.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: All-in timing

## Overview

## Early lane

- Make every Q count in the first levels. She looks to trade near walls right after your Q is spent, so a wasted Q is her invitation to engage.
  sources: [anguish, raideru]
  heading: Q discipline

- Keep early trades to the Q-passive-Q pattern and apply the shield read from the first exchange. Autos punish a magic-resist shield and spells punish an armor shield, which keeps even short trades profitable.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Levels 1–3
  abilities: [Akali.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Treat Hookshot as her only way in: hold E for it every fight and reset with shroud when late. She refuses long fights inside shroud, so her disengage is your cue to re-engage on cooldown advantage.
  sources: [anguish, raideru]
  heading: Mid-game pattern
  abilities: [Akali.E, Akali.W, Camille.E]

- Track wall angles before skirmishes. She trades best near walls where Hookshot has a line, so pull fights into the open where her E finds nothing.
  sources: [raideru]
  abilities: [Camille.E]
  heading: Positioning

## Win condition

- All-in only from a health lead with E2 and R2 held. Chunk her first, follow her R landing, and close before Grasp plus cookies scaling makes her side of the short trades too strong to contest.
  sources: [anguish, raideru]
  heading: Closing rule
  abilities: [Akali.E, Akali.R]
