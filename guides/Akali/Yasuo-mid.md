## Key tips

- Skill lane, slightly Akali favored: respect it anyway.
  sources: [anguish]

- Q when he Qs minions, and dodge his Q: his farm animation is your poke.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Yasuo.Q]

- Wait out Wind Wall: without it he is usually a kill.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He is stronger levels 1-5: survive to your all-in levels.
  sources: [v222yasuo]

- Do not walk up without shroud: his Q punishes the exposed.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

## Mid lane

- E as he Es: dash for dash keeps you glued.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E, Yasuo.E]

- Post-6, force away from his tower: your all-in wins in the open.
  sources: [v222yasuo]

## Win condition

- Shroud discipline plus Wind Wall patience: the Yasuo who never touches you never wins.
  sources: [anguish]
