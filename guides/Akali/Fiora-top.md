## Key tips

- Trade only in short Q-passive-Q bursts and never eat multiple vitals in one go. Every extra vital she procs swings the trade, so land the passive auto and leave instead of chasing a second one.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Short bursts
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Akali.P, Fiora.P]
  patch: "26.20"

- Never send E into Riposte. If she holds W, the mark gets parried and the whole combo fizzles, so bait the parry with Q harass first and only commit E once W is gone.
  sources: [xsagi, justsad42-mf]
  heading: Respect Riposte
  abilities: [Akali.E, Fiora.W]

- Do not walk up without shroud available. Her R movement speed lets her run Akali down in the open, so shroud is the only safe way to take an extended trade.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Shroud discipline
  abilities: [Akali.W, Fiora.R]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade cautiously in the first levels and refuse her run-down. She wants to stick on Akali and chase, so take the quick Q trade and disengage before she turns it into an extended all-in.
  sources: [xsagi, justsad42-mf]
  heading: Early caution
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Fiora.Q]

- Punish her before her mobility comes online, but do not overcommit after level 6. Her pre-6 answers are limited, while her R all-in out-damages Akali's if both sides commit fully.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Level 6 flip
  abilities: [Fiora.R]
  patch: "25.21"

## Mid lane

- Leave shroud on a new angle, not where she expects. She answers shroud with Q or Tiamat procs onto the nearest vital, so reposition inside the cover and force her to guess the exit direction.
  sources: [justsad42-mf, anguish]
  heading: Shroud angles
  abilities: [Akali.W, Fiora.Q]
  patch: "25.21"

- Track her R path when choosing an escape line. Running straight lets her line up vitals one after another, so cut across her and break the chain instead of giving her a runway.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Break the chase
  abilities: [Akali.E, Fiora.R]
  patch: "25.21"

## Win condition

- Hug a wall while her R is active to deny the fourth vital. One blocked proc is often the difference between surviving her ultimate at low health and dying to the final burst.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Wall the finale
  abilities: [Akali.W, Fiora.R, Fiora.P]
  patch: "26.20"

- Close the game before her two-to-three-item spike. Her plan is to survive to that point and then take every duel, so Akali's window is picks and short resets before Fiora reaches it.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Beat the spike
  patch: "25.21"
