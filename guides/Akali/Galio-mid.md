## Key tips

- Anti-AP champion: even a won lane turns into full MR and lots of CC in fights.
  sources: [anguish]

- His E cancels your E2, R1 and R2: never commit into a held E.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Galio.E, Akali.E, Akali.R]

- Trade when he spends everything on the wave: empty Galio is safe Galio.
  sources: [anguish]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Avoid standing in minions when his passive auto is up: the slam is free damage.
  sources: [anguish]

- Poke around the passive: autos only when the glow is gone.
  sources: [anguish]

## Mid lane

- Keep forcing spent-wave trades: his waveclear is your window.
  sources: [anguish]

- Track his E before every R: canceled ults lose games.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.R, Galio.E]

## Win condition

- Won lane plus denied fights: MR cannot stop what never engages badly.
  sources: [anguish]
