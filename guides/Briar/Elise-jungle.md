## Key tips

- Elise is one of Briar's hardest matchups because both of her E answers stop a run-in: Cocoon stuns it and Rappel untargetably dodges it. Never force the duel; answer her instead.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Join fights after they start, not first. Her control lands easiest on the opening diver, so be the second entry and close with W-Q ({cd:233:W}) once her attention is split.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.Q, Elise.E]

- Fire W1 only from range where Q lands instantly after. A long W1 run with a late Q is the exact sequence she punishes with Cocoon or Rappel.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.Q, Elise.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- When Elise shows on a gank, path to her and countergank rather than forcing your own play elsewhere. She wins the straight duel, so meet her at her fight instead of taking a fair one.
  sources: [loganjg]
  heading: Answer, don't force

- Let skirmishes start before you enter. Cocoon ({cd:60:E}) is hardest to land on a mover who arrives late, and a spent Cocoon is your cue to W-Q in.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Elise.E, Briar.W, Briar.Q]

- Approach from fog. Closing across open ground lets her pre-aim Cocoon or hold Rappel for your Q; brush and flank angles cut that reaction window down.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

## Mid lane

- Set up mid-game approaches from brush. Starting W1 out of vision shortens the distance she has to react to your entry.
  sources: [loganjg]
  heading: Brush approaches
  abilities: [Briar.W]

- Make W1 and Q one motion. Q the instant you are close; the gap between a far W1 and a late Q is where her E answers live.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.Q]

- Watch her E before committing. If Cocoon just caught a teammate, her best peel is down ({cd:60:E}) and your W-Q window is open.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Elise.E, Briar.W, Briar.Q]

## Win condition

- Win as the second engage, never the first. Let someone else draw Cocoon and Rappel, then enter late and stay on the backline through the cleanup.
  sources: [loganjg]
  heading: Second entry
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Keep playing the countergank game around objectives. Elise wants picks with her control, so cover the lane she leans toward and turn her attempt into your fight.
  sources: [loganjg]

## Itemization

- Go bruiser instead of full damage. Sterak's Gage plus Spirit Visage lets you eat the Cocoon focus, live through the Rappel stall, and still land the Q follow-up.
  sources: [loganjg]
  items: [3181, 3065]
