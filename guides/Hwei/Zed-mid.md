## Key tips

- Your E, purple fear, is the key spell: E down means his trade, so hold it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Hwei.E]

- Your passive proc punishes closeness: zone him off it.
  sources: [v222]

- He engages when you use Q: spent cooldowns are his window, so stagger them.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- His WEQ from range is free damage: keep moving, never channel.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- He holds max range early: distance keeps your passive offline, so push him in.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His Edge of Night rush calms the lane: end it before it lands.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Fear up plus Q up is the denial: layered cooldowns turn every trade into your poke.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
