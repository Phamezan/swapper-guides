## Key tips

- Hard matchup: your spells are easy to dodge and she heavily outranges you.
  sources: [shok]

- She holds max range: outspacing is her whole plan, so break it once.
  sources: [shok]

- Your level 6 burst: an R hit can kill, so set it up.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She dodges and pokes from range: simple spells, simple sidesteps, so vary yours.
  sources: [shok]

- She buys Mercs: tenacity covers one fear, so land the second.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Post-6 she never stands still: R range is death range, so cut it off.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

- She keeps the range: you cannot answer what you cannot reach, so close once.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Range closed plus burst landed: the Syndra who never respects R never wins.
  sources: [shok]
