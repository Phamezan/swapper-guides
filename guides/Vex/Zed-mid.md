## Key tips

- He never engages into fear: your mark wins every contested trade, so show it.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He punishes spent fear after 6: the window opens when the mark fades, so hide it.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Fear down plus E wasted is his all-in: never show both down.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He out-pokes early: shadow Qs cost HP for free, so dodge laterally.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- His W swap dodges empowered fear: hold fear until he lands.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Post-6 he tracks fear like a summoner: its absence is his engage timer, so fake it.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Fear held into burst: patience with marks beats his shadow windows.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
