## Key tips

- Her dashes refill your passive: every E feeds your fear.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- She dodges spells, especially fear on red: marked means death, so mark her.
  sources: [anguish]

- When stacked, your W cancels dashes: hold it for her E.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Vex.W, Akali.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She pokes without dashing: Qs do not feed the mark, so answer the Qs.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- She plays feet-first early: walk her down before blades.
  sources: [anguish]

## Mid lane

- Never waste passive on waves: empty marks invite engages.
  sources: [anguish]

- She tracks the red glow: passive-up means she backs off, so cast to zone.
  sources: [anguish]

## Win condition

- Passive fed plus dashes punished: the Akali who always fears you never wins.
  sources: [anguish]
