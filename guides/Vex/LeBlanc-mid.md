## Key tips

- You are built to counter her: respect the player, not just the pick.
  sources: [pumptm]

- She never Q+Ws into a full passive bar: your empowered spell cancels her dash, so hold it.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.Q, LeBlanc.W]

- She spaces with chain: ranged harass is her only safe damage, so dodge laterally.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She takes TP and desyncs backs: stay healthier than her, match the timer.
  sources: [pumptm]

- She baits your spells before trading: no spells means no counter, so hold one.
  sources: [pumptm]

## Mid lane

- She tracks your passive bar: full means she backs off, so fake with it.
  sources: [pumptm]

- She punishes spent passives: the window is short, so hide it.
  sources: [pumptm]

## Win condition

- Passive held plus backs synced: the LeBlanc who never dodges never wins.
  sources: [pumptm]
