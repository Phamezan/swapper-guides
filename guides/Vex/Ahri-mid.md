- She dodges E with W speed (about {cd:711:E}): cast where she lands.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Vex.E]

- She never dashes in while fear is up: every ult dash procs passive into fear, so hold it.
  sources: [shok-ahri, irdefender]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She plays around your E and W answers it: change the angle.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Vex.E, Ahri.W]

- Your Q poke outranges hers: press range, deny answers.
  sources: [irdefender, peng04]
  abilities: [Vex.Q]

## Mid lane

- From 6 she secures vision and plays fog: sweep and hold fear.
  sources: [peng04]

- You hold fear for her commits: never show it early.
  sources: [irdefender]
  abilities: [Vex.W]

## Win condition

- Commits answered with fear plus shield: the Ahri who always dashes blind never wins.
  sources: [irdefender]
