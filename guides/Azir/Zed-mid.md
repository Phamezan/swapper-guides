## Key tips

- He finds you annoying: a good Azir keeps him out, so hold the line.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He Ws before R to dodge yours after ulting: hold R until the shadow lands.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Azir.R]

- He looks for kills after Voltaic: end it before the spike.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He gives you prio and looks for Ignite all-ins: respect the sums.
  sources: [v222]

- He WEQs when you Q, positioned to cut off your E: Q from max range.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Azir.Q]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He refuses to R near your tower: your R shoves him in, so hold it for dives.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Azir.R]

## Win condition

- R held plus soldiers placed: the Zed whose shadow never dodges never kills.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
