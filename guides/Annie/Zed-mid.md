## Key tips

- Your range annoys him: he survives early, so punish before Voltaic.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- His Voltaic spike flips it: end it before the item.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He buffers your stun on pop-out: hold Q until he lands.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Annie.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- His Mercs rush: tenacity covers one stun, so chain.
  sources: [v222]

- He pokes and backs off: hold stun for his commit.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He watches your flash: burn his attention, engage when sums are down.
  sources: [v222]

## Win condition

- Flash burned plus stun held: the Zed whose R never lands never kills.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
