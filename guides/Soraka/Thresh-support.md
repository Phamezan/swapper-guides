## Key tips

- Open level 1 behind your minions and trade autos plus Q whenever their carry steps for farm: entering level 2 ahead on health takes away the all-in they queued for.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Soraka.Q]

- Dodge hooks by walking straight back out of range instead of sidestepping: lateral movement keeps you inside the hook's path longer, while a straight retreat exits it.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

- Hold silence for the diver onto your carry, then answer with Q and heals: spending E early leaves nothing for the engage that actually kills.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Soraka.E, Soraka.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Click his portrait at level 1 to see whether he started Q or E from the buff icon: when his E is missed or absent, run the lane down while he has no trade-back.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q, Thresh.E]

- Do not blow summoners over a level-1 hook: without ranks and backup damage it cannot kill you, so save your spells for the level-2 window that matters.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

## Mid lane

- Base when he bases so you share his roam timer: matching his return keeps him from finding a free roam while you are stuck holding a wave.
  sources: [henry-t]

- Track him when he goes missing and shade toward his roam with ult ready: arriving second with Wish turns his pick attempt into a lost play.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Soraka.R]

## Win condition

- Rush Knight's Vow out of early health first when they run all-in burst: the health plus damage redirection keeps your carry alive through the hook-plus-jump window.
  sources: [henry-t]
  items: [3109]
  abilities: [Soraka.W]

- Win by staying alive in the backline with your team topped up: as long as you are up with heals and ult ready, their pick comp cannot finish a fight, so never facecheck without minions or backup.
  sources: [henry-t]
  abilities: [Soraka.R]
