## Key tips

- Stand behind your minions at level 1 and poke him and his carry down: entering level 2 behind on health removes the all-in he queued for.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Soraka.Q]

- Never stand in his hook range while his wave crashes into your tower: getting hooked under your own tower is the one death pattern you cannot heal through.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Nautilus.Q]

- Track his ult ({cd:111:R}) and give ground while it is up: his engage without it is only a hook, so bait it out, then zone him for its full duration.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Nautilus.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take the side bush first for early poke, then play behind your wave: if he does land a hook, steady healing out-sustains it as long as the wave is not crashing into you.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Soraka.W]

- Start extra health into his engage and build toward Knight's Vow first: the early health plus redirection blunts his all-in better than rushing your enchanter core.
  sources: [henry]
  items: [3109]

## Mid lane

- Save silence for the follow-up diver instead of spending it on him: his hook is the entry but the carry dash is the kill, so hold E for that.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Soraka.E]

- Ping his roams the moment he leaves and hover within reach of mid: he cannot do much alone, so answering his move denies the pick.
  sources: [henry]

## Win condition

- Set up objectives behind walls and let him come to you: with terrain breaking his hook lanes, he has to walk into your silence and focus to start a fight.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Nautilus.Q, Soraka.E]

- Once Knight's Vow is done and Moonstone is coming online, bait his engage onto yourself and heal through it: with his cooldowns spent and your redirection plus healing up, your team wins the extended fight.
  items: [3109]
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Soraka.W]
