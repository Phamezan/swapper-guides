## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not eat free poke: her Qs set up Charm-cancels.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- Landings hurt badly: one E2 is the whole trade.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

## Mid lane

- After 6, steal her R to chase her down the lane.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Sylas.R, Ahri.R]

- She calls ganks: track her jungler when you commit.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Win condition

- Chain landed plus stolen-R chase: the Ahri who never dodges never wins.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]
