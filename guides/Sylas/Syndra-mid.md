## Key tips

- Her E2 decides every trade: hit means you lose, miss means you punish hard.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

- Take Bone Plating: it covers the trade you fail to dodge.
  sources: [trisend3]

- Stand near creeps: her E turns awkward, but not where her Q hits you and the wave.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Deny her the snowball: your E2 is the only trade she fears.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- Farm behind offset creeps: awkward angles cut her accuracy.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Do not let her keep the wave on her side for free: crash to deny the setup.
  sources: [shok]

- Engage only when her E is spent: no scatter means no answer.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- E2 dodged plus Plating held: the Syndra who never lands E never wins.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
