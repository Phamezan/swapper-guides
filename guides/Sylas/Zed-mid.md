## Key tips

- Zed-favoured: he outranges and outscales your windows.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He refuses trades before Voltaic: your window is the pre-spike lane.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He plays to deny your R steal with R-first: hold your steal for his commit.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Sylas.R, Zed.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Force before Voltaic: poke and E2 while his shadows are weak.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- He positions inside the wave or far back: E the wave-stander, Q the farmer.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- After Voltaic his poke finds you: track shadows before engaging.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Track his W cooldown: shadow-down is your engage.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: pre-aim E at the exit when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Sylas.E]

## Win condition

- Pre-spike banked plus steal held: the Zed who never snowballed never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
