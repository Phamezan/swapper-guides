## Early lane

- She E-dodges your E: hold the chain until her dash is spent.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E, Sylas.E]

## Mid lane

- Her E2 interrupts your dashes: E once, then walk.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- Hold the stolen R for her engage: the later R wins.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

## Win condition

- E varied plus stolen-R held: the Akali who never dodges everything never wins.
  sources: [anguish]
