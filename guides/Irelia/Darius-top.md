## Key tips

- Punish him after he misses Decimate. The whiffed Q is your entry: step in while it is down instead of waiting for the next cast.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Punish window
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Take favorable fights at levels 4–5. That is your early peak against him, before his later scaling answers take over the lane.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Timing window

- Never chase him through minions; sidestep Apprehend. He chains Q off your dashes, so vary your dash targets and break the stun line instead of running it straight.
  sources: [houcs, federals1-darius]
  heading: Stun discipline
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Darius.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not concede the level-1 XP zone. He wants to deny you before your passive stacks, so hold minion contact and stack on the wave before trading.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level 1
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Approach from angles his E cannot cover. Chain Q through different minions on each trade so his pull guesses fail and his Q edge never catches the full dash.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Dash angles
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Darius.E, Darius.Q]

## Mid lane

- After level 6, lead with the R slow into E. He fears walking into your ultimate because the slow sets up the stun, so cast R first and land E on the slowed target.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Post-6 combo
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.E]

- Rush Blade of the Ruined King to punish every missed dodge. Each Q he fails to land gets more costly once the item is done, so force all-ins instead of farming it out.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: First item

## Win condition

- Beat him to the item turn. His Bramble Vest plus Steelcaps window answers your rush, so complete Blade of the Ruined King first and run him down before his armor stack lands.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Item race

- End fights before five stacks once Spear of Shojin is done. After that spike he looks for five stacks into the ultimate, so take short all-ins and never offer the extended brawl.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Post-Shojin rule
  abilities: [Darius.R]

## Itemization

- Build Blade of the Ruined King first for the all-in turn, since the matchup swings on that spike more than on any rune choice.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
