## Key tips

- You are rarely killable: he plays for timers, not the solo kill.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He stays off the wave and away from casters: your Q resets on minions to reach him, so push.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- He trades only with Bone Plating up or Ignite ready: without one you out-trade, so force.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He pokes, never commits: short shadow Qs are his whole plan, so heal through.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- He concedes CS to keep HP: his health is for jungle plays, so zone him off gold.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His R dodges your E or removes your Q mark: hold E until after shadows.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Irelia.E, Irelia.Q]

## Win condition

- Duel decided, wave watched: roams and jungle setups never save him.
  sources: [aikopanda]
