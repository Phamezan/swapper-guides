## Key tips

- Set up minions to dash through before trading. Prepared Q targets give you entry and exit; without them, never start the exchange.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Dash setup
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Bait Counter Strike, then commit. Feint a dash to draw E, and take the real trade only once the shield is down.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Bait first
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- All-in after he misuses E. The wasted shield is your window, so convert it immediately instead of resetting to neutral.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Punish window
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Stagger the stun because he holds E for it. Never throw E on pattern; vary the moment so his shield guesses fail.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Stun timing
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Jax.E]

- Keep the passive stacked to zone him. He tracks your stacks to decide trades, so a stacked wave holds him off without fighting.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Stack pressure

## Mid lane

- Keep an exit minion behind you. Every committed dash needs a way back, so fight on your prepared side of the wave.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Exit discipline
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Vary dash targets because he counts resets. Dashing the same minion twice lets him time the stun; spread the chain across the wave.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dash variety
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Jax.E]

## Win condition

- Fight fully stacked and finish before E returns. The stacked all-in outdamages him, but only inside the shield downtime.
  sources: [houcs, ph45]
  heading: Stacked finish
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Never dash into a held Counter Strike when stacked. Losing full stacks to the stun reverses the lane, so wait out the shield first.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Shield respect
  abilities: [Jax.E, Irelia.Q]
