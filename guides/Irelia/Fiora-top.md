## Key tips

- Take Press the Attack with Ignite. The setup turns your stacked all-ins lethal, which is the only exchange pattern that beats her dueling.
  sources: [houcs]
  heading: Setup

- Never give her an obvious E. Riposte answers predictable stuns, so stagger the cast and force her to guess.
  sources: [houcs, justsad42-mf]
  heading: Stun discipline
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Fiora.W]

- Expect level-1 minion denial and the alcove ambush. She zones you off the wave and waits around corners, so path wide and concede what you must.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Level-1 respect

## Overview

## Early lane

- Stagger Q instead of dashing on pattern. She holds her Q until you spend yours, so threaten without committing and dash only on your terms.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Dash patience
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Fiora.Q]

- Time E for after her Q-step, not before. Dashing into her held Q hands her the trade; answer the step-in instead of opening into it.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Answer, don't open
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Fiora.Q]

## Mid lane

- Commit the ultimate only when Riposte is down. She saves W for R above all, so the full combo needs the parry spent first.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Ult timing
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Fiora.W]

- Dash to minions, not to her. She follows your dash to punish the landing, so chain through the wave and strike from its cover.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Wave cover
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Win condition

- Take stacked all-ins inside the W downtime. With Riposte spent, your PTA plus Ignite pattern outdamages her answerless dueling.
  sources: [houcs, justsad42-mf]
  heading: All-in window
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

- Hold R as the finisher once W is gone. The blades land clean without the parry threat, closing the stacked fight she cannot leave.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
  heading: Finisher
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Fiora.W]

## Itemization

- Take Ignite over Teleport, with early Executioner's Calling only when skipping Ignite. The lane is decided by all-in lethality, not sustain.
  sources: [justsad42-mf]
