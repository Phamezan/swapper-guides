## Key tips

- Free for him after 6: your window is pre-6, so press it.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- His Voltaic farms you: end it before the spike.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- You fly straight during W: his shadows land free, so W unpredictably.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He stays behind minions early: your Q needs the angle, so find it.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q]

- He Rs to follow your W dash: never W predictably.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He shadows your R: hold R until he lands.
  sources: [v222]

## Win condition

- Pre-6 survived plus W varied: the Zed whose Voltaic never flips it never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
