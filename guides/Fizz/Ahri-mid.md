## Key tips

- Fizz-favoured: she must answer your E, not the reverse.
  sources: [spaceturtlez]

- She baits E by fake-stepping in: do not E the feint.
  sources: [peng04]

- Her main cooldown to track is your E (about {cd:105:E}): spend it only to dodge or kill.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She bullies levels 1-2, then lets the wave back: concede the first waves.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Dodge her fox-fire W with E and trade her mana into your Bone Plating.
  sources: [spaceturtlez]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Fizz.E]

## Mid lane

- If you commit an obvious Q she Charms mid-dash: Q only when her Charm is down.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q, Ahri.E]

- She pushes by default: match the shove and outscale.
  sources: [spaceturtlez]

## Win condition

- E held plus Charm dodged: the Ahri who never catches you never wins.
  sources: [peng04, spaceturtlez]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]
