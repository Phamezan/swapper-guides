## Key tips

- You are punishable at 1-2 and she is strong at 3 and 6: the lane flips on spikes.
  sources: [shok]

- Take Bone Plating: it blunts her combo.
  sources: [trisend3]

- Her E is trade tool and waveclear in one: bait it before diving.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Deny her crash: meet the wave before it breaks.
  sources: [shok]

- Respect 3 and 6: her spikes are your stop signs.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Dive with E when her E is down: troll pole beats scatter.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Fizz.E, Syndra.E]

- She takes Barrier, Exhaust or early Null-Magic: expect the defensive setup.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Spikes survived plus E dives: the Syndra who never zones you never wins.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
