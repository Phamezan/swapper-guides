## Key tips

- He takes Bone Plating and answers your Q-in with double-W backwards: respect the answer.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q]

- Trade only with your Plating up: no Plating means no trade.
  sources: [v222]

- Your E is the trade tool and the dive: spend it to kill, not to poke.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Poke, do not commit: his backwards combo punishes engages.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Q in only when his W is spent: shadow-down is your window.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- Dive with E when his answers are down: troll pole beats shadows.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Keep trading with Plating: the pattern never changes.
  sources: [v222]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: E away from the exit when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Fizz.E]

## Win condition

- Plating traded plus dives timed: the Zed who never catches you never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
