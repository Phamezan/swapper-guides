## Key tips

- She keeps E, especially after 6, to break trades and cancel your R: the stun is the lane.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Katarina.R]

- She refuses daggers: place them where she stands, not where she will be.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
  abilities: [Katarina.Q]

- Your level 2 and 6 are the windows: force them.
  sources: [shok]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Outside your windows you are weak: concede, then strike at 2 and 6.
  sources: [shok]

- Your roams are the pressure: shove and leave when she must answer.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Do not get locked in: her lead comes from pinning your roams.
  sources: [shok]

- Bait her E before R: no scatter means free Death Lotus.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Katarina.R]

## Win condition

- E baited plus roams taken: the Syndra who never pins you never wins.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
