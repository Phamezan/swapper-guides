## Key tips

- You out-damage him: take the straight trade.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He stays away from his wave: you E through it to reach him, so do.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Qiyana.E]

- He never Rs first: you R back and reverse it, so hold yours.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Qiyana.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He respects your level-2 burst: press at 2.
  sources: [v222]

- He pokes with range and gives no openings: his damage must be free, so close.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He needs W plus R: R alone gets countered by yours, so bait the shadow first.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.R, Qiyana.R]

## Win condition

- Sweeper plus Exhaust: play around his answers, never engage into them.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
