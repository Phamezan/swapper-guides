## Key tips

- She bullies you very hard at levels 1-2 with autos and Q: you have no answer yet, so concede.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- She holds Charm for your engage: bait it before all-inning pre-6.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- After 6 she keeps away from walls and holds Charm (about {cd:246:R}): fight away from walls.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Qiyana.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She abuses the first two levels, then sets the wave middle for your 3 spike: crash into it.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- She keeps the wave off your side: a long lane is your engage room, so stretch it.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- From 6 she roams with priority: match or punish the crash.
  sources: [peng04]

- She tracks your engage and answers with defensive Charm: engage only on downtime.
  sources: [shok-ahri, peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- She roams with prio: her map pressure beats your duel, so never let her leave free.
  sources: [peng04]
