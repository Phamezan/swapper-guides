- Your early auto and Q damage: the first levels belong to your ball.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- He baits Q by stepping to max range: never throw the first Q.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- He combos only when Q and E are down: always keep one banked.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q, Orianna.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- His boots first back: movement baits your Q, so vary placement.
  sources: [v222]

- He survives to Voltaic: punish before the spike lands.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His bait-then-combo is the whole pattern: a Q spent on a fake means death.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

## Win condition

- Win the bait war: the Zed who never sees Q never combos.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
