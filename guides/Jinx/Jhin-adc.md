## Key tips

- You outscale him, so going even through lane is winning. Refuse coinflip fights for lane pride; every even recall keeps you on your scaling curve while his early window closes.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Shove him under his tower. He cannot answer your push, and a Jhin stuck catching waves cannot hold the short-trade lane he wants.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Force long fights on your chompers. His whole plan is short trades precisely because standing to trade lets you root him under Flame Chompers ({cd:222:E}).
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jinx.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open by shoving the first waves. Whoever holds the push decides who gets to trade and who has to catch, so make him the one catching.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jinx.Q]

- Spoil his early-lead plan: give the first plate rather than dying for it. A scoreless lane that you shoved is his loss, not yours, because he needs the lead and you do not.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Stand off the dying-minion line while his fourth shot is loaded. His grenade bounce into the execute shot is his max-damage trade, so concede that cannon rather than eating the combo for it.
  sources: [omibro]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

## Mid lane

- Keep the waves shoved through the mid game. Every wave he answers under his tower is a wave he is not pressuring you with.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Seed Flame Chompers ({cd:222:E}) on the objective choke before your team starts it. He has to walk into the roots to contest, which turns his short-trade plan against him.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jinx.E]

## Win condition

- Drag the game past the laning phase once even. You outscale him, so every minute after his early window closes grows your edge in the fights that follow.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Fight the decisive fight long: kite back through pre-placed chompers instead of bursting. The longer he stands to trade, the more roots he eats.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jinx.E]
