## Key tips

- She wins the lane hard: play to survive, not to press.
  sources: [shok]

- She keeps you pushed in: a TF under tower cannot roam, so break out.
  sources: [shok]

- Pushed waves invite ganks: your jungler is your lane partner.
  sources: [shok]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She does not force: take the free farm and scale.
  sources: [shok]

- Her Q spam has a plan: sidestep the pattern, punish the dry spells.

## Mid lane

- Her page is built for push and map: match TP with your ult.
  sources: [shok]

- Every wave she crashes denies a roam: thin the wave before it lands.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Never held plus jungler dodged: the Syndra who never keeps you in never beats you.
  sources: [shok]
