## Key tips

- The lane is fine for him but you roam: his job is making every roam cost you.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He always shoves: the minions you miss and plates he takes are the matchup.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He Rs after your Gold Card: throw it only with backup.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Twisted Fate.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Your stacked-deck auto is your trade: force when the glow is up.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.E]

- He pokes to keep you in lane: chunked HP cannot roam, so stay healthy.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He tracks your R and pings every vanish: roam only on crashed waves.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

## Win condition

- Win the map, not the duel: a Zed whose shove never holds you is a Zed who loses.
  sources: [aikopanda]
