## Key tips

- Time Haymaker as his stun is about to land. Hold W through Counter Strike and fire at expiry, turning his engage into your true-damage answer.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Stun timing
  abilities: [Sett.W, Jax.E]

- Max W and take Conqueror or Grasp. The matchup turns on W hits rather than extended autos, so build the lane around the cooldown.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Setup
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Bully levels 1–3 while bulky. He concedes early brawls against your damage, so force health off him before his E timing sharpens.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Early bully

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade Q only after his E is spent. He blocks Q with Counter Strike, so bait the shield first and commit once it is down.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Q timing
  abilities: [Sett.Q, Jax.E]

- Take short brawls and reset. Never auto into an active E; hit and step out instead of offering the stunned exchange.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Mid lane

- Bait E with Q feints before the R combo. Once the shield is gone, the full commit has no answer left.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Combo setup
  abilities: [Sett.Q, Jax.E, Sett.R]

- Aim W at his dodge exit. He sidesteps the center as you cast, so delay until Counter Strike expires and fire where he lands.
  sources: [ph45, autolykus]
  heading: W placement
  abilities: [Sett.W, Jax.E]

## Win condition

- Set up the throw toward your tower. After level 6, fight near your side so R drags him under the tower instead of across open ground.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: R setup
  abilities: [Sett.R]

- No W, no all-in. The cast is the kill condition, so disengage while it is down and re-engage only with it ready.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: W discipline
  abilities: [Sett.W]

## Itemization

- Max W first and choose Conqueror or Grasp for the lane, since both key off the short-trade pattern into his shield timing.
  sources: [autolykus]
