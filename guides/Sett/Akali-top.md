## Key tips

- Open Grasp with W max and a defensive setup. Answer her Q poke with a short of your own, then bank the grit instead of chasing into her kite.
  sources: [autolykus, anguish]
  heading: Setup and stance
  abilities: [Sett.W]
  patch: "26.20"

- Hold Haymaker for spent mobility. She dodges W with E or R, so fire the true-damage center only after her E or R is gone, or into her shroud exit.
  sources: [anguish, autolykus]
  heading: Haymaker discipline
  abilities: [Sett.W, Akali.E, Akali.R]
  patch: "26.20"

- Hold Facebreaker for her commit. Never throw E at max range into shroud; E her as she comes out of it, when she can no longer fade the pull.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Beating shroud
  abilities: [Sett.E, Akali.W]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Levels 1 to 3, meet her Q poke with a Q chase while the move speed lasts. Her rule is never auto-auto-Q early because you win the extended trade, so enforce exactly that whenever grit is ready.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Levels 1–3
  abilities: [Sett.Q, Akali.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

- Stack Grasp on the wave without overextending. Each proc plus grit is the resource her short trades try to deny, so keep building it while refusing her kite.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Grasp banking
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Never burn both W and E on the wave. If either hits minions, she all-ins into the missing answer, so keep one cooldown held every single wave.
  sources: [anguish]
  heading: Cooldown banking
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.E]
  patch: "26.20"

- When she shrouds defensively, hold center W for the fade. She must reappear, she cannot dodge forever, and the true-damage center punishes the exit.
  sources: [anguish, autolykus]
  heading: Shroud timing
  abilities: [Sett.W, Akali.W]
  patch: "26.20"

## Win condition

- With W max and Grasp stacked, force the extended fight she refuses: chase with Q, hold E for her E or R commitment, and finish with center W. One full-grit stat-check is the whole win condition.
  sources: [autolykus, anguish]
  heading: The turn
  abilities: [Sett.Q, Sett.W]
  patch: "26.20"
