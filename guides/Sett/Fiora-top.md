## Key tips

- Bait Riposte before using Facebreaker. If she parries the pull, your whole extended-trade plan falls apart, so show pressure until her W is gone.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Pull discipline
  abilities: [Sett.E, Fiora.W]
  patch: "26.20"

- Build early Bramble and armor boots and accept the volatile lane. Her vital procs out-trade you without armor, and the lane snowballs on the first kills.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Item setup
  patch: "26.20"

- Keep grit banked instead of spending Haymaker on the wave. She engages the moment your W is down, so a waved Haymaker is her all-in invitation.
  sources: [justsad42, autolykus]
  heading: Grit discipline
  abilities: [Sett.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Concede the bush level 1 and never face-check her opener. Her first auto Q from fog wins the all-in, so start on the wave and let her show first.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Level 1

- Level Q and run her down once her max-range poke is down. Her Q vital poke has to miss or be on cooldown before your Q move speed chase sticks.
  sources: [justsad42, autolykus]
  heading: Chasing her poke
  abilities: [Sett.Q, Fiora.Q]
  patch: "26.20"

## Mid lane

- Pull her with E only after her Riposte is spent, then commit the full combo. An E into a held W gets stunned and turns your engage into her kill window.
  sources: [autolykus, justsad42]
  heading: Pull timing
  abilities: [Sett.E, Fiora.W]
  patch: "26.20"

- Aim Haymaker's true-damage center at the vital she is finishing. She commits position to complete each proc, and the center hitting her committed spot cannot be sidestepped.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Haymaker aim
  abilities: [Sett.W, Fiora.R]

## Win condition

- Carry her out of her ult healing with your ult before the last proc. If she starts R in the open, grabbing her mid-proc wastes the healing she positioned for.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Ult answer
  abilities: [Sett.R, Fiora.R]

- Force fights inside the wave where your grit stacks fastest. Away from minions she controls spacing with free Q pokes, but in the wave your W comes online first.
  sources: [justsad42, autolykus]
  heading: Wave fights
  abilities: [Sett.W, Fiora.Q]
  patch: "26.20"
