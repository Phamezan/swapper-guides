## Key tips

- Even matchup: respect his triple-Q burst, nothing more.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

- When he jumps on you, double-W Q-E backwards: retreat while landing everything.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Locke.W, Locke.Q]

- Ignite before his W recast: less healing means the trade sticks.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Locke.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open with Q stacks: the burst threat starts the lane.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

- Do not E into his shadows blindly: spent mobility is death.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- His W is the engage tool: backwards combo every time he commits it.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Ignite timing decides trades: cast before the recast, never after.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: pre-aim E behind yourself when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Locke.E]

## Win condition

- Burst respected plus backwards combos: the Zed who never dives you never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
