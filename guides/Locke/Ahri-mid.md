## Overview

## Early lane

- Level 1, do not trade: her W speed procs Electrocute on you.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- She spaces your nails and backs off as they stack: walk her down before the third lands.
  sources: [penglocke]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

## Mid lane

- She Ignites your Soul Ignition healing: expect it, bait it or power through.
  sources: [penglocke]
  abilities: [Locke.W]

- After 6 play with your jungler: you own the early, convert with help.
  sources: [sephyrixx]

## Win condition

- R-for-R is fine: yours returns sooner ({cd:805:R}), so trade ults and re-engage in the window.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Locke.R, Ahri.R]
