## Key tips

- Take dash angles that do not cross her: she aims Charm where you will be, so vary every E path.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E, Ahri.E]

- Wind Wall her Charm, then go in while it is down ({cd:157:W}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W, Ahri.E]

- Do not give her gank setup: track her jungler instead of overpushing blindly.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- E through minions to dodge: never dash predictably while in Charm range.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E, Ahri.E]

- After dashing through a minion that path is closed for a while, so plan the next dash.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E]

## Mid lane

- Bait Charm with E movement, then commit once it is down.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E, Ahri.E]

- Play around Wind Wall uptime: engage with it up, disengage without it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

## Win condition

- Finish by chaining dashes: she gets one Charm, so make her spend it on the wrong dash.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E, Ahri.E]
