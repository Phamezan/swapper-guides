## Key tips

- Dodge her Q with E and trade: every missed spell is your window.
  sources: [v222yasuo, shok]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E, Syndra.Q]

- Save Wind Wall for her E stun; never waste it on poke.
  sources: [v222yasuo]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W, Syndra.E]

- Kill her early and in the sidelane; do not let her scale into teamfights.
  sources: [v222yasuo]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Start E and run her down when she wastes mana.
  sources: [v222yasuo]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E]

- Force spells out of her: only her sure-thing spells threaten you.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- After her mana and boots come online, play for jungle and map instead of forcing.
  sources: [v222yasuo]

- Track her jungler: her lane gets much harder with gank setup behind it.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Sidelane kills are your win condition; group only when ahead.
  sources: [v222yasuo]
