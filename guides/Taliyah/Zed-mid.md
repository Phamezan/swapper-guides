## Key tips

- Easy for him: you have no dashes besides TP, so position safe.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He dodges W and blanks E: cast W where he lands, not where he stands.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Taliyah.W]

- He Ws before R to dodge your W: hold W until the shadow lands.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Taliyah.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- His shadow poke is undodgeable: heal through, never stand in doubles.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- If you overstep, he Ignites and Ghosts and runs you down: never overstep.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Your W is your only peel: hold it for his all-in, not poke.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Taliyah.W]

## Win condition

- W landed plus E followed: the Zed who never respects W never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
