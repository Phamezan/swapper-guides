## Key tips

- He dodges Q: it is your main poke and starts everything, so vary.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

- Your egg punishes half-commits: position so he must fully commit.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]

- He respects your wall: it decides where fights happen, so place it.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Anivia.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He sidesteps Q on cooldown: every miss is lane control you lose, so hold.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

- With his W down, your W-Q is hard to avoid: track his shadow cooldown.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Anivia.W]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- After 6, wall him into R when he commits blindly: time his W.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Anivia.R]

## Win condition

- Q landed plus wall placed: the Zed who never dodges never kills.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
