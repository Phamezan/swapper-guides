## Overview

## Early lane

- She trades every walkup: your cooldowns do not cover the wave, so give it.
  sources: [shok]

- She builds the pre-6 lead: it keeps growing after 6, so survive to it.

## Mid lane

- Harder for her to punish after 6: your waveclear lands, so stall.
  sources: [shok]

- She keeps dodging Q: the rule never expires, so vary.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

## Win condition

- Farmed plus scaled: the Syndra whose pre-6 bank never grew never wins.
