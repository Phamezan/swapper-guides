## Key tips

- His hook travels faster than yours, so never hook first into him: he can answer yours on reaction and turn it around.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Nautilus.Q, Thresh.Q]

- He has real kill pressure on your carry, so keep your roams short and return before he punishes your absence.
  sources: [razing42]

- Poke him out at level 1 from behind your wave, so he cannot take his level-2 all-in at full strength.
  sources: [henry]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Contest level 1 with autos and spells through the wave: every bit of health he loses weakens his level-2 spike.
  sources: [henry]

- Never get hooked while the wave crashes into you: hooked under your own tower is the one place his engage kills.
  sources: [henry]

## Mid lane

- Track his ult ({cd:111:R}) and give ground while it is up; take the fight only after he has spent it.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Nautilus.R]

- Rotate behind terrain instead of through open river: walls break his hook angles, and he hooks whatever walks into his lane.
  sources: [henry]

## Win condition

- Force out his ult without committing, then turn and zone him while it is down for its full cooldown.
  sources: [henry]
  abilities: [Nautilus.R]

- When he hooks your carry, answer with the lantern shield instead of engaging back: surviving his window wins you the fight.
  sources: [razing42, henry]
  abilities: [Thresh.W, Nautilus.Q]
