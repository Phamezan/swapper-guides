## Key tips

- She has no early heal, so hook her and lantern out: start the fight with Q and leave with W before she can turn it.
  sources: [rhino-nami]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q, Thresh.W]

- Punish a missed bubble, but do not overcommit: her slows let her carry chase you down after a long chase.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Nami.Q]

- Check summoners at load-in: if she skipped Heal, every landed hook is a kill angle because she has no save for herself.
  sources: [rhino-nami]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Force plays early while she has no heal and no Mikael's: this window before her first sustain item is the strongest part of your lane.
  sources: [razing42, rhino-nami]

- Throw the hook the moment her bubble misses ({cd:267:Q}): patient hooks land, while forced attempts feed straight into her disengage.
  sources: [razing42, rhino-nami]
  abilities: [Nami.Q, Thresh.Q]

## Mid lane

- Set dragon vision on your item timing, and only place wards where the wave lets you defend them.
  sources: [rhino-nami]

- Once she completes Mikael's, full hook combos stop working on her carry: switch to picks elsewhere instead of repeating the same play.
  sources: [rhino-nami]

## Win condition

- Kill Nami first in fights: without her, the enemy duo loses both its sustain and its disengage.
  sources: [rhino-nami]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q, Thresh.R]

- End the lane phase ahead: once her duo finishes its first items and levels, her peel outscales your pick potential.
  sources: [rhino-nami]
