## Key tips

- Punish him when he spends his shield on trades: without it he cannot stand in front of his carry.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Braum.E]

- A first-hook kill attempt is a valid early call: go when the wave and your carry let you collapse on him.
  sources: [rhino-braum]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

- Read the duo at load-in, not just him: hard-engage carries with Braum out-threaten poke duos, and that decides whether the lane stays quiet or turns bloody.
  sources: [razing42, rhino-braum]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Look for the early hook kill before his defensive line settles: do not wait for a perfect later angle that never comes.
  sources: [rhino-braum]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

- Trade into him while his shield is down ({cd:201:E}): that is when your autos and spells actually stick.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Braum.E, Thresh.Q]

## Mid lane

- When your carries are ahead, play for lantern over forcing hooks: repositioning them wins more than fishing for catches.
  sources: [rhino-braum]
  abilities: [Thresh.W]

- Ward only where the wave lets you defend the ward: a dead ward is worse than a late one.
  sources: [rhino-braum]

## Win condition

- Stop frontlining for hooks once fights start: peel with lantern and Flay so your carry plays the fight instead of watching you fish.
  sources: [rhino-braum]
  abilities: [Thresh.W, Thresh.E]

- If their carry outscales yours, force the bloody version of the lane early: quiet even lanes favor Braum's wall.
  sources: [razing42]
