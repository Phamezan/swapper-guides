## Key tips

- Farm safely until Teemo has boots. His Q is nearly impossible to dodge on foot, so concede pressure early rather than bleeding health before Teemo can move.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: Boots first

- After dodging his Q, land a few autos or a Q and then back off. The cooldown window is short, and staying longer turns a won exchange into his full combo.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: Window trading
  abilities: [Teemo.Q, Aatrox.Q]

- Track his W at all times. It is his main way to force the combo when Teemo holds an escape, so keep the wave near the middle and always leave room to run from the pull.
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: Respect the pull
  abilities: [Aatrox.W, Teemo.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Prioritize dodging Q sweet spots over dealing damage. Leave the center of W the moment it lands, since the knock-up into Q chain is the entire lane threat.
  sources: [temoonlyifok]
  heading: Dodge first
  abilities: [Teemo.Q, Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.W]

- Never start a short trade close to his turret while abilities are down. He chases Teemo down in that space with cooldowns to spare, so only trade with an exit already planned.
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: No turret trades
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Teemo.W]

## Mid lane

- Do not all-in him after level 6 unless Ignite is ready. Without it the extended fight favors him through R healing, so treat Ignite as the permission slip for every mid-game commit.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: Ignite rule
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

- Farm and scale instead of forcing kills. Teemo cannot match his R combo in a fair duel, so hold CS parity and let boots plus levels do the work.
  sources: [temoonlyifok, manco1]
  heading: Scale it out

## Win condition

- Win by denying the full combo, not by out-dueling him. Every fight Teemo takes should be a dodged Q into autos and out; the moment he lands a clean three-cast rotation, the lane is lost.
  sources: [temoonlyifok, persimannon]
  heading: Deny the rotation
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Teemo.Q]

- Keep the wave short after level 6. His kill pattern needs room to chase through W into Q, and a short lane plus shroomed exits starves exactly that.
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: Short lane
  abilities: [Aatrox.W, Teemo.R]

## Itemization

- Rush boots before anything else, with Swifties first. Movement speed is the whole dodge plan against Q, so complete them before trading with intent.
  sources: [manco1, temoonlyifok]

- Take Ignite over anything defensive. The post-6 all-in only exists with it, and without it Teemo has no way to answer his R healing in the one fight that matters.
  sources: [manco1]
