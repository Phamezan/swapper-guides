- Edge of Night second blocks his E, then kill.

- A wasted E is the window: go when it misses.

## Overview

## Early lane

- His long-range poke: stay healthy, never trade chunked.
  sources: [v222]

- If he turtles under tower, shove and roam.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Block then close: EoN eats E, W-W-R finishes.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.R]

## Win condition

- E down plus item online: the VelKoz who never misses E never wins.
  sources: [v222]
