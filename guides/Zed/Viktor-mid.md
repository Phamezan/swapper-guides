## Key tips

- Do not take First Strike: his E outranges you and wins the trade.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- Wait for Voltaic: the spike turns his range edge into your kill window.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Never fight inside his W: wait the field out, then re-engage when it fades.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Viktor.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- When his Q lands, do not stand for the empowered auto: walk off before it connects.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q]

- Farm to Voltaic: his E outranges you, so the lane starts when your spike lands.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- When he oversteps, Ignite and stay on him: the full commit is the kill.
  sources: [v222]

- All-in sequence: R first, W1 outside his field, auto plus E, W2 out plus Q.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Zed.W, Viktor.W]

## Win condition

- Post-Voltaic fights outside his W are yours: R-first combos with Ignite close every all-in.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
