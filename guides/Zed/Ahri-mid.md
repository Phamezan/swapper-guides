## Key tips

- Kill angles are rare against a good Ahri: she saves Charm and ult to stop yours.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.R]

- Never R while her Charm is up; if you must, buffer Q through it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Ahri.E]

- After she uses an ult dash, W-Q her.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Ahri.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Poke with shadows; outside her crowd control the lane is yours.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Threaten swaps so she must hold Charm: a Charm she cannot use freely is half its value.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- Swap shadows before ulting: never give her a static R target.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.R]

- In all-ins, buffer Q as you exit Death Mark.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.Q, Zed.R]

## Win condition

- Close when her answers are down: Charm or ult spent means a free R window.
  sources: [aikopanda]
