## Key tips

- The lane is fine but he roams: your job is making every roam cost him.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Always shove: the minions he misses and the plates you take are the matchup.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Hold R until he throws gold card: ulting into a held stun reverses the kill.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Twisted Fate.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Avoid his stacked-deck auto: trade only when the glow is gone.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.E]

- Poke to keep him in lane: chunked HP cannot roam.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Track his R timer and ping every vanish: your team must know before he appears.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

- Take plates on every roam: shove and hit tower while he shows elsewhere.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Win the map, not the duel: a Twisted Fate who never roams for free is a Twisted Fate who loses.
  sources: [aikopanda]
