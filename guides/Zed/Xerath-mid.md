## Key tips

- Any gap-close is a kill: with no disengage he cannot shake your R.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Stand away from the wave: force him to choose between poking you and pushing.
  sources: [v222]

- His E is his whole defense: wasted E is the all-in signal.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Xerath.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Skip First Strike: his range denies the proc.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Buy early boots: every sidestepped Q is free HP.

  abilities: [Xerath.Q]

## Mid lane

- After Voltaic you farm him: the spike ends his poke phase.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Bring your jungler: no disengage means every gank converts.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Edge of Night second, then hunt: spellshield plus your R closes every fight.
  sources: [aikopanda]
