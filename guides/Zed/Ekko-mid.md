## Key tips

- Read his rune in loading screen: Hail of Blades plus E is a dangerous early, Electrocute is a calm poke lane.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Never let him proc his passive for free: keep spacing after his E-Q connects so the third hit never lands.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Ekko.E]

- When he E-Q's in, answer with backwards WEQ: damage while retreating beats his commit.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Versus Hail of Blades, concede the early: take no extended trades in the first levels while his attack speed runs.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Versus Electrocute, poke calmly and stack small wins: the lane only kills once you have chunked him.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Keep poking until he is killable: Electrocute lanes are won by accumulated poke, not one engage.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Ignite early in all-ins: cutting his R heal turns his rewind into your kill.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Ekko.R]

## Win condition

- Win by poke into all-in, never by brawling: with Ignite cutting his heal and no free passive procs, your combo decides it.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
