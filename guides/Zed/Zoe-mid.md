## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect her passive autos early: do not walk up for free.
  sources: [v222]

- Take Cleanse or TP: the summoner is your safety net.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Time a triple Q on her R return spot: she always comes back to it.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q, Zoe.R]

- Aim shurikens at her return spot after R: the reappearance is the free hit.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zoe.R]

## Win condition

- Sleep answered plus return punished: with Cleanse up and Qs timed, her tricks run out.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
