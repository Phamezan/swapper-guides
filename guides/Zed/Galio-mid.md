## Key tips

- You outrange and outscale: play your range, never his melee.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- If he E's in, double-W Q-E backwards: retreating damage wins the trade.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Galio.E]

- Keep CS and XP even or better: parity now is sidelane kills later.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Short trades only: poke and back off, never extended fighting.
  sources: [v222]

- Backwards WEQ every time he E's in: same answer, no exceptions.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Galio.E]

## Mid lane

- When he ults away, shove under his tower immediately: his roam is your CS lead.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Galio.R]

- Shove on every roam: the wave you crash is damage he never recovers.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Sidelane him later: even-or-better CS plus your range ends it after laning.
  sources: [v222]
