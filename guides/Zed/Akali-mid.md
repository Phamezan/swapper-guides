## Key tips

- Give up early walk-ups: her trades win until Voltaic gives you poke back.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Wait for Voltaic, then poke and find kills.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Sweep before 6 so she cannot hide in shroud.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
