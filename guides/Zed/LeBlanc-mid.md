## Key tips

- Basically unplayable lane: play for survival and high-level punishment, not for winning trades.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- When she jumps on you, double-W Q-E backwards: retreating damage dodges her W burst.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, LeBlanc.W]

- Stay healthy before 6: the lane only becomes playable once your R answers hers.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade nothing early: your only job pre-6 is arriving at 6 with full HP.
  sources: [v222]

- Combo backwards when she Q-W's in: never stand still to return damage.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.Q, LeBlanc.W]

## Mid lane

- After 6, cast R just before she returns with W: she lands back into your damage.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, LeBlanc.W]

- When you R and she W's away, W toward her and Q back to her old W spot: most LeBlancs return.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.Q]

## Win condition

- Hexdrinker covers the burst gap: with it plus backwards combos, post-6 all-ins swing to you.
  items: [3155]
  sources: [v222]
