  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

- Respect her wall: it decides where fights happen.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Sidestep Q on cooldown: every miss is lane control you keep.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

- With your W down, her W-Q is hard to avoid: track your own shadow cooldown.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Anivia.W]

## Mid lane

- After 6, watch your W timing: she walls you into R when you commit blindly.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Anivia.R]

- Keep her low: the pressure of a cracked egg shapes the whole lane.
  sources: [v222]

## Win condition

- Wall respected plus egg finished: dodge Q, time W, and close only with lethal.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
