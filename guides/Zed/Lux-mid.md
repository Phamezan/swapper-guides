## Key tips

- Her Q enables everything: a missed Q removes most of her threat.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]

- Never R while her Q is up: ulting into a held binding reverses the kill.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Lux.Q]

- Engage when she spends Q: the cooldown is your entire window.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Lux.Q]

## Early lane

- Stand far back to dodge E: max range is where her poke stops landing.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

- Respect Q-E-R: a single binding converts into her full combo.

- Buy Edge of Night: blanking one Q buys the engage.
