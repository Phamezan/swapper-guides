## Key tips

- Stay away from your own minions: he dashes on them to reach you.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Poke after Voltaic; do not spam W early.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Respect Wind Wall, but sidelane fights are winnable with outplays.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Farm and scale; turn the moment he forces a trade onto you.
  sources: [v222]

- Poke with Q and hold W early: pre-Voltaic shadows commit you for little damage.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- After Voltaic, poke and look for kills.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Take sidelane fights with outplays, respecting Wind Wall.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

## Win condition

- Outscale to sidelane kills; never force into stacked waves.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
