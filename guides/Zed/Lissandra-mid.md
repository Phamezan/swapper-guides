## Key tips

- She is rarely killable: her R plus jungle and support camping beats all-ins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Lissandra.R]

- Do not line up with her and the wave; her Q extends through it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Lissandra.Q]

- Good TP matchup: match her map pressure instead of forcing kills.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Careful with W usage: she Es onto you when it is down.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Lissandra.E]

- Concede kills and farm for TP plays: this lane pays in map pressure.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Track jungle and support: the lane is a gank setup against you.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Shove and look elsewhere when she plays safe: your R finds other lanes.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Win the map: TP plays and roams decide it, not the 1v1.
  sources: [aikopanda]
