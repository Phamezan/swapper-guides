## Key tips

- Respect her early auto and Q damage: the first levels belong to her ball.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- Bait Q by stepping to max range and back, or faking last hits: every missed Q is your opening.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- Combo only when both Q and E are down: without them she cannot fight back.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q, Orianna.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Boots first back: movement is how you keep baiting Q.
  sources: [v222]

- Survive to Voltaic: with the spike you poke and kill regularly.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Bait then combo is the whole pattern: Q spent on a fake means shadows land free.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- Poke on every cycle after Voltaic: that is when your damage sticks.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Win the bait war: the Orianna who holds Q holds the lane, so never let her.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
