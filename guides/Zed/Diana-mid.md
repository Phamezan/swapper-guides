## Key tips

- Strong lane like Sylas: take no early trades, or she snowballs past you.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Poke only after Voltaic: her W shield wins early HP races.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Diana.W]

- When she jumps in, backwards WEQ instantly: damage while leaving denies her follow-up.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Diana.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Her Q curves from her right side: walk into it to make it miss.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Diana.Q]

- Never all-in while her W shield is up: wait for it to fade, then look.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Diana.W]

## Mid lane

- Hold R to dodge her R: her engage reverses when your ult blanks hers.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Diana.R]

- Keep poking after Voltaic: every chunked HP bar is an all-in she can no longer take.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Win the patience game: backwards combos plus the R-dodge turn her all-ins into your kills.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
