- Ward early: his team plays around his R, so track the jungler before he hits 6.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- Land Q early and often to keep his passive shield down: combos only land when the shield is gone.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- Your window is his E on the wave: combo him while he spends it pushing.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Malzahar.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take Scorch: it grinds him through the shield downtime between your Qs.
  sources: [v222]

- Play at range and outscale: nothing he does early forces you off the wave.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Buy QSS early: with it his R never holds you, and his one threat is blanked.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- Keep punishing his E-windows: every wave he pushes is damage you bank for free.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Malzahar.E]

## Win condition

- Outscale and answer the map: with QSS up your R decides fights while his R decides nothing.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
