## Key tips

- Skill matchup: a good Syndra out-spaces you and slightly outranges shadow poke.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Wasted E is the all-in window; W over her E can bait it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Zed.W]

- After 6, R through her R: R her as she Rs so she cannot dodge it.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Syndra.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect early Q poke: trade only after it is down.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q]

- Most Syndras are killable: test her spacing early with shadow poke.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- Bait E with W movement, then all-in.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Syndra.E]

- Combo after she spends E; her main answer is down.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- Kill her repeatedly: she cannot answer chained all-ins.
  sources: [aikopanda]
