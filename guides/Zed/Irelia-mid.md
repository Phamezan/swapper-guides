## Key tips

- Rarely killable: do not play for the solo kill, play for timers.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Stay off the wave and away from caster minions: her Q resets on minions to reach you.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Trade only with Bone Plating up or Ignite ready: without one she out-trades.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Poke, never commit: short shadow Qs are the whole early plan.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- Concede CS to keep HP: you need health for jungle plays more than gold.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- R dodges her E or removes her Q mark: hold ult for her setup, not yours.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Irelia.E, Irelia.Q]

- Play for jungle and roam timers: your kills come with help or elsewhere.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Win off her wave: roams and jungle setups decide what the duel cannot.
  sources: [aikopanda]
