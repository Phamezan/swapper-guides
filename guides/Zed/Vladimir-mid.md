## Key tips

- Respect his early and his scaling: stronger now, outscales later, so your window is the middle.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Never take a free empowered Q: every one you eat buys his scaling time.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q]

- All-in only after his pool: R after it, with Ignite already on.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Vladimir.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- From level 3, start poking: with W online your shadows answer his Qs.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Farm First Strike procs while farming: free gold for the mid-game all-in.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Bring your jungler: he has no disengage, so every gank is a kill setup.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- All-in the moment pool is spent: R after pool plus Ignite leaves him no escape.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Vladimir.W]

## Win condition

- Close before scaling: pool-down Ignite all-ins with jungle help end the lane.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
