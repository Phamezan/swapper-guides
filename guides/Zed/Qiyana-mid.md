## Key tips

- She out-damages you: never take the straight trade.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Stay away from your wave: she E's through it to reach you.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Qiyana.E]

- Never R first: she R's back and reverses it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Qiyana.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect her level-2 burst: give ground at 2.
  sources: [v222]

- Poke with range and give no all-in openings: your damage must be free.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- Double-W backwards combo when she engages: retreat while landing everything.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- You need W plus R: R alone gets countered by hers, so only commit with shadow ready.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.R, Qiyana.R]

## Win condition

- Sweeper plus Exhaust close it: stealth denied and burst answered turn her engages into your kills.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
