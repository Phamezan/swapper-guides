## Key tips

- Stand inside the wave or far back so his E2 cannot reach you.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- If he E-W's you, answer instantly with backwards W-Q-E.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Sylas.E]

- Do not trade much before Voltaic: save HP for the poke war after the spike.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Farm from max Q range early: walking up feeds his E.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q, Sylas.E]

- Be positioned to trade back if E2 lands.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- If he steals your R: R first and he usually mirrors immediately, then triple-Q him as he appears.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Sylas.R]

- If he Rs first, W backwards and re-engage when W is back.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Sylas.R]

## Win condition

- Save Qs for when he exits his stolen-R animation.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- Avoid HP races unless setting up the all-in.
  sources: [v222]
