## Key tips

- Never engage into fear: her mark wins every contested trade.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Punish spent fear after 6: the window opens when the mark fades.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Fear down plus E wasted is the all-in: both conditions, then commit.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Out-poke her early: shadow Qs cost her HP for free.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- Save W for empowered fear: dodging it with the swap denies her trade.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

## Mid lane

- Buy Mercs: tenacity shortens every fear you fail to dodge.
  sources: [v222]

- Post-6, track fear like a summoner: its absence is your engage timer.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Out-poke into fear-down all-ins: patience with shadows beats her burst windows.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
