## Key tips

- Against AP Katarina: dodge daggers and predict her Shunpo landing with Q.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q, Katarina.E]

- Against AD Katarina: answer her R with yours and W away; play from range, never melee.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Katarina.R]

- Do not R first unless it is a sure kill.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect her early levels and daggers; your range wins trades after that.
  sources: [v222]

- Track daggers and stand away from them.
  sources: [v222]

## Mid lane

- After she Ws, step back so she cannot E onto you.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Katarina.W, Katarina.E]

- W away from her R; re-engage after it ends.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Katarina.R]

## Win condition

- Win from range: never give her the melee fight she wants.
  sources: [aikopanda]
