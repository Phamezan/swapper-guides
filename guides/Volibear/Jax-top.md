## Key tips

- Bait Counter Strike before committing Q plus W. His E dodges the full combo and reduces area damage, so feint first and engage into the spent shield.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Bait first
  abilities: [Jax.E, Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]

- Never commit into an active E. The shield plus stun reverses the exchange, so break off and re-engage after it expires.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Shield respect
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Dive with the ultimate once he is low. R disables the tower, so convert a won exchange into a kill instead of letting him turtle.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dive finish
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Win short trades before his resistances stack. Your early bulk beats his pattern, so force the issue while the exchange still favors you.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Early press

- Punish his E expiry. The moment the shield falls is your engage window; step in as it ends rather than waiting for the next cycle.
  sources: [federals1-volibear, ph45]
  heading: Expiry punish
  abilities: [Jax.E]

## Mid lane

- Mark him with W, then decide. If the mark sticks, stay for the second hit; if he leaves the zone, take the won trade and reset.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Mark play
  abilities: [Volibear.W]

- Roam the tower line post-6 with R ready. The dive threat zones him off farm even uncast, so posture aggressively while it is up.
  sources: [ph45]
  heading: Dive threat
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

## Win condition

- All-in before his scaling answers you. His dueling grows past yours, so close the lane on your spike instead of farming it out.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Close early

- Chain Q plus W inside spent-E windows. The full combo with no shield to meet it is your kill condition at every stage.
  sources: [federals1-volibear, ph45]
  heading: Combo window
  abilities: [Volibear.Q, Volibear.W, Jax.E]

## Itemization

- Consider Warden's Mail against repeated damage. The armor blunts his auto pattern while your combo pattern decides the lane.
