## Key tips

- Respect levels 1–2 and the bleed stacks. Take short contacts only; every extra auto feeds the passive that kills you.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Early respect

- Step inside his Q and time R against the pull. The inner ring denies his heal and stacks, and R answers the E he uses to catch you.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Q denial
  abilities: [Volibear.R, Darius.E]

- Make level 3 your all-in window. Q plus marked-W sustained trading is your strongest pattern, so force it before his items answer.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-3 turn
  abilities: [Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Concede his level-1 punish. He zones the opening wave, so give the CS rather than feeding him the zone plus first blood.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Level-1 concede

- Step inside before the Q edge casts. Meet each Q windup by closing in, denying the heal and the outer-ring damage together.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: Step inside
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

## Mid lane

- Force the marked all-in. Land Q, stick for the marked W, and extend only while the mark pays out.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Mark commit
  abilities: [Volibear.Q, Volibear.W]

- Close before his Shojin spike. Once Spear of Shojin completes, his stacked trades outscale yours, so end the lane phase ahead.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Spike clock

## Win condition

- Hold R for his pull. The flip answers your escape, so spend R only after E is down or to answer the cast directly.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
  heading: R discipline
  abilities: [Volibear.R, Darius.E]

- Fight before Sterak's plus Shojin stack up. Your window is the mid game; grouped fights late favor his resets over your dive.
  sources: [federals1-darius, federals1-volibear]
  heading: Mid-game window

## Itemization

- Respect his stacking with early armor and Warden's Mail value. Blunt the repeated damage while your Q-plus-W pattern decides the lane.
  sources: [federals1-volibear]
