## Key tips

- He outranges and outscales: never his melee, always your patience.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- His double-W Q-E backwards answers your E: expect retreating damage every engage.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Galio.E, Zed.W]

- Keep CS and XP even or better: parity now denies his sidelane later.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He pokes and backs off: never follow into extended fighting.
  sources: [v222]

- His backwards WEQ answers every E: no exceptions, so bait first.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Galio.E]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- When you ult away, he shoves: your roam is his CS lead.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Galio.R]

## Win condition

- Roams converted plus parity kept: the Zed who never sidelaned you never ends it.
  sources: [v222]
