## Key tips

- She pokes you constantly and you cannot engage her alone: respect her mobility plus Charm.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Dodge her Charm and the true-damage return of her Q: sidestep both halves.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.Q]

- Go Doran's Shield plus Second Wind: farm, play safe, play with your jungler.
  sources: [hexeria]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Concede the early poke: DShield plus potions outlast her mana.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Trade on your Q (about {cd:3:Q}): the cooldown is your window.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Galio.Q]

## Mid lane

- When her Charm is spent, all-in: E dash into W taunt.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Galio.E, Galio.W]

- Her W speed answers your E: bait before committing.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Galio.E]

## Win condition

- Farmed plus roamed: you outscale her and kill her easily later.
  sources: [hexeria]
