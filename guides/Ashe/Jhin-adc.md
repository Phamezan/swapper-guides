## Key tips

- Your range is the problem he cannot solve. Hold max distance and make every trade happen at your auto range, never at his.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- He trades only in flashes of Q plus the fourth shot, then leaves. Answer the burst, then re-engage while his shots are spent.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- He rushes Swiftness and takes Fleet plus Cleanse into you. Respect that setup: hold Volley ({cd:22:W}) slows for real commits and hold your arrow for the guaranteed hit, not poke.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Ashe.W, Ashe.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep Volley pressure on the wave. He must wait for its cooldown to engage, so never give him a free window without it.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Ashe.W]

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and give ground while he holds it. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Walk up when he wastes Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) dry. Without the follow-up root he has no answer to your slows.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Mid lane

- Force the long game at your range. He avoids fighting and wants only brief trades, so extended slow pressure breaks him.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Sweep the objective flanks for his traps and fog picks. Denied vision denies his casts, and he cannot set up what he cannot see.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Force his Cleanse with slows first, then land the arrow while it is down. The kill chain only works in that order against his setup.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Ashe.R]

- Break vision and stay mobile once his root connects. The instant Curtain Call shot after it is guaranteed unless you are already moving.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Spend for fight damage, not utility. His whole plan is avoiding you, so raw stats that win the fights he cannot refuse beat extra slow tools.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
