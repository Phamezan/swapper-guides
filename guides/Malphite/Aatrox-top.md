## Key tips

- Use Q poke as the main lane pattern. Ranged harass is your safe damage; never take close trades you do not need.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: Poke pattern
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]

- Rush early Bramble Vest. His combo autos trigger the heal cut, so the item must be online before his first real all-in, not after.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: Bramble rush

- Answer his ultimate with your own. When he ults to all-in, meet it with R; the exchange stays manageable instead of one-sided.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: Ult answer
  abilities: [Malphite.R, Aatrox.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- If Q1 lands, be ready to absorb the full combo. His sequence is hard to avoid once started, so shield up and ride it out rather than flashing into worse.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: Combo respect
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

- Keep Q movement speed cycling so he never gets a clean W combo. He looks for the engage as your speed fades, so reposition the moment it expires.
  sources: [lilpicky, federals1-malphite]
  heading: Speed discipline
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]

## Mid lane

- Leave his W zone with Q speed plus tier-two boots. The combination walks you out before Q2 lands; without it, the chain completes.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: W escape
  abilities: [Aatrox.W]

- Take short W plus E trades, then back away. Stack armor until his penetration item lands; before Serylda's or Cleaver, your armor wins the exchange.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: Short trades
  abilities: [Malphite.W, Malphite.E]

## Win condition

- Group after one item instead of side-laning. Your teamfight R decides more than a split exchange, so convert the spike into grouped fights.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
  heading: Group timing
  abilities: [Malphite.R]

- Watch his shield timer before committing. Strike as his passive shield fades rather than into it, so your combo lands on bare health.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Shield watch

## Itemization

- Prioritize early Bramble Vest into Plated Steelcaps and armor. Delay damage until his penetration purchase forces the resistance race.
  sources: [federals1-malphite]
