## Key tips

- She has strong early kill pressure and matches your push: concede the early.
  sources: [xblademojo]

- Track her Charm and stand behind minions: the wave blocks it.
  sources: [xblademojo]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Keep your spell shield for her Charm timing: no shield means no walk-up.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- You are among the weakest early: almost no trading tools, so farm, do not fight.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- Protect your voidlings: she kills them on spawn to manage the wave.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- After 6 she plays with her jungler: ward and never step without cover.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

- Cast R only when her Charm is down: she times Charm on your cast.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R, Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Early respected plus shield tracked: the Ahri who never catches you never wins.
  sources: [xblademojo, peng04]
