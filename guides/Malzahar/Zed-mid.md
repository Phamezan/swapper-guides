## Key tips

- He outranges you and wins the lane: play for survival and jungle.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- His team plays around his R: track the jungler before 6.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Keep your shield up through his Q: poke Qs strip it before the combo.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He combos when you E the wave: spend E carefully.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Malzahar.E]

- Farm from max range: his Q out-trades yours.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Ward and play with your team: his R decides fights, not lane.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Punish spent shadows: W-down is your only window.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: face the exit when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

## Win condition

- Survived plus warded: the Zed who never snowballed never kills you.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
