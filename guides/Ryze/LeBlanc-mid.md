## Key tips

- Bone Plating plus RoA (sometimes Mercs) make you too tanky: low cooldowns give high DPS, so force extended trades.
  sources: [pumptm]

- She short-trades only after Bone Plating is gone: your plating gates every trade.
  sources: [pumptm]

- Past early she will not kill you unless far ahead: concede nothing, scale everything.
  sources: [pumptm]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She waits out Bone Plating: trade into her patience.
  sources: [pumptm]

- She keeps E for your chase: never chase into a held chain.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.E]

## Mid lane

- She always TPs to answer your R: R where her TP cannot follow.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [Ryze.R]

- Her E answers your chase: sidestep the chain, run her down.
  sources: [pumptm]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.E]

## Win condition

- Tanky plus scaling: the LeBlanc who never got ahead never kills you.
  sources: [pumptm]
