## Key tips

- She tracks your cooldowns and punishes each one: Q, W and E all open a window (about {cd:101:Q}).
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q, Xerath.W, Xerath.E]

- Her W speed bursts dodge your skillshots: lead the target.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- She fakes autos to bait your spells: never throw the first spell.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She forces the choice: poke her or push; push first, poke second.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- She baits first: hold spells until she walks in.
  sources: [peng04]

## Mid lane

- After 6 she calls her jungler: ward and hold E for ganks.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Xerath.E]

- You stand behind minions to block Charm: keep the wave between you.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

## Win condition

- Deny her free engages all game: angles never stop mattering.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
