## Overview

## Early lane

- Her dodge-first laning beats predictable Qs: hold until she commits.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q]

- She waits for spent spells: never double-cast into her window.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Your cooldowns are much longer: every wasted spell is her window.
  sources: [shok]

- She walks at you when you waste spells: keep max range, never concede position.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Range plus mana: the range advantage is won on windows, not poke.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
