## Key tips

- Any gap-close is a kill: with no disengage you cannot shake his R.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- He stands off-wave: Q both him and the wave.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q]

- Your E is your whole defense: a wasted E is his all-in signal.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Xerath.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He skips First Strike: your range denies his proc.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He buys early boots: every sidestepped Q is HP you never get.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- After Voltaic he farms you: the spike ends your poke phase.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- Spellshield plus his R closes every fight: end it before his second item.
  sources: [aikopanda]
