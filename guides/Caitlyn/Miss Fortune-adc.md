## Key tips

- Never stand near low-health minions. Her Double Up ({cd:21:Q}) bounces off the dying wave into you, so farm from positions off the bounce line.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Never all-in her. Poke from max range and leave — your edge is range she cannot answer, not dueling.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"

- Her setup is Make It Rain ({cd:21:E}) slow into short support trades. Save 90 Caliber Net ({cd:51:E}) to break that chain, and poke hardest while her slow is down.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E, Caitlyn.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Shape the wave so she never gets free bounce angles. When she lines up a Double Up off your dying minions, shove or reposition so no low minion sits between her and you.
  sources: [rowzkit, yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- She looks for short trades with her support, never alone. Poke her when she is isolated and concede the trade the moment her support walks up.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"

## Mid lane

- Keep your net or Flash up in every skirmish. Without disengage ready, her all-in plus her support's chain runs you down before your range matters.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E]

- Seed traps on the chokes her support uses to walk up. If they cannot reach the short-trade setup, she is stuck eating poke from outside her reply range.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.W]

## Win condition

- Fight away from minion waves at objectives. With no dying wave there is no Double Up bounce, and her poke falls off without it.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Turn every poke lead into plates and towers at once. Do not trade kills into her support's short-trade game when the turret is free.
  sources: [rowzkit, yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"

## Itemization

- No single item spike decides this lane. Keep buys tempo-positive and keep pressing range — your wins come from lane pressure, not an item break.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
