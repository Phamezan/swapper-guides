## Key tips

- You out-range him all lane. Keep every trade at max auto and Piltover Peacemaker distance, where he cannot answer with fourth-shot trades.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.Q]

- Never linger at low health. His Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) finishes low targets, so reset or hold your net to break the line instead of greedying one more wave.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.R, Caitlyn.E]

- An even lane belongs to him. He outscales hard and is content going 0/0/0, so every even minute is his win — press the lead while your range rules.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Race him to level 2 with your support. The first engage usually decides who holds the wave for the next minutes.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and give ground while he holds it. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Never commit to a trade without 90 Caliber Net ({cd:51:E}) or Flash up. If both are down, concede the wave rather than offering him a kill.
  sources: [rowzkit]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E]

## Mid lane

- Force fights through your support, not your autos. He is useless once crowd controlled, so call the engage and follow it instead of trading into his range alone.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- His Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) only follows support crowd control, never dry poke. When their support misses, that is your window to walk up and poke freely.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Win condition

- Convert the range lead into plates and dragons. Sitting even lets his scaling erase your lane, so every lead must become map pressure.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- In fights, hold your net for his root-into-Curtain Call ({cd:202:R}) starter. Dash out the moment support control lands on you so his guaranteed first shot never comes.
  sources: [rowzkit, omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Caitlyn.E, Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- There is no item race to win here. Even gold favors him because of his scaling, so base on clean crashes and keep tempo instead of forcing buys for a spike.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
