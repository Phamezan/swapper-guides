## Key tips

- Never stand in the outer ring of her sweep. Dodge into the inner cone or Q completely out, since the outer hit sets up the trade you cannot win.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Sweep positioning
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Camille.W]

- After her shield auto lands, Riposte the poke and Q away. Trading through her passive shield wastes damage, while the parry-plus-disengage turns her shield window into yours.
  sources: [justsad42, raideru]
  heading: Shield answer
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Fiora.Q]

- If she does not misplay, wait for a clear Hookshot opening. Forcing into her terrain loses; patience for her E is the whole matchup.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Patience rule
  abilities: [Camille.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Fight in the middle of the lane, never near walls. Her Hookshot needs terrain, so open ground starves her engage while your vitals stay contestable.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Terrain rule
  abilities: [Camille.E]

- Spend Q and W on offense, not on dodging every ability. The more you burn defensively, the harder the next fight gets, so take some poke to keep answers ready.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Cooldown economy
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Fiora.W]

## Mid lane

- Bait her Hookshot in the open, then Flash Q W toward the wall she grapples to. The wall-bait turns her engage angle into your stun setup.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Hookshot bait
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Camille.E]

- Save Riposte for her ult when a gank arrives. Blocking the lockdown lets you walk out of the dive instead of dying with W unused.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Gank answer
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Camille.R]

## Win condition

- Kite her second Q and sweep with vital and ult move speed. She needs the standing second proc, so never give her a stationary target once her Q1 lands.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Kiting Q2
  abilities: [Fiora.R, Camille.Q]

- Vary your own Riposte timing since she varies Hookshot to bait it. She holds E waiting for your W, so show trades without parrying before spending it.
  sources: [raideru]
  heading: Riposte mindgame
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Camille.E]
