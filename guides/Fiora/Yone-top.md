## Key tips

- Parry his Q3 or R on commitment. When Yone dashes in on the third cast or fires his ultimate, answer with Riposte: the stun turns his engage into your trade.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Parry the commit
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Yone.Q, Yone.R]

- Take extended trades. His answers are max-range Q pokes or kiting on E movement while you work vitals, so start fights where neither escape is free.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Extendeds favor you
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Yone.E]

- Never force a heavy trade while his E offers a free return. Make him spend E for distance first, since a held E lets him dictate every exchange.
  sources: [justsad42, zacheryzztop]
  heading: Respect held E
  abilities: [Yone.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade back when he lands from E within reach. Take the free autos on his return instead of chasing into the escape window he just used.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: E-return punish
  abilities: [Yone.E, Fiora.Q]

- Match his first boots timing. He rushes boots to kite around your vitals, so complete yours on the same reset and his Q spacing never goes unanswered.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: Boots race

- Keep Riposte ready while he holds R after Q3. His outplay line is R behind a severe Q, and your held stun inverts it the moment he commits.
  sources: [justsad42, zacheryzztop]
  heading: R behind Q3
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Yone.Q, Yone.R]

## Mid lane

- Fire Riposte on his commit instead of holding it forever. He cannot cleanse the W stun with E, so waiting out the spirit form only wastes your best answer.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: No cleanse
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Yone.E]

- Read his escape before chasing. If his spirit is pulling back or he holds Q3 as an exit, either one ends your chase, so stop and reset instead of following into his return.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Chase rule
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.Q]

## Win condition

- Take the side-lane 1v1. You are favored there once his two kite windows run out, so hold the side and let him come to you.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Side rule
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- Start clean fights outside his kite windows. Never follow his E movement speed while he is still landing vitals; engage when the speed fades and the marks are yours.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Clean engages
  abilities: [Yone.E, Fiora.Q]
