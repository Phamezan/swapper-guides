## Key tips

- Take Q vital trades only while Counter Strike is unavailable, then leave at once. Extending into his ready stun flips a winning trade into his.
  sources: [justsad42, ph45]
  heading: Trading rule
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Jax.E]
  patch: "26.21"

- Hold Riposte for his leap or empowered strike, not his stun by default. Parrying mid-leap and angling it sideways stuns him as he lands and opens the fight after his E ends.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Riposte discipline
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Jax.Q]

- Never be the first to commit to a full fight when both cooldowns are up. The first engager usually loses this matchup, so force his Counter Strike or leap before committing yours.
  sources: [justsad42]
  abilities: [Jax.E]
  heading: Engagement rule

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hide the start of your Q by feinting in and out of range or last-hitting. Obvious Q approaches are easy for him to answer with a timed Counter Strike.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Concealing Q
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Jax.E]

- Match his Ignite if he takes it, or expect a much harder lane. Without your own Ignite you cannot answer his committed all-ins at low health.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Summoner check

## Mid lane

- After his Counter Strike ends, that is your window to fight. Trade into the cooldown gap rather than testing Riposte against an unreadable stun timing.
  sources: [justsad42, ph45]
  heading: Post-stun window
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Jax.E]
  patch: "26.21"

- Crash waves and keep pressure so he cannot reset bad vitals for free. He gives up unfavorable vital positions through vision plays, so deny him the quiet resets.
  sources: [ph45, justsad42]
  heading: Vital control
  abilities: [Jax.E]
  patch: "26.21"

## Win condition

- Take even fights and punish wasted Counter Strikes. When his stun misses or hits only the wave, he has no answer left for your full rotation.
  sources: [ph45, justsad42]
  heading: Punish wasted E
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Jax.E]
  patch: "26.21"

- If he leaps onto someone else while using Counter Strike, Q into him and start your ult from that less predictable angle. His engage on a teammate is your cleanest R setup of the fight.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Teamfight entry
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Fiora.R, Jax.E]
