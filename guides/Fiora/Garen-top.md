## Key tips

- Poke with max-range Q to a vital and leave at once. Q into melee range lets him answer with his spin, while max-range procs deal damage he cannot return.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Poke rule
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Garen.E]

- Bait out his spin before taking a longer trade. Committing into a held E wastes your health, so make him spend it on nothing first.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: E bait
  abilities: [Garen.E]

- Hold Riposte for his silence or ult, never spend it early. He fakes Q to draw your W, so answer only the committed Q or the ult itself.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: W discipline
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Garen.Q, Garen.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use the vital's movement speed to exit his spin. Tanking the full spin makes the matchup far worse, so proc and leave the moment he commits.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Spin exits
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Garen.E]

- Expect him to cancel his spin early into a surprise Q. Do not overcommit on a shortened E, since the silence follow-up is the real threat.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: E-cancel watch
  abilities: [Garen.E, Garen.Q]

## Mid lane

- With Conqueror and high health, run him down the lane. Your dueling at full health beats his, so keep the lane long and press the health advantage.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Run-down rule

- Take vitals on the open side when he walls off the marked angle. His wall positioning denies one side, so circle to the angle he cannot cover.
  sources: [yshuro13]
  heading: Wall answer

## Win condition

- Maintain pressure instead of yielding, from early through outscale. You are ahead at every point of the lane, so conceding waves gives him the one thing he needs: time.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Pressure rule

- Chase his split with your threat, but never into his team. Your split-push runs him down solo, while his numbers answer is the only thing that stops it.
  sources: [justsad42, yshuro13]
  heading: Split threat
