## Key tips

- Answer his Q cast with Q into W. As he swings, dash in and parry: the block eats the damage and the stun converts his swing into your trade.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Q-W answer
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Fiora.W, Aatrox.Q]

- Wait out his E, then parry the follow-up. Everything before the dash is bait, so hold Riposte for the Q knock-up or W pull that comes after it.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Post-E parry
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Aatrox.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Vary Riposte timing every trade. He refuses to dash into a held parry and hides his vitals, so a predictable W is one he plays around for free.
  sources: [lilpicky]
  heading: Unreadable W
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Let him hold the first waves. His sustain wins the opening, so farm without assuming you dictate and save challenges for windows he gives you.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Concede the open

- Re-angle when he walls off a vital. He pre-positions because he fears the proc, so Q to a new mark instead of tunneling a denied one.
  sources: [lilpicky, justsad42]
  heading: Denied vitals
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

## Mid lane

- Leave chains with Q or movement speed and save W for damage. Spending the parry as an exit is exactly what his E-bait wants from you.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Exits without W
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Fiora.W, Aatrox.E]

- Never fire W at a non-damaging effect by habit. Each wasted parry makes the rest of the lane harder, so keep it holstered until a real cast starts.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Parry discipline
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

## Win condition

- Parry his W pull only when the landing spot continues your fight. Refusing a bad pull denies his setup without spending your answer for nothing.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Pull position
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Aatrox.W]

- Play for the item turn. At even gold your outscale arrives around one item, and from behind it waits until two, so force nothing before the spike lands.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Item timing
  abilities: [Fiora.R]
