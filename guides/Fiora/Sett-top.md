## Key tips

- Poke with max-range Q to a vital, then disengage before he answers. Giving him contact after the proc lets him stick with Q move speed and build the grit that wins him the trade.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Poke rule
  abilities: [Fiora.Q, Sett.Q]

- Save Riposte for his Haymaker, not his pull. His W is the fight decider, so bait Facebreaker at max range and keep W until the true-damage center matters.
  sources: [justsad42, autolykus]
  heading: Riposte discipline
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Sett.W, Sett.E]

- Leave before he builds grit or move the fight outside the minion wave. Fighting inside his stacked wave feeds his W while your vitals go unanswered.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Grit awareness
  abilities: [Sett.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Contest level 1 only with a first auto Q from a bush. That opener wins the all-in, but a face-to-face level 1 without it belongs to him.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Level 1
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

- If he uses Facebreaker early while he has no grit, take the heavier trade. With no W behind it his E is just an engage, and your saved Riposte answers the follow-up Haymaker.
  sources: [justsad42, autolykus]
  heading: Punishing early E
  abilities: [Sett.E, Sett.W, Fiora.W]

## Mid lane

- After level 6, stand behind him so his ult carries him away from your turret. When he grabs you under tower, the displacement throws him into a bad spot instead of you.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Beating his ult
  abilities: [Fiora.R, Sett.R]

- Trigger your ult heal early enough to matter, or delay the proc until it executes him. Popping all four vitals without the heal timing just gives him a carried target.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Ult timing
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

## Win condition

- Aim fights toward a wall so his ult cannot carry you out of your healing. The wall shortens his displacement and keeps you inside the ult zone you already started.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Positioning
  abilities: [Fiora.R, Sett.R]

- Build early armor into his lane since the matchup stays volatile. Bramble and armor boots blunt his extended damage while your side-lane scaling takes over.
  sources: [autolykus]
  heading: Item setup
  patch: "26.20"
