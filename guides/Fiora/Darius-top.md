## Key tips

- Concede levels 1–2 entirely, then open trading at 3. His level-1 all-in beats yours, so max-range Q pokes are the whole early plan until your kit comes online.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Level-3 rule
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

- Parry his W rather than his E. The W read lands more consistently, and his held E exists to bait the greedy Riposte.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: W over E
  abilities: [Fiora.W, Darius.E, Darius.W]

- Never start a long trade into a held Ghost without a cooldown opening. A skilled Darius holds E and W for exactly that kite, so force shorts until the summoner is spent.
  sources: [justsad42, federals1-darius]
  heading: Ghost respect
  abilities: [Darius.E, Darius.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Touch him at level 1 only with max-range Q. Anything closer feeds the one all-in you lose, so poke the vital and leave.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Level-1 poke
  abilities: [Fiora.Q]

- Never throw Q at his spacing. He gives no vitals for free and answers committed dashes with W plus Q, so enter on his whiff, not on your cooldown.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: No dry Qs
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Darius.W, Fiora.Q]

## Mid lane

- Enter only after he spends Stridebreaker on the wave. The active guarantees his Q edge if you dash in blind, so let it resolve on minions first.
  items: [6631]
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: Stridebreaker window
  abilities: [Darius.Q, Fiora.Q]

- Start Blade Waltz only with his back to open ground. A walled back denies a vital and kills both the proc and the healing zone.
  sources: [federals1-darius]
  heading: R walls
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

## Win condition

- Take the side-lane 1v1 once the early concede ends. That duel is yours when you pick the timing instead of answering his.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Side take
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- Never fight him at five stacks without a team edge, even from ahead. His reset chain swings the fight off a single R, so disengage the stacked state.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: Reset fear
  abilities: [Darius.R]
