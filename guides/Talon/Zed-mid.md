## Key tips

- He refuses melee, using range: close with W-Q.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He respects your burst and pokes from range: his trades are bad, so force good trades.
  sources: [v222]

- His Sweeper finds Q during your R stealth: R from fog.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Overview

## Early lane

- His range poke forces bad trades: W-Q in, R out.
  sources: [v222]

- He plays the skill matchup at range: your W-Q is the only engage, so make it count.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His backwards combo answers your W-Q in: Q after he shadows.
  sources: [v222]

## Win condition

- He Rs your R or Ws to kite: bait the answer before committing.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Zed.W]
