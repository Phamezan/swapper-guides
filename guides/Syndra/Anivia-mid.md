## Key tips

- Punish pre-6 when she walks up to CS: long cooldowns and short range.
  sources: [shok]

- Dodge her Q or take big damage: the sidestep is mandatory.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

- She is very gankable: call your jungler.
  sources: [shok]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade every walkup: her cooldowns do not cover the wave.
  sources: [shok]

- Build the pre-6 lead: it keeps growing after 6.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Harder to punish after 6, but the lead carries: keep pressing with wards.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep dodging Q: the rule never expires.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q]

## Win condition

- Pre-6 bank plus Q dodged: the Anivia who never farmed never fights.
  sources: [shok]
