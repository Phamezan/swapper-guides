## Key tips

- Weak early with long cooldowns and mediocre sustain: the first five levels are yours.
  sources: [shok]

- Build the lead by 5: after 6 and first back he gets strong.
  sources: [shok]

- After 6 he pools to dodge your R: R immediately after landing a stun.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.R, Syndra.E, Vladimir.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish when his Q is down, especially right after empowered Q: the window is binary.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q]

- Abuse his poor waveclear: shove and poke while his spells are down.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Post-6, stun-first combos only: naked Rs die to the pool.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Syndra.R, Vladimir.W]

- Track his first back: the strong window opens on his item.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Lead banked plus stun-first Rs: the Vladimir who never stabilized never outscales.
  sources: [shok]
