## Key tips

- The lane revolves around his E2: hit means you lose, miss means you punish hard.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Sylas.E]

- Stand near creeps: his E turns awkward, but not where Q hits you and the wave.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Sylas.E, Sylas.Q]

- Take Bone Plating: it covers the trade you fail to dodge.
  sources: [trisend3]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Give him nothing early: without a lead he stays manageable.
  sources: [shok]

- Farm behind offset creeps: awkward angles cut his E2 accuracy.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- After the first waves, keep the wave on your side: short lane, short trades.
  sources: [shok]

- Hold E to disengage: the stun breaks his extended trade.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- Missed-E2 punishment plus safe waves: the Sylas who never snowballed is yours.
  sources: [shok]
