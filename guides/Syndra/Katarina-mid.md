## Key tips

- Never stand on daggers: the blades are the whole lane.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]

- Respect level 2 and 6 all-ins: her windows are binary.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep E, especially after 6, to break trades or cancel her R: the stun is your R-answer.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Katarina.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Outside her windows she is weak: poke between the level 2 and 6 spikes.
  sources: [shok]

- She roams for kills: ward and ping every missing.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Lock her in lane or kill her: either puts you far ahead.
  sources: [shok]

- If E is used aggressively, play safer: no stun means no R-answer.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- Daggers denied plus R canceled: the Katarina who never roams for free never wins.
  sources: [shok]
