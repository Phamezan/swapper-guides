## Overview

## Early lane

- Against Hail of Blades, concede the first wave: his E-dash wins level 1.
  sources: [trisend3]

- Otherwise bully from the first minute: Q poke sets the tone.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q]

## Mid lane

- Use R as poke: damage he cannot rewind is permanent.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.R, Ekko.R]

- Keep the stun for his engage: E held is E feared.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- Bully plus poke-R: the Ekko who never stabilized never threatens.
  sources: [shok]
