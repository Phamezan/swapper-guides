## Key tips

- You win the 1v1 against Ahri clearly: play for the duel, not for survival.
  sources: [shok]

- Her gank setup is the real danger: track her jungler before committing.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- You are the lane bully but very gankable: pressure with wards, never blind.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Establish the bully dynamic early: she must respect your stun range.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

- She answers with W speed: expect dodges and delayed Charms, not clean hits.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Ahri.E]

## Mid lane

- After your stun lands, expect her buffered return spell: she pre-casts E or Q during the stun to trade back.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.Q]

- Vary your Q-E timing: a predictable stun gets dodged first, then buffered against.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- Win the 1v1, lose nothing to ganks: warded aggression closes the lane.
  sources: [shok]
