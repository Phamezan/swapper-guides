## Key tips

- Well ahead before 6, hard after: the lane has a timer.
  sources: [shok]

- She is hard to shove out: accept the wave state, do not force pushes.
  sources: [shok]

- Run First Strike and scale: your lead compounds if you survive.
  sources: [shok]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Your E is her whole problem: hold it and she cannot engage freely.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

- Build the pre-6 lead: every chunk now is survival later.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- When she drops shroud, give up the CS and leave the area: contested CS is death.
  sources: [trisend3]

- She baits wave pushes by standing in the wave: do not blow everything into a stacked wave.
  sources: [anguish]

## Win condition

- Pre-6 bank plus shroud discipline: the Akali who never snowballed loses to your scaling.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
