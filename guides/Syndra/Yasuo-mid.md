## Key tips

- Never waste mana: throw only spells you are sure will land.
  sources: [shok]

- Take Stormraider's or First Strike: movement over damage.
  sources: [shok]

- He dodges Q with dashes: cast where he lands, not where he stands.
  sources: [v222yasuo]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Yasuo.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play the early for survival: he runs Grasp or Lethal Tempo to kill you.
  sources: [v222yasuo]

- Expect his Wind Wall for your E: bait it before committing the combo.
  sources: [v222yasuo]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W, Syndra.E]

## Mid lane

- Once he has boots, he plays for jungle and map: match with wards and shoves.
  sources: [v222yasuo]

- A strong AP jungler on his side makes it harder: track jungle before trading.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Outscale to teamfights: sure-spell discipline beats his dodge war late.
  sources: [shok]
