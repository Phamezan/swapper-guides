## Key tips

- He is favoured in the raw 1v1: respect his ray, do not stat-check him.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- It is a skillshot check: movement and landing spells decide it.
  sources: [shok]

- Take Second Wind: his poke war is answered in sustain.
  sources: [trisend3]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Move between every cast: standing still loses the check.
  sources: [shok]

- Trade only when your spells land: a whiffed Q-E is HP gifted.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Syndra.E]

## Mid lane

- He is very gankable: set up your jungler rather than forcing solo kills.
  sources: [shok]

- Favour 2v2 and 3v3 skirmishes: with help you outfight him.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Win through map and skirmish: ganks plus Second-Wind sustain grind out the favoured 1v1.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
