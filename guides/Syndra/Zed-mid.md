## Key tips

- Levels 1-2 are abusable: force him to pick CS or eating Q.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q]

- From 3, never eat both shurikens of W-E-Q: sidestep one shuriken always.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- After 6, do not walk up: his R range is the whole lane.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Cloth Armor early, held in inventory: it cuts his lethality all lane.
  items: [1029]
  sources: [shok]

- Respect his spacing edge: played well, he outranges your shadow poke.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- R him while he is stunned or after he has used R: never ult into a held dodge.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.R, Zed.R]

- He answers your R with his: force his R first, then combo.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Syndra.R, Zed.R]

- Death Mark lands him behind you: pre-aim E behind yourself when he ults.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Syndra.E]

## Win condition

- The 1-2 abuse plus held Cloth decides it: a Zed who never snowballed never kills you.
  sources: [shok]
