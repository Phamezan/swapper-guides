## Key tips

- Strong laner, but you edge a small lead and outscale: play for edges.
  sources: [shok]

- Stand behind creeps to block QQ: minions are your shield.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q]

- Stay far when he nukes the wave: QE and EE are not yours to contest.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q, Hwei.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Rush tier-2 boots: dodge volume beats poke volume.
  sources: [shok]

- He is weak in early skirmishes: call the 2v2, not the 1v1.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- He is very gankable: ganks pay, so set them up.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep the small lead growing: outscale is the plan, not the all-in.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Edges plus ganks plus outscale: the strong laner who never snowballed loses.
  sources: [shok]
