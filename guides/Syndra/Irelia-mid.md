## Key tips

- Dominant in lane and sidelane, but she must snowball to matter: denial is victory.
  sources: [shok]

- Respect her level 2 and 6 all-ins: the spikes are lethal.
  sources: [shok]

- Hold E to break off her trades: the stun is your exit.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Not dying matters more than CS: concede gold, never HP.
  sources: [shok]

- Take Bone Plating and play safe: the page blunts her spikes.
  sources: [trisend3]

## Mid lane

- Keep the wave on your side and set up ganks: short lane, jungle help.
  sources: [shok]

- Stay away from low-HP minions: her Q reset starts there.
  sources: [trisend3]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Win condition

- Starved plus peeled: the Irelia who never snowballed is a fighter with no lead.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
