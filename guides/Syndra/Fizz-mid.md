## Key tips

- Levels 1-2 are yours, 3 and 6 are his: the lane flips on his spikes.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- His E is trade tool and waveclear in one: it is the only cooldown that matters.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Take Bone Plating: it blunts the all-in you cannot dodge.
  sources: [trisend3]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish him hard at 1-2: every HP bar chunked now is safety later.
  sources: [shok]

- Crash 2-3 waves under his tower, then let it bounce back: the bounce is your safe farm.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Do not play pushed up from 3: his kill zone opens at 3 and 6.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Barrier, Exhaust, or early Null-Magic Mantle: a defensive pick eases the whole lane.
  items: [1033]
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Deny the dive: crashed waves plus defensive setup starve his spike turns.
  sources: [shok]
