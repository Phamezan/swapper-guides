## Early lane

- Dodge-first laning: his Q only matters if it lands.

## Mid lane

- Punish his much longer cooldowns: every wasted spell is your window.
  sources: [shok]

- Walk at him when he wastes spells: the punish is positional.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Boots plus sustain plus patience: the outranged lane is won on windows, not range.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
