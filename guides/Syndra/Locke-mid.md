- His E goes over your stun: never rely on E to stop him.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Locke.E]

- Keep the wave on your side and play to outscale: an even lane is a big win.
  sources: [shok]

- He punishes after your stun: once E is down, give ground until it returns.
  sources: [sephyrixx]

## Early lane

- He spams Q and blinks your stun: respect the triple-Q pattern early.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

## Mid lane

- Punish only clear mistakes, like a wasted Q: patience is the plan.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

- Track his E dashes: each spent E is seconds you can breathe.
  sources: [sephyrixx]
  abilities: [Locke.E]

## Win condition

- Outscale: the lane you drew is the game you win.
  sources: [shok]
