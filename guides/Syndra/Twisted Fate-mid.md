## Key tips

- You win the lane hard: play to press, not to survive.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep him pushed in: a Twisted Fate under tower cannot roam.
  sources: [shok]

- Watch the enemy jungler: pushed waves invite ganks.
  sources: [shok]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He is hard to kill: take early Mercs and grind, do not force.
  sources: [shok]

- Manage mana: Q spam without a plan leaves you dry.

## Mid lane

- Comet or Phase Rush plus TP: the page is built for push and map.
  sources: [shok]

- Push before he can look: every wave crashed is a roam denied.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- Pushed in plus jungler tracked: the Twisted Fate who never leaves wins you the game.
  sources: [shok]
