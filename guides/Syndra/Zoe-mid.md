## Key tips

- Skill matchup, slightly yours: play for edges, not stomps.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep creeps between you to block Q and sleep bubble: the wave is your shield.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Zoe.Q, Zoe.E]

- If she wastes bubble, play very aggressive: the cooldown is your window.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Zoe.E]

## Early lane

- Respect the summoners and items she picks up: check what she holds.
  sources: [shok]

- Call ganks: she is very gankable with weak waveclear.
  sources: [shok]

## Mid lane

- Respect her all-ins: burst windows end, your pressure does not.
  sources: [shok]

- After 6, if she ults forward, step up and stun her as she snaps back.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Zoe.R]

## Win condition

- Edges banked plus return punished: the Zoe who never snowballed loses the skill check.
  sources: [shok, trisend3]
