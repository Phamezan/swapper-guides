## Key tips

- He crushes you early, so concede nothing. Every minion and experience point matters because your outscale is the entire win condition.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Do not give him level 2 first. His early aggression off the race decides the lane, so match his push from the first wave.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Drag the game out. He must close early because you outscale if it drags — every minute you survive even is a minute won.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Stack Super Scorcher Breath ({cd:901:Q}) on the wave safely. Never trade autos for stacks — the stacks are worth more than any single trade.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Smolder.Q]

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and give ground while he holds it. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Walk up when he wastes Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) dry. Without the follow-up root he has no answer to your stacking.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Mid lane

- Farm sides and answer groups, but never duel. His deny-CS plan only works if you show up to be denied — take the safe farm and leave.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Sweep the objective flanks for his traps and fog picks. Denied vision denies his casts, and he cannot set up what he cannot see.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Once stacked, take the front-to-back he fears. He spent the whole game trying to stop your stacks — make him fight into them.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Break vision and stay mobile once his root connects. The instant Curtain Call shot after it is guaranteed unless you are already moving.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Spend for scaling, not for dueling. Your win is the late game he is racing, so buy the stacking curve rather than early fight stats.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
