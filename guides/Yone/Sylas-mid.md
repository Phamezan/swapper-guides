## Key tips

- Run Lethal Tempo with Bone Plating, one more offensive rune from Resolve, and the flat-health shard instead of scaling health. Plating turns his short burst from half-health chunks into trades you win.
  sources: [mauro2347]

- Answer his level 1 directly. Most Sylas players open E plus two autos and run; tank it on Plating, keep autoing with Q through Lethal Tempo, and with Ignite up he is dead if he stays instead of retreating under tower.
  sources: [mauro2347]
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Sylas.E]

- Take Ignite. He heals through your kill window without it, and this lane only swings on real kill pressure since it snowballs for both sides.
  sources: [mauro2347]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Fight him after his rotation ends, not during it. Chase with Soul Unbound once he has spent his burst: you destroy him in the long trade, so the extended fight is always yours.
  sources: [mauro2347]
  abilities: [Yone.E]

- Only take his short trades while Bone Plating holds. Without it he chunks half your health bar for free, so respect its cooldown and give ground until it returns.
  sources: [mauro2347]

## Mid lane

- Never hand him free Kingslayer heals. Do not poke him behind his tower, where he takes your poke and heals it back with W before walking away even.
  sources: [mauro2347]
  abilities: [Sylas.W]

- Keep the same rule past the opener: let him start his trade, then run him down with E while his cooldowns are gone. He has no disengage without his E, which is a large part of his early damage.
  sources: [mauro2347]
  abilities: [Yone.E, Sylas.E]

## Win condition

- Build Immortal Shieldbow first. Once it lands, the 1v1 is over for him unless he already got fed, so force the duel instead of waiting.
  sources: [mauro2347]
  items: [3133, 6673]

- Treat the lane as a snowball matchup for both sides. Meet the setup requirements, stay even, and your scaling wins; fall behind and he melts you, so take no coinflip fights from behind.
  sources: [mauro2347]

## Itemization

- First item is Immortal Shieldbow for the duel: it ends his 1v1 chances and converts your even lane into a won one.
  sources: [mauro2347]
  items: [3133, 6673]
