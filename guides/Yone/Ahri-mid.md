## Key tips

- Do not Q3 predictably: she plays around its knockup, so vary the timing.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Commit E only when her Charm is down; her whole lane is holding it for your engage.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Yone.E, Ahri.E]

- Concede level 1: do not take free harass for CS you cannot win yet.
  sources: [sjorry]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect her Q3 and E windows: back off when they are ready.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Yone.E]

- Give ground on her poke and trade back after, not into it.
  sources: [peng04, shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- Ult onto her when Charm is down; expect the behind-her Charm if it is up.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Yone.R, Ahri.E]

- Track her jungler: her game is gank setup, so do not overextend for run-downs.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Win condition

- Close through E and ult commits when Charm is down.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.R, Ahri.E]

- Do not chase into fog or river without vision: her game is picks and ganks.
  sources: [shok-ahri, sjorry]
