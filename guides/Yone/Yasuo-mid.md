## Key tips

- Concede levels 1–2. His passive shield plus his dashes let him choose every early fight, so do not walk up for the wave into his level 2; play safe until level 3, when the lane becomes a real matchup.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]
  abilities: [Yasuo.P]

- Pop his passive shield with Spirit Cleave before every trade, and only commit while your shield is up and his is down. Your shield is on demand and his is not, which is what turns trades winning.
  sources: [dennissuarez69, nicolaslamelas]
  abilities: [Yone.W]

- Release Fate Sealed at close range. From far away his Wind Wall ({cd:157:W}) eats the wave, but the point-blank knockup lands before the wall matters, so step in before casting it.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Yasuo.W, Yone.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Start Doran's Blade, not anything greedy: without its stats he simply walks over you in the opening trades.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]
  items: [1055]

- Take Second Wind and Shield Bash second. He procs Bone Plating for free with a passing Q and disengages before you can answer, which wastes the rune; Second Wind keeps working through his poke pattern.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]

- Take Exhaust. It cripples his committed all-in far more than Ignite punishes him, and if he takes Exhaust while you take Ignite you lose the duel on summoners alone.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]

## Mid lane

- From level 6 with Blade components, E-trade him every window. Stack Lethal Tempo first, then Q him in the face as he dashes to one of your minions and cast Soul Unbound while the stacks hold.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yasuo.E]

- Answer his last-Q knockup with your E snapback instead of eating it, and never cast a ranged Q3 through a ready Wind Wall: the wave dies and you stand exposed through its full cooldown ({cd:157:W}).
  sources: [mmjyn]
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.Q, Yasuo.W]

- Rush attack speed first: Berserker's plus Recurve Bow. The movement speed dodges his Qs and the attack speed stacks Tempo so you out-damage him once both shields are down.
  sources: [dennissuarez69]

## Win condition

- Finish Blade of the Ruined King and the lane flips. Past that item your E trades win every time and he becomes a non-factor in the duel, so stop respecting him and start running at him.
  sources: [dennissuarez69, nicolaslamelas]
  abilities: [Yone.E]

- Play for the scale, not the kill. Your shield is available on demand through W and E while his passive comes back on its own timer, so every even minute that passes favors you.
  sources: [nicolaslamelas]
  abilities: [Yone.W, Yone.E]

## Itemization

- Rush Blade of the Ruined King. It is the only first item with the dueling stats to match him, and both the components and the completion are powerspikes in this lane.
  sources: [mmjyn, nicolaslamelas, dennissuarez69]
  items: [3124]
