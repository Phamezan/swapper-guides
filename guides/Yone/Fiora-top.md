## Key tips

- Never throw a naked Q3 at her. She holds Riposte for the predictable tornado timing, so fire Q3 only after she shows it or aims it elsewhere.
  sources: [zacheryzztop, differenthumanpal]
  heading: No naked Q3
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Fiora.W]

- Concede level 1 to Press the Attack and Ignite. She zones you off the wave with vitals up, so farm with Q at max range instead of contesting autos you lose.
  sources: [justsad42, differenthumanpal]
  heading: Level-1 concession
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Fiora.P]

- Fight only on good vitals. A bad vital forces a reset, so track how she circles and walk out rather than trading into the side she wants.
  sources: [zacheryzztop, differenthumanpal]
  heading: Vital selection
  abilities: [Fiora.P]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Give the first wave and poke from outside her Q range. Max-range Q is one of your only two winning patterns, so take the farm it gives and skip the all-in she is holding.
  sources: [justsad42, differenthumanpal]
  heading: First wave
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Fiora.Q]

- Hold Q3 through her Riposte timing every trade. Firing it carelessly loses the exchange on the spot, so keep the tornado until her parry window passes.
  sources: [zacheryzztop, differenthumanpal]
  heading: Q3 patience
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Fiora.W]

## Mid lane

- Bait Riposte with a Q3 aimed elsewhere, then engage. A wasted parry is your snowball opening, so spend the tornado to pull it out before committing E.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Parry bait
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Yone.E, Fiora.W]

- Kite with E movespeed when she keeps fighting into you. Her kit loses to the kite, so snap back and let her chase into your Q range rather than standing toe to toe.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: E kite
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.Q]

- Respect Grand Challenge by kiting away while dealing damage. Standing inside all four vitals ends the fight, so move out along the wall side and punish the procs she misses.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: Versus her R
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

## Win condition

- Snowball off a baited Riposte before she outscales. She wins the 1v1 once even, so convert a forced parry into plates or a kill instead of settling for farm.
  sources: [differenthumanpal, justsad42]
  heading: Close early

- Build space between body and spirit with every E. Your E no longer cleanses her Riposte, so the snapback gap is what keeps both halves safe from the stun.
  sources: [justsad42]
  heading: E spacing
  abilities: [Yone.E, Fiora.W]
