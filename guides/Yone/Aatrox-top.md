## Key tips

- Move E around his Q swings instead of through them. A dodge that becomes a trade wins the exchange his Q started, since he cannot track the reposition.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: E angles
  abilities: [Yone.E, Aatrox.Q]

- Bait his E with Q3, then cast R after it fades. He holds the dash for your instant post-Q3 ult, so spend the knock-up first and ult the window it leaves.
  sources: [zacheryzztop, godkingaatrox]
  heading: R bait
  abilities: [Yone.R, Yone.Q, Aatrox.E]

- Give ground while your ultimate is down. You become his all-in target at any approach angle without it, so concede space until it returns.
  sources: [naayil]
  heading: R-down defense
  abilities: [Yone.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Rush boots before anything else. The kite around every Q zone starts with movement, and without it his sweet spots land for free.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: Boots first

- Never trade on a stacked-against-you wave. He contests bad wave states first, so fix the wave, then look for the exchange.
  sources: [naayil]
  heading: Wave first

## Mid lane

- Refuse the spacing poke war. He outsustains short trades, so take only committed all-ins where you land first and leave after your combo.
  sources: [godkingaatrox]
  heading: No poke war
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Shove out a stacked wave before leaving it. If you must go while minions pile against you, expect the full crash and a level gap on return.
  sources: [naayil]
  heading: Crash answer

## Win condition

- Hold the side lane once ahead and join fights on your terms. From behind he has no answer in either setting, so keep the pressure split.
  sources: [zacheryzztop]
  heading: Side press

- Shove the side wave before each Dragon, then rotate to the fight. The shove is what lets you leave without donating plates for the objective.
  sources: [naayil]
  heading: Objective shove
