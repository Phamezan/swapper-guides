## Key tips

- Kill him pre-6 with Ignite or the lane flips. You can force the kill every time with an early lead and a freeze, but without that lead his post-6 all-in runs you down.
  sources: [differenthumanpal, snekirl]
  heading: Pre-6 kill window

- Trade heavy at level 1 from the bush and get level 2 first. Sit in the side bush, hit him as he walks to the wave, and all-in the moment you hit 2 while he is still level 1.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Level-1 bush trade
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Disengage the instant he Withers you. Your trades are only oppressive with E up and clean movement, so walk out of every Wither instead of fighting slowed.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Wither rule
  abilities: [Nasus.W, Yone.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Run Ignite with Sudden Impact for maximum early damage. The bonus damage is what turns the level-2 all-in into a kill, so take the aggressive setup when he has no escape.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Ignite setup

- Crash, bounce, then do not touch the wave at levels 3-5. Let it come back to your tower and perma-freeze there, because a freeze denies him the stacks he needs to come back.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Freeze setup

- Trade with him every time he walks up for a stack. Stacks are the only moment he must stand still near you, so punish each one instead of letting him farm for free.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Punish stacks

## Mid lane

- Kite out his ultimate and never take the post-6 all-in even. He stomps you from level 6 on unless you are hugely ahead, so snap back, shed the Wither, and re-engage only after it fades.
  sources: [snekirl, differenthumanpal]
  heading: No even all-ins
  abilities: [Nasus.R, Nasus.W, Yone.E]

- Tell your jungler to path top and play for the dive. He is immobile and runs Ghost, so with your early freeze in place he cannot survive a gank.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Jungle setup

## Win condition

- Keep your abilities for him, not the wave. Stand on the melee side toward him so Q is always ready to punish a stack attempt, because wave damage buys him the freeze break he wants.
  sources: [differenthumanpal]
  heading: Save cooldowns
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Win through the map, not the 1v1, once both ultimates are up. A free-farming Nasus beats you down two levels and a full item, so convert your freeze into ganks and objective pressure instead of rematching him.
  sources: [snekirl, differenthumanpal]
  heading: Map over duel
  abilities: [Nasus.R]
