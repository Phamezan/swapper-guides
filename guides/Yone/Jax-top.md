## Key tips

- Never E onto him while Counter Strike is up. He holds it to answer your E and runs you down after it ends, so bait it with Q pokes and E only once the dodge is gone.
  sources: [kakaglad]
  heading: E discipline
  abilities: [Yone.E, Jax.E]

- Press levels 1-3 while you are the stronger stat-check. He concedes that you out-damage him pre-3, so trade through Q range and match his push before his rune setup comes online.
  sources: [kakaglad, nottheredad]
  heading: Early press
  abilities: [Yone.Q]

- Refuse even all-ins once his Sheen is done. His win condition is a near-tower freeze into a Sheen plus Ignite stat-check, so keep trades to Q poke and snap back instead of taking the second round.
  sources: [kakaglad]
  heading: Sheen respect
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Yone.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect his level-1 E walk-up from the fog bush. He wants to start the lane by running Counter Strike into you, so give the bush, Q-poke from range, and match the first three waves so he never gets a free crash and reset.
  sources: [nottheredad, kakaglad]
  heading: First three waves
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Jax.E]

- Do not throw Q3 at his walk-up feints. He steps forward and back to bait the tornado, which opens a window for him to engage for free, so hold Q3 until he commits Counter Strike or goes for the minion.
  sources: [kakaglad]
  heading: Q3 discipline
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Jax.E]

- Back off after both Es are traded. His Counter Strike returns before your Soul Unbound does ({cd:24:E}, {cd:777:E}), so bank the damage dealt and reset stance instead of extending into his second rotation.
  sources: [nottheredad]
  heading: E-cooldown gap
  abilities: [Yone.E, Jax.E]

## Mid lane

- Keep mid-game trades to one rotation. He wins extended fights once stacked, so E in, land Q and autos, and snap back before his passive and tempo stack up against you.
  sources: [kakaglad]
  heading: One rotation only
  abilities: [Yone.E, Yone.Q]

- Break his freeze before his Sheen spike. A wave held near his tower becomes an Ignite stat-check you cannot win, so thin with Q, threaten with a held E, and force the crash before he sets it.
  sources: [kakaglad]
  heading: No freeze
  abilities: [Yone.Q, Yone.E]

## Win condition

- Run him down only while his E is down. He disengages by leaping to a minion or ward after wasting Counter Strike, so hold your E for that moment and chase while he has no answer.
  sources: [kakaglad, nottheredad]
  heading: E-down punish
  abilities: [Yone.E, Jax.E, Jax.Q]

- Take an early lead into skirmishes and objective fights instead of re-challenging a frozen lane. The even post-Sheen duel is his win condition, so convert pressure elsewhere rather than handing him the stat-check.
  sources: [kakaglad]
  heading: Leave the freeze
