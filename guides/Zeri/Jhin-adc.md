## Key tips

- You win extended trades. He must avoid the 1v1, so chase every trade past his comfort point instead of letting him hit and leave.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- He bullies the lane early. Respect his fourth-shot poke while farming, and answer only with full combos rather than standing trades.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Scaling is roughly even. Neither side gets a free clock, so the lane winner keeps the edge — do not hand him an early lead for nothing.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and give ground while he holds it. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Walk up when he wastes Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) dry. Without the follow-up root he has no answer to your slide.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

- Stick to him with Spark Surge ({cd:221:E}) once the trade starts. Your mobility turns his short-trade plan into the extended fight you win.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Zeri.E]

## Mid lane

- Meet his objective groups and force the duel. His W-plus-R plan needs range and setup; your extended damage inside it wins.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Sweep the objective flanks for his traps and fog picks. Denied vision denies his casts, and he cannot set up what he cannot see.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Kite forward through fights, not away. Every extra second of the extended trade favors you over his short-trade plan.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Zeri.E]

- Break vision and stay mobile once his root connects. The instant Curtain Call shot after it is guaranteed unless you are already moving.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Build to extend fights. Your win is the long mobile duel he refuses, so spend for sustained damage rather than poke.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
