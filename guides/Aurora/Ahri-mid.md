## Key tips

- She dodges your Q and E poke, especially walking to CS: lead her.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q, Aurora.E]

- She steps in only when Q or E is down: stagger cooldowns.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q, Aurora.E]

- Whoever lands first wins: match her range and strike first.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She does not force trades in levels 1-5: your poke wins them, so press.
  sources: [peng04]

- She wins trades by dodging at CS time: hold Q until she commits.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q, Aurora.E]

## Mid lane

- The lane turns into a waveclear contest: shove and skirmish better.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- She matches your shove and never hands free control: out-shove her.
  sources: [shok-ahri, auroraaddict]

## Win condition

- She wins the skirmish game around shoves, not the 1v1: pick the 1v1.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
