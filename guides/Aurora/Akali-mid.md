## Key tips

- Bad matchup for her: her Doran's plus Second Wind only delays.
  sources: [anguish]

- If her E lands she holds E2 to track through invis and R dashes: break it.
  sources: [anguish]
  abilities: [Akali.E, Aurora.R]

- Her engages are straight lines: your linears hit her on the way in, so hold them.
  sources: [anguish]

## Mid lane

- She tracks through every dash: held E2 is her only stick, so multi-dash.

  sources: [anguish]

## Win condition
