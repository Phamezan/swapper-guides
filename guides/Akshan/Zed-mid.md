## Key tips

- Early favours you: high poke means he concedes the first levels.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Your E engage: the swing is your kill setup, so vary it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Akshan.E]

- He pings every roam: your map movement is the real threat, so move.
  sources: [v222]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He starts Doran's Shield: poke through it anyway.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- He refuses to die, farming with Q: HP now is his kills later, so zone him off.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His R breaks your E chain: if you grapple, expect the ult to snap it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Akshan.E]

## Win condition

- Spike denied plus roams converted: the Zed whose Voltaic never flipped it never wins.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
