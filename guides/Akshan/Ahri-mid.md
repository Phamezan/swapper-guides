- She watches your E once minions thin out: swing only into cover.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Akshan.E]

- She plays around your Q and E timers: stagger them.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Akshan.Q, Akshan.E]

  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Akshan.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- From 6 she wins 2v2s: roam first, never answer.
  sources: [peng04]

- Every E you use is a gank opening for her jungle: never swing blind.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Akshan.E]

## Mid lane

- Her support roams on your E: track the support before swinging.
  sources: [peng04]

- Thin minions invite her E-watch: keep the wave healthy.
  sources: [peng04]

## Win condition

- Swung plus roamed: the Ahri whose timers never lined up never wins.
  sources: [peng04]
