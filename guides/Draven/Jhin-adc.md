## Key tips

- You are his permaban. He fears your axes, so force the lane on your terms from level 1 instead of letting him set a safe short-trade lane.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- You are useless once crowd controlled. Track their engage cooldowns and force fights only with an exit ready — never walk in blind.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

- Win the level-2 race with your support. The first engage decides who holds the wave for the next minutes.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"

## Overview

## Early lane

- Make him answer your shove. A Jhin stuck catching waves under his tower cannot hold the short-trade lane he wants.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Draven.Q]

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and respect it while held. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Walk up when he wastes Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) dry. Without the follow-up root he has no answer to your axes.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Mid lane

- Meet his objective groups and force the fight. His W-plus-R plan needs range and setup; your axes inside it win.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Sweep the objective flanks for his traps and fog picks. Denied vision denies his casts, and he cannot set up what he cannot see.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Close before his scaling erases the axe gap. Convert every kill into towers and dragons rather than sitting on a farm lead.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Break vision and stay mobile once his root connects. The instant Curtain Call shot after it is guaranteed unless you are already moving.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Build to duel. Your win is the straight fight he refuses, so spend for axe damage rather than poke.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
