## Key tips

- Your cage gates his all-ins: hold it while he looks.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- Cage down means he runs you down: the cooldown is his kill signal, so track it.
  sources: [v222]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- His R answers your R: never ult a Zed holding Death Mark.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Veigar.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- He chips with shadow poke: dodge shadows, stack anyway.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
  abilities: [Zed.W]

- He farms to Voltaic: punish before the spike.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- His Edge of Night second: with it your cage cannot catch him after R, so end it first.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

## Win condition

- Cage up plus R held: that combination denies the kill every time.
  sources: [aikopanda, v222]
