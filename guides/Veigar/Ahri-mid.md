## Key tips

- She dodges your cage with W speed (about {cd:45:E}): cast it where she lands.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W, Veigar.E]

- She pushes from level 1: concede the wave, stack the minions.
  sources: [starzall, shok-ahri]

- She punishes your last hits in levels 1-5: you are very weak early, so give CS.
  sources: [peng04]

## Overview

## Early lane

- She forces the choice: Q her or last-hit; stack first.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- She keeps you pinned: you cannot hold the wave, so call the jungler.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

## Mid lane

- After 6 she shoves and roams to snowball: match the shove late, stack under tower.
  sources: [shok-ahri]

- She coordinates dives with her jungler: a diver plus Charm beats cage from 6 on, so track it.
  sources: [starzall]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Veigar.E]

## Win condition

- She ends before your scaling matters: your power only grows, so survive.
  sources: [shok-ahri]
