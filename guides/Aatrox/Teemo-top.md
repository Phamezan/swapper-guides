## Key tips

- Keep the wave at or above the lane midpoint. A short lane starves his chase space after every short trade, which is exactly how he runs you down.
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: Short lane

- Start W only when his escape is gone. The pull into the Q combo is the kill pattern, and it fails whenever he still holds Move Quick ({cd:17:W}).
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: W when escape is down
  abilities: [Aatrox.W, Teemo.W]

- Take every post-6 all-in when he lacks Ignite. Your R healing wins the extended fight he cannot cut, so his summoner decides the fight before it starts.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: No-Ignite all-ins
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish him before boots. Q is nearly undodgeable on foot, so force trades in the first levels instead of conceding pressure you will never get back.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: Pre-boots punish
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

- Never start a short trade near his turret with your abilities down. He chases you down in that space with cooldowns to spare, so trade only with an exit already planned.
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: No turret trades
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Teemo.W]

## Mid lane

- Do not hand him the free Q-dodge window. Hold your casts until he commits to an auto exchange, then put the sweet spot where he stands instead of where he was.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: Cast on commit
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Teemo.Q]

- Farm through his poke and hold CS parity into level 6. He cannot match the R combo in a fair duel, so the level spike flips the lane math by itself.
  sources: [manco1]
  heading: Parity to six
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

## Win condition

- Win by landing the full rotation, not by poking. One clean three-cast combo decides the lane, so set it with W and finish it instead of bleeding him in pieces.
  sources: [persimannon, manco1]
  heading: Full rotation
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.W]

- Keep the lane short after 6. His kill pattern needs room to chase through W into Q, and a short lane plus swept exits leave him no angle to run you down.
  sources: [persimannon]
  heading: Deny chase room
  abilities: [Aatrox.W, Teemo.R]
