## Key tips

- Survive levels 1–3 inside his ranged poke, then take the strong trade window from level 3–4. One clean max-range Q can put him low enough to lose lane pressure entirely.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Level 3–4 window
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Jayce.Q]

- Never repeat small trades into his movement-speed proc. He breaks contact before Conqueror stacks matter, so wait out the speed, then commit to the longer fight.
  sources: [looca, dang366]
  heading: Wait out speed

- Treat levels 7–8 as his window, not yours. A clean all-in goes Conqueror Jayce's way there, so concede those two levels and retake the lane after.
  sources: [dang366]
  heading: Concede 7–8

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not bleed to Q-plus-auto poke before your first sustain. The chip sticks and makes the level 3–4 trade unwinnable, so give ground early to keep health for the window.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: No pre-trade chip
  abilities: [Jayce.Q]

- Respect his level-2 spike on the next minion. Do not walk into the EQ threat while he holds the level lead; the burst plus gate speed ends the lane on the spot.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Level-2 respect
  abilities: [Jayce.Q, Jayce.E]

- His early EQ is mana-heavy, so pressure right after he spends it. A Q fired into the wave leaves his best cooldown unavailable and his trade gutted.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Punish spent EQ
  abilities: [Jayce.Q]

## Mid lane

- Pull the wave and read his answer. If he stays in the settling wave instead of recalling, that is the kill window — he has accepted a fight on your terms.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Settling-wave read

- Spend the first-item lead only with the enemy jungler tracked. The strength gap means nothing if his jungler is waiting behind him, so ward before converting.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Tracked leads only

## Win condition

- Take side waves opposite him, then rotate to the team. When he shows on one side, pressure the other and cover mid only if your team leaves the wave unattended.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Opposite sides

- Blue-trinket the long lane before extending. An approaching Jayce spotted early is a free disengage; an unseen one is a collapsed flank.
  sources: [looca]
  heading: Trinket first

## Itemization

- Into his Stormraider's disengage, Electrocute makes your few short trades count. Take it when you expect no extended fight and must win the lane in bursts.
  sources: [dang366]
  heading: Electrocute option
