## Overview

## Early lane

- Force straight trades with your support. His burst loses them, so make him fight your fight from the first waves.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"

- Trade while his fourth shot is spent and give ground while he holds it. The loaded shot zones you off the wave even without firing.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Walk up when he wastes Deadly Flourish ({cd:202:W}) dry. Without the follow-up root he has no answer to your burst.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Mid lane

- Meet his objective groups and force the duel. His W-plus-R plan needs range and setup; your burst inside it wins.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Sweep the objective flanks for his traps and fog picks. Denied vision denies his casts, and he cannot set up what he cannot see.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Win through duels, not casts. Chase him off objectives with your dash rather than trading W-plus-R volleys at his range.
  sources: [yunfang]
  patch: "26.20"
  abilities: [Lucian.E]

- Break vision and stay mobile once his root connects. The instant Curtain Call shot after it is guaranteed unless you are already moving.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

## Itemization

- Build to duel. Your win is the 1v1 and the short trades he refuses, so spend for burst rather than poke.
  sources: [antiwis]
  patch: "26.21"
