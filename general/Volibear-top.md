## Key tips

- Use Volibear's early dueling strength to pressure trades and look for a kill lead.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- Level six adds a major duel and tower-dive option; use Stormbringer when the opponent is low and the dive is supported.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

- In late fights, use Stormbringer to get onto a backline target, then fall back to peel for allies.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep the wave even or just on your side when not ahead; the longer lane helps run the opponent down and reduces gank exposure.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- Use lane brush and Sky Splitter poke to make short trades safer before committing to a chase.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]
  abilities: [Volibear.E]

- The first item component boosts his damage and durability enough to take more frequent duels.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- Grievous Wounds reduces his healing and weakens his all-in and dive pattern, so respect enemy anti-heal timings.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

## Mid lane

- Split push around side objectives and challenge the champion sent to stop you.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- Avoid grouping when the side lane can pressure a nearby objective, but be ready to join when there is no objective to play toward.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- At level nine the first ability is maxed, improving damage and dueling.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- A second point in Stormbringer improves its damage and availability for team fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

## Win condition

- Group with the team and approach from the side or an unwarded brush to make the engage harder to answer.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- Do not remain split away from the team late; opponents can force a fight while Volibear is missing.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]

- Wait for Stormbringer before committing to a late fight; if it is down, delay and disengage.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]
  abilities: [Volibear.R]

- Expect Volibear to shift into a frontline and peel role as the game goes longer, rather than relying on him to duel everyone.
  sources: [mobalytics-volibear-top]
