## Key tips

- Survive a weak lane on spacing plus shield and heal value instead of forcing frequent all-ins.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Default to Heal and treat Ignite as the rare pick after aggressive builds were nerfed.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Win through roaming, disengage, peel, and playmaking while guarding allies with movement and control.
  sources: [1yanou]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hunt Q angles with little escape room, such as along a turret edge.
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.Q]

- Fleeing a gank, lay Q across the pursuer path to seal access rather than aiming at their body.
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.Q]

- Trust accurate Qs to make kills while accepting the practice they demand.
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.Q]

- Impair enemies with W slow or Q knock-up to shorten E cooldown through its movement-impair refund.
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.W, Janna.Q, Janna.E]

- Hold safe positioning always, since a caught Janna punishes fast.
  sources: [1yanou]

## Mid lane

- After a big crash under the enemy turret, spend the long window roaming mid or top or setting a jungler invade or gank.
  sources: [1yanou]

- After a small crash, take only a quick ward, jungle invade, or mid check, returning before the next bot wave.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Travel both ways in fog so enemies lose Janna tracks and misread ADC isolation.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Read the jungler spot and activity before picking any roam.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Treat Moonstone Renewer into Redemption and Monsoon as the later sustain spike for stronger healing.
  items: [6617, 3107]
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.R]

- Prefer Dawncore third for more enchanter scaling.
  items: [6621]
  sources: [1yanou]

## Win condition

- Respect the unreliable engage and the ally-wrecking side of Monsoon; aim every R knock with the landing in mind.
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.R]

- Scale on consistent shield and heal value over any early damage plan.
  sources: [1yanou]

## Itemization

- Pick Summon Aery over Arcane Comet for shorter-cooldown repeated poke plus stronger shields.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Take Manaflow Band for sustain and skip Axiom Arcanist in this setup.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Choose Celerity for movement speed over Transcendence haste, since Q and W impairment already recharges E.
  sources: [1yanou]
  abilities: [Janna.Q, Janna.W, Janna.E]

- Take Gathering Storm for the scaling plan, or Waterwalking when map movement matters more.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Pair Font of Life with Revitalize for ally healing and shields, preferring Revitalize over Bone Plating with safe positioning.
  sources: [1yanou]

- Start Dream Maker, swapping Celestial Opposition into heavy burst.
  items: [3870, 3869]
  sources: [1yanou]

- Rush Boots of Swiftness for movement over defensive or Ionian cooldown boots.
  items: [3009]
  sources: [1yanou]

- Core Moonstone Renewer into Redemption and Dawncore.
  items: [6617, 3107, 6621]
  sources: [1yanou]

- Add Mikael's Blessing third or fourth for its cleanse, Staff of Flowing Water with a fed AP ally, and Ardent Censer with attack-speed carries like Twitch, Jinx, or Vayne without rushing it.
  items: [3222, 6616, 3504]
  sources: [1yanou]

- Sit on Oblivion Orb for team anti-heal before finishing Morellonomicon past core; buy Mejai's only far ahead and safe, and Shurelya's Battlesong as a later situational pick.
  items: [3916, 3165, 2065]
  sources: [1yanou]
