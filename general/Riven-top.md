## Key tips

- Build the early game around pressure and a favorable wave, not a forced all-in: slow-push by hitting the wave slightly more than the opponent to earn a level edge, a safer trade, and a crash that enables a recall, plates, or first move.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Weave basic attacks between Q recasts and delay the third Q until near the end of its recast window so the sequence returns sooner after the combo.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q]

- For a major objective, buy and push the opposite side lane roughly one to two minutes before it spawns, then move over with a flank route instead of walking down the river into vision.
  sources: [xinkelol]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade when the opponent steps forward to last-hit, but weigh each exchange against losing your own minion; keep farming when the trade is not clearly worthwhile.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Track level-up timings at levels two, three, and six, holding the wave and Q timing so a new skill or the ultimate lands inside a prepared trade.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.R]

- Take fights closer to your own side of the lane and stop the trade before it extends under the enemy turret.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Start Doran's Blade, Health Potion, and Stealth Ward, with Long Sword plus Refillable Potion or Pickaxe as the early purchase options.
  sources: [xinkelol]

## Mid lane

- Use Riven's mobility to keep collecting side-lane waves for solo experience and gold, then rotate the moment a fight or objective turns valuable.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Never share a side wave unnecessarily: when several opponents vanish to answer the push, retreat unless the fight is winnable, and return before the wave reaches your own turret.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Concede the objective rather than walking into a poor fight when the side lane cannot create pressure or a numbers edge; Teleport, vision, or a pick can change that call.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Enter teamfights from fog or a flank toward the backline or another high-value target, coordinating with allied engage or peel instead of charging a grouped team.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Riven.E, Riven.R]

- Choose fights on advantage: more champions present, key enemy abilities unavailable, and both sides' summoner spells accounted for.
  sources: [xinkelol]

## Win condition

- Turn lane pressure from controlled waves and timed trades into side-lane experience, tower damage, or a flank at the next major objective.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Commit only on a favorable angle at a valuable target, then disengage or concede when enemy numbers and cooldowns make the fight poor.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.R]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Haste, Last Stand, Cash Back, and Jack of All Trades for extended fights and ability hits, starting Flash and Ignite.
  sources: [xinkelol]

- Build Axiom Arc into Endless Hunger and Death's Dance, then Lord Dominik's Regards, with a more defensive Death's Dance start when behind; max Q first, E second, and W last, taking R at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [xinkelol]
  abilities: [Riven.Q, Riven.W, Riven.E, Riven.R]
