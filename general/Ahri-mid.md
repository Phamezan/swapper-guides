## Key tips

- Keep trades short with auto and W combos for Electrocute, unless already holding a health lead.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- Hold Charm for an enemy misstep or an allied gank instead of throwing it blind.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- After level 6, shove for priority and use the extra R mobility to threaten fog picks and jungle ganks.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Aim Q through both the opponent and the wave to poke while clearing.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- Use W movement speed to hold spacing for a quick auto, W, auto exchange.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.W]

- At level 2, close a small gap with W before Q, then step back as Q returns instead of overextending.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q, Ahri.W]

- Cast Q while the opponent is locked into a last-hit, when their path is easiest to read.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- At level 3, add a second Q point for safer clearing or take Charm for gank and all-in setups, depending on the matchup.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q, Ahri.E]

- Put two ranks in Q to remove caster minions fast, opening a cheater recall or less time exposed to a bully.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.Q]

- At level 6, pair R mobility with jungle or support ganks against a low-mobility or Flash-less target.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

- After pushing at level 6, drift into fog to threaten sides or wait by a mid brush for an engage.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

## Mid lane

- Catch a side wave safely, and when opponents rotate over, clear it and leave with R while teammates play elsewhere.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

- Bring an engage ally such as Vi, Pantheon, or Xin Zhao to strengthen side-lane catches.
  sources: [peng04]

- After reaching the wave first, swing through a side brush for a 1v1 or 2v1 with the jungler, or stay hidden to build a numbers edge.
  sources: [peng04]

- When the enemy contests river vision while their side laner is still catching the wave, force the 4v3 before that player arrives.
  sources: [peng04]

- Hide teamfight approaches with Oracle Lens and Control Wards so Charm angles stay threatening.
  items: [3364]
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E]

- Fit the fight role to the draft: guard the ADC, flank a carry, or damage the first reachable target.
  sources: [peng04]

- Treat level 11 as a stronger pick window with the second R rank, but keep R ready when a dragon or Baron fight is near.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

## Win condition

- Turn mid priority from waveclear into hidden side picks or early objective numbers.
  sources: [peng04]

- Start fights with a fog Charm when the team needs engage, or save mobility to peel a carry against divers.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.E, Ahri.R]

- Skip long trades without an edge and never waste R right before a major objective.
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

## Itemization

- Run Electrocute with Taste of Blood, Grisly Mementos, and Ultimate Hunter, plus Manaflow Band and Transcendence, to match the short W and auto trade pattern.
  sources: [peng04]

- Start Doran's Ring with a Health Potion, then buy Lost Chapter early so mana stops limiting trades.
  items: [1056, 2003, 3802]
  sources: [peng04]

- Pick Malignance when Ahri is the main engage or the enemy is ranged, since its lower R cooldown feeds repeat picks; pick Blackfire Torch for heavier damage against melee teams or with allied setup.
  items: [3118, 2503]
  sources: [peng04]
  abilities: [Ahri.R]

- Add Lich Bane when autos can be woven safely and a side lane needs pressure.
  items: [3100]
  sources: [peng04]

- Take Cosmic Drive for mobility and spacing into melee, or Horizon Focus for repeated casts and flank vision, but skip buying both together.
  items: [4629, 4628]
  sources: [peng04]

- Use Rylai's as a cheaper utility pick into melee-heavy or low-mobility teams, or when behind.
  sources: [peng04]

- Take Cryptbloom with several AP allies or into stacked MR, Bloodletter's Curse for durability into melee and AP crowds, and Shadowflame with Sorcerer's Shoes to one-shot carries.
  items: [3137, 8010, 4645, 3020]
  sources: [peng04]

- Buy Rabadon's as the usual third-item spike, Cryptbloom or Void Staff into MR, and Zhonya's against assassins or heavy engage.
  items: [3137, 3135]
  sources: [peng04]

- Take Morellonomicon into heavy healing, with Orb as an early stop; pair Liandry's with Blackfire or long fights, and Banshee's into AP threats or one engage-blocking spell.
