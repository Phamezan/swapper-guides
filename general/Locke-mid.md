## Key tips

- Hold the wave near your side early instead of shoving, buying chase room and earlier gank vision.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]

- At level 6, spend the added R damage on kill pressure instead of staying passive.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

- Reach backlines through fog and W without crossing the map alone into unknown enemies.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play safely into levels and the first major spike; falling behind delays all-in threats.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

- Test matchups for extended fighting; without it, farm into the next power spike.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

- Against hard-to-kill lanes, roam strongly after level 6 to build pressure with allies.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]
  abilities: [Locke.R]

- Contest harder once the first component lands and Q points stack trade damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]
  abilities: [Locke.Q]

## Mid lane

- Pick lone movers with R, then spend the numbers on an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]
  abilities: [Locke.R]

- After bot first tower, leave the old lane for side pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

- With several items done, group and spend R area damage around tight terrain.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]
  abilities: [Locke.R]

- Wait out important enemy control before engaging, since hard lockdown ends Locke fast.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]

## Win condition

- Start snowballs on isolated, overextended squishies and turn picks into Baron or map objectives.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

- Never cross alone without enemy positions; one catch can donate Baron or end the game.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]

- Against grouped enemies, land R on several targets; his area damage favors grouped fights with a safe entry.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke]
  abilities: [Locke.R]

## Itemization

- Run Flash and Ignite with Electrocute, Sudden Impact, Sixth Sense, and Ultimate Hunter plus Bone Plating and Revitalize on adaptive-force and health shards.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]

- Order Lich Bane, Sorcerer's Shoes, Shadowflame, Zhonya's Hourglass, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Void Staff while maxing Q, then E, then W.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]
  abilities: [Locke.Q, Locke.E, Locke.W]

- Slot Banshee's Veil fourth defensively into burst or one key control spell.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]

- Keep the Stormraider's Surge alternative and the Conqueror off-meta setup as separate rune and build paths.
  sources: [mobalytics-locke-build]
