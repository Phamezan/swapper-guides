## Key tips

- Avoid fighting through the first three levels; look for an all-in only after Tahm has his basic tools.
  sources: [luffrey]

- At level three, use Abyssal Voyage to start a trade when the enemy is vulnerable and the carry can follow; add Tongue Lash and Thick Skin to finish the short fight safely.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W, Tahm Kench.Q, Tahm Kench.E]

- In a fight, use Abyssal Voyage to reach the enemy carry, then use Devour to hold the target while the team follows up.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W, Tahm Kench.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- The example combo is Abyssal Voyage, three attacks, Tongue Lash, three attacks, then Devour to finish the target.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W, Tahm Kench.Q, Tahm Kench.R]

- The author recommends a tank build because opponents can underestimate Tahm Kench's damage and durability.
  sources: [luffrey]

- Commit to the level-three engage only when the carry is positioned to join; the engage is much less useful without follow-up.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W]

## Mid lane

- In a team fight, either approach on foot before committing Abyssal Voyage or begin with it when it gives a reliable entry.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W]

- If the carry is unhelpful, roam and look for a two-versus-one opportunity on bot lane.
  sources: [luffrey]

- Use the normal item set of Heartsteel, Thornmail, Spirit Visage, and Unending Despair.
  sources: [luffrey]

- Against three or four ability-power enemies, start Null-Magic Mantle and Spectre's Cowl, then take Mercury's Treads and Spirit Visage before the main tank items.
  sources: [luffrey]

- Against three or four attack-damage enemies, start Cloth Armor and Bramble Vest, then take Plated Steelcaps before Heartsteel and Thornmail.
  sources: [luffrey]

## Win condition

- Tahm's level-three Abyssal Voyage engage relies on his durability and damage to threaten a kill when the carry follows.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.W]

- Hold Devour for the enemy carry during the fight and keep the target held while allies collapse.
  sources: [luffrey]
  abilities: [Tahm Kench.R]

## Itemization

- Take Grasp of the Undying, Shield Bash, Second Wind, Unflinching, Triumph, and Legend: Alacrity as the standard rune page.
  sources: [luffrey]

- Take Flash and Ignite as the listed summoner spells.
  sources: [luffrey]

- In the heavy attack-damage example, add Unending Despair, Spirit Visage, Dusk and Dawn, and Rabadon's Deathcap after the earlier armor and tank choices.
  sources: [luffrey]
