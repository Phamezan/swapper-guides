## Key tips

- On the opening walk, plant stored turrets for bush control, never where a cannon minion instantly kills them.
  sources: [sanygame]

- At level 6, threaten gank baits and 1v2s much harder with R, while still farming toward one or two items over every enemy roam.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.R]

- Push side waves behind the turret line, then roam or keep pressing to force an enemy answer.
  sources: [sanygame]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Farm while shoving opponents off minions, using the push for plates but checking turret and R cooldowns before river skirmishes.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.R]

- At level 2, take E to punish enemies entering turret range, or W for poke and clearing; never spend mana freely before the first mana item.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.E, Heimerdinger.W]

- At level 3, chunk opponents with E while keeping gank-path awareness to play the turret setup defensively.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.E]

- Answer enemy roams by shoving the wave and banking plates, while still checking early neutral fights with the jungler.
  sources: [sanygame]

## Mid lane

- Let turrets tank Dragon, Herald, or Baron, keeping R banked whenever enemies can contest.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.R]

- Break grouped enemies with the upgraded E multi-target stun before they reach the carries.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.E]

- Build standard pushes as a triangle with the first turret behind minions inside overlapping turret range.
  sources: [sanygame]

- Into strong poke, pull turrets nearer the tower so they survive clearing and cover safer farming.
  sources: [sanygame]

- Against linear skillshots, hide turrets inside minions so opponents cannot target them cleanly.
  sources: [sanygame]

## Win condition

- Turn lane pressure into plates and towers, then spend the room on Dragon, Herald, or Baron.
  sources: [sanygame]

- In teamfights, plant turrets around allies for space while E interrupts mobile threats and carries.
  sources: [sanygame]
  abilities: [Heimerdinger.E]

- Pull opponents sidelane off objectives with pushes, Teleporting into key fights or back to defend as needed.
  sources: [sanygame]

## Itemization

- Run the poke page of Arcane Comet, Manaflow Band, Absolute Focus, and Gathering Storm with Legend: Haste and Cut Down.
  sources: [sanygame]

- Recommend Blackfire Torch, Sorcerer's Shoes, Liandry's Torment, and Zhonya's Hourglass as the general items.
  sources: [sanygame]

- Swap First Strike in against melee-heavy lanes where it triggers reliably.
  sources: [sanygame]
