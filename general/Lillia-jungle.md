## Key tips

- Farm camps and experience first through a weaker start to reach level 6 quickly.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]

- Add ganks on pushed-up lanes through low-vision windows without abandoning full clears for low-percentage plays.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]

- Play every catch and skirmish around R, whose sleep opens the team follow-up window.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]
  abilities: [Lillia.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Contest neutral objectives with teammates when the map allows; objective control compounds into long-term advantage.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]

- After level 6, open ganks by landing Q for passive stacks into R sleep on the marked target.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia-build]
  abilities: [Lillia.Q, Lillia.R]

- Cast R ready to help allies finish kills, especially into several catchable enemies.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]
  abilities: [Lillia.R]

- Thread E through mid on the way back to jungle routes to threaten setups without detouring.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia-build]
  abilities: [Lillia.E]

## Mid lane

- Keep farming between plays so R hunting never tanks jungle tempo.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]

- Secure objectives with the team, spending successful picks on Baron or Dragon numbers.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]

## Win condition

- Stay with the team late and land R on an exposed squishy or a clustered group.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]
  abilities: [Lillia.R]

- Flank toward the backline but hold arrival range so the fight start never beats Lillia there.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]

- Refuse forced fights with R down and wait for the next strong engage window.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]
  abilities: [Lillia.R]

- Ping before multi-target R commits so teammates hold damage until the sleep lands.
  sources: [mobalytics-lillia]
  abilities: [Lillia.R]
