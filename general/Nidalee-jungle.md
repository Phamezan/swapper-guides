## Key tips

- Default to a full clear and only invade with information: have mid ward the enemy Raptors or Red, confirm the enemy start, and check that nearby lanes have priority before committing.
  sources: [tahe]

- Reach the objective area ahead of the spawn, seed traps in the approach bushes and choke points, and throw spears from fog instead of walking into the enemy team first.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.W, Nidalee.Q]

- A landed spear is not an engage order on its own: Nidalee is strongest in the midgame, so only chase the poke when the resulting fight favors your whole team.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- On the loading screen, pick the lane to path toward, judge whether an invade fits the team scaling, and decide which build suits that game before minions spawn.
  sources: [tahe]

- Ask mid to ward the enemy Raptors or Red so the route can bend toward the buff the enemy jungler is likely to clear toward; Red helps the duel but is not mandatory.
  sources: [tahe]

- Confirm allied lane priority before entering: with nearby support you can stay, without it leave quickly, and never treat one good invade read as permission to live in the enemy jungle.
  sources: [tahe]

- Speed the clear with animation cancels such as auto-Q in cougar form and quick heal and trap transforms; open damage with a long spear or kite into a closer spear, then finish in cougar form.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.Q, Nidalee.W, Nidalee.E, Nidalee.R]

- When a target crosses a trap, use the movement window to kite or reposition before swapping forms and committing.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.W]

## Mid lane

- Trap the likely approach paths before each objective contest and hold a vision edge so spears fly from fog while Nidalee stays out of reach.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.W, Nidalee.Q]

- Treat the midgame as the strongest window: soften opponents with poke first, then contest rather than forcing even-health fights.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.Q]

- Guard against tunnel vision after a spear connects: reassess numbers, health, and positioning before chasing, and let the poke go unanswered when the fight looks bad.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.Q]

- In late skirmishes, layer traps, spears, and heals on allies, and retarget to whichever enemy gives the easiest safe spear angle instead of fixating on one champion.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.W, Nidalee.Q, Nidalee.E]

## Win condition

- Convert poke into contested objectives: arrive early, trap the chokes, soften from fog, and only commit when the teamfight that follows favors your side.
  sources: [tahe]
  abilities: [Nidalee.W, Nidalee.Q]

- Keep Zhonya's Hourglass as the deliberate exception: an engage is acceptable when its active baits the enemy commitment on your terms, not as a default way to start.
  items: [3157]
  sources: [tahe]

## Itemization

- Lock the build choice during the loading-screen assessment, matching it to the invade plan and the scaling comparison rather than defaulting blindly.
  sources: [tahe]

- Value Zhonya's Hourglass as the one engage-enabling purchase: its active turns a baited enemy commitment into Nidalee's fight window.
  items: [3157]
  sources: [tahe]
