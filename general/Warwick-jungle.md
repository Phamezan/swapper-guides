## Key tips

- Open Red Buff, Krugs, and Raptors from either side for a flexible level-three window, then decide between a real side-lane gank, a mid gank, or a full clear by checking which lane shows an exposed target before finishing Raptors.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

- Interrupt the clear or skip a camp for a genuinely good gank or countergank, using Blood Hunt to respond fast, but never force low-odds ganks into safe, healthy, or highly mobile opponents.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]
  abilities: [Warwick.W]

- Sequence ganks as activate E, hold Q through the target, then reactivate E to fear them toward allies; after the ultimate ends, chain a held Q to follow a Flashing enemy.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]
  abilities: [Warwick.E, Warwick.Q, Warwick.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hold Q during ganks when the enemy may escape: the quick bite finishes a stationary target while the held Q follows through displacement or Flash.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]
  abilities: [Warwick.Q]

- Time the leap through incoming control spells, since Warwick is crowd-control immune during R flight, and use that engage to secure the pick.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]
  abilities: [Warwick.R]

- Lean on the sustain to recover while clearing and to attempt early dragon, remembering that the Cloud Drake fights back harder at low levels than the slower-attacking Mountain Drake.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

## Mid lane

- Favor playing around bot when engage supports are common, but skip the forced play whenever team compositions lack reliable setup.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

- Keep a control ward active where possible and carry one toward Baron or dragon when the team is not already covering that vision.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

- Treat early dragon as a possible solo objective on Warwick's sustain and duel strength, weighing the drake type before committing.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

## Win condition

- Warwick's sustain and dueling are the objective tools: convert picks and vision around Baron and dragon into secured calls rather than rushed attempts.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

- Arrive first at the next dragon or Baron setup with the control ward already placed, using Blood Hunt's response speed to turn the pick into an uncontested start.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]
  abilities: [Warwick.W]

## Itemization

- Run Press the Attack with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, and Coup de Grace to burst targets toward the low-health thresholds, with Magical Footwear and Cosmic Insight plus attack speed, adaptive force, and scaling health shards.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]

- Keep the Press the Attack burst setup while rechecking current shop numbers before locking purchases; rune and item numbers here are a patch 26.14 snapshot.
  sources: [chemtechpuppy]
