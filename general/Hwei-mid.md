## Key tips

- Plan every trade around mana, E availability against retaliation, and the resulting wave; never spend clearing and reset mana.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.E]

- Pair damaging spells for Signature of the Visionary, worth most against grouped enemies.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- Skip split-pushing by default with low solo-kill pressure and weak turret damage; his teamfight value runs far higher.
  sources: [softheadpats]

## Overview

## Early lane

- In most ranged lanes, start Q and poke QQ away from the wave or near minions for area connection, or QE for instant priority.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q]

- Starting E unlocks EE poke through minions at the cost of lane priority.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.E]

- Into aggressive melee champions, open E for an EQ interrupt, hold E defensively, and keep the wave near center.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q, Hwei.E]

- At level 2, pick Q or E for damage combos or W for empowered autos and mana recovery, usually favoring Q or E without WE-less spam.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q, Hwei.E, Hwei.W]

- At level 3, unlock the last school and run the full spellbook, with later Q ranks adding damage.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q]

- Pressure opponents off last hits when safe, or neutralize hard lanes and farm from range while scaling.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- Clear fast with QE into WE into EE only when the exposed aftermath stays acceptable.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q, Hwei.W, Hwei.E]

- Freeze short to farm safely when priority runs low; ahead freezes zone but should usually fund team help instead.
  sources: [softheadpats]

## Mid lane

- Build slow pushes for long recall, roam, or turret-harass windows while respecting the higher gank risk.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- Build fast pushes for recall, dive, roam, or river windows, skipping them into unbreakable enemy freezes.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- Carry priority into first vision and jungle help, hovering outside enemy vision so opponents must respect a fake roam.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- Commit to side ganks only on highly likely plays, since Hwei trails roaming specialists there.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- Collect side waves only inside vision cover, tracking timers and rotating to objectives and fights on spawn.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- With Teleport, set up opposite the next objective; without it, hold the objective side for a prompt join.
  sources: [softheadpats]

## Win condition

- Control space and movement with area effects first, then punish anyone stepping wrong.
  sources: [softheadpats]

- In fights, deal QQ single-target damage, QE area slow and zoning, and EE passive triggers across several enemies.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.Q, Hwei.E]

- Keep E banked for defense and spend WQ or WW to move allies or help them absorb damage.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.E, Hwei.W]

- Land R with allied control or tighter terrain against its slow flight, aiming vulnerable carries first.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.R]

- Never hold R forever; its delayed blast dodges easily but the wide slow and disruption still pay even onto a frontline target.
  sources: [softheadpats]
  abilities: [Hwei.R]

- Vary spell picks by fight, protect the immobile mage first, then punish low or separated targets.
  sources: [softheadpats]
