## Key tips

- Open aggressively red side through Raptors, Krugs, then Red, saving Smite for Red into a level-3 gank.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Hold first-reset items before contesting the first Void Grubs spawn.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Peak around the first finished item, dip through mid game, then rise late; refuse low-value fights in the weak stretch.
  sources: [mo100z]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Invade after the three-camp open only when the enemy jungler is weak early and vision or late laners reveal their start.
  sources: [mo100z]

- After a top kill, recall and return to bot-side camps; after a mid kill, run Wolves, Gromp, and Blue.
  sources: [mo100z]

- After a bot play, reset before the Blue side so camps are never entered low on health and items.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Without a play, pick the invade-or-farm call from enemy jungler information and lane priority.
  sources: [mo100z]

## Mid lane

- Prize the second Void Grubs spawn, since level 6 opens a longer Voidling window; trade a lost first spawn for Dragon and prepare the second.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Bend items to the threats: the Jax, Akali, Xin Zhao, Varus, and Janna example runs Kraken Slayer into Titanic Hydra and Jak'Sho, adding Kaenic Rookern and Frozen Heart once Varus and Akali feed.
  items: [6672, 3748, 2504, 3110]
  sources: [mo100z]

- Spend gold before Dragon and arrive early so the team sets vision and position first.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Give Dragon when the top laner cannot move and enemy top rotates; spend the time on waves and towers instead of a losing contest.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Before Dragon or Baron, push mid and plant vision so the enemy must answer the wave while the team sets up.
  sources: [mo100z]

## Win condition

- Convert early level-3 pressure into camps, objectives, and evolution marks instead of chasing without a map payoff.
  sources: [mo100z]

- In late fights, target the main damage dealer or the enemy holding a needed form coral, depending on the fight.
  sources: [mo100z]

- After Baron, split between side pressure and grouping mid on the empowered wave, hunting picks as opponents answer.
  sources: [mo100z]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, and Coup de Grace, plus Magical Footwear and Cosmic Insight.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Take Flash and Smite, with Gustwalker as the selected jungle pet.
  sources: [mo100z]

- Open Kraken Slayer into Guinsoo's Rageblade, then choose among Death's Dance, Wit's End, and Guardian Angel around enemy damage and control.
  items: [6672, 3124, 6333, 3091, 3026]
  sources: [mo100z]

- Use Titanic Hydra and Jak'Sho for the durable route, adapting every defensive seat to the enemy composition.
  items: [3748]
