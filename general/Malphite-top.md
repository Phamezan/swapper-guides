## Key tips

- Absorb short trades on the passive shield, then back off cleanly until it recharges off damage.
  sources: [raen]

- Treat R as the entire hard engage; without it available, no other hard control exists.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R]

- Build enough defense to live after R entries instead of assuming the knock-up alone wins fights.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Slow targets with Q while stealing their movement speed for follow-up or disengage windows.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]

- Open trades from range with Q, then ride the stolen movement speed for better spacing.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.Q]

- Trade inside the W armor window, tripled while the passive shield holds.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.W]

- Answer committing auto attackers with E for its attack-speed cut.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.E]

- Seek auto-attack-heavy and AD opponents as favorable lanes, leaning on W armor there.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.W]

- Dodge extended exchanges into AP-heavy opponents while defenses stay mostly armor.
  sources: [raen]

## Mid lane

- Aim R at grouped targets to cash its area knock-up as teamfight initiation.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R]

- Follow R engages with E for the attack-speed cut, then W empowered attacks inside range.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R, Malphite.E, Malphite.W]

- Bank R for approaching objective fights over low-value solo plays.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R]

## Win condition

- Start team fights with R, then stay close to spread E attack-speed cuts across enemy carries.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R, Malphite.E]

- Prioritize AD and frequent basic attackers when choosing engage spots and targets.
  sources: [raen]

- Refuse forced fights with R down whenever the team needs his initiation.
  sources: [raen]
  abilities: [Malphite.R]
