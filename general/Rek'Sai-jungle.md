## Key tips

- Open Raptors, Krugs, and Red, look for a level-three gank, then take the blue-side Scuttle or Blue by timer and finish through Gromp; accept running about two camps behind a double full-clear for the earlier map pressure.
  sources: [lordgrox]

- Pick ganks by win condition, objective access, killability, and scoreboard state: check enemy escapes and mobility, allied follow-up crowd control, and self-peel before spending the tunnel engage.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.E]

- Time R to avoid high-impact damage or control with its brief untargetability rather than saving it only as a finisher, and recast W during its travel to arrive burrowed with an instant knock-up ready.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.R, Rek'Sai.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Run Hail of Blades for the quick-burst empowered E sequence, and switch to Press the Attack or Conqueror when the fight continues past the opening combo.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.E]

- Take Blue Smite for its movement speed and playmaking access on the early gank route.
  sources: [lordgrox]

- Never precommit to one lane before the match develops; let the win condition and objective side choose the gank after the first clear.
  sources: [lordgrox]

## Mid lane

- Treat tunnels as both engage and escape: save E tunnels when a gank or skirmish is likely, and only lay utility tunnels while moving through quiet areas.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.E]

- Track Grounded effects before choosing a route, since they block Tunnel, R, and Flash alike.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.E, Rek'Sai.R]

- While burrowed, respect the compressed senses: Nearsight shrinks the already reduced sight radius, Soraka's silence stops the burrowed knock-up, and Teemo's blind denies the auto-attack Fury generation the combo needs.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.W]

- Drill camp kiting and combo execution in Practice Tool, learning how far each camp can be pulled before it resets; that pull distance is the guide's core clear-speed skill.
  sources: [lordgrox]

## Win condition

- Use R's travel to expose certain invisible targets as a situational team reveal, not as a replacement for real vision setup.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.R]

- Arrive at the decisive fight burrowed and ready: R in, knock up with W immediately, and let the team follow rather than opening unburrowed into control.
  sources: [lordgrox]
  abilities: [Rek'Sai.R, Rek'Sai.W]

- For jungle macro beyond fixed routes, study early-game paths and enemy camp tracking through play or coaching, since no written page covers every game state.
  sources: [lordgrox]

## Itemization

- Choose the identity first: bruiser trades some burst for health and skirmish durability, while the crit path makes picks deadlier but leaves Rek'Sai vulnerable to crowd control.
  sources: [lordgrox]

- Build around Titanic Hydra as the common core, taking Spear of Shojin first on bruiser when the game fits; the listed bruiser core adds Sterak's Gage, Death's Dance, and Overlord's Bloodmail as a May 2026 snapshot.
  items: [3748, 3161, 3053, 6333, 2501]
  sources: [lordgrox]
