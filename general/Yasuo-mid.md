## Key tips

- Trade behind the passive shield and deny opponents free pops: avoid positions where they remove it with poke while taking no return trade.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Balance early aggression against ganks: pressuring and shoving is fine, but drawing jungle attention only helps when the play survives it.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- When carrying, wait for major enemy crowd control to be spent before entering, blunting the return damage with Wind Wall; otherwise follow up the engage and start with Last Breath only when teammates can capitalize.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W, Yasuo.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Build lane priority and place control wards around Scuttle to help the jungler win the early river fight.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Track the tornado stacks and never force an all-in just after the knock-up expires, when the engage and ultimate setup are gone.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.Q, Yasuo.R]

- Step through minions with E before committing to the champion, building dash damage and keeping chase options open.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.E]

- Skip roaming for its own sake: favor lane pressure, plates, and solo kills, diving side lanes mainly when the jungler can help.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

## Mid lane

- Once the first towers fall around twelve to fifteen minutes, move to a side lane to collect gold and use the dueling strength against mages.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Without Teleport, take the side lane nearest the next objective and let the top laner with Teleport cover the opposite side.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Push the side wave toward the enemy tier-two tower, then take nearby enemy camps for experience while denying it to the other team.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Keep control wards down and clear enemy vision while pressuring the side lane, so the objective join never walks into unseen opponents.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

## Win condition

- When a fed ally carries the main damage and Yasuo is behind, peel instead: block threats with Wind Wall and knock up enemies that reach the carry.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- In disruption duty, save Wind Wall for high-value projectiles or ultimates and buy time with repeated knock-ups for allied damage dealers.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W, Yasuo.Q]

- When ahead, contest vision around Baron and force the fight from that control; when behind, defend the objective and deny enemy vision rather than taking an isolated fight.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Blade into melee lanes and Doran's Shield with Second Wind into ranged poke; run Lethal Tempo, Absorb Life, Legend: Alacrity, Cut Down, Second Wind, and Overgrowth.
  items: [1055, 1054]
