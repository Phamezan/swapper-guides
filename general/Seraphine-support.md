## Key tips

- Leave spawn promptly to reach the bot-side brush before opponents; if delayed against a hook threat, ward over the wall instead of face-checking.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- Use Beat Drop on an already-slowed target to root it, or on a rooted target to stun it; set up the target's status before casting.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.E]

- Remember that Encore can extend its reach by passing through allied or enemy champions, allowing more flexible casts into a clustered fight.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Ask the lane partner to coordinate an engage chain, such as their slow followed by Seraphine's root or stun.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.E]

- Poke the enemy carry with spells rather than spending all lane pressure on the opposing support.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- If the enemy support catches you or your partner with crowd control, answer by aiming Beat Drop and Surround Sound at their carry to discourage the follow-up.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.E, Seraphine.Q, Seraphine.W]

- Encourage the lane partner to save wave-clear spells when using them on minions would waste mana and miss a useful trade window.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- Hold mid lane as a default position so Seraphine can rotate toward whichever side of the map needs her.
  sources: [scottysseus]

## Mid lane

- Poke with Surround Sound before a team fight instead of waiting until the fight has already started.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.Q, Seraphine.W]

- Use the build's crowd control and poke to soften targets before the team commits to the engage.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- Take the Encore angle through a champion when a direct cast is blocked, so the extended reach still finds the grouped targets.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.R]

## Win condition

- Favor damage and crowd control over a dedicated healing-and-shielding setup, to contribute more directly to picks.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- Follow a successful Beat Drop root or stun with team damage, and use Encore to swing a clustered fight rather than casting it without setup.
  sources: [scottysseus]
  abilities: [Seraphine.E, Seraphine.R]

- After a won fight, keep lanes pushed toward enemy territory so the team can convert its advantage into towers or inhibitors.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- Keep the team together after a fight; splitting off for camps or shopping gives away the initiative.
  sources: [scottysseus]

- Adapt to teammates instead of assuming they will coordinate perfectly: assess risk before following a risky play, while still helping with vision, roams, and objectives.
  sources: [scottysseus]
