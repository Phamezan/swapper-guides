- Reach level 3 fast with a strong leash, then gank a lane with a clear approach using Q.

- Once R unlocks, prioritize neutral-objective fights and coordinate bot lane help to secure them.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.R]

- Favor choke-point fights where Q lands and controls approaches; never chase mobile champions across open ground.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hide in the Scuttle lane brush to ambush the opposing jungler with Q.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q]

- Recover through clears with jungle passive sustain.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]

- Land Q and W control in ganks and skirmishes, since alone fights hinge on those spells connecting.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.Q, Cho'Gath.W]

- Treat early ganking as situational: the game plan wants level-3 ganks, while the spike section says farm to level 6.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]

## Mid lane

- Finish core items promptly to empower whichever damage or durability route the game needs.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]

- Flank teamfights to reach the backline and cut exposed carries off from their frontline.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]

- Spend R on an accessible high-value target when it removes that carry from the fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.R]

- Max E by level 9 and pair its sustained damage with Q on controlled targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.E, Cho'Gath.Q]

- At level 11, press the second R rank as another kill-pressure spike.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.R]

## Win condition

- Flex between damage and teammate protection based on whether Cho'Gath runs ahead or behind.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]

- Attend neutral-objective fights consistently, using R for both control and champion kills.
  sources: [mobalytics-chogath]
  abilities: [Cho'Gath.R]

- At level 16, finish enemies the team catches with the final R rank.
