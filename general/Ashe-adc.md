## Key tips

- Control the wave and vision before stepping up to trade, since Ashe has no mobility escape.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]

- Kite with passive slows and hold enough distance to make up for the missing escape.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]

- Fire R only when allies stand ready to act on the stun.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Slow a target with an attack before a close-range R, since the shorter reaction window lands the stun more reliably.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.R]

- When lane pressure gets hard, freeze and farm safely while scaling toward the later timing.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]

- Send E ahead before pushing or rotating to an objective; vision that finds a kill can also add an assist.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.E]

## Mid lane

- Use E to track enemy rotations so the team never walks blind into an unseen move.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.E]

- Open plays with long-range R for teammates, then stay positioned to follow with slows and steady attacks.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.R]

## Win condition

- Use R to enable teammates or rescue a fight rather than spending it with no follow-up.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.R]

- Scout with E before a push or objective, then catch a priority target with R for the team to convert.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
  abilities: [Ashe.E, Ashe.R]

- After scaling, stay with the team and deal sustained damage from safety instead of chasing past the frontline.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-ashe]
