## Key tips

- Treat early game as the weak phase: frequent spell use drains mana fast, with one full level-three combo costing roughly half the pool, so avoid forcing trades without a reason.
  sources: [zileanaire]

- Look for early kills mainly with jungler help or when an opponent makes a major mistake; otherwise keep the lane controlled, defending well under turret especially once level-six Chronoshift can deter a dive.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.R]

- Aim Chronoshift at the teammate most likely to carry the fight, usually waiting until the ally is close to dying but casting earlier when several opponents collapse and a late cast would not land.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Max by lane need: Q max gives more poke, damage, and longer stuns into lanes where ranged poke can chip opponents down or force bad recalls, while E max gives stronger slows where locking down movement matters more.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.Q, Zilean.E]

- When maxing Time Bomb first, max Rewind next; when maxing Time Warp first, max Rewind second.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.Q, Zilean.E, Zilean.W]

- Run Summon Aery with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, Scorch, Deep Ward, and Relentless Hunter, with Flash and Heal.
  sources: [zileanaire]

## Mid lane

- Grow influential after level six with an item or two, when the crowd control and revive can create or deny plays across the map.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.R]

- Speed allies toward vision, a roam, an objective, or a return from base with Time Warp, and slow an enemy to limit escape or engagement.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.E]

- Stay at the back of fights so Zilean can speed allies or slow and stun enemies without being caught by crowd control.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.E, Zilean.Q]

- Land Redemption from range to heal a fight or support a retreat, timing it shortly after Chronoshift revives an ally for extra sustain.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.R]

- Pair Shurelya's Battlesong with Time Warp to accelerate an engage, chase, retreat, or objective rotation.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.E]

- Hold Mikael's Blessing for a teammate caught by crowd control, remembering it cannot remove crowd control from Zilean himself.
  sources: [zileanaire]

## Win condition

- Accept an even lane as a favorable outcome: Zilean scales into a strong mid- and late-game control role for a team-dependent enabler.
  sources: [zileanaire]

- When an ally revives in the middle of enemies, double-bomb their position so the stun disrupts opponents clustering around them.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.Q]

- Guard Zilean's own position: dying early to crowd control costs the team both the control spells and the ultimate threat.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.R]

## Itemization

- Build the utility core of Solstice Sleigh, Ionian Boots of Lucidity, Bandlepipes, Redemption, and Shurelya's Battlesong, prioritizing ability haste throughout since a haste-focused Zilean can see the ultimate return during a long fight.
  sources: [zileanaire]
  abilities: [Zilean.R]

- Read the author as broadly useful on Solstice Sleigh, strong on Bandlepipes as a team buff, and early-defensive on Locket of the Iron Solari; add Morellonomicon with an early Oblivion Orb when enemy healing is significant, staying flexible across tank, mage, enchanter, or utility picks by game.
  sources: [zileanaire]
