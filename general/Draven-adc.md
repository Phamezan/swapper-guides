## Key tips

- Keep Spinning Axes spinning and catch every return, holding up to two axes at once.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.Q]

- Punish the enemy marksman with W plus two axe hits around cannon minions, then freeze when the pressure forces them back.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.W]

- Cash banked Adoration stacks through champion kills and avoid risky deaths before the likely payout.
  sources: [granmuhh]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Steer axes by moving toward the wanted spot just before an attack lands, drilling one axe first and then two.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.Q]

- Press S to stop on a marker for tighter placement and a controlled next attack, especially near towers and in teamfights.
  sources: [granmuhh]

- Force the back-or-starve choice with health pressure: freeze the wave when the opponent backs, or threaten kills when they stay to farm.
  sources: [granmuhh]

- Run the Double Moon engage by hitting a minion with Q, stopping to catch, then W-ing on the next marker so the catch refreshes W for the chase.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.Q, Draven.W]

- Run Sleight of Hand from a passive farm by casting E onto the opponent while walking to a marker, then pursuing on the refreshed W through the wave.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.E, Draven.W]

- Trade simply with auto, E, auto to fold Stand Aside into an axe exchange.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.E]

- Drill Q-only last-hitting toward 100 CS by 10 minutes with no items as the axe-control benchmark.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.Q]

## Mid lane

- When early pressure fails or the support matchup gives little, farm on and guard Adoration stacks instead of forcing bad fights.
  sources: [granmuhh]

- Remember that death halves Adoration stacks while kills convert them to gold.
  sources: [granmuhh]

- While behind, farm toward one high-value fight and cash stored stacks on a high-bounty takedown.
  sources: [granmuhh]

## Win condition

- Turn lane damage and clean catches into a takedown so stored Adoration becomes bonus gold.
  sources: [granmuhh]

- Keep catches reliable in fights, stopping with S when useful to preselect the next target first.
  sources: [granmuhh]

- Aim Whirling Death at a valuable target whose return finishes them, securing the return on a high-gold enemy.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.R]

- Break up a pursuer with Stand Aside, then resume speed and attack rate with Blood Rush after an axe catch.
  sources: [granmuhh]
  abilities: [Draven.E, Draven.W]
