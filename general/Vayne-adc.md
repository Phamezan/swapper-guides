## Key tips

- Space every exchange and never open at maximum range: use Q to dodge incoming pressure, and save the first real all-in window for level six when R arrives.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.Q, Vayne.R]

- Plan the recall before the level-six spike so the return buys an attack-damage component plus an attack-speed item, arriving ready to fight with the ultimate.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.R]

- Learn the R-Q fadeaway and keep movement unpredictable: stealth the roll so its direction stays hidden instead of rolling toward the opponent's expected aim.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.Q, Vayne.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take the short level-one trade since Q resets the basic-attack timer, but treat it as a small exchange rather than an all-in attempt.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.Q]

- Run Presence of Mind because the small mana pool otherwise leaves Vayne unable to cast after E trades; the displayed summoners are Barrier and Flash.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.E]

- Adjust runes by lane: Bone Plating into all-in pressure, Second Wind into poke, usually Overgrowth after, or Revitalize when extra shielding and healing matter.
  sources: [spook8]

- Play the standard page of Lethal Tempo, Presence of Mind, Legend: Alacrity, Cut Down, Cash Back, and Triple Tonic, with attack speed, adaptive force, and health shards.
  sources: [spook8]

## Mid lane

- Use R stealth on Q to reposition mid-fight, keeping the roll direction unreadable while the fight moves.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.Q, Vayne.R]

- Combine Flash with E for a surprise Condemn stun when the wall collision can decide a fight already in progress.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.E]

## Win condition

- Stay healthy and resourced through early lane into level six with an item component, then use the ultimate's combat window to force the all-in.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.R]

- Time attacks with Q and control targets with E when a stun angle appears, keeping the Q stealth movement and Condemn threat as the payoff for the patient lane.
  sources: [spook8]
  abilities: [Vayne.Q, Vayne.E]

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Bow and build the crit line of Yun Tal Wildarrows, Infinity Edge, Gluttonous Greaves, Phantom Dancer, Lord Dominik's Regards, and Bloodthirster, with Guardian Angel as the late option.
  items: [1086, 3032, 3031, 3008, 3046, 3036, 3072, 3026]
  sources: [spook8]

- Vary by game with Mortal Reminder for armor penetration and Randuin's Omen, Kaenic Rookern, Immortal Shieldbow, Edge of Night, or Mercurial Scimitar for defense; prefer Legend: Alacrity with Gluttonous Greaves and Bloodline on other boot paths, with Cut Down, Coup de Grace, or Last Stand as viable final-row picks.
  items: [3033, 3143, 2504, 6673, 3814, 3139, 3008]
  sources: [spook8]
