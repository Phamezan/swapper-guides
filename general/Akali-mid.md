## Key tips

- Accept a weaker lane before level 6, with the most early pressure available into melee opponents.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Treat level 6 plus the first finished item as the moment to fight for objectives and kills.
  sources: [janlon99]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

- Value mid game highly, since carries often still lack magic resistance at that stage.
  sources: [janlon99]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Start Doran's Shield into poke or burst lanes to survive the early stretch.
  items: [1054]
  sources: [janlon99]

- Start Doran's Ring with two potions into melee lanes without poke for a more aggressive edge.
  items: [1056]
  sources: [janlon99]

- Take Flash and Ignite as the standard summoner pair.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Pick Electrocute into squishy teams for burst, or Conqueror for longer trades into bruisers and tanks.
  sources: [janlon99]

## Mid lane

- Use the level-6 and first-item windows to contest objectives and hunt kills.
  sources: [janlon99]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

- Turn one successful roam or teamfight into recovery from a bad lane, since a kill can start a gold and damage snowball.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Spend mobility and shroud on escaping ganks and repositioning inside fights.
  sources: [janlon99]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

## Win condition

- Reach squishy targets with burst around the level-6 and first-item spikes, using the ultimate execute.
  sources: [janlon99]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

- Aim for the backline in teamfights, but wait out dangerous crowd control and engage cooldowns before committing.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Refuse tanky, magic-resistance-heavy teams as burst targets, since her damage falls off sharply into them.
  sources: [janlon99]

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror for extended fights and bruiser or tank crowds, with Presence of Mind for energy, Legend: Tenacity, and Coup de Grace.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Take Electrocute for squishy teams or when allies handle tanks, with Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, and Ultimate Hunter.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Pick Resolve by matchup: Second Wind into poke, Bone Plating into close-range burst, Unflinching when crowd control matters, Overgrowth for extra health otherwise.
  sources: [janlon99]

- Follow the displayed standard build of Hextech Gunblade into Sorcerer's Shoes, Shadowflame, Zhonya's Hourglass, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Void Staff.
  items: [3146, 3020, 4645, 3157, 3089, 3135]
  sources: [janlon99]
