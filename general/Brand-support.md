## Overview

## Early lane

- At level 3, sequence the full Q, W, and E rotation for the first complete trade.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]
  abilities: [Brand.Q, Brand.W, Brand.E]

- Watch mana through repeated trades, since running dry ends all pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Poke from range to finish the support warding quest faster.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Stay out of extended spots without vision; Brand cannot move away from ganks and crowd control.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Respect enemy poke as a squishy target and play calmer while Flash stays down.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

## Mid lane

- Before teamfights, keep poking with W and commit only after opponents lose health.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]
  abilities: [Brand.W]

- Hold vision around mid and wherever the bot duo currently plays.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Stick with the ADC through mid game, since either half of the duo dies alone.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Value the first component of the first full item plus extra W ranks as stronger poke.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]
  abilities: [Brand.W]

- At level 9, press frequent max-rank W lanes and build kills for Brand or the ADC.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]
  abilities: [Brand.W]

- As teams group, spread area damage across as many enemies as positioning allows.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

## Win condition

- Keep a safe firing position so damage never stops; getting caught ends all contribution.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Never place late vision alone, since one support pick converts into enemy objective numbers.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Set Dragon and Baron vision to manufacture picks and safe objective takes.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Into defensive buyers, target squishies or the least protected enemies first.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]

- Expect bigger grouped-fight damage at the level-11 and level-16 ultimate ranks, but only with survival to cast them.
  sources: [mobalytics-brand]
  abilities: [Brand.R]
