## Overview

## Early lane

- Watch mana closely, since short cooldowns invite overspending through repeated casts.
  sources: [mobalytics-cassiopeia]

- At level 2, force an early all-in window when the lane partner can follow it up.
  sources: [mobalytics-cassiopeia]

- At level 3, press the clearer spike with all basic abilities online.
  sources: [mobalytics-cassiopeia]

- Turn the first finished item into stronger trades, using landed Q plus repeated E for a big health lead.
  sources: [mobalytics-cassiopeia]
  abilities: [Cassiopeia.Q, Cassiopeia.E]

## Mid lane

- Take a side lane for experience and gold whenever no teamfight brews.

- Flank before teamfights to open a stronger R angle.

  abilities: [Cassiopeia.R]

- Favor tight terrain where R catches several opponents, or lock down one key target with it.

  abilities: [Cassiopeia.R]

- Stack Tear of the Goddess by mid game to ease mana through sustained casting.
  sources: [mobalytics-cassiopeia]

- At level 9, convert maxed E into heavier follow-up once Q lands.
