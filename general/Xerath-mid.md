## Key tips

- Prioritize last hits over poke: take poke only when it costs no farm, and recover mana with passive autos, preferring safe champion hits for the larger return.
  sources: [hofibauer]

- Recall toward a twelve-hundred gold first buy for Lost Chapter; when forced back earlier, take Dark Seal to begin collecting stacks.
  items: [3802, 1082]
  sources: [hofibauer]

- In team fights, stay behind the frontline and poke low-health carries before using the ultimate to start or finish the fight, reaching the longer level-sixteen sequence to pressure isolated carries into defensive summoners or kills.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Xerath.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open Doran's Ring with two Health Potions as the standard setup.
  items: [1056]
  sources: [hofibauer]

- At level six, look for nearby kills and objective fights with Rite of the Arcane while tracking the opposing mid laner.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Xerath.R]

## Mid lane

- Protect the mid tower with safe waveclear; the first mid tower gives important map control.
  sources: [hofibauer]

- When another lane takes mid and Xerath moves to a side lane, clear waves on his own side of the map and rotate back toward mid.
  sources: [hofibauer]

- Never roam alone through river while the enemy mid and jungler are missing; Xerath alone there is an easy catch.
  sources: [hofibauer]

- Contribute to skirmishes from a safer position with the long-range ultimate, then return to lane without giving up unnecessary farm.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Xerath.R]

## Win condition

- Remember that E is the hard stun and its duration grows with the orb's travel distance; lead with longer-range casts for the longer lock.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Xerath.E]

- Stay off extended side lanes: Xerath is immobile and weak alone, so never push where opponents can collapse on him.
  sources: [hofibauer]

- Chip the frontline rather than forcing a risky dive when no carry angle is available, keeping the poke threat alive for the next ultimate window.
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q, Xerath.R]

## Itemization

- Run Arcane Comet with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, and Scorch plus Cut Down and Presence of Mind, pairing Comet with the lane-poke plan and Presence of Mind with mana sustain.
  sources: [hofibauer]

- Build Luden's Echo first for damage and waveclear, then Horizon Focus for cooldowns and reliable ultimate access, preferring Horizon Focus over Shadowflame in the standard route while keeping Shadowflame as the snowball purchase when ahead.
  items: [6655, 4628, 4645]
  sources: [hofibauer]
  abilities: [Xerath.R]

- Into tank-heavy teams take the Blackfire Torch, Sorcerer's Shoes, Liandry's Torment, Void Staff, Rabadon's Deathcap alternative, with Shadowflame, Rabadon's Deathcap, Void Staff, and Sorcerer's Shoes as standard endgame options and Zhonya's Hourglass for survival.
  items: [2503, 3020, 6653, 3135, 3089, 4645, 3157]
  sources: [hofibauer]
