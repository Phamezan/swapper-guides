## Key tips

- Harass with Plasma Fission and Void Rift; the Void Rift slow helps set up Plasma Fission and Tectonic Disruption, while the stun can punish a target that steps out of position.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.Q, Vel'Koz.W, Vel'Koz.E]

- Keep Tectonic Disruption for peel when the enemy can engage; spending the only self-peel carelessly leaves the immobile support open to an all-in.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.E]

- At level six, use Life Form Disintegration Ray to finish or pressure grouped enemies; first poke them down and channel from a safe position because crowd control can interrupt it.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- After the bot outer tower falls, rotate mid, pressure the tower, and poke the defender away from the objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]

- Use the Void Rift slow to help allies land their crowd control when a direct kill is not available.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.W]

- Hold the stun for an approaching diver rather than throwing it into the wave when the enemy pair is healthy.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.E]

## Mid lane

- Delay a full fight while Plasma Fission poke lowers the enemy team, then commit when targets are softened.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.Q]

- Check the next major objective and establish vision early from a safe route, then retreat to the team.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]

- Do not cross the map or ward alone; the support is easy to catch without allies nearby.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]

- Level nine maxes Plasma Fission for long-range poke; level eleven adds an ultimate point for finishing low targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.Q, Vel'Koz.R]

- At level thirteen, Void Rift reaches max rank and adds more damage to the Plasma Fission follow-up.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.W, Vel'Koz.Q]

## Win condition

- Ward major objectives and high-traffic paths late so the team can see the enemy approach.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]

- Stay with allies and avoid solo warding unless the enemy position is known; getting picked creates a four-versus-five.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]

- Use Tectonic Disruption to peel and protect the carries through the fight rather than trying to stand in front.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.E]

- Use fog and range to poke or finish squishies, but keep enough safety to complete the ultimate channel.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-support]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.R]
