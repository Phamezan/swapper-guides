## Key tips

- Survive weak early lanes safely and make level 6 the first goal.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Aim for two items plus boots around the third Dragon fight or Atakhan as the main timing.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Never start a teamfight without W and R ready; stack Q on nearby minions or camps first.
  sources: [arthapsic]
  abilities: [Gwen.W, Gwen.R, Gwen.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Pull the wave toward Gwen by letting opponents draw minion aggro or cast area spells inside it.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Against a big incoming wave, skip trades unless ahead, donate a few last hits, and bank the experience under tower.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- With wave control, stack Q on minions before trading, then push safely with jungler vision up.
  sources: [arthapsic]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q]

- Into strong early laners, poke with Q then E away instead of long trades, warding before any push.
  sources: [arthapsic]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q, Gwen.E]

- Against a freeze from a stronger opponent, proxy a wave to reset only while the enemy jungler stays off top side.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- While ahead, slow-push a big wave and let the next one return for a freeze and a longer chase lane.
  sources: [arthapsic]

## Mid lane

- Hold a side lane until the second-item spike whenever the map allows, then spend it on objectives or fights.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Split to end faster, to cover a team that cannot win front-to-back, or to drag pressure for an objective.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- While behind with ahead teammates, farm side towers for gold and pull defenders instead of forcing low-value fights.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- With Teleport up, push opposite the next objective and join by weighing R, team need, flank vision, and whether the defender loses.
  sources: [arthapsic]
  abilities: [Gwen.R]

## Win condition

- Follow a teammate engage instead of initiating alone, since Gwen brings little control beyond slows.
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Bunch enemies for Q and R area damage onto priority targets the team can pin down.
  sources: [arthapsic]
  abilities: [Gwen.Q, Gwen.R]

- Work through the frontline in front-to-back fights, letting W ignore pressure from outside the mist.
  sources: [arthapsic]
  abilities: [Gwen.W]

- Force the split-or-objective dilemma with side pressure, Teleporting in when the team needs her and the fight favors it.
  sources: [arthapsic]

## Itemization

- Open Doran's Ring standard or Doran's Shield into ranged pressure, then list Nashor's Tooth and Ionian Boots ahead of AP items.
  items: [1056, 1054, 3115]
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Finish Nashor's Tooth, Ionian Boots of Lucidity, Rabadon's Deathcap, Shadowflame, Void Staff, and Zhonya's Hourglass, with Riftmaker as an alternative.
  items: [3115, 3158, 3089, 4645, 3135, 3157, 4633]
  sources: [arthapsic]

- Run Conqueror with Presence of Mind, Legend: Alacrity, and Cut Down plus Demolish and Bone Plating, swapping Second Wind in against ranged lanes.
  sources: [arthapsic]
