## Key tips

- Lane safely by collecting minions and punishing opponent mistakes instead of forcing early duels.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Expect ranged mid laners to pressure Galio before he can reach them with engage.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Join skirmishes over holding side lanes, since control plus the ultimate can flip an ally fight.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Call the jungler when an opening shows; knock-up into taunt sets up the gank.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.E, Galio.W]

- Max Q in a solo lane to reset the passive faster, add percentage-health damage, and clear waves sooner.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.Q]

- Shove the wave under the enemy turret with Q and passive hits, then look to roam.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.Q]

- Weave Colossal Smash between spells whenever possible, since landed abilities shorten its cooldown and skipped hits waste damage.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.P]

- Run the realistic engage of E knock-up into a passive hit, W taunt, Q, and another passive hit.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.E, Galio.W, Galio.Q]

- Take Flash default with Ghost second for sticking or escaping, or Teleport for hard lanes and faster joins.
  sources: [hexeria]

## Mid lane

- Turn pushed waves into first moves toward teammates and river plays across his wave-pressure, roam, invade, and teamfight strengths.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Respect long cooldowns by valuing haste; after an early engage dump, little remains until spells return.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Stack haste and cycle the passive reset to sustain damage and control through fights.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Drop Hero's Entrance to shield an ally or flip a fight by knocking up nearby enemies.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.R]

## Win condition

- Choose each fight between locking opponents down and peeling a carry through layered control.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Layer Shield of Durand taunts on groups with Justice Punch and Hero's Entrance knock-ups.
  sources: [hexeria]
  abilities: [Galio.W, Galio.E, Galio.R]

- Never default to split-pushing; teamfights carry far more of his value.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Stay useful while behind purely through engage, protection, and control.
  sources: [hexeria]

## Itemization

- Run Aftershock with Shield Bash, Second Wind, and Overgrowth, plus Nimbus Cloak and Transcendence.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Recommend Abyssal Mask, Mercury's Treads, Imperial Mandate, Zhonya's Hourglass, Riftmaker, and Jak'Sho on the panel.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Rush Ionian Boots as a cheap early spike for long cooldowns; swap Mercury's Treads into magic damage or control, Steelcaps into basic-attack threats, and Sorcerer's Shoes for damage builds.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Rate Abyssal Mask highly for its low cost, defensive stats, and nearby-enemy magic-damage amplification.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Add Cosmic Drive for health, AP, haste, and movement speed behind repeated spell and passive cycles.
  sources: [hexeria]

- Pick Dusk and Dawn as the AP-bruiser spellblade, or Fimbulwinter from an early Tear for mana, health, and haste.
