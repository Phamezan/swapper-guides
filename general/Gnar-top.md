## Key tips

- Before level 6, bank last hits, health, and small trades, since early kills usually risk too much.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Time Mega to land under a minute before a fight starts when planning around objectives.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Reach two items plus boots by the third Dragon fight or Atakhan, treating the 20 to 30 minute window as the key influence period.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Stay healthy so ganks and dives find no opening while Mega timing stays unreliable.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Value even a modest experience or farm lead, since it pays later without lane kills.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- After level 6, punish harder with longer Mini range and a more dependable Mega.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Reassess at levels 4 or 5 instead of assuming weakness everywhere, especially into juggernauts.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- In low-kill lanes, wait for the first item before demanding kill pressure.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

## Mid lane

- Farm well and hold a side lane until the second-item spike, especially Sterak's Gage.
  items: [3053]
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Split only when matchup and map support it; otherwise farm the side and ready the group.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Shift lane assignments before objectives so a teammate banks the big wave while Gnar builds Mega.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- As Mini, poke the frontline and hold space without starting fights on an expiring Mega.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

## Win condition

- Spend Mega control on allied-engage follow-ups or peeling divers off the backline.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Never jump groups as Mini; the mere Mega threat already creates space without a forced engage.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Keep poking and spacing as Mini while waiting for a useful transformation.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Take teamfights over late splits unless the map offers a clearly safe side trade.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]

- Flex to the fight need: follow engages, peel carries, or hold space instead of one fixed pattern.
  sources: [dragonlord1022]
