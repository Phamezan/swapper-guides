## Key tips

- Pick the clear by the lane targeted at the end: red-side routes to bot or a Wolves start toward top.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Treat counter-ganks as the core strength by tracking the enemy jungler toward their likely attack.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Link roots, shields, brush, and knock-ups with teammates, since Daisy plus E teamfighting carries his value.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Run the red-side full clear by tapping Red, Raptors, Wolves, Blue, and Gromp, then freeing Raptors and Red before Smiting Krugs and finishing the rest.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Run the top-side route from Wolves into Blue, Gromp, Raptors, and Red before freeing them, saving Smite for Krugs.
  sources: [iverngott]

- On blue side, clear fully toward bot by tapping Blue, Wolves, Raptors, Red, and Krugs, then freeing Blue, Smiting Gromp, and continuing on.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Keep camp timers synced by clearing in groups instead of letting one side drift behind.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Respect invade vulnerability and predictable paths by watching for early invades and demanding nearby priority before contesting.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Ride Q through camps for faster travel, rooting a chaser behind for an escape in a pinch.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.Q]

## Mid lane

- Plant Brushmaker in fights for its bonus damage to Ivern and nearby allies, keeping Daisy around brush too.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.W]

- Root with Q so allies can dash into range, using its dash recast and monster cooldown cut as confirmed.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.Q]

- Cast Triggerseed on an ally or Daisy before they enter so its shield and delayed burst hit nearby opponents.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.E]

- Steer Daisy so her third straight hit on a champion or epic monster knocks up nearby enemies where it matters.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.R]

- Start or stretch teamfights with Daisy under E shielding.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.R, Ivern.E]

## Win condition

- Turn enemy movement reads into counter-ganks: shield the threatened ally and flip the response with Q or Daisy.
  sources: [iverngott]
  abilities: [Ivern.Q, Ivern.R]

- Guard the carrying ally from close range so support-build shields, heals, and utility all connect.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Never rest the whole plan on solo counter-jungling; predictable routes get punished without teammates.
  sources: [iverngott]

- Shape routes and timers around the stronger side to arrive first at likely skirmishes.
  sources: [iverngott]
