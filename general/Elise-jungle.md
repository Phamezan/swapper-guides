## Key tips

- Gank reliably at level 3 with E online; the level-2 gank works only when E comes second.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Spend early strength on frequent ganks while still farming camps between them.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Peel for carries late and hit the nearest enemy instead of diving alone.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Turn early leads into lane advantages for teammates rather than solo plays.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Guard Blue buff when possible, since losing it cuts casts and invites invades.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Take Dragon and other early objectives with teammates for safety even though Elise can solo them.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Open picks with long-range human E, then follow with Q and W on the controlled target.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E, Elise.Q, Elise.W]

- Save Spider E to dodge key crowd control or stall a lost all-in; careless casts delete the escape.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Treat the first finished item as an early kill-pressure spike, especially into low-magic-resistance squishies.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

## Mid lane

- Catch out-of-position enemies with E picks, then bring nearby teammates to cash them into objectives.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Keep taking Dragons and clearing camps so gold never slides behind.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- At level 9, duel and contest harder with maxed Q.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.Q]

- Never treat levels 6 or 11 as spikes; the ultimate runs from level 1, so enemy junglers may gain more at those ranks.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.R]

- After the first item, challenge most junglers 1v1 with the first move.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

## Win condition

- Turn early ganks and picks into Dragon and objective control.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Catch someone mid-game with E only with allies close enough to hold them.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Group late and skip plan-less side lanes; Elise fades fast when behind.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Throw late E picks beside allies on its shorter cooldown for more catch chances.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E]

- Never bank on level 16 as an ultimate spike; keep playing around picks and E.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
  abilities: [Elise.E, Elise.R]

## Itemization

- Rush the first finished item as the major kill-pressure spike the page describes without naming it.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]

- Protect Blue buff as the resource plan, since its loss means fewer casts and open invasions.
  sources: [mobalytics-elise]
