## Key tips

- Death's Hand hits harder when more bolts land, so move close enough to catch an opponent with several bolts when the trade is safe.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.Q]

- Use Nevermove as a catch tool from long range; its return can affect multiple enemies and help create a follow-up opportunity.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.E]

- Enter as the second or third engager so enemies spend attention on another frontliner before Swain commits his damage and control.
  sources: [kaziz]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Swain is vulnerable early and has shorter range than many support mages, so avoid taking focus before allies can follow up.
  sources: [kaziz]

- Use Vision of Empire to scout bushes or interrupt a recall; after allied crowd control, it is easier to land and can add passive stacks.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.W]

- Cast Vision of Empire across the enemy carry's escape route or near a last-hit window to force a slower or less convenient path.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.W]

- A max-range Nevermove catch returns quickly, making it a stronger pick attempt than throwing the ability without a clear target.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.E]

## Mid lane

- Walk or Flash after casting Nevermove when needed to change the return path of the claw and catch opponents from an unexpected angle.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.E]

- Demonic Ascension drains enemies around Swain; keep several champions or minions in its area to sustain through a drawn-out fight.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.R]

- Save the second cast of Demonic Ascension to slow enemies when that control is more valuable than using it only for extra damage.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.R]

- Swain can fit tank, bruiser, utility-mage, or damage-mage roles, but have another frontliner on the team.
  sources: [kaziz]

## Win condition

- Avoid being the only frontline; Swain's sustain is less useful if the entire enemy team can focus him before he acts.
  sources: [kaziz]

- In lower-ranked games support Swain works as a carry option, while still relying on crowd control and team follow-up.
  sources: [kaziz]

- Use Vision of Empire to zone exits and Nevermove to catch grouped enemies, then stay near them with Demonic Ascension to sustain and pressure through the fight.
  sources: [kaziz]
  abilities: [Swain.W, Swain.E, Swain.R]
