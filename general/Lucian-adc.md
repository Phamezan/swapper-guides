## Key tips

- Spend short-range windows pressuring with Q through minions plus passive-enhanced attacks.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]
  abilities: [Lucian.Q]

- Start all-ins after the support lands control or the enemy burns an important escape or defense.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

- Flank teamfights from side angles; Lucian plays closer than long-range ADCs without ever frontlining.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Check the enemy jungler before committing; a mistimed close trade can lose the lane outright.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

- Hold passive-enhanced attacks off the wave unless pushing stays intentional, keeping the lane safe.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

- Poke Q directly when open, or thread it through a minion to reach opponents outside direct range.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]
  abilities: [Lucian.Q]

## Mid lane

- Pressure tanks and the frontline with R, then E out once an assassin closes in.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]
  abilities: [Lucian.R, Lucian.E]

- Threaten with brushes and flank setups instead of walking blind into unseen space.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

## Win condition

- Turn lane pressure into early Dragon and Baron control; Lucian fades later, so close while the lead lives.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

- Flank the backline only after control and peel threats are spent before stepping close.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]

- Spend E defensively on divers, since its mobility answers lockdown best.
  sources: [tokyodamonsta]
  abilities: [Lucian.E]
