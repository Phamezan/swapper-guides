## Key tips

- Look for a level-2 all-in with Q and W the moment the lane partner can follow.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.Q, Braum.W]

- Save E for a meaningful threat; without it, both Braum and the ADC turn punishable.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.E]

- Accept the missing routine engage tool and start fights through an R pick or a Flash-assisted initiation.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Answer enemy engages with Q and the passive, especially against melee divers.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.Q]

- Position tightly into ranged lanes so poke never forces an early recall.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- At level 3, use the full basic kit for both defensive answers and extra lane pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- At level 6, carry a pick and initiation threat by pairing R with Flash for a safe start.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]

## Mid lane

- Roam with R ready to catch someone, then bring nearby allies to turn the pick into an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]

- Cover mid and the ADC area with vision to cut down all-in risk.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- Stay with the ADC, since either lone member invites a collapse.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- Rush upgraded boots for earlier roams and first arrival at lane skirmishes.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- At level 9, use maxed Q and its lower cooldown for more catch attempts.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.Q]

- At level 11, press the second R rank for extra damage and a shorter cooldown as teams group.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]

- Break grouped opponents apart with the R area knock-up through mid-game fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]

## Win condition

- Stay with the team late and shield carries with E and frontline presence.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.E]

- Spend R to disrupt a fight or catch someone in the jungle, then take the nearby objective with teammates.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]

- Never ward late alone; one catch hands the enemy numbers around an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- Hold Baron and Dragon vision so the team keeps securing objectives and finding picks.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]

- At level 13, rely on maxed E for stronger carry protection.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.E]

- At level 16, use the third R rank as another teamfight spike with a shorter wait until the next cast.
  sources: [mobalytics-braum]
  abilities: [Braum.R]
