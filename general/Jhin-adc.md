## Key tips

- Last-hit low-health casters with Dancing Grenade so its bounce threatens the enemy marksman.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Finish weakened opponents with the fourth shot, which crits for damage based on missing health.
  sources: [antiwis]

- Aim Curtain Call at low-health targets rather than opening on full-health frontliners, since it also scales with missing health.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hold Q briefly for bounce angles that push enemies off minions, casting once they step into range.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Answer allied control with long-range Deadly Flourish, leading the dodge direction.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

- Spend Captive Audience to slow targets, check bushes, and add fight damage.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

- Trade aggressively early and join four-player skirmishes, especially beside damage supports like Zyra, Xerath, or Brand.
  sources: [antiwis]

- On support roams, let the wave come in unless opponents can dive safely.
  sources: [antiwis]

## Mid lane

- Keep farming with the team instead of taking a solo side lane.
  sources: [antiwis]

- Track Dragon and Baron timers and bank Curtain Call for the important objective fights.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.R]

- Follow team engages from range with W and R to pressure low-health enemies.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Seed E traps for vision and to slow enemies walking into fights.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.E]

## Win condition

- Pair W roots and long-range R follow-up with teammates who start fights or chain takedowns as the execution marksman.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]

- Punish exposed mistakes late with fourth shots, W, and R from behind the frontline.
  sources: [antiwis]
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]
