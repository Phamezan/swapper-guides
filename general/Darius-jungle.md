## Key tips

- Reach level 3 before the first gank, since all three basics must be online to engage well.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Spike hard at level 6, when R adds an execute threat to duels and skirmishes.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- Peel in teamfights and hit the closest enemy instead of diving the backline alone.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

## Overview

## Early lane

- After level 3, path to pushed lanes around enemy vision so targets get less warning.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Counter-gank whenever possible and turn the enemy arrival into a winning skirmish.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Check enemy inventories for early Grievous Wounds before dueling, and refuse commits into a buyer.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Treat the first finished component as a trading spike, contesting most junglers only when matchup and health allow.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

## Mid lane

- At level 9, press maxed Q for stronger damage and all-in threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- At level 11, use the second R point as another damage spike for fights and picks.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- Without a forming fight, push a side lane and duel the defender when the 1v1 favors Darius.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Time R as the execute to secure the takedown and carry its reset onto the next target.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- After a couple of items, blend meaningful damage with enough durability to stay in fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

## Win condition

- Build the lead through ganks, counter-ganks, and favorable duels off the early level and item spikes.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Stay with teammates in fights and chain R takedowns into reset kills.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.R]

- Flank only on routes that still rejoin the team fight promptly.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]

- Hold near the team at neutral objectives; threaten the backline while ahead, or peel with E for allies while behind.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.E]

- Convert the early and mid advantage fast, since Darius fades late as opponents stack items and durability.
  sources: [mobalytics-darius]
