## Key tips

- Thresh is strong early but has no sustain: skip the long-range poke lane when you cannot reach the brush or create an opening, and only fight with a real path in.
  sources: [razing42]

- Aim hooks at the opponent's likely dodge direction, tracking whether the ADC or the support tends to evade left or right before throwing.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

- Create picks from fog with Death Sentence, then lantern an ally into the play or carry one out of danger, keeping Flay ready to peel or interrupt the next dash.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q, Thresh.W, Thresh.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- After pushing a wave into the enemy tower, choose between a reset, a plate attempt, poke or a possible dive, or a roam; when roaming, never leave the ADC exposed.
  sources: [razing42]

- A crashed wave opens a brief roam window before the next wave is likely to reach your tower; return before it dies if the ADC has not pushed it back out.
  sources: [razing42]

- Leave plate gold for the ADC when safe, and threaten poke or a dive with the hook while the opponent sits under tower.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

- Against Cleanse, hold Ignite until after the opponent uses it on Death Sentence so both effects are not removed together.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q]

## Mid lane

- Place the lantern in the direction the teammate is already moving to make it easier to click, repositioning allies for follow-up or peel.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.W]

- Use the lantern to check a bush or cross a wall instead of face-checking, but keep it available when a gank may be coming.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.W]

- Remember the lantern has unit collision: it can obstruct an enemy's path but can also accidentally block teammates, so place it deliberately.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.W]

- Save Flay to peel or interrupt a dash, and against an opponent who escapes right as the hook ends, hold Flay for that exact moment.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q, Thresh.E]

- Land Death Sentence, place The Box during its crowd control, and push the target into a wall with Flay for the stronger slow.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.Q, Thresh.R, Thresh.E]

- Ward while moving through fog and before objectives, adapting depth to enemy jungle mobility and the changed terrain around the third dragon and Baron; practice placements in Practice Tool until each ward lands repeatably.
  sources: [razing42]

## Win condition

- Convert a pushed bot wave into a safe reset, tower pressure, a dive threat, or a short roam, always returning in time to protect the ADC's experience.
  sources: [razing42]

- In teamfights, flex between engage and disengage: follow a pick with The Box, but preserve lantern and Flay when a teammate needs protection.
  sources: [razing42]
  abilities: [Thresh.R, Thresh.W, Thresh.E]

## Itemization

- Follow the patch 26.21 default of Ionian Boots of Lucidity, Solstice Sleigh, Locket of the Iron Solari, Knight's Vow, Redemption, and Bandlepipes, favoring Locket as the broadly useful first core.
  sources: [razing42]

- Take Knight's Vow for single-target damage, Redemption for slower front-to-back fights, and Bandlepipes with several auto attackers; choose Second Wind into poke, Bone Plating into early trading, Unflinching into trade-pattern crowd control, Aftershock into engage lanes or solo-frontline games, and Glacial Augment into double-ranged or dive-heavy opponents.
  sources: [razing42]

- Round out defenses by threat: Frozen Heart against auto-attack damage, Thornmail for grievous wounds, Abyssal Mask with AP teammates, and Kaenic Rookern for more magic resistance.
  sources: [razing42]
