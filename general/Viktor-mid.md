## Key tips

- Play as the evolution scaler: prioritize safe last hits early and use takedowns to reach ability upgrades sooner.
  sources: [intaggar]

- Trade Q for its shield in safer short exchanges, or poke and farm from range with E when ranged opponents make Q trades hard.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q, Viktor.E]

- Evolve Q for movement speed when survival and disengage matter most, and E for poke and waveclear when the lane needs control and room to rotate.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q, Viktor.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use W to interrupt an approach or cover an escape, and to set up a gank or catch an exposed target.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.W]

- Against strong early all-in opponents, skip forcing level-two or level-three trades and wait for a safer opening or jungler help.
  sources: [intaggar]

- Treat Aery as the aggressive option for extra damage and shielding, and First Strike as the scaling pick that adds value into tankier teams.
  sources: [intaggar]

## Mid lane

- Treat the post-lane stretch from around minute ten onward as Viktor's first stronger phase, once evolutions and early sustain items come online.
  sources: [intaggar]

- Poke from range without offering an easy return trade, tracking both junglers before setting up a play.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- Pair the stronger E poke and waveclear with safer pushes and rotations, while the evolved Q movement covers positioning against divers.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.E, Viktor.Q]

- Recall into Lost Chapter first, then follow the normal route of Blackfire Torch, boots, Liandry's Torment, Hextech Rocketbelt, Zhonya's Hourglass, Void Staff, and Rabadon's Deathcap; take the Rod of Ages, Rocketbelt, Void Staff, Zhonya's, Rabadon's defensive line or the Lich Bane and Shadowflame burst line by game.
  sources: [intaggar]

## Win condition

- Play from the back line in developing fights, keeping opponents at range with repeated poke and never stepping forward for damage that exposes Viktor to a dive.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.E]

- Use Teleport to collect a side wave and rejoin for the objective when the team groups elsewhere.
  sources: [intaggar]

- Treat Q movement and the W stun as limited escape tools: when both are unavailable, Viktor is especially vulnerable to champions who can reach him.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q, Viktor.W]

- Hold the ultimate to interrupt channeled casts, including Katarina's Death Lotus, rather than spending it only for damage.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.R]

## Itemization

- Favor Lucidity boots for the cooldown-oriented longer trades and repeated Q use, Sorcerer's Shoes as the general damage option, Plated Steelcaps into physical damage pressure, Mercury's Treads into magic damage and crowd control, and Swifties when extra movement is needed.
  sources: [intaggar]
  abilities: [Viktor.Q]
