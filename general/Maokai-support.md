## Key tips

- At level 2, W onto a target and Q them toward the allied ADC to split them off, adding Ignite and allied damage for the catch.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.W, Maokai.Q]

- Spike at level 6 for objective control by pairing jungler coordination with saplings plus Nature's Grasp over vision and approaches.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.R]

- Commit each game to engaging or peeling by first item: Trailblazer for engage range, Locket for a vulnerable carry or heavy dive.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat level 1 cautiously with weak sustain and no built-in damage mitigation.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Start Q for standard lanes or W for invades and level-1 brush plays, taking E third on its far longer cooldown than Q or W.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.Q, Maokai.W, Maokai.E]

- Press W plus Q plus Ignite for kill pressure with an ADC, especially into ranged or squishy supports, after checking health and wave spots.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.W, Maokai.Q]

- Extend engages with Flash beyond W range, keeping Hexflash from brush as a second engage route when available.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.W]

- Track the enemy jungler before engaging; without Flash, sapling vision must cover the punish risk.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Swap to Oracle Lens after the first warding trip or early recall to sweep lane brushes toward a possible mid gank.
  items: [3364]
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

## Mid lane

- Buy basic boots on the first recall for roam and rotation speed, often finishing Boots of Swiftness before any full item.
  items: [3009]
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Check bushes with saplings first, then plant deeper enemy-jungle wards inside safe roam windows.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Read each fight for engage or peel duty, knocking threats away from allies with Q when protection matters.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.Q]

- Combine Trailblazer and Locket when the team wants both movement speed and a protective shield.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

## Win condition

- Build early leads on lane W plus Q catches with Ignite, but never force them without enough follow-up damage.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.W, Maokai.Q]

- Turn level 6 into safer objective control with sapling scouting and R root threats.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]
  abilities: [Maokai.R]

- Pick initiation or main-carry protection and play it fully; the first-item fork exists for that split.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Scale as a utility tank through control, vision, and objective setup over raw damage.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

## Itemization

- Anchor the tank page on Aftershock, whose extra defenses cover Maokai fight entries while he stays close to deal its damage.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Default to Font of Life second-row, or Demolish when lane pressure promises turret plates.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Take Bone Plating into aggressive lanes, Conditioning for teamfight durability with low lane-death risk, and Second Wind only into oppressive double poke.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Close Resolve with Unflinching usually, or Overgrowth away from the main-control spotlight against little enemy control.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Add Celerity and Nimbus Cloak for movement plus extra engage and disengage speed after Flash or Ignite.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Pair Flash plus Ignite for lane kills by default, or Exhaust to shut down a dangerous assassin or cover Ignite-dead tanky teams.
  sources: [funnybunnyh]

- Start World Atlas into Solstice Sleigh generally, Celestial Opposition into burst or picks, and Bloodsong for catch play into healing-heavy teams.
