## Key tips

- Use Plasma Fission poke to make the opponent respect the range; wave pressure with Plasma Fission and Void Rift can also give room to help with objectives.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.Q, Vel'Koz.W]

- Before level six, keep the wave nearer your side because Vel'Koz is fragile and lacks a reliable escape.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Save Tectonic Disruption as the only self-peel rather than spending it casually; a blink or ranged crowd control can punish it being down.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Do not push up until the position of the enemy jungler is known; his immobility makes ganks dangerous.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Life Form Disintegration Ray roots Vel'Koz in place, so channel from a safe angle where ranged crowd control cannot interrupt it.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.R]

- At level six, the ultimate completes his kit and makes clumped fights more dangerous for the enemy.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.R]

- The first item component lets Void Rift clear waves more often, helping prevent the opposing mid from roaming freely.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.W]

- Trade Plasma Fission from maximum range and return to the wave immediately instead of lingering in range of gap closers.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.Q]

## Mid lane

- When allies rotate into mid after taking their lane tower, move to a side lane to keep collecting farm and experience.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Clear before sieging or defending a siege, then look for a favorable trade while landing poke.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Around Dragon or Baron, use abilities to catch someone out of position; convert a kill into the objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Two ultimate points at level eleven improve follow-up damage, especially when fights are clumped near an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.R]

- The passive can stack across several enemies in close fights, so chokepoints are useful places to contest.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.P]

## Win condition

- Stay with the team late rather than splitting; an isolated Vel'Koz can be picked before a fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Look for isolated targets, then collapse with allies to win the numbers advantage.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Poke before committing to a fight and keep checking positioning so the attempt to harass does not get punished.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]

- Use chokepoints to make multi-target ability hits and passive procs more likely.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid]
  abilities: [Vel'Koz.P]

## Itemization

- Take Arcane Comet, Manaflow Band, Transcendence, and Scorch with Cut Down and Legend: Haste as the 16.21 rune page.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid-build]

- Build the popular path of Luden's Echo, Sorcerer's Shoes, Shadowflame, Zhonya's Hourglass, and Rabadon's Deathcap.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid-build]

- Start Doran's Ring, a Health Potion, and Stealth Ward.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid-build]

- Choose Horizon Focus as the fourth-item replacement for Zhonya's when wanting more long-range damage or facing immobilized targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid-build]

- Max Q, then W, then E, taking Life Form Disintegration Ray at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-velkoz-mid-build]
