## Key tips

- Stack the passive to four before isolated duels; without a wave or stacks, the 1v1 collapses.
  sources: [houcs]

- Peak through early-to-mid game and spend item and level leads on skirmishes there.
  sources: [houcs]

- Respect crowd-control-heavy and high-armor teams that stall her movement and damage.
  sources: [houcs]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Track Q damage each rank; a minion left alive breaks the reset and strands Irelia.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Prep low-health minions with W for clean Q resets.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.W, Irelia.Q]

- Keep a wave around before fighting, since R plus W can clear it and erase the next Q targets.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.W, Irelia.Q]

- Q to a minion and cast E with the cursor held on it to hide the first blade animation.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Irelia.E]

- Open E1 and hold E2 to force reactions under pressure.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.E]

- Cast R before E when possible so the blade field cuts escape paths and lands E easier.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.E]

- Build stacks fast with E, W, Q, Q when no wave can supply them.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Irelia.W, Irelia.Q]

- At level 9, learn the max-rank Q plus Blade of the Ruined King backline threshold so resets never drop.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Mid lane

- Escape through Q on nearby minions or jungle monsters when no champion target stays safe.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Hold a minion wave before engaging so Q keeps resetting and carrying mobility through the fight.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Constrain movement with R into E, then chain Q across marked targets.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.E, Irelia.Q]

- Play carefully into the difficult control and armor teams the guide flags.
  sources: [houcs]

## Win condition

- Win extended fights on wave access plus four stacks, refusing stack-less isolated duels.
  sources: [houcs]

- Lock targets with R and E for the teamfight follow-up instead of entering with no wave or reset path.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.E]

- Flee bad fights on Q hops across nearby minions or camps.
  sources: [houcs]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, and Last Stand plus Bone Plating and Unflinching.
  sources: [houcs]

- Swap Resolve to Second Wind and Overgrowth on the alternate page into ranged opponents.
  sources: [houcs]

- Take Flash and Teleport for top lane.
  sources: [houcs]

- Rush Blade of the Ruined King first, pairing it with Terminus and Hullbreaker in the common build.
  sources: [houcs]

- Add Wit's End into AP threats and Mercury's Treads into heavy AP plus control; against full AD take Iceborn Gauntlet, Death's Dance, and Plated Steelcaps, and into multi-AP add Maw of Malmortius and Kaenic Rookern.
  sources: [houcs]
