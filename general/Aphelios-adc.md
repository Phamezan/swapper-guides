## Key tips

- Default to caution in bot lane and scale aggression to the matchup instead of forcing every trade.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Treat the two to three item stretch as the weakest stage; keep farming and skip uncertain fights.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Play for the four-item spike, when teamfight damage jumps sharply.
  sources: [wargreymon]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hold Gravitum ready when support or jungle follow-up is near, since its slow and root set up picks and ganks.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Open Calibrum and Severum, then spend Severum first to reach the Calibrum and Gravitum pairing sooner.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Use Severum Q for a short all-in that stacks Press the Attack fast, or for movement speed while leaving.
  sources: [wargreymon]
  abilities: [Aphelios.Q]

- Mark at long range with Calibrum Q, then extend the catch with a Gravitum attack and root for allied engage.
  sources: [wargreymon]
  abilities: [Aphelios.Q]

- Near level 6, preserve a useful pair like Calibrum and Crescendum or Severum and Crescendum for an all-in or dragon fight.
  sources: [wargreymon]
  abilities: [Aphelios.R]

- Plan the next gun pair before emptying one: each gun holds 50 ammo, Q spends 10, and each basic attack spends 1.
  sources: [wargreymon]
  abilities: [Aphelios.Q]

## Mid lane

- Skip doubtful fights through the low-pressure item window unless numbers or setup clearly favor the team.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Keep collecting farm and avoid deaths so the build still reaches its later spike.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Around objectives past level 6, pick gun pairs for the coming fight instead of following the rotation blindly.
  sources: [wargreymon]

## Win condition

- Prioritize guns late as Infernum, Severum, Crescendum, Calibrum, then Gravitum, bending to matchup and utility needs.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Spread Infernum area damage into groups, especially with Infernum and Gravitum sharing slow and root setup across targets.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Match Moonlight Vigil to the fight: Calibrum marks, Severum heals, Gravitum slows, Infernum adds area damage, Crescendum grants extra Chakrams.
  sources: [wargreymon]
  abilities: [Aphelios.R]

- Hold a safe firing spot with no mobility to spare, and refuse low-value mid-game fights.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Enter fights with useful weapon effects ready, since disciplined cycling drives late damage and control.
  sources: [wargreymon]

## Itemization

- Run Press the Attack with Triumph, Legend: Bloodline, and Cut Down, plus Cash Back and Triple Tonic, fitting the short-trade pattern.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Build The Collector into Berserker's Greaves, Infinity Edge, Lord Dominik's Regards, Runaan's Hurricane, and Bloodthirster, with Guardian Angel in the core list.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Choose first buys by gold and recall timing among Serrated Dirk, Noonquiver, B. F. Sword, and Berserker's Greaves.
  sources: [wargreymon]

- Keep situational picks ready: Mortal Reminder, Stormrazor, Immortal Shieldbow, Maw of Malmortius, Mercurial Scimitar, Hexoptics C44, Rapid Firecannon, Phantom Dancer, and Zhonya's Hourglass.
  sources: [wargreymon]
