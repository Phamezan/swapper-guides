## Key tips

- Play around Doom calling fear as both a trade tool and protection; check whether the passive is ready before stepping into danger.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.P]

- Keep trades short and built around cooldowns until the ultimate is available; pair a short trade with Ignite as the kill threat once it unlocks.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

- In ranged matchups, use the range advantage to pressure a last-hit window and avoid extended trades if the opponent's burst cooldowns are ready.
  sources: [izianni]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Into melee lanes, keep wave control and look for a Mistral Bolt with an attack, Looming Darkness, Mistral Bolt, and attack trade before backing away.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.W, Vex.E, Vex.Q]

- When the matchup is unsafe, clear with Mistral Bolt from range until level six instead of forcing a fight.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.Q]

- Pressure the opponent's last hits with superior range in ranged lanes, then step out before their burst window opens.
  sources: [izianni]

- Keep vision around mid and avoid dying to ganks; losing gold and experience undermines the lane lead.
  sources: [izianni]

- At level six, the stronger Shadow Surge plus Ignite combination is the main kill threat, so set up the all-in rather than poking aimlessly.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

## Mid lane

- Once mid priority is established, extend vision into enemy territory and use it to choose jungle skirmishes or objective pressure.
  sources: [izianni]

- Push a wave before roaming; weak control and poor mobility away from minions make unplanned roams less reliable.
  sources: [izianni]

- Use Shadow Surge to reach a high-value target and snowball a skirmish when the team can follow.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

## Win condition

- Pick Vex into squishy teams with dashes, where her fear can interrupt their approach and the ultimate can threaten the backline.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

- If the matchup is difficult, play for waveclear through level six and then use the ultimate to create map pressure.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.R]

- Use wards and lane priority to guide which objective or jungle fight the team takes; do not chase low-value fights without vision.
  sources: [izianni]

- When the enemy burns key crowd control, look for a follow-up engage; otherwise use fear defensively to protect Vex and the allies.
  sources: [izianni]
  abilities: [Vex.P]
