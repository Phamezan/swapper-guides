## Key tips

- Accept a pushed-in early lane: stay healthy to levels two and three with Q farming from range, and only look for the kill when your support can start or follow the trade.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]
  abilities: [Samira.Q]

- Track the passive style grade in every fight: without S rank there is no R, so keep participating with Q and stacked autos instead of committing before the grade is ready.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]
  abilities: [Samira.Q, Samira.R]

- Play as cleanup, not engage: watch enemy cooldowns, follow allied crowd control onto a vulnerable backline route, and hold the entry until enemies spend the tools that could stop or kill you.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]
  abilities: [Samira.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- In most lanes Samira is support-reliant, so wait for an allied setup instead of forcing the opening yourself.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

- Take an early river or jungle skirmish only when it lets Samira build passive style and chain takedown resets; enter fights that develop favorably and skip the rest.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

- Open Doran's Blade with a Health Potion, and run Flash and Exhaust as the standard summoners.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

## Mid lane

- Follow allied crowd control rather than being the first champion to engage; Samira is burst-oriented but punished fast when she dives alone.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

- Build style with the two listed sequences, auto into E-Q into auto into W into auto, or the faster auto into W-one into E-Q into W-two into auto.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]
  abilities: [Samira.Q, Samira.W, Samira.E]

- Alternate damaging attacks and abilities so the grade climbs: autos between Q and E keep the pressure up while R stays locked.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]
  abilities: [Samira.Q, Samira.E, Samira.R]

## Win condition

- Preserve health through the weak early lane, then use allied setup and skirmishes to reach the burst and reset windows.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

- Stay close enough to contribute without starting the full fight alone; enter with a safe path to keep damaging targets and trigger takedowns.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Bloodline, Last Stand, Cash Back, and Biscuit Delivery, with adaptive force twice and a health shard.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]

- Build The Collector, Infinity Edge, Lord Dominik's Regards, Immortal Shieldbow, Plated Steelcaps, and Bloodthirster; recall into Serrated Dirk, Pickaxe, Vampiric Scepter, or Refillable Potion by gold and sustain needs.
  sources: [v1gorous1-samira]
