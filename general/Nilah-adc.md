## Key tips

- Treat levels one and two as the weakest window: hold experience range and health rather than contesting every minion with a hard trade.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]

- Strike at advantage moments: an experience lead, level three, or level six, when a new ability or the ultimate can surprise opponents who still expect the weak early laner.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.R]

- In fights, weave basic attacks between Q and E casts, hold R until an ally starts the fight or opponents commit, and use W and R to shield a teammate under focus.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.Q, Nilah.W, Nilah.E, Nilah.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Give up individual minions before levels three and six if contesting them means taking a bad trade; Nilah's early game is about surviving to her windows.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]

- Track the experience race, including the extra experience from nearby allied minions, and look for the all-in exactly when Nilah holds the level edge.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]

- Use Q to damage turrets and take plates when the opposing bot lane is forced to recall; Nilah removes towers quickly once she has the opening.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.Q]

- Spending R to chunk the enemy ADC into a recall is acceptable when it converts into plates; treat the ultimate as a lane-investment tool, not only a kill tool.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.R]

## Mid lane

- Enter and exit exchanges instead of standing inside the enemy team; time W and R around what opponents do rather than spending them on schedule.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.W, Nilah.R]

- Keep R ready for the follow-up moment: after an ally engages or the enemy commits, when its area damage and ally healing swing the exchange.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.R]

- Play as follow-up, not the initiator: reach the backline only when a teammate creates the entry, and protect a pressured teammate with W and R.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.W, Nilah.R]

- Keep damage flowing by alternating autos with Q and E instead of chaining abilities back to back without attacks between them.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.Q, Nilah.E]

## Win condition

- Protect health through levels one and two, then turn a level lead or a level-three or level-six all-in into plates or a tower.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.R]

- In teamfights, wait for allied engage or an enemy commit, guard allies with W and R, and sustain damage with autos between abilities.
  sources: [v1gorous1-nilah]
  abilities: [Nilah.W, Nilah.R]
