## Key tips

- At level 3, stack Q, the empowered fifth attack, and W for three passive marks and the stun, adding another auto when safe.
  sources: [guest-652087]
  abilities: [Kennen.Q, Kennen.W]

- At level 6, engage with E, then Flash and R with W and Q for the stun combo, ideally with the opponent near half health.
  sources: [guest-652087]
  abilities: [Kennen.E, Kennen.R, Kennen.W, Kennen.Q]

- Deliver teamfight value by reaching clustered or vulnerable targets with R and layering the stun.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Start E for early poke and mobility, with Q as the smaller poke alternative.
  sources: [guest-652087]
  abilities: [Kennen.E, Kennen.Q]

- Pressure enemy last-hits while protecting personal farm and experience.
  sources: [guest-652087]

- Track the enemy jungler and cut extended pressure short when a gank looms.
  sources: [guest-652087]

- On jungler arrivals, run the level-6 engage combo, or E plus Flash into Q and W before level 6.
  sources: [guest-652087]
  abilities: [Kennen.E, Kennen.Q, Kennen.W]

## Mid lane

- Play around objective vision and flank the enemy backline.
  sources: [guest-652087]

- Without an imminent objective, keep farming and press a side lane.
  sources: [guest-652087]

- Set isolated picks from bushes when the map allows.
  sources: [guest-652087]

## Win condition

- Call each fight between protecting carries and diving the enemy backline; both jobs matter late.
  sources: [guest-652087]

- Expect hard games into tanks, heavy shields, and teams with reliable disengage or vision.
  sources: [guest-652087]

- Coordinate the area engage so teammates follow the control and cash in.
  sources: [guest-652087]
  abilities: [Kennen.R]
