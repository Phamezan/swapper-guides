## Key tips

- Treat lane as a Q skillshot test: landing Dark Sphere pushes opponents off the wave, while a careless E leaves a window they can punish.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Syndra.E]

- At level six, leave two spheres on the ground and open with E into the stun; after earlier poke, the ultimate can then threaten the kill.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q, Syndra.E, Syndra.R]

- Arrive early for dragon to chunk opponents or secure a pick, then use Syndra's zone control to make the enemy approach painful.
  sources: [shok]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Play for a lead through CS first and a solo kill second, using Syndra's strong lane and scaling rather than forcing constant fights.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep the combo chapter's video sequences as reference, but in lane prioritize the two-sphere E stun into the level-six burst as the core written pattern.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E, Syndra.R]

- Open Doran's Ring with two Health Potions to support the Q-poke plan.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q]

## Mid lane

- Farm side waves and nearby camps to keep CS high and accelerate item income, balancing that income against reaching objectives on time.
  sources: [shok]

- With Teleport up, push side waves before grouping; without vision, stop short of extending where Syndra can be caught.
  sources: [shok]

## Win condition

- In fights, either finish a vulnerable carry with Unleashed Power or use the sphere setup to land a wide stun for the team.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.R, Syndra.E]

- Save E for the moment it can interrupt a key enemy engage, since getting engaged on is Syndra's main danger.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

- Position carefully and play more cautiously without Flash; strong objective setup plus a well-placed stun is how Syndra's teamfight wins happen.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.E]

## Itemization

- Run Arcane Comet with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, Scorch, Legend: Haste, and Cut Down to pair with the Q-poke plan.
  sources: [shok]
  abilities: [Syndra.Q]

- Build Blackfire Torch into Sorcerer's Shoes, Cosmic Drive, Rabadon's Deathcap, Cryptbloom, and Zhonya's Hourglass, or the Stormsurge, Sorcerer's Shoes, Imperial Mandate, Rabadon's Deathcap, Cryptbloom, Shadowflame second path; add Mercury's Treads into double AP and crowd control.
  sources: [shok]

- Keep Mejai's Soulstealer for snowballs, Morellonomicon for healing, Banshee's Veil for safety, Liandry's Torment for long fights, Horizon Focus for zone control, and Luden's Echo as further options by game need.
  sources: [shok]
