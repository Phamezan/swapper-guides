## Key tips

- Tryndamere can pressure early, so look for aggressive trades and kills when the opponent gives an opening.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Treat Undying Rage as the commitment tool for a real all-in and save it for a window the matchup calls for rather than spending it before the fight matters.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R]

- In late game, punish an isolated or out-of-position enemy and use the resulting numbers advantage to take a major objective or tower.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Build Fury through attacks; a full Fury bar improves his damage and the healing available through Bloodlust.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.Q]

- Keep the wave even or a little on your side when not already ahead, so the opponent has farther to retreat and you expose yourself less to ganks.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Use lane brush to hide the approach and surprise the opponent.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Use Bloodlust to recover after taking trade damage when that sustain helps you remain in lane.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.Q]

- His mobility and ultimate can help him stay on a target or escape, so save an all-in for a window when Undying Rage is ready if the matchup calls for it.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R, Tryndamere.E]

## Mid lane

- Apply split-push pressure around objective timers and challenge the enemy sent to answer you.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Take the duel when the responder is a favorable target; otherwise keep the side-lane pressure and make the enemy spend time answering.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Join the team when there is no useful objective-side split to create.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- With more items, use his dueling strength to keep pressuring side lanes and draw a response.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

## Win condition

- If the enemy groups, group with the team; look for a flank toward an accessible squishy target while staying close enough to rejoin the allies.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Avoid entering a long front-to-back fight where the enemy can kite, disengage, or lock him down; those conditions blunt his access to targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]

- Track Undying Rage availability: he is much easier to stop when the ultimate is down, so avoid forcing a risky fight in that window.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.R]

## Itemization

- Take Hail of Blades, Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, and Treasure Hunter, plus Last Stand and Legend: Alacrity, adjusting if the matchup calls for another setup.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top-build]

- Build the popular item path of Ravenous Hydra, Berserker's Greaves, Phantom Dancer, Infinity Edge, and Lord Dominik's Regards.
  items: [3074, 3006, 3046, 3031, 3036]
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top-build]

- Start Doran's Bow, a Health Potion, and Stealth Ward.
  items: [1086, 2003, 3340]
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top-build]

- Choose Mortal Reminder as the alternative fourth item to Infinity Edge against substantial healing.
  items: [3033, 3031]
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top-build]

- Keep Blade of the Ruined King, Guardian Angel, Death's Dance, Navori Flickerblade, and Mercurial Scimitar as situational choices, matching the defensive or anti-tank option to the opposing team.
  items: [3026, 6333, 6675, 3139]
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top-build]

- Max Q, then E, then W, taking Undying Rage at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-tryndamere-top-build]
  abilities: [Tryndamere.Q, Tryndamere.E, Tryndamere.W, Tryndamere.R]
