## Key tips

- Clear fully into Scuttle, then pick the next fight from what the map actually shows.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Never start Blood Frenzy without a target and direction that favor the fight, and keep E ready to cancel it out.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.E]

- Convert Q picks on isolated enemies into camps, vision, or objectives instead of sitting in long full-team fights.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Try the one-camp invade only when tracking puts the enemy jungler elsewhere and nearby lanes can move.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Counter-gank early skirmishes hard, since Briar duels well once an enemy shows or hits a teammate.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Track the opposing jungler through map sightings and camp states before entering their side.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Close gaps under control with W1 into Q for target priority, then spend W2 only when the return strike stays safe.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.Q]

- Trade across the map when an ally already loses a lane instead of re-spending time on a hopeless rescue.
  sources: [loganjg]

## Mid lane

- Pick skirmishes with teammates or positional edges, and leave drawn-out team brawls early.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Invade only with a known enemy jungler spot plus lane backup nearby.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Before an objective, read the map and wait for the entry Briar team wins.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Steal Dragon with W over the wall into Q priority plus W2 and Smite only as a high-risk last resort.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W, Briar.Q]

- Around Baron, respect its knockback, which can break the W2-plus-Smite timing.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W]

## Win condition

- Snowball informed skirmishes and invades into finished items.
  sources: [loganjg]

- Let the enemy show an opening late, then flank or pick with Certain Death and turn it into an objective or another kill.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.R]

- Stay a calculated skirmisher around camps and objectives; never charge blind into groups or W without an exit.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.W]

- Punish mispositioning with the Q stun and plan every fight around both it and the E escape.
  sources: [loganjg]
  abilities: [Briar.Q, Briar.E]
