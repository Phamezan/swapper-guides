## Key tips

- Path toward bot after the sixth camp, flipping top only when bot loses without priority or the 3v3 looks bad.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Hunt isolated targets with level-6 camouflage and vision angles instead of walking into set front lines.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Burst a carry from a surprise angle, then leave with Last Caress when the fight turns.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- On blue side, open Blue, Smite it when alone or save Smite for Gromp with a leash, then full-clear.
  sources: [gnarz]

- On red side, open Raptors leash-less, then take Red and Krugs.
  sources: [gnarz]

- At level 3 on red side, hold the skill point until top shows gankable; take W for the play, else reset or clear bot-side.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.W]

- After a good level-3 gank, recall for Dark Seal and Amplifying Tome before resuming bot-side.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Open full clears with Q, E, Q, and throw Q over the wall to shorten the Wolves approach.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.Q, Evelynn.E]

- Cast Whiplash near a camp end so its movement speed carries into the next route leg.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.E]

- Land the first Hate Spike, since a miss weakens every follow-up spike.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.Q]

- Fully charge Allure into high-magic-resistance targets so the charm and shred both apply.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.W]

## Mid lane

- Stack Dark Seal through successful ganks, upgrading to Mejai's only with a lead safe enough to guard.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Finish camps with E so Whiplash speed rushes the next play.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.E]

- Keep pathing toward the carry side; swing top when bot cannot contest or the top play runs clearly stronger.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Hover Evelynn in a full-AP draft and ask for an AD mid laner before locking her in.
  sources: [gnarz]

## Win condition

- Snowball safely around picks and guard Mejai's stacks by skipping low-value fights.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Charge W into high-MR targets for its resistance cut before unloading the combo.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.W]

- Plan the Last Caress backward blink so escapes and Baron steals never strand Evelynn out of a winning follow-up.
  sources: [gnarz]
  abilities: [Evelynn.R]

## Itemization

- Run Electrocute with Sudden Impact, Sixth Sense, and Relentless Hunter, plus Triumph and Coup de Grace with adaptive-force and health shards.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Take Flash and Smite, preferring Gustwalker for its movement speed.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Buy Sorcerer's Shoes and Dark Seal early, with Blasting Wand plus Dark Seal as the 1,200-gold fallback.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Core Sorcerer's Shoes, Lich Bane, Mejai's Soulstealer, Rabadon's Deathcap, Void Staff, and Banshee's Veil.
  sources: [gnarz]

- Keep Mejai's only while stacks stay reasonably safe; close with Banshee's Veil or Zhonya's defensively, or Shadowflame for damage after lost stacks.
  sources: [gnarz]
