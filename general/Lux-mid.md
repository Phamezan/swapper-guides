## Key tips

- Last-hit routinely with basic attacks and hold the wave stable; habitual spell clearing wastes mana, invites ganks, and drops farm.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- Spend spells on waves only to secure cannons, catch crashes under tower, or build priority before objectives and roams.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- Follow allied area engages with R across grouped teams instead of saving it purely for single low-health finishes.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]
  abilities: [Lux.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Against melee opponents, auto them on step-up CS attempts and hold a freeze while respecting their threatening spells and extra minion aggro.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- Never donate a valuable last hit just to land one more auto on the opponent.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

## Mid lane

- Roam with pushed waves toward extended bot lanes and no camps worth taking; never leave while the own wave crashes inward.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- On enemy mid roams, ping both sides and hold vision before following; never walk blind into a mobile opponent path.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- Answer unsafe-to-follow roams by shoving mid for plates, banking gold and experience over late arrivals.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- Take nearby Raptors only after pushing out far enough to return, before a healthy mana-filled recall, or on explicit jungler permission.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

## Win condition

- Chip vulnerable opponents with E before teams commit to ease the coming engage.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

- Aim burst at the enemy ADC or mage when possible over the first rooted tank, respecting defensive answers that can block it.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]

- Peel divers off the ADC by shielding with W and rooting attackers with Q so the carry keeps firing.
  sources: [luxthegreywarden]
  abilities: [Lux.W, Lux.Q]
