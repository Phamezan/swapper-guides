## Key tips

- Play AP Irelia as a mid-to-late scaler with explosive area damage across W, E, and R.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.W, Irelia.E, Irelia.R]

- Value passive stacks in lane and early fights on both the AP and auto-attack builds.
  sources: [skandallotv]

- Zone or catch several opponents with R, parking the main target where follow-up damage lands.
  sources: [skandallotv]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Reposition through targets with Q, keeping resets alive when other damage kills the target mid-dash.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Absorb AP dives behind Defiant Dance, whose reduction climbs steeply with AP.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.W]

- Read W offense as physical and weaker into heavy armor, holding it as defense there instead.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.W]

- Stun groups with E, delaying the second blade and opening from out of vision to hide the setup.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.E]

- After a confirmed E stun, charge W at once and follow with R near peak charge.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Irelia.W, Irelia.R]

## Mid lane

- Reposition through fights with Q, resetting on E, R, or low-health targets to keep an exit open.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q, Irelia.E, Irelia.R]

- Engage post-6 with E into W into R, releasing W near its mid-charge damage peak instead of full-holding.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.E, Irelia.W, Irelia.R]

- Start R onto targets without Flash, letting its slow set up the E follow.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.R, Irelia.E]

- When outnumbered, back out through the ultimate area so pursuers eat its damage.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.R]

- Thread E across multiple enemies for squishy threats, since the AP build exists for area burst.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.E]

## Win condition

- Land skillshots before committing, since the build leans on them with few escapes.
  sources: [skandallotv]

- Treat W as a defensive cooldown too, with real squishiness underneath it.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.W]

- Spend the AP burst window directly, saving the auto-attack build for lanes that allow a free split plan.
  sources: [skandallotv]

## Itemization

- Prioritize AP first, then cooldown reduction, sustain and utility, health, magic penetration, and defense in that order.
  sources: [skandallotv]

- Run Electrocute with Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, and Ultimate Hunter plus Manaflow Band and Transcendence, with Flash and Teleport as the safe spells.
  sources: [skandallotv]

- Call Blackfire Torch the highest-damage AP route, Malignance the long-range initiation pick into squishy ranged teams or beside divers, and Lich Bane the melee-target answer.
  items: [2503, 3118, 3100]
  sources: [skandallotv]

- List Hextech Gunblade first or second for sustain and burst, with Lich Bane, Hextech Rocketbelt, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Cryptbloom across the panels.
  items: [3146, 3100, 3152, 3089, 3137]
  sources: [skandallotv]

- Take Rocketbelt second against spell shields that eat poke, valuing its extra dash with no Q target around.
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.Q]

- Buy Liandry's Torment into health stackers since W, E, R, and Q all apply it, and Rabadon's Deathcap third while ahead or into attack-heavy teams.
  items: [6653, 3089]
  sources: [skandallotv]
  abilities: [Irelia.W, Irelia.E, Irelia.R, Irelia.Q]
