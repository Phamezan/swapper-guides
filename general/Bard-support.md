## Key tips

- Survive the first 15 minutes patiently; Bard stays weak early, so refuse forced lane wins.
  sources: [tndd]

- From about 15 to 25 minutes, play as a map and objective champion with early vision and Magical Journey routes.
  sources: [tndd]
  abilities: [Bard.E]

- In late game, catch the enemy carry with Tempered Fate and sync the team follow-up to finish the target.
  sources: [tndd]
  abilities: [Bard.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Track the enemy jungler on the map and bend lane movement around their position.
  sources: [tndd]

- Chain a basic attack into Q, since slowed targets past five chimes line up far more easily.
  sources: [tndd]
  abilities: [Bard.Q]

- Place and clear wards boldly even when enemies sit unseen, trusting Bard escape tools to support deep vision.
  sources: [tndd]

- Check collection against roughly two chimes per game minute without blindly abandoning waves for them.
  sources: [tndd]

## Mid lane

- Reach objectives early to plant vision, opening Bard and teammate routes with Magical Journey.
  sources: [tndd]
  abilities: [Bard.E]

- Spend Tempered Fate as objective control and setup, not as a damage-only button.
  sources: [tndd]
  abilities: [Bard.R]

- Keep gathering chimes through mid game, since meep attacks gain effects including a stronger slow as the count climbs.
  sources: [tndd]

## Win condition

- Let chime-enhanced attacks slow more late, backing the pick plan into the later game.
  sources: [tndd]

- Coordinate the follow-up the moment Tempered Fate lands its brief stasis, and never let it stall allied damage.
  sources: [tndd]
  abilities: [Bard.R]

- Follow the full arc: survive the weak lane, build mid-game plays through roams and vision, then find a carry pick with the team.
  sources: [tndd]
