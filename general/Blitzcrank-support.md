## Key tips

- Hit level 2 first and spend the temporary lead on a Q and E engage.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q, Blitzcrank.E]

- Hold Q until the enemy spends their dash; a miss removes almost all lane pressure while it returns ({cd:53:Q}).
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

- Hook squishy carries late, since dragging a tank hands the enemy a free engage.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep enough mana for Q plus E together so a landed hook turns into the full combo.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q, Blitzcrank.E]

- Roam once the lane sits safe, especially with boots done, but never leave an ADC open to a dive.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]

- At level 6, add R burst and shield removal to the bot skirmish after Q connects.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q, Blitzcrank.R]

## Mid lane

- Roam for Q picks, then call the objective while the caught enemy stays down.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

- Ward mid and the ADC lane path so the team sees incoming engages early.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]

- Ambush rotating opponents, above all a support stepping out to ward.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

- In grouped fights, silence several opponents with R to interrupt the spells the team must reposition around.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.R]

- Let one or two finished defensive items carry frontline duty, but hold a safe spot while Q is down.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

## Win condition

- Turn a post-dash hook into a kill or objective instead of throwing hopeful attempts.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

- Hold vision around Baron and Dragon, but ward with allies late so a lone trip never gets punished.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]

- After a late Q miss, walk back toward teammates instead of forward without backup.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q]

- Open picks with Q, then strip shields and silence rescuers with R.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
  abilities: [Blitzcrank.Q, Blitzcrank.R]

## Itemization

- Buy boots early to unlock stronger roaming across the map.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]

- Add defensive items through the game so Blitzcrank can frontline for teammates.
  sources: [mobalytics-blitzcrank]
