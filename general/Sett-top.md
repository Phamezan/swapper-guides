## Key tips

- Let the opponent's trade pattern choose the major rune, and make Sett's lane goal control enough of the wave to farm and stay useful rather than winning every exchange.
  sources: [autolykus]

- Line up Facebreaker with enemies on both sides of Sett to land the stun instead of only the slow, after sticking to the target with Knuckle Down's movement speed toward visible champions.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.E, Sett.Q]

- Wait for incoming damage before firing Haymaker with its Grit shield, aiming the cone's center at the target for the shield and true-damage payoff.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use the alternating punches' extra range and on-hit effects on the right punch to punish a step-up without overextending.
  sources: [autolykus]

- Save approach tools against kiters until Sett can actually stay on the target; walking in early lets them disengage for free.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.Q, Sett.E]

- Knuckle Down empowers the next two attacks and grants movement speed toward visible champions, so open with it once the all-in is real.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.Q]

## Mid lane

- Build attack damage first and health close behind, since health feeds Haymaker's shield and damage pattern; add attack speed only when Sett can stay on targets.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

- Treat teamfighting as the stronger role: look for flanks or punish an enemy who steps too far forward, since a straight open-vision engage is easy to read.
  sources: [autolykus]

- Dive offensively or answer a dive: turret damage adds Grit, and the resulting shield plus crowd control wins the exchange under the tower.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.W]

## Win condition

- Carry a frontline target into clustered enemies with The Show Stopper, whose slam damages and slows the area while scaling with the grabbed target's bonus health.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.R]

- Ride the enemy frontline's commitment into the backline, then disrupt with Haymaker and Facebreaker once their engage is spent.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.W, Sett.E, Sett.R]

- Reach ranged threats with a flank, Flash, or the ultimate instead of walking through their poke; Sett still matters late but loses some isolated-duel strength, where Blade of the Ruined King buys dueling at the cost of durability.
  sources: [autolykus]
  abilities: [Sett.R]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, Last Stand, Shield Bash, and Second Wind; take Lethal Tempo for sustained duels, Fleet Footwork into poke-and-disengage lanes, Grasp where it can trigger repeatedly, and never Hail of Blades.
  sources: [autolykus]

- Follow the bruiser damage path of Stridebreaker and Black Cleaver, the Blade of the Ruined King route, or the tankier Heartsteel and Titanic Hydra route; value movement speed for starting fights and skip critical strike and unreliable attack speed into kiters.
  sources: [autolykus]
