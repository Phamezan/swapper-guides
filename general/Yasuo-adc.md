## Key tips

- Treat Yasuo bot as weak before level three: prioritize experience and health, accept missing early CS over repeated poke, and hold the wave near the turret while building a slow push to reach three first.
  sources: [visionsofv]

- When one minion remains before level three, take W on level-up, dash in with Exhaust while the support follows, and commit only when the positioning supports the all-in.
  sources: [visionsofv]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- After a successful trade or kill, shove the wave and reset: the guide lists Recurve Bow after a kill, or Cull and a Control Ward when the fight yields no kill.
  items: [1043, 2055]
  sources: [visionsofv]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Ward the middle bot-lane brush before minions meet; when opponents zone from the first lane brush, ask the support to ward it so the pressure stays visible and the wave stays manageable.
  sources: [visionsofv]

- Collect minions from range with Q while setting up the level-three window rather than trading into the stronger early enemy.
  sources: [visionsofv]
  abilities: [Yasuo.Q]

- Run Lethal Tempo with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, Cut Down, Second Wind, and Revitalize, with attack speed, adaptive force, and scaling health shards; prefer Fleet Footwork when heavy poke or control makes extended fights difficult.
  sources: [visionsofv]

## Mid lane

- When ahead, coordinate with the jungler around Dragon and other bot-side objectives; when even, first ward or track enemy jungle and mid positions before looking for the all-in.
  sources: [visionsofv]

- Keep the wave near the turret when seeking a fight, then use Exhaust and dash access to close distance while holding the tornado threat as pressure.
  sources: [visionsofv]
  abilities: [Yasuo.Q, Yasuo.E]

- When behind, wait for enemy mistakes or jungle and mid-lane assistance instead of forcing a fight without a setup.
  sources: [visionsofv]

- Before major late-game objectives, seek a pick and establish vision with Control Wards in high-traffic areas.
  sources: [visionsofv]

- Treat Wind Wall timing as the fight skill: denying projectile abilities at the right moment protects the carry or opens the pick.
  sources: [visionsofv]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

## Win condition

- Convert the level-three lane window and support-assisted pressure into a lead, then turn that lead into objective control and picks.
  sources: [visionsofv]

- In late fights, read the game state to choose the role: deal damage, peel for the team's strongest player, or initiate when the team needs it.
  sources: [visionsofv]

## Itemization

- Build Kraken Slayer, Berserker's Greaves, Immortal Shieldbow, Death's Dance, Infinity Edge, Guardian Angel, and Bloodthirster as the standard line; the snowball example runs Stormrazor, Berserker's Greaves, Immortal Shieldbow, The Collector, Infinity Edge, Blade of the Ruined King, Lord Dominik's Regards, and Death's Dance.
