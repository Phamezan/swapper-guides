- Stack the passive before duels so the opening attacks trigger Double Strike sooner.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

- Bank Alpha Strike against important enemy control, dodging it or following once the threat burns.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Spend Alpha Strike as the gap closer on escaping targets, but never auto-spend protection that could negate damage.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.Q]

- Dive by targeting the champion with Alpha Strike to dodge turret shots through its untargetable travel.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.Q]

- Cut incoming damage with Meditate, including turret hits, to stall for allies or basic-ability cooldowns in bad fights.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.W]

- Hold Meditate to bank Double Strike stacks too, staging a follow-up attack while stalling.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.W, Master Yi.P]

- Commit with Wuju Style active for true damage on every attack.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.E]

- Respect heavy control and untargetable answers as the main disruptors, keeping Alpha Strike banked for key spells where possible.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.Q]

## Mid lane

- Clear by lane states without a dependable first-clear route, ignoring rigid red-start rules as law.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

- Pick exposed squishy targets as the easiest Yi kills.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

- Into multiple tanks, fight sustained front-to-back with Lethal Tempo instead of diving past the line.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

- Farm into item spikes with no safe target around; the guide offers no objective-timing or vision setup.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

- While losing, skip forced isolated duels into control and keep the standard build over the snowball-only crit route.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

## Win condition

- Open on Highlander for a first target and ride refreshed duration only while the fight stays favorable.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.R]

- Dodge dangerous spells with Alpha Strike and return damage rather than leading every fight on it.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.Q]

- Take accessible carries against squishy teams, or grind frontlines with sustained damage into several tanks.
  sources: [bobthedodo]

- Start nothing into layered control or untargetable enemies unless Alpha Strike covers the key response.
  sources: [bobthedodo]
  abilities: [Master Yi.Q]

## Itemization
