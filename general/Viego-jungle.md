## Key tips

- Play the featured crit style for solo-queue burst and snowballing, reaching takedown resets through aggression; save bruiser builds for coordinated competitive games.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Combine efficient farming with pressure on mid and bot rather than treating ganks and farm as mutually exclusive, prioritizing mid skirmishes for snowball and bot pressure for early dragon control.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Skip or delay a camp whenever reaching a fight saves an ally from dying; never treat the full clear as an automatic rule.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Sustain the clear with the passive mark on camps and attacks, keeping health high enough to answer the first fight.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Invade only with vision and a numbers advantage, and emphasize counterganks when teammates sit exposed.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Run Flash and Smite as the jungle summoners on a Conqueror page built for extended skirmishes with repeated attacks and abilities.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

## Mid lane

- Group for dragons and skirmishes through the midgame, then look for isolated targets that can start a possession chain.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Chain these burst strings: W into attack into Q into R, or W into E into attack into Q into attack into R, remembering that R consumes the Q mark so its timing decides the follow-up.
  sources: [harrowedpath]
  abilities: [Viego.W, Viego.Q, Viego.E, Viego.R]

- Keep vision and objective setup in mind when choosing a flank, since Baron and Elder Dragon positioning decides whether Viego gets a safe reset.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

## Win condition

- In late fights stay connected to the team and seek a safe flank onto a priority target, using successful possessions to continue while avoiding getting caught before an opening appears.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Start an alternate string with Q and R before attacks and W where the line fits, but always account for the consumed Q mark in the follow-up plan.
  sources: [harrowedpath]
  abilities: [Viego.Q, Viego.R, Viego.W]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, and Cut Down for takedown healing and attack speed through resets, plus Magical Footwear and Cosmic Insight for boot savings and lower summoner-spell cooldowns.
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Spend by target: The Collector for burst into squishier enemies, Lord Dominik's Regards for armor penetration into durable opponents, and Infinity Edge only as a later high-cost spike since an early buy stalls the curve; avoid Immortal Shieldbow early and use Guardian Angel or Death's Dance when trading damage for survivability.
  items: [6676, 3036, 3031, 6673, 3026, 6333]
  sources: [harrowedpath]

- Choose Synchronized Souls for roaming tempo or Berserker's Greaves for attack speed, with Plated Steelcaps and Mercury's Treads as the safer defensive boots.
  items: [3006, 3047, 3111]
  sources: [harrowedpath]
