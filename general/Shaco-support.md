## Key tips

- Treat area and vision control as the main support value: stack prepared boxes as stored damage and control, up to twenty-one in one area, backed by wards or blue trinket coverage.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

- Contest the middle brush on arrival with Oracle Lens ready, placing a box to block or punish an enemy whose outline shows in a side brush; against a hook support, approach only when reaction timing is reliable.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W, Shaco.Q]

- Begin objective areas about two minutes ahead, shifting toward a blue trinket after level nine so vision stays up while boxes deny entry to the river fight.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- When the enemy contests the center brush, spend a box to deny them the space; when safe, check whether the tower-side brush is warded before returning to the middle brush.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

- Use brush and river entrances as unseen approach points, tracking minion vision and probing reactions with a brief Q feint or a step-out-and-Q-back.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.Q]

- Drill box-blocking and Q buffering in Practice Tool: catching a hook with a box or dodging one yourself buys the ADC room to farm and trade.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W, Shaco.Q]

- Spend the E-then-Q quick trade only when retaliation is unlikely: it lands Two-Shiv with a backstab nearly together but uses Shaco's only escape.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.E, Shaco.Q]

## Mid lane

- Cover a river choke with three boxes as the vision bridge, expanding into the adjacent enemy jungle only while the team can hold that ground.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

- In a split with one ally sidelaning and two mid, place boxes and vision in the connecting jungle to cover both routes and leave fallback traps behind the mid siege.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

- When the team cannot coordinate that split, siege slower while covering both flanks and placing retreat boxes behind the group.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

## Win condition

- Against stronger direct-fight teams, set the side-lane threat before committing: arm boxes first, avoid pushing early, and force opponents to spend time answering it.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W]

- For Baron, prepare the enemy top-side jungle, then draw enemies through the armed area while the team starts it; stay off the objective, distract with the clone or chained fear, and stop the play when the team turns disadvantaged.
  sources: [fearthejester]
  abilities: [Shaco.W, Shaco.R]

- After Baron, reset and threaten a side lane when Teleport is available, or return to the siege and split plan without it.
  sources: [fearthejester]
