## Key tips

- At level 3, hunt exposed, pushed-up lanes for the first gank threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

- At level 6, add the flank and isolation threat to help allies collapse on a target.

- Never start a late fight without R, since control strands him under ranged damage and peel.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep clearing between gank looks so neither XP nor gold income stalls.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

- Invade for enemy camps when lanes grant a safe contest, shrinking the opposing jungler income.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

## Mid lane

- At level 9, fight harder on max-rank Q sustained damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]
  abilities: [Hecarim.Q]

- At level 11, spend the more frequent second-rank R windows on picks.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]
  abilities: [Hecarim.R]

- Approach through jungle fog during lane swaps and ward rotations so enemies spot the engage late.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

- Contest the remaining dragons and Rift Herald, turning nearby skirmish wins into objective control.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

- After committing R, peel back to allies when a second dive turns unsafe, especially from behind.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]
  abilities: [Hecarim.R]

## Win condition

- Split grouped teams with R and isolate a valuable target for allies to finish.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]
  abilities: [Hecarim.R]

- Set vision and ride fog into flanks; established enemy vision answers direct engages too easily.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

- Find a pick first late, then spend the temporary numbers on Baron or a forced fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-hecarim]

## Itemization

- Lean on runes and lifesteal for sustain through fights, adding tenacity where crowd control demands it.
