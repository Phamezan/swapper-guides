## Key tips

- Accept a weaker early game built on last hits and gold, surviving rough matchups until items lift damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]

- Guard W as the key escape; while it stays down, ganks threaten and summoners may be needed to live.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.W]

- After level 6, watch the map and spend R on allied fights and skirmishes elsewhere.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Chip safely with Q and hide E barrel setups inside lane brushes.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.Q, Gangplank.E]

- Protect barrels until detonation, since destroyed barrels erase most early damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.E]

## Mid lane

- Split-push without a forming fight to pull defenders while teammates take space or start plays.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]

- Keep farming into item buys and the gold that upgrades the ultimate.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.R]

- Keep checking the map from the side lane and spend R on ally fights worth backing.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.R]

- Upgrade R as soon as the gold allows; the stronger effect and lower cooldown lift objective fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.R]

- At level 9, press the maxed-ability damage spike on whichever build maxed Q or E.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]

- After the first core item, hunt favorable 1v1s with clearly better dueling.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]

## Win condition

- Pull opponents sidelane with pressure, then let teammates spend the numbers edge on the next play.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]

- Soften and scramble enemies with barrel poke before late fights, waiting for a strong detonation.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.E]

- Refuse fights with R unavailable, since it carries much of his fight impact.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.R]

- Group more late even as a splitter, since nearby allies make barrel follow-ups safer.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]

- Shape neutral-objective fights with the upgraded global R while holding a favorable side-lane trade.
  sources: [mobalytics-gangplank]
  abilities: [Gangplank.R]
