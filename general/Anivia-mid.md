## Key tips

- Play a scaling lane and rush Malignance or Rod of Ages so damage comes online sooner.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Block only caster minions to build a slow push back; a full-wave block works on sides, not mid.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.W]

- Set R and use W to drag the dragon behind the pit wall for a hidden take when the area sits unwarded.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.R, Anivia.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Land the quick Q and E combo so Frostbite connects as Flash Frost arrives, before targets like Gangplank can cleanse the stun.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q, Anivia.E]

- Spend short trade combos to punish a bad step and trigger Electrocute or Phase Rush.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q, Anivia.E]

- Drop W behind the turret to stall minions and farm safely, but expect the wave to slow-push toward the enemy.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.W]

- Never block waves on autopilot, since a bad push can expose Anivia or park the wave where she cannot hold it.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.W]

- Start Doran's Ring with a Health Potion, or Refillable Potion plus Dark Seal as the alternate open.
  sources: [aniviabot]

## Mid lane

- Spike first on Malignance; buy Rod of Ages before or after it for safer scaling with health and mana.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Play around the stacked Rod of Ages level gain as a major experience jump.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Add Liandry's Torment later for sustained damage next to Rod durability.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Take Cosmic Drive later for movement and haste, pairing it with Approach Velocity and upgraded Ionian Boots.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Swap Cosmic Drive for Zhonya's Hourglass into heavy dive, since the ultimate keeps running through stasis.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.R]

- Use W to steer side waves, forcing a push or breaking an enemy freeze.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.W]

## Win condition

- Scale through the first mana and damage item, then fight heavier around the Rod of Ages level spike.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Control space around fights and objectives with Q, E, R, and W instead of chasing a lane-kill plan.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q, Anivia.E, Anivia.R, Anivia.W]

- Attempt hidden W objective setups only with clear vision, since the trick fails when the pit or approach is warded.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.W]

## Itemization

- Run Stormraider's Surge with Manaflow Band, Celerity, and Gathering Storm, plus Triple Tonic and Approach Velocity, as the displayed page.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Rush Malignance for earlier damage, or Rod of Ages first for safer health and mana scaling, then complete the other as the second core.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Add Liandry's Torment as the later mid-setup damage item, and Morellonomicon when the team lacks Grievous Wounds.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Pick Zhonya's into heavy dive, Cosmic Drive for movement and haste, and Banshee's Veil as the alternate defense.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Wear Sorcerer's Shoes for damage, Mercury's Treads for resistance and tenacity, Ionian Boots for ability and summoner haste, and Plated Steelcaps into physical auto attackers.
  sources: [aniviabot]

- Consider Fimbulwinter as a rare durability pick, since Q or W can trigger its shield.
  sources: [aniviabot]
  abilities: [Anivia.Q, Anivia.W]

- Take Flash and Ignite, opening Doran's Ring or Refillable Potion plus Dark Seal.
