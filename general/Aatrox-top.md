## Key tips

- Stay safe before level 4 and farm for experience; the second Q rank at level 4 is a major damage spike.
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

- Treat mid game as the strongest phase and use a lead to press side lanes toward objectives.
  sources: [forlid]

- Activate R only when its movement speed, bonus damage, and healing help reach or survive the fight, and chase a takedown to extend it.
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Prioritize farming and experience over forcing trades while weak before level 4.
  sources: [forlid]

- Keep Q ready since its early cooldown is long; a missed cast leaves Aatrox without pressure while it returns ({cd:266:Q}).
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q]

- Avoid risky early trades and keep health high for the level 4 and level 6 spikes.
  sources: [forlid]

- Give space to Darius and Sett when they step close, since both punish Aatrox inside their range.
  sources: [forlid]

- When the jungler plays the other side, hold the wave instead of pushing and ward the river.
  sources: [forlid]

- Take Void Grubs with the jungler when topside and holding lane priority.
  sources: [forlid]

- Cast E during Q to move the sweet spot onto the target rather than spending the dash on its own.
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.E]

- Pair W with the first or second Q cast so the tether slows a target trying to walk out.
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.Q, Aatrox.W]

## Mid lane

- Use the mid-game window to extend a lead rather than stalling on even farm.
  sources: [forlid]

- Push a side lane to build pressure, then rotate to an open objective or a teammate who needs help.
  sources: [forlid]

- Ward key map areas before walking into the river or enemy territory, and skip fights without vision.
  sources: [forlid]

- Track the enemy top laner while split so the push can be answered or the next objective joined in time.
  sources: [forlid]

- In teamfights, dive the enemy carry on an assassin setup or soak and sustain for the team on a bruiser setup.
  sources: [forlid]

- Take Teleport to rejoin fights from a side lane, or Ignite for extra kill pressure without that map option.
  sources: [forlid]

## Win condition

- Start late fights with R ready and group around Baron and Elder Dragon.
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

- Pick engages or peel based on the fight; protect carries late even when the team expects an engage.
  sources: [forlid]

- Stop splitting late and never enter a fight without R, since little threat remains while it is down.
  sources: [forlid]
  abilities: [Aatrox.R]

- Stay alive through late objective setups, since a long death timer can hand the enemy a decisive window.
  sources: [forlid]

## Itemization

- Take Conqueror for extended fights, since its stacking damage also feeds Aatrox healing.
  sources: [forlid]

- Take Triumph so a takedown can heal through a teamfight, a 1v2, or a tower dive.
  sources: [forlid]

- Take Alacrity into low-mobility lanes for more autos, or Haste when ability haste matters more.
  sources: [forlid]

- Take Bone Plating into melee lanes with little poke, or Second Wind with Doran's Shield into ranged and poke lanes.
  items: [1054]
  sources: [forlid]

- Take Revitalize to raise healing and the Sterak's Gage shield on bruiser and lethality setups.
  items: [3053]
  sources: [forlid]

- Open Sundered Sky or Spear of Shojin first; pick Voltaic Cyclosword into mobile targets, Eclipse for duels, and Black Cleaver into tanks.
  items: [6610, 3161, 6699, 6692, 3071]
  sources: [forlid]

- Follow with bruiser survival such as Sterak's Gage and Death's Dance, plus Maw of Malmortius against burst AP.
  items: [3053, 6333, 3156]
  sources: [forlid]

- Match boots and defense to the lane: Plated Steelcaps into AD, Mercury's Treads into AP and crowd control, Hexdrinker against AP burst.
  items: [3047, 3111, 3155]
  sources: [forlid]
