## Key tips

- Shove safely, then poke E so the enemy ADC picks between eating damage and collecting farm.
  sources: [guest-653465, henrysjg]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

- Play without a reliable escape in these setups, keeping distance after spending Q or E.
  sources: [guest-653465]
  abilities: [Lux.Q, Lux.E]

- Hunt catches and burst over front-to-back anchoring; Lux never plays the sustained team anchor.
  sources: [guest-653465]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Match E poke to First Strike windows and repeat the trade as the rune returns.
  sources: [henrysjg]
  abilities: [Lux.E]

- Spend the passive mark with a safe basic attack whenever one opens.
  sources: [guest-653465]

## Mid lane

- Ride Shurelya's Battlesong plus Boots of Swiftness movement to reposition, escape, and rotate faster.
  items: [2065, 3009]
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Track enemy squishies and ward likely catch routes, since vision sets up Lux own picks.
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Watch the minimap and rotate after shoves; supports never need to stay for last hits.
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Stay off enemy vision before catch attempts the team can follow.
  sources: [guest-653465]

## Win condition

- Bind and burst squishy targets, then leave on movement speed before opponents turn back.
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Check the team needs an AP damage support before locking Lux, as the guide frames composition first.
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Snowball early leads and close before prolonged fights, where she fades.
  sources: [guest-653465]

## Itemization

- Run Aery with Manaflow Band, Celerity, and Scorch plus Cheap Shot and Relentless Hunter, picking movement speed for map safety and speed.
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Build Shurelya's Battlesong into Boots of Swiftness and Stormsurge, recovering AP cheaply with Dark Seal after the mobility rush.
  items: [2065, 3009, 4646, 1082]
  sources: [guest-653465]

- Run Arcane Comet with Manaflow Band, Absolute Focus, and Gathering Storm plus Cash Back and Jack of All Trades on the HenrySJG page, with First Strike as the repeated-poke and gold alternative.
  sources: [henrysjg]

- Build Stormsurge first for burst into Sorcerer's Shoes and Shadowflame, then Rabadon's Deathcap and Cryptbloom in the order that completes Jack of All Trades and Cash Back bonuses efficiently.
  items: [4646, 3020, 4645, 3089, 3137]
  sources: [henrysjg]

- Pick Zaz'Zak's Realmspike as the default support item, or Celestial Opposition into assassins.
  items: [3871, 3869]
  sources: [henrysjg]
