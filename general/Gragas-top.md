## Key tips

- Unlock the full rotation at level 3 for a stronger all-in when the matchup allows.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]

- At level 6, displace targets with R into tower range or isolation during a gank.

- Frontline and start fights on a tank build, or land the combo from range on an AP build.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Poke with Q while clearing the wave in the same cast.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.Q]

- Guard E carefully when pushed up and ward before aggressive casts, since the jungler punishes its long cooldown and forward spot.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.E]

- Beat minion blocks on E with lane-brush angles or Flash for a clean engage.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.E]

- Read the first item fork: a tank route survives longer while an AP route swaps durability for damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]

## Mid lane

- Help the jungler take Rift Herald or invade whenever the lane state frees Gragas.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]

- Split enemies from their team with R in fights, finishing immobile targets without escape first.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.R]

- Roam mid on wave time, since helping the mid laner can buy later support for top.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]

- At level 9, duel harder on the first maxed ability, strongest with an AP route.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]

- At level 11, initiate and displace more often with the second R point.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.R]

- Hunt clumped areas and neutral objectives where R disrupts several opponents.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.R]

## Win condition

- Peel for carries first, then combo a safe target and return to the carries.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]

- Find enemy-jungle picks with the jungler through R displacement.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.R]

- Build a death brush with teammates and drag an unsuspecting target into range with R.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.R]

- At level 16, pick high-priority targets with the final R rank and its extra damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-gragas]
  abilities: [Gragas.R]
