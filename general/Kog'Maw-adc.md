## Key tips

- Respect early vulnerability with farm and experience first, skipping extended fights in the opening levels.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

- Gate aggression on W: each point improves the damage window, so trade only with W up and a range edge, longer over short after the first item.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw-build]
  abilities: [Kog'Maw.W]

- Stay grouped by the support at all times; peel converts directly into damage while isolation means death.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

## Overview

## Early lane

- At level 6, soften low-health targets with long-range R poke, conserving mana since the AD setup rates its combat value limited.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]
  abilities: [Kog'Maw.R]

- Check suspicious bushes with R instead of face-checking them on foot.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw-build]
  abilities: [Kog'Maw.R]

- After the bot outer turret, rotate mid and pressure that tower for safer map movement.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

## Mid lane

- Force nothing while behind, especially with Baron open for the enemy.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

- Commit to aggression only with nearby allies ready to respond and protect.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

- Hold behind the frontline and support instead of walking forward to extend chases.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

## Win condition

- Fight late at maximum attack range while moving with the team, dealing damage without isolation.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

- Shave enemies pre-fight with W range and R poke from safe positions.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]
  abilities: [Kog'Maw.W, Kog'Maw.R]

- Start nothing without peel against engage and control.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw]

## Itemization

- Run Flash and Barrier with Lethal Tempo, Triumph, Legend: Bloodline, and Last Stand plus Conditioning and Overgrowth on attack-speed, adaptive-force, and health-scaling shards.
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw-build]

- Order Guinsoo's Rageblade, Berserker's Greaves, Navori Flickerblade, Terminus, Jak'Sho, and Runaan's Hurricane while maxing W, then Q, then E.
  items: [3124, 3006, 6675, 3302, 3085]
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw-build]
  abilities: [Kog'Maw.W, Kog'Maw.Q, Kog'Maw.E]

- Open Blade of the Ruined King early for sustain and high-health targets, pairing it later with Guinsoo's, Runaan's, Terminus, and Jak'Sho.
  items: [3302]
  sources: [mobalytics-kogmaw-build]
