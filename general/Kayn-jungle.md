## Key tips

- Choose the form before the game: Shadow Assassin into several reachable squishies, Rhaast for frontline, sustain, and long fights.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Respect early vulnerability with clean farm and realistic ganks, never forcing bad fights for form orbs.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- After unlocking form, convert the early lead into ganks, picks, objective pressure, and farm between plays.
  sources: [ractickttv]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open reliably with a full clear, contesting Scuttle when right before the first real gank or invade window.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Start three or four camps fast when nearby lanes offer control, kill pressure, or a volatile matchup worth touching early.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Check nearby lane priority before invading; even winning jungle duels fail when lanes cannot move first.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- After Scuttle with nothing on, take a safe respawning camp or recall instead of forcing a low-odds gank.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Keep clearing respawns after the first recall while watching for real gank windows; powerfarming beats risky form chasing.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Drop the stealth ward before the first recall and usually swap to Oracle Lens after, keeping yellow only for a planned invade.
  items: [3364]
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Skill three early Q points into maxed W, then finish Q and max E last; Rhaast may prioritize Q sooner into tankier teams for its max-health damage.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.Q, Kayn.W, Kayn.E]

- Hold W briefly to land it more often, and aim Q into terrain to shorten its animation in clears and fights.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.W, Kayn.Q]

- Stretch E exits with Q as Shadow Step expires to land on the intended terrain side.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.Q, Kayn.E]

## Mid lane

- Flank or reach isolated carries with Shadow Assassin E, then push the lead through picks, objectives, and enemy-jungle pressure.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.E]

- Approach blue-form teamfights from the side or backline, scouting and poking late with E and W before committing.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.E, Kayn.W]

- Hunt 3v3 and 4v4 skirmishes and objective fights on Rhaast where area damage, knock-up, and healing hit several enemies.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Disrupt groups with Rhaast W while cycling Q and sustaining through the fight.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.W, Kayn.Q]

- Hold Rhaast R for peak value: low health or a high-health target for bigger healing, a key ability dodge, and a live return.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.R]

- Match every mid and late plan to the form: high-value plays, objectives, farm between chances.
  sources: [ractickttv]

## Win condition

- Snowball reachable squishies fast on blue before defensive items blunt the picks.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- Grind extended fights on red against melee or tankier teams with repeated Q and W damage plus healing.
  sources: [ractickttv]
  abilities: [Kayn.Q, Kayn.W]

- Keep form aligned to access and need: blue for squishy carries, red for frontline and sustained teamfighting.
  sources: [ractickttv]

- While behind or gank-less, farm into the form and refuse shutdown donations through forced plays.
  sources: [ractickttv]
