## Key tips

- Start no fights personally; follow ally engages and guard whoever can carry them.
  sources: [mystral]

- Answer dives with E shield and W polymorph on reaction timing rather than on cooldown.
  sources: [mystral]
  abilities: [Lulu.E, Lulu.W]

- Behind ADCs, move every save to the stronger teammate instead of defaulting to the ADC.
  sources: [mystral]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade back into squishy poke supports, but against Lux hold approaches until her spells run out.
  sources: [mystral]

- Into Leona or Thresh, steal autos while they last-hit or trade; on a Leona engage, shield the ADC, polymorph her partner, retreat, and slow pursuers with Q.
  sources: [mystral]
  abilities: [Lulu.E, Lulu.W, Lulu.Q]

- With no wards up, refuse pushes and sit behind minions to cut gank exposure.
  sources: [mystral]

## Mid lane

- Ward the nearby jungle around the group against surprise flanks, especially ambush and long-range engage champions.
  sources: [mystral]

- Sit behind the team near the ADC, shielding the frontline through poke while tracking the enemy carry or assassin.
  sources: [mystral]
  abilities: [Lulu.E]

- Slow advancing enemies with Q and back away, speeding retreating allies with W.
  sources: [mystral]
  abilities: [Lulu.Q, Lulu.W]

- Set dragon vision when opponents start it fast or the team plans a contest.
  sources: [mystral]

## Win condition

- Time E and W responses over cooldown spam when guarding damage dealers.
  sources: [mystral]
  abilities: [Lulu.E, Lulu.W]
