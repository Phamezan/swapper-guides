## Key tips

- Farm safely to level 6 and refuse repeated pre-R trades that park Kassadin too close to opponents.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]
  abilities: [Kassadin.R]

- After the first item, shove waves when safe and hunt kill chances in other lanes.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

- At level 16, spend the third R rank on repeated picks and backline access.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]
  abilities: [Kassadin.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Ward early because weak-lane Kassadin invites camps and farm denial.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

- Use unlocked R both to trade and to disengage when exchanges turn bad.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]
  abilities: [Kassadin.R]

## Mid lane

- Farm gold and experience over forcing low-value fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

- Take only favoring fights; with grouped enemies and a bad approach, return to farming.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

- When the ADC and support rotate mid, farm side waves for free resources without contesting center.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

- At level 11, burst harder with the second R rank and its more frequent access.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]
  abilities: [Kassadin.R]

- At level 9, answer ganks and all-ins better with the first maxed ability and its higher damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

## Win condition

- Flank mispositioned targets for the burst, then R out before the collapse lands.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]
  abilities: [Kassadin.R]

- Turn each pick into a push or objective instead of chasing further.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

- Group with a team that wins grouped fights; otherwise pull opponents sidelane while allies press objectives elsewhere.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]

## Itemization

- Reach first-item and later spikes through safe farming.
  sources: [mobalytics-kassadin]
