## Key tips

- Win the level-2 race and all-in through it, engaging only with the ADC able to follow.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]

- At level 6, threaten reliable picks by saving R for targets the team can reach and finish.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]
  abilities: [Leona.R]

- After engaging, turn back to peel the ADC instead of chasing past the team.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Stay healthy through ranged poke, stepping outside auto range while key abilities cycle.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]

- Hold the wave even or near your side; a permanently pushed wave kills engage angles.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona-build]

- Commit to all-ins fully or not at all; never dive in with allies too far to follow.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]

- Clear wards fast with the AA, Q, AA sequence to deny vision.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona-build]
  abilities: [Leona.Q]

- Roam from base or while the ADC sits safely, using R availability to pressure mid.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]
  abilities: [Leona.R]

## Mid lane

- Group with the team and ward only with enough enemy-position knowledge to avoid pre-objective picks.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]

- Punish overextended walk-ups with R catches that start fights or buy objective windows.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]
  abilities: [Leona.R]

## Win condition

- Start fights the allies can follow, then disrupt the nearest threat with Q and E to shield carries.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]
  abilities: [Leona.Q, Leona.E]

- Stay glued to the ADC in late fights, falling back to the nearest enemy after the initial engage.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]

- Against scattered opponents, catch one with R and convert the pick into a fight or objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona]
  abilities: [Leona.R]

## Itemization

- Run Flash and Ignite with Aftershock, Font of Life, Bone Plating, and Unflinching plus Biscuit Delivery and Cosmic Insight secondary.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona-build]

- Take Ability Haste and Health Scaling shards, bending the defensive shard to the lane matchup.
  sources: [mobalytics-leona-build]

- Order Celestial Opposition, Plated Steelcaps, Locket of the Iron Solari, Knight's Vow, Bandlepipes, and Thornmail while maxing W, then E, then Q.
  items: [3869, 3047, 3190, 3109, 2524, 3075]
  sources: [mobalytics-leona-build]
  abilities: [Leona.W, Leona.E, Leona.Q]

- Swap Mercury's Treads or other defense for Steelcaps by enemy damage and control, with Protoplasm Harness as the fourth-item tank alternative.
  items: [3111, 2525]
  sources: [mobalytics-leona-build]
