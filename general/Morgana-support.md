## Key tips

- Poke to build a health advantage first, then commit only once the enemy is low enough for an all-in.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]

- Throw Dark Binding only on a likely hit: account for minions blocking it and wait for a clear path or a target that has stepped out from behind the wave.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.Q]

- After taking the bottom outer tower, rotate mid to help open the map and claim the next tower.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use Dark Binding for picks or favorable trades only when a hit is likely; a missed binding leaves the lane without pressure and Morgana exposed.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.Q]

- Against a strong level-one engage support, start Black Shield instead of binding or pool so you can block the opening crowd control.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.E]

- Level three is a spike because Morgana can then combine crowd control, damage, and ally protection.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.Q, Morgana.W, Morgana.E]

- Save Black Shield for a carry who is about to be hit by hard crowd control instead of spending the shield without a clear threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.E]

- At level six, shield the primary carry before entering range to use Soul Shackles in the stronger teamfight.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.E, Morgana.R]

## Mid lane

- Stay with teammates while moving around the map; solo warding and traveling alone make support Morgana easy to catch.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]

- Set vision around the next major objective early and retreat to safety once the area is covered.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]

- For picks, wait until a target uses its dash or escape before throwing Dark Binding; approaching to bait that mobility out first also works.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.Q]

- Use Dark Binding from fog or a jungle brush to catch an immobile target, then convert the death timer into tower or objective pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.Q]

- A second point in Soul Shackles is a cooldown and fight-frequency spike, so coordinate fights around the availability of the ultimate.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.R]

## Win condition

- In late game, ward objectives and high-traffic paths so the team can see opponents moving or starting the objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]

- Do not ward alone unless enemy positions are known; getting caught leaves the team to fight outnumbered.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]

- Peel for allies to keep them alive, using Black Shield against hard crowd control and chaining Dark Binding into Soul Shackles onto a threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.E, Morgana.Q, Morgana.R]

- Use Dark Binding picks to start a numbers advantage, then take an objective, a tower, or additional safe kills before the enemy respawns.
  sources: [mobalytics-morgana-support]
  abilities: [Morgana.Q]
