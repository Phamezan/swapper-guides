## Key tips

- Open Raptors into a full clear, then run a second Raptors and Krugs cycle before the first recall.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Spend that recall on Dark Seal plus Blasting Wand, upgrading to Mejai's only when stacks stay safe or a risky comeback demands it.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Play tank Diana to absorb pressure through defenses aimed at the damage that can actually reach her.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take Flash and Smite, treating Flash as the practical escape tool.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Start the Dusk and Dawn path through Blasting Wand, Ruby Crystal, Dagger, and Sheen.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- With no lane-state gank route or objective schedule given, pick a full-clear direction that tracks the enemy jungler and keeps a nearby lane reachable.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

## Mid lane

- Buy Riftmaker second on the tank setup for damage that scales with the defense bought afterward.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Default to Plated Steelcaps, switching to Mercury's Treads once enemy crowd control justifies them.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Buy Randuin's Omen into multiple critical threats and Thornmail into healing or on-hit attackers.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Take Unending Despair as the general armor pick outside those cases, usually with Spirit Visage unless Kaenic Rookern fits better.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Favor Spirit Visage as the general magic-resistance pick around allied healing or shielding, and Kaenic Rookern when burst protection matters more.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Reserve Force of Nature for heavy damage-over-time or burn teams, skipping it when Diana dies before its stacking pays off.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- When even full defense cannot survive, pivot toward the bruiser damage setup instead.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

## Win condition

- Pick AP Diana through Nashor's Tooth, Sorcerer's Shoes, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Zhonya's Hourglass when the team needs offense.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- On AP Diana, buy Zhonya's for dives, especially when stasis lets Moonfall damage resolve before displacements land.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]
  abilities: [Diana.R]

- Protect the Dark Seal lead and never rush Mejai's while deaths remain likely.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

## Itemization

- Run the tank page of Conqueror, Triumph, Legend: Haste, and Cut Down with Absolute Focus and Waterwalking.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Build Dusk and Dawn into Plated Steelcaps and Riftmaker, then two defenses from Randuin's Omen, Kaenic Rookern, Spirit Visage, Force of Nature, Thornmail, Frozen Heart, and the other listed options.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Add Spirit Visage with enchanter or healing support behind the long-fight plan, or Kaenic Rookern into concentrated burst.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]

- Start the alternate AP page on Nashor's Tooth, Sorcerer's Shoes, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Zhonya's when Diana can survive the entry and the team needs damage.
  sources: [bullwhipgriffin]
