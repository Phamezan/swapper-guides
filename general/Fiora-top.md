## Key tips

- Run the aggressive setup of Press the Attack with Flash and Ignite for early duel pressure.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Spend Riposte as a damage trading tool too, blocking spikes like Riven R, Jax W, or Jayce Q when they threaten.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

- Split as the default win path and group only for clearly favorable teamfights.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat most lanes as even or losing and hunt an outplay instead of assuming free wins.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Skip picking Fiora without confidence in her lane patterns.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Play for the two-item point, which usually passes the lane opponent; refuse risky early all-ins before it arrives.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

## Mid lane

- After laning, take a standing top outer with Ravenous Hydra, spending R when an opponent contests.
  items: [3074]
  sources: [dracoiwnl]
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- Heal heavily with the Hydra active inside a wave, then take long trades while it sustains.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Once the outer falls, move to an inner-tower lane and keep banking gold and experience there.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Track who can stop the split, since opponents arriving before wave healing still win the trade.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

## Win condition

- Farm sides and press towers until gold and experience outscale most top laners.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Land R on a fighter to raise the healing zone for Fiora and nearby teammates.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- Make sure R actually fires in teamfights; dying first likely loses the fight.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]
  abilities: [Fiora.R]

- With Death's Dance and Plated Steelcaps, play front-to-back more safely while Riposte stays up.
  items: [6333, 3047]
  sources: [dracoiwnl]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

- Flash onto a squishy or low-health finisher when it converts, keeping W ready against burst.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]
  abilities: [Fiora.W]

## Itemization

- Run Press the Attack with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, and Last Stand, plus Biscuit Delivery and Jack of All Trades with two adaptive shards for stronger early trades.
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Rush Ravenous Hydra first for waveclear, sustain, and taking the top turret.
  items: [3074]
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Follow the example build of Trinity Force with Plated Steelcaps, Death's Dance, Spear of Shojin, and Endless Hunger.
  items: [3078, 3047, 6333, 3161, 2517]
  sources: [dracoiwnl]

- Swap to Mercury's Treads and Maw of Malmortius against a strong AP threat, else keep Plated Steelcaps.
