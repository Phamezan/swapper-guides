## Key tips

- Check the full trade list before committing: level advantage, available summoners and key cooldowns, support follow-up, rune strength, and minion count. When several run against Yunara, farm safely and wait for a better window.
  sources: [infinem]

- Treat the charged Q as the level-one two-versus-two tool: build stacks with basic attacks, champion hits adding more than minion hits, and only fight with the empowerment ready.
  sources: [infinem]
  abilities: [Yunara.Q]

- Default to a slow push by last-hitting into a big crash wave; hard-push only to clear quickly and freeze only to hold the wave near the turret, running the two-wave plan of slow-push first wave into hard-push second wave.
  sources: [infinem]

## Overview

## Early lane

- At six Q stacks, trade when a champion hit finishes the stacks, activating Q to use its attack reset inside the exchange.
  sources: [infinem]
  abilities: [Yunara.Q]

- Treat level two as a two-player race, but never spend too much health contesting it when the support's level-one setup is weaker; repeat that judgment at later level-up windows.
  sources: [infinem]

- Prioritize attack damage in early component buys since Q supplies the attack speed; take Doran's Bow for the smoother start or Doran's Blade for stronger raw lane-trading stats.
  items: [1086, 1055]
  sources: [infinem]
  abilities: [Yunara.Q]

- Run Lethal Tempo with Triumph, Legend: Bloodline, Cut Down, Cash Back, and Triple Tonic for extended trades, with attack speed, adaptive force, and base health shards.
  sources: [infinem]

## Mid lane

- Stay in bot lane until the lane quest completes to collect its extra minion and takedown gold, expected around ten to fifteen minutes into the game.
  sources: [infinem]

- After the quest, move mid by default when the bot tower is down so the pair can reach either side, ward, and contest objectives; stay bot when its tower or an imminent Dragon makes that the better play.
  sources: [infinem]

- After pushing a wave, spend the time before the next one warding, preparing for an objective, taking a safe jungle camp, recalling, or joining a team move, aligning vision with the area the team intends to play around.
  sources: [infinem]

- Never push extended side lanes without vision, and choose safe routes back to teammates so neither Flash nor the ultimate gets spent just crossing fog.
  sources: [infinem]
  abilities: [Yunara.R]

- Recall well before an objective, then help the support establish vision so the team arrives present and informed for the setup fight.
  sources: [infinem]

- Treat the charged Q and the level-six ultimate as the skirmish spikes, and completed legendary items as the fight windows.
  sources: [infinem]
  abilities: [Yunara.Q, Yunara.R]

## Win condition

- Build through the crit curve and use the extended objective fights for sustained attacks, taking only trades and skirmishes with enough support, cooldowns, and numbers advantage.
  sources: [infinem]

- In teamfights, hit the nearest safe target and track enemy engage and burst cooldowns, growing aggressive only as the abilities that threaten survival go used.
  sources: [infinem]

- Preserve health and positioning for objective fights, choosing presence, vision, and front-to-back damage over an isolated split-push plan.
  sources: [infinem]
