## Key tips

- Respect the weak early game: control the lane bushes and count the minions to level two before deciding whether an engage is actually available.
  sources: [daltonfresh]

- Punish from the bushes when the enemy ADC locks into a last-hit animation or wastes an ability: W in, add an auto and Q, then E back to safety, extending with a second auto only when it is safe.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.W, Rakan.Q, Rakan.E]

- From level six, roam when the ADC is safe or ahead, cast R during E travel to hide the approach, and keep Flash ready for a fast R or W engage.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.R, Rakan.E, Rakan.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take the opening only when opponents offer it: engage with W and E or Q and W, and otherwise keep W available to threaten or to answer an engage.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.W, Rakan.Q, Rakan.E]

- After a winning trade crashes the wave, spend the time on a river or enemy-jungle ward; after a lost trade, wait for a safer window instead of forcing another attempt.
  sources: [daltonfresh]

- Start Q, then max W followed by E, with Flash and Heal as the standard summoners on the Guardian page.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.Q, Rakan.W, Rakan.E]

## Mid lane

- Buy Oracle Lens after the first recall and use it to pressure bushes and clear river vision.
  items: [3364]
  sources: [daltonfresh]

- Roam to help mid, assist the jungler around Grubs, or plant deeper vision when the ADC can hold alone; when roaming would expose the ADC, stay to ward entries, cover dragon, or set up the jungler's approach.
  sources: [daltonfresh]

- Check mid after every wave crash: a pushed-up opponent plus Rakan's movement and crowd control is a quick gank before returning bot.
  sources: [daltonfresh]

- Against enemies who can dodge or interrupt a direct R-W entry, play as secondary engage or counter-engage instead of starting the fight yourself.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.R, Rakan.W]

## Win condition

- Play as the disruptor who charms several opponents and knocks them up, but generally let a teammate start the fight so allies can follow the entrance.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.R, Rakan.W]

- Pick the mode each game: engage favorable bot skirmishes, roam when the ADC holds safely, or save W and E to peel and counter-engage.
  sources: [daltonfresh]
  abilities: [Rakan.W, Rakan.E]

- Keep enough ability haste and health to repeat engage and disengage cycles; Rakan cannot fight alone and always needs allied follow-up.
  sources: [daltonfresh]

## Itemization

- Follow the patch 26.21 meta example of Celestial Opposition, Dark Seal, Ionian Boots of Lucidity, Zeke's Convergence, Bandlepipes, Locket of the Iron Solari, and Knight's Vow, and avoid dying while building Dark Seal's value.

  sources: [daltonfresh]
