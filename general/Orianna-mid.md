## Key tips

- Into ranged lanes, run Comet with three early ranks in W so Q-W trades hit hard, but respect those cooldowns and track ganks while pressuring.
  sources: [shok-orianna]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q, Orianna.W]

- Into melee, harass with Q often for its low cost, and after level three hold the wave near your side so divers have less room to run you down.
  sources: [shok-orianna]
  abilities: [Orianna.Q]

- Fight front to back with tanks ahead: a threatening ball and a held Shockwave constrain enemy carries, so deliver it on an engager or keep it to shield and peel your ADC.
  sources: [shok-orianna]
  abilities: [Orianna.E, Orianna.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- If mana is already low at level two against ranged opponents, stop leaning on W and wait for its second rank at level three before trading heavily again.
  sources: [shok-orianna]
  abilities: [Orianna.W]

- Build the small CS lead Orianna can take in most matchups by tying every trade to a safe last hit rather than trading for its own sake.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Treat level six as a lane tool: Shockwave adds a poke and kill threat against ranged laners who previously felt safe.
  sources: [shok-orianna]
  abilities: [Orianna.R]

## Mid lane

- Keep farm and experience flowing while arriving for every major objective fight; never sacrifice the wave timer for a rotation with no payoff.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Push a side wave only as far as the map looks safe, tracking both junglers before extending past the river line.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Without Teleport, group and collect side waves as they reach your tower; take longer side assignments only with an easy matchup, a team lead, and vision.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Recall roughly a minute before dragon to spend gold and restore health and mana, then group with a full inventory for the setup.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Once Seraph's Embrace is built, clear nearby friendly jungle camps, and consider an enemy camp when your jungler is far away and the area is safe.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

## Win condition

- Use narrow chokes and small spaces to limit where opponents can move, keeping the ball threat over the fight while your frontline holds.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Hit the frontline by default, but switch the moment a squishy steps out of position rather than tunneling the nearest target.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Hold position against dangerous enemy tools: wait out effects such as a hook before committing, since they punish Orianna's positioning hardest.
  sources: [shok-orianna]

## Itemization

- Run Arcane Comet with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, and Scorch plus Legend: Haste and Cut Down; open Doran's Ring and Health Potion, with Tear and two potions as the greedy alternative.
  items: [1056, 2003]
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Build Seraph's Embrace into Sorcerer's Shoes, Shadowflame, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Cryptbloom, finishing with Zhonya's Hourglass; take Mercury's Treads into double AP and crowd control.
  items: [3020, 4645, 3089, 3137, 3157, 3111]
  sources: [shok-orianna]

- Adapt the last slots by threat: Morellonomicon for healing, Void Staff or Cryptbloom into magic resistance, Zhonya's against physical burst, Banshee's against dangerous spells, with Mejai's, Liandry's, Cosmic Drive, or Lich Bane for snowball, long-fight, mobility, or attack-weave games.
  items: [3165, 3135, 3137, 4629, 3100]
