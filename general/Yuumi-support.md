## Key tips

- Play this as the damage and anti-heal setup for games with substantial enemy healing or fragile targets to pressure, accepting weaker healing and shielding in return.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Harass with Q and the passive while helping restore the ADC's health, staying patient and conserving mana early instead of casting continuously.
  sources: [hoffmann]
  abilities: [Yuumi.Q]

- Save E for the most important incoming damage or a critical save rather than spending it casually.
  sources: [hoffmann]
  abilities: [Yuumi.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Respect the detached vulnerability: check bushes with Q range before approaching, and ward from a safe distance or alongside an ally.
  sources: [hoffmann]
  abilities: [Yuumi.Q]

- Never stay attached to the ADC automatically when a different ally will carry more; switch with care since attached time decides which ally becomes Best Friend.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Leave a damaged bot turret early when the enemy threatens a dive or the tower will fall anyway, moving sooner to a safer teammate.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Run the damage page of Arcane Comet, Axiom Arcanist, Transcendence, Gathering Storm, Legend: Haste, and Coup de Grace with Exhaust and Ignite.
  sources: [hoffmann]

## Mid lane

- When the bot turret falls, rotate to whichever ally benefits most from Yuumi rather than remaining tied to the ADC by default.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Move with the jungler while waves push, scouting with Q from safety and looking for opportunities around camps and ganks.
  sources: [hoffmann]
  abilities: [Yuumi.Q]

- Switch to the utility build when the team needs healing and support more than AP damage: Dream Maker, Mikael's Blessing, Knight's Vow, Redemption, and Staff of Flowing Water.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Never ward alone while detached: coordinate with the ADC or jungler and retreat when the area may hide an ambush.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- When the attached ally cannot survive a fight, detach and retreat instead of donating a second kill; the preserved Yuumi keeps later support alive.
  sources: [hoffmann]

## Win condition

- Chip opponents patiently in the damage setup, prioritizing vulnerable or healing-dependent targets while anti-heal cuts their sustain through the fight.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Stay alive attached to the ally best placed to use the healing, slows, and buffs, avoiding risky solo wards and chases.
  sources: [hoffmann]
  abilities: [Yuumi.E]

- Rotate off a failing lane before its tower falls whenever another ally can use the support more effectively.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- Answer danger with R as an escape path and Exhaust against attackers as a possible response, not a guaranteed rescue.
  sources: [hoffmann]
  abilities: [Yuumi.R]

## Itemization

- Build the AP damage and anti-heal line in order: Dark Seal, Oblivion Orb, and Lost Chapter before Luden's Echo, then Liandry's Torment and Blackfire Torch, with Mejai's Soulstealer and Morellonomicon as later purchases toward Zaz'Zak's Realmspike, Mejai's, Morellonomicon, Liandry's, Luden's Echo, and Blackfire Torch.
  sources: [hoffmann]

- For support utility instead of AP damage, build Dream Maker, Mikael's Blessing, Knight's Vow, Redemption, Staff of Flowing Water, and Morellonomicon.
  sources: [hoffmann]
