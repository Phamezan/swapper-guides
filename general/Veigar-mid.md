## Key tips

- Prioritize farm and experience; Veigar is weak early and needs time and gold to come online.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]

- Keep Event Horizon ready to prevent an all-in; do not spend his only self-peel casually.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- At level six, use Event Horizon to control space and connect Dark Matter and Baleful Strike, then look for Primordial Burst as the finishing damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.E, Veigar.W, Veigar.Q, Veigar.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use Baleful Strike to collect passive stacks when it does not expose Veigar to a bad trade.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- Avoid fighting until there is an item advantage or another clear opening.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]

- Against all-in champions, hold Event Horizon through the trade window instead of spending it for poke.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- When the enemy bot lane leaves mid after taking its tower, move to a side lane to keep farming and gaining experience.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]

## Mid lane

- Side-lane to keep stacking while the map is quiet, then group when the enemy begins grouping.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- Use Event Horizon to catch an isolated opponent; turn the pick into Dragon or Baron pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- Maxing the first ability by level nine makes Baleful Strike a substantial hit on squishy targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- Two ultimate points at level eleven increase his ability to finish targets after the team controls them.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.R]

- Passive stacks add ability power over time, so take safe farm and stacks rather than forcing unnecessary fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.P]

## Win condition

- Stay with the team late; do not walk off alone and give the enemy a four-versus-five opening.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]

- Use Event Horizon to produce a pick, then Primordial Burst to finish the target before the team takes a nearby objective or sieges.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.E, Veigar.R]

- If ahead, use Primordial Burst on an accessible squishy to remove a key damage source.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.R]

- Use choke points to make Event Horizon catches easier and preserve the cage as a way to secure picks.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

## Itemization

- Take First Strike, Magical Footwear, Biscuit Delivery, and Cosmic Insight with Manaflow Band and Transcendence as the 16.21 rune page.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid-build]

- Build the popular path of Rod of Ages, Sorcerer's Shoes, Archangel's Staff, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Zhonya's Hourglass.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid-build]

- Start Doran's Ring, a Health Potion, and Stealth Ward.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid-build]

- Choose Shadowflame as the fourth-item alternative to Rabadon's against healing and shielding champions.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid-build]

- Max Q, then W, then E, taking Primordial Burst at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-mid-build]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q, Veigar.W, Veigar.E, Veigar.R]
