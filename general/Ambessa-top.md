- Hold the wave on your own side for safety, freeze control, and a longer chase lane.

- Use the level 3 to 4 window with the full kit to seek early skirmishes.

- Commit R only when allies can follow, since a lone dive can throw the fight.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep the wave near your side so all-ins stay safer and freezes hold longer.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- Plan around the jungler with Ambessa all-in and control tools, then take the nearby objective after a good gank.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- When behind, roam mid with R as the wave pushes toward an ally, sending the target into their follow-up.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- At level 6, add R kill pressure to roams and skirmishes without treating it as a pure duel tool.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Reassess trade strength after a first component or finished item, since her damage jumps sharply.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

## Mid lane

- While ahead, split-push alongside the team push so opponents must spread out.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- In teamfights, sync with the ADC on an enemy carry and spend R only with backup ready.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- Split farming time with grouping instead of sitting sidelane while the team needs help.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- At level 9, use maxed Q for heavier skirmish and trade damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.Q]

- At level 11, treat the second R rank as the cue to group and hunt teamfight uses.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

- With several items done, keep side pressure but return whenever the team needs the ultimate.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Win condition

- Turn early all-ins and jungler follow-up into a lead, then spend it on pressure or nearby objectives.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- Stay near allies late, pick isolated targets, and start fights off a numbers edge.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- At level 16, use the third R rank for more frequent late all-ins on separated carries.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
  abilities: [Ambessa.R]

## Itemization

- Treat the first component or finished item as a damage spike and press harder once it lands.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]

- When behind, shift toward defensive items so enemy carries stop running over fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-ambessa]
