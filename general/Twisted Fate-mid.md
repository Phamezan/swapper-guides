## Key tips

- Play for reliable farming and a controlled wave before level six; burst and melee lanes punish Twisted Fate early, so respect their windows.
  sources: [jammed]

- At level six, push and use Destiny to threaten side lanes, counter a dive, or reveal enemy positions; after Lich Bane, shove the side wave and hover near mid or the bot-side jungle for picks.
  items: [3100]
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

- In fights, poke with Q and wait for a safe Gold Card window instead of frontlining; use Destiny to find an isolated target, arrive with Gold Card ready, then follow with an auto and Wild Cards.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.Q, Twisted Fate.W, Twisted Fate.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use Wild Cards for low-risk poke and ranged wave trimming, and Pick a Card to last-hit safely, including under tower.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.Q, Twisted Fate.W]

- Take W at level one, Q at two, E at three, then max Q, W, E while taking R at six, eleven, and sixteen; take the early E point into aggressive melee lanes for stronger trades.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.W, Twisted Fate.Q, Twisted Fate.E, Twisted Fate.R]

- Trade Gold Card into Wild Cards plus an auto to add Stacked Deck damage; clear with Red Card plus Q and restore mana with Blue Card plus Q.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.W, Twisted Fate.Q, Twisted Fate.E]

- Freeze near your tower when behind or threatened by an assassin, slow-push before a planned Destiny roam, and fast-push before a recall or map move.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

- Ward river entrances defensively early, and with mid priority ward enemy Raptors or a Red Buff entrance to track the jungler and open safer roams.
  sources: [jammed]

## Mid lane

- After one or two core items, shove the assigned side wave and hover near mid or the bot-side jungle for picks rather than sitting in lane.
  sources: [jammed]

- Keep side-lane pressure within vision; otherwise group or collect waves as they reach your tower.
  sources: [jammed]

- Before Dragon, Rift Herald, or Baron, control nearby vision and push a side wave so the enemy must answer it.
  sources: [jammed]

- Use Destiny to reveal the enemy jungler's location during a clear, choosing between a roam, a reset, or an objective from that information.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

## Win condition

- Protect carries with Gold Card when an enemy diver reaches them; focus on pick setup and map pressure rather than frontline damage.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.W]

- Late game, stay with the team unless Destiny is available and side-lane vision makes a split safe; never face-check fog.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

- Extend Gold Card threat with Rapid Firecannon for picks and sieges, using a catch to start Baron, Elder Dragon, or a tower push.
  items: [3094]
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.W]

- Activate Destiny before or during an objective fight when the reveal exposes enemy positions or a flank.
  sources: [jammed]
  abilities: [Twisted Fate.R]

## Itemization

- Choose Electrocute for burst in short-range lanes, First Strike as the safer scaling and gold-acceleration page, or Unsealed Spellbook for utility and flexible summoner spells; the First Strike page pairs Cash Back, Triple Tonic, and Jack of All Trades with Celerity and Gathering Storm.
  sources: [jammed]

- Run Flash by default with Teleport as the consistent ranked pairing, Ignite for aggressive kill pressure, Exhaust against burst, or Ghost for extended skirmishes with limited enemy crowd control.
  sources: [jammed]

- Follow the Luden's path from Doran's Ring and potions into Luden's Echo, Sorcerer's Shoes, Lich Bane, Rabadon's Deathcap, and Void Staff, ending with Mejai's or Rapid Firecannon; take Rod of Ages into the safer first-item route with Lich Bane and defenses, or the burn path into three or more tanks.
  items: [1056, 6655, 3020, 3100, 3089, 3135, 3094, 6657]
  sources: [jammed]
