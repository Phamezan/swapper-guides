## Key tips

- Use Trundle's Q as an auto-attack reset for the short trade, extending with Flash into a surprise all-in only when a kill is genuinely available.
  sources: [trundledaddy]
  abilities: [Trundle.Q]

- After building wave-clear and split-push items, repeat the cycle of clearing a side wave, pressuring the turret, and regrouping instead of sitting in one lane indefinitely.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Before an objective, split on the opposite side with Teleport when holding the stronger side-lane position and able to respond; otherwise group instead of forcing the split.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat levels one and two as the pressure window, but follow no fixed wave-by-wave script from this page; its detailed lane phases live in the matchup spreadsheet and are out of scope here.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Run Flash and Teleport to support lane plays plus later split-push and teamfight arrivals; take Ghost over one of them only when the enemy team is so ranged that a Flash engage never sticks.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Into sustain lanes against ranged poke, start Doran's Shield with Second Wind; prefer Conditioning as the scaling choice and Bone Plating against strong all-in patterns.
  items: [1054]
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Value Trundle's passive healing from nearby unit deaths as part of the lane sustain plan.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

## Mid lane

- Turn side-lane pressure into room for the team: make opponents answer Trundle while checking whether the side-lane contest is actually winnable.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Buy Hullbreaker as the midgame split-push purchase, but never rush it; build it after the earlier core items.
  items: [3181]
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- When the team needs a frontline body, treat Sterak's Gage as a later fifth- or sixth-slot option with less isolated-dueling value than the damage choices.
  items: [3053]
  sources: [trundledaddy]

## Win condition

- Convert early lane pressure into a strong side-lane item curve, then turn cleared waves into tower damage that spends enemy players on the answer.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Keep choosing between split-push and teamfight by relative side-lane strength, Teleport availability, and the upcoming objective; play Trundle primarily as a sidelaner.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

## Itemization

- Run Lethal Tempo with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, Last Stand, Demolish, and Revitalize, with attack speed, adaptive force, and scaling health shards.
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Open Ravenous Hydra as the recommended first core, then Blade of the Ruined King or Wit's End by the opponent's damage and durability, with Hullbreaker after the first two items for the split-push plan.
  items: [3074, 3091, 3181]
  sources: [trundledaddy]

- Vary buys by game: Black Cleaver against tanks, the safer Trinity Force and Hullbreaker alternate build, Death's Dance, Randuin's Omen, Frozen Heart, Thornmail, Spirit Visage, Overlord's Bloodmail, Force of Nature, Knight's Vow, and Sterak's Gage later, with Boots of Swiftness standard and Plated Steelcaps or Mercury's Treads as defensive swaps.
