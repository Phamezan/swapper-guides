## Key tips

- Stage a low-health minion so Q bounces through it onto the champion as a repeatable poke pattern.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Cast E before R so its slow makes Bullet Time connect more easily.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E, Miss Fortune.R]

- Poke before longer trades; she loses sustained races to most attack-speed ADCs, so all-in only for finishes.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Drop E between self and pursuers on ganks to slow their approach.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E]

- While ahead, zone opponents off last hits and punish every farming step forward.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]

## Mid lane

- Enter objective fights on a safe R angle, never channeling where opponents focus easily.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.R]

- Make space with E and slow pursuers, repositioning fully before committing to Bullet Time.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E, Miss Fortune.R]

- Hold extra distance from assassins and divers because your low mobility leaves you vulnerable.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]

## Win condition

- Hunt multi-target Bullet Time that flips fights, connecting the channel through the E slow.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.R, Miss Fortune.E]

- Threaten high burst from outside attack range with a prepared Q bounce off a low minion.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Snowball safe leads by zoning the enemy duo and banking damage before any all-in.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]

- Respect R positioning always: it turns fights but punishes every exposed channel.
  sources: [misterfirstblood-mf]
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.R]
