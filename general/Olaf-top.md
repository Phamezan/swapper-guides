## Key tips

- At level one, throw Q so it hits both the wave and the opponent, then convert that priority into one of three wave plans: dive a low-health enemy at the third cannon wave, crash the second wave to make it push back, or crash the third and proxy.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q]

- Weave W between attacks since it resets the basic attack, save it for low health for a larger shield, and use E to last-hit because a killing blow refunds its health cost.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.W, Olaf.E]

- Play as an item-dependent diver: reach the item spikes, then group and run at the backline, flanking roughly a minute before the objective when the route safely reaches the carries.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat farm as equal to kills: a lower-kill lane with clearly more CS can still leave Olaf ahead, so protect the farm line while pressuring.
  sources: [1kinglol]

- Respect the all-in commitment: with no real disengage, a missed Q or a mistimed engage loses the fight, so only commit when the axe will land.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q]

- Against opponents who dodge Q, close distance before throwing, then move off-line while retrieving the axe to avoid return skillshots.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q]

- Remember the ultimate's short active window can be extended by attacking or landing E on champions; plan the all-in around keeping it up.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.R, Olaf.E]

## Mid lane

- Let item spikes dictate movement: when a side wave completes an item before the Dragon, take the brief side farm, recall, and group for the fight.
  sources: [1kinglol]

- Join meaningful fights while strong and summoners are available; when too far behind to survive a teamfight, farm the opposite side or wait for summoners instead.
  sources: [1kinglol]

- When ahead with backline access, run at the carries with Q and movement speed; without access, grind the frontline until a switch onto the backline opens.
  sources: [1kinglol]
  abilities: [Olaf.Q]

## Win condition

- Turn the early lead into side-lane items, then convert cleared waves into tower damage that forces the enemy to spend players answering the split.
  sources: [1kinglol]

- Push for the tower only when the side duel is winnable and the dive is safe; otherwise preserve farm and join cross-map fights where Olaf can matter.
  sources: [1kinglol]

- Choose split-push or teamfight by objective timing, summoner availability, team composition, and whether Olaf can dive his side-lane opponent.
  sources: [1kinglol]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, and Last Stand plus Cash Back and Approach Velocity, starting Doran's Blade or Doran's Shield with a Health Potion.
  items: [1055, 1054, 2003]
  sources: [1kinglol]

- Rush Ravenous Hydra, then take Blade of the Ruined King or Wit's End by matchup, with Infinity Edge or Lord Dominik's Regards as swaps; into ranged opponents, open Ravenous Hydra into Experimental Hexplate.
  items: [3074, 3091, 3031, 3036, 3073]
  sources: [1kinglol]
