## Key tips

- Cover weak base early damage with repeated soldier harass instead of hunting early burst.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- Keep Shifting Sands plus a standing soldier ready as the only escape route against ganks.
  sources: [bodythosefools]
  abilities: [Azir.E]

- Group harder for late objectives while still catching side waves when safe.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Slide soldiers through the opponent with Q and follow with an auto as the basic short trade.
  sources: [bodythosefools]
  abilities: [Azir.Q]

- On Hail of Blades, spend its available attacks inside the trade instead of stopping after one hit.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- Expect weaker early trading on the scaling Lethal Tempo page than on Comet or Hail of Blades setups.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- Build lane priority through harass in most lanes, with Xerath and Yone named as the lanes that can contest it.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- Preserve a Sand Soldier whenever possible so E keeps a retreat dash target.
  sources: [bodythosefools]
  abilities: [Azir.E]

## Mid lane

- After laning, push a side lane into its turret, then regroup with the team.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- Ward around the side lane before extending, since getting caught erases pressure everywhere else.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- During a siege, plant a soldier behind Azir to feed the extra attack-speed effect and hold an E escape line.
  sources: [bodythosefools]
  abilities: [Azir.E]

- Drop the passive turret near the end of a side siege as another defensive layer.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

## Win condition

- With Teleport, set up opposite the next objective to threaten a side wave and still join; without it, stay on the objective side.
  sources: [bodythosefools]

- Never enter unwarded ground late, since one pick can cost an objective or the game.
  sources: [bodythosefools]
