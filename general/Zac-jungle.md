## Key tips

- Choose the route from lane composition and likely gank access instead of one fixed opening: Red, Krugs, Raptors by default, or Blue, Gromp, Wolves when the allied bot lane offers an easy early gank.
  sources: [stean]

- Respect the weak early Scuttle fight: contest only with lane help, arrive before the enemy jungler, or take the other side instead of forcing it.
  sources: [stean]

- Be patient around targets whose crowd control can interrupt E; commit the jump only when the landing is likely to connect.
  sources: [stean]
  abilities: [Zac.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- When the enemy takes the opposite half of Zac's jungle and allies are watching, trade into the enemy jungle and look for a lane gank rather than conceding both sides.
  sources: [stean]

- When a camp loss is noticed late, look for a gank or a Scuttle contest with lane support instead of chasing the stolen camp blindly.
  sources: [stean]

- From around level seven, E range gives Zac more room to threaten a gank; keep vision on the jungle so farming never leaves the team open to counterplay.
  sources: [stean]
  abilities: [Zac.E]

- Take neutral objectives when the map allows it, but never treat the objective call as automatic when the team cannot support it.
  sources: [stean]

## Mid lane

- Fight the basic sequence as Q two nearby enemies, E into the space between them, then R with repeated W casts while collecting blobs.
  sources: [stean]
  abilities: [Zac.Q, Zac.E, Zac.R, Zac.W]

- Add Q plus Flash as the unexpected catch variant when the standard angle is covered.
  sources: [stean]
  abilities: [Zac.Q]

- Fly E across the dragon pit and pair it with Smite midair for the steal, using the travel time to stay unseen when the route permits.
  sources: [stean]
  abilities: [Zac.E]

## Win condition

- Play the preferred late-game job: engage onto a backline carry and hold them with crowd control while allies follow.
  sources: [stean]

- Stay with the team when behind; isolated play is a poor risk unless Zac is already ahead.
  sources: [stean]

## Itemization

- Prefer the author's own testing over habit for Unending Despair, and take Frozen Heart when the team lacks that attack-speed defense.
  items: [2502, 3110]
  sources: [stean]

- Hold Dark Seal without upgrading while the team is not ahead, and buy Mejai's Soulstealer only when the lead looks secure enough to preserve its stacks; take Protoplasm Harness against heavy burst and skip it when the enemy lacks that damage.
  items: [1082, 3041, 2525]
  sources: [stean]
