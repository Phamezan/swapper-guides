## Key tips

- Play the first levels cautiously unless the lane holds a clear edge such as an engage support.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Once R unlocks, sync with an all-in support to stretch chases and start fights.

  abilities: [Kalista.R]

- Fight late from open space near the support, kiting the nearest safe target.

## Overview

## Early lane

- In winning extended trades, kite inside range while running opponents down.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Break enemy vision with unwarded brushes to reposition mid-trade.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Read lane pressure through the support; without their skirmish help, extended trades die.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

## Mid lane

- After the bot tower, rotate mid and pressure its tower to open the map.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Stay glued to the support or another ally; lone Kalista dies easily.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Group over split-pushing without a specific reason to leave.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Set picks with the support or jungler and weigh Baron on the numbers edge after one.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]

- Help lock neutral objectives with E alongside the jungler Smite.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]
  abilities: [Kalista.E]

- Keep kiting room in fights and spend E only when its reset or execute secures a kill.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]
  abilities: [Kalista.E]

- With waves pushed, send R to help the support roam or engage another lane.
  sources: [mobalytics-kalista]
  abilities: [Kalista.R]

## Win condition

- Keep vision and allies close; chained control stops her mobility and ends fights.

- Against heavy control, buy Quicksilver Sash to cut the lockdown odds.
