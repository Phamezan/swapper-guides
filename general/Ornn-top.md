## Key tips

- Play safely for the first few levels and fight only with a clear advantage; Ornn is vulnerable to early pressure before his abilities and health items come online.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- Level six is the first major spike because Call of the Forge God adds a reliable way to start fights and threaten an overextended opponent.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]
  abilities: [Ornn.R]

- In late game, hold the ultimate for a fight or a pick on an isolated enemy; a well-timed cast sets up the jungler and can knock up several opponents.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]
  abilities: [Ornn.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep the wave closer to your side of the lane for protection while Ornn is still fragile.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- Before level six, use lane brushes and trade when opponents step close or overstay; avoid telegraphed trades into a dominant early laner.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- The first item component improves lane damage and durability, and preserving health can let you stay in lane and keep collecting farm.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- Give up aggressive positioning against stronger early laners and wait for the level-six ultimate or item timings to contest the wave.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]
  abilities: [Ornn.R]

## Mid lane

- Push a side lane near upcoming objectives and use Ornn's dueling strength against opponents who come to stop you.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- Be ready to regroup for a five-versus-five when no nearby objective is available; split only when you can convert the pressure instead of staying isolated without purpose.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- A second point in Call of the Forge God increases its damage and lets you create plays more often.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]
  abilities: [Ornn.R]

- Max Searing Charge first, followed by Bellows Breath and then Volcanic Rupture, starting Q, W, E and upgrading the ultimate at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top-build]
  abilities: [Ornn.W, Ornn.Q, Ornn.E, Ornn.R]

- Once multiple completed tank items are online, participate in fights where the team can follow the initiation and let Ornn absorb damage for allies.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

## Win condition

- Look for an isolated, overextended target when the ultimate is available, then help the team convert the catch into an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]
  abilities: [Ornn.R]

- Peel for carries and keep allies alive; Ornn needs teammates who can follow up on the targets he catches.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]

- Fight near terrain when possible, where Ornn can better set up his crowd control.
  sources: [mobalytics-ornn-top]
