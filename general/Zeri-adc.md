## Key tips

- Treat Zeri as weak before level six: stay alive, farm, and take only short trades with empowered W or passive attacks instead of extended fights, rating level one as especially weak and levels two and three as only a small improvement.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.W]

- Preserve E for escaping or repositioning rather than spending the dash for a small damage increase, and use wall angles to open movement and W paths.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.E, Zeri.W]

- After lane, join teamfights and deal damage instead of farming a wave through an important breaking fight, positioning safely while keeping E available for the moment the fight shifts.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.E]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Spend W once per wave for waveclear and save further casts for an enemy misposition or allied crowd control that creates a clear follow-up.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.W]

- Open Doran's Bow by default, switching to Doran's Shield when lane priority is unavailable or the lane is going poorly.
  items: [1086, 1054]
  sources: [junniseoul]

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, Cut Down, Celerity, and Gathering Storm, with attack speed, movement speed, and scaling health shards.
  sources: [junniseoul]

## Mid lane

- Play around walls: they create better E repositioning and W angles, so use E sparingly and keep it ready as the fight's position changes.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.E, Zeri.W]

- Treat Zeri as burst-oriented in the early and mid game, transitioning toward sustained hypercarry damage as she scales.
  sources: [junniseoul]

## Win condition

- Farm through the weak pre-six lane, use the level-six ultimate window to contest fights, then scale into sustained teamfight damage with the crit build.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.R]

- Keep wall routes for mobility and attack angles, holding E so Zeri can adjust position instead of getting trapped after committing.
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.E]

- Show up to teamfights and maintain damage uptime, choosing safety and useful positioning over an isolated duel.
  sources: [junniseoul]

## Itemization

- Build Doran's Bow into Yun Tal Wildarrows, Infinity Edge, Lord Dominik's Regards, Hexoptics C44, and Berserker's Greaves, with Endless Hunger, Bloodthirster, and Guardian Angel as fifth- or sixth-slot options.
  items: [1086, 3032, 3031, 3036, 2523, 3006, 2517, 3072, 3026]
  sources: [junniseoul]

- Take the safer alternative of Yun Tal Wildarrows, Runaan's Hurricane, Infinity Edge, and Gluttonous Greaves before situational armor penetration and late picks; treat the Q-W-E ability order as ambiguous since the page also emphasizes maxing W, and do not lock a firm max order from it.
  items: [3032, 3085, 3031, 3008]
  sources: [junniseoul]
  abilities: [Zeri.Q, Zeri.W, Zeri.E]

- Keep Mortal Reminder for armor penetration, Mercurial Scimitar for cleanse, Edge of Night or Serpent's Fang by game, and Zhonya's Hourglass, Maw of Malmortius, Death's Dance, or Spirit Visage as defensive options.
  items: [3033, 3139, 3814, 6695, 3157, 3156, 6333, 3065]
  sources: [junniseoul]
