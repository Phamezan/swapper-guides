- After Scuttle, check the two nearest lanes for ganks, often pressing mid, then cycle back to Raptors and Krugs.

- Commit to major fights with Chronobreak ready, since it escapes and re-enters after risky plays.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Skill W at level 2 and clear Krugs with Smite plus Red, then take E at level 3 ahead of Wolves, Gromp, Blue, and Scuttle.
  sources: [guest-653345]
  abilities: [Ekko.W, Ekko.E]

- Favor volatile lanes or reliable setup for ganks; never force an invade without nearby priority, pings, and a read on the enemy jungler.
  sources: [guest-653345]

- Value early takedowns and Void Grubs over an early Dragon, bending the trade to lane state and contest strength.
  sources: [guest-653345]

- Target these item timings: first finished item near 9 to 10 minutes, second near 14 to 16, third near 19 to 20.
  sources: [guest-653345]

## Mid lane

- Default back to Raptors and Krugs with no confident play, protecting the fast income curve into core items.
  sources: [guest-653345]

- Invade only when nearby lanes move first and the enemy jungler spot stays known or findable.
  sources: [guest-653345]

- Before objective fights, verify enemy positions, health, vision, and summoners, preferring fights where the map favors Ekko.
  sources: [guest-653345]

- Initiate as needed when the ultimate banks a safe exit, but guard the lead and finished items over low-value trades.
  sources: [guest-653345]
  abilities: [Ekko.R]

## Win condition

- Turn an early resource lead into picks and map pressure through assassin threat and strong scaling.
  sources: [guest-653345]

- Hunt isolated or vulnerable targets in ganks and skirmishes, skipping long fights where lockdown sticks without the ultimate ready.
  sources: [guest-653345]
  abilities: [Ekko.R]

- Set the ultimate return point as the safety plan before risky entries instead of relying on it after getting trapped.
  sources: [guest-653345]
  abilities: [Ekko.R]

- Convert each kill or forced recall into camp access, a neutral objective, or vision toward faster item completion.
  sources: [guest-653345]
