## Key tips

- Use Pantheon's early pressure to poke and threaten kills; a lead can snowball quickly.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]

- Use Aegis Assault to block damage in a committed trade, but play more carefully while it is on cooldown because the opponent can punish that window.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.E]

- Late-game picks are safer than repeated full-team fights; if Grand Starfall is unavailable, wait or disengage until the next engage window.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open the lane aggressively with Comet Spear pressure and threaten kills whenever the opponent offers a window.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

- Keep the wave even or slightly on your side so you can run an opponent down while reducing gank exposure.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]

- Level six adds cross-map Grand Starfall plays, but it does not guarantee winning every lane all-in against a stronger enemy ultimate.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Use Comet Spear to poke or finish a low target; lane and river brush positioning can let you pressure without taking return damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

## Mid lane

- Split for side objectives and use Pantheon's dueling strength against opponents who come to stop you.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]

- Regroup for five-versus-five when there are no nearby objectives to take alone.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]

- Use Grand Starfall and Shield Vault to set up picks with the team; focus high-priority targets and convert the catch into an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R, Pantheon.W]

- A second ultimate rank gives a modest damage increase but lowers its cooldown, so it creates more windows for map plays.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Max Comet Spear first, then Aegis Assault, then Shield Vault, starting Q, W, E and upgrading the ultimate at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top-build]
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q, Pantheon.E, Pantheon.W, Pantheon.R]

## Win condition

- Pantheon is strongest when early pressure and picks let the team enter mid game with a lead; avoid letting a won lane turn into aimless fighting.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]

- In late game, flank from fog of war to reach enemy carries, or peel for allies after using the ultimate to enter the fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Avoid starting an ultimate engage beyond the team's ability to follow; a landing position without allied support can leave Pantheon stranded.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- When the team needs a frontline, use a tankier build and protect allies rather than forcing a backline dive.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-top]

- Build Bastionbreaker, Plated Steelcaps, Voltaic Cyclosword, Lord Dominik's Regards, and Edge of Night as the 26.21 top core.

- Take Stormraider's Surge, Manaflow Band, Transcendence, Scorch, Triumph, and Cut Down as the current rune page.

- Choose Serpent's Fang as the situational replacement for Lord Dominik's Regards when you need to pressure a team with heavy shielding.
