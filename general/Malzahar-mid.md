## Key tips

- Play the first levels cautiously, fighting only on clear edges while guarding health behind the passive shield.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- At level 6, bring the jungler to convert Nether Grasp into a kill, and roam with R ready.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- Refuse late commits without R available, since its suppression carries his teamfight control.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take longer trades once a few levels bank instead of only short poke.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- Respect every all-in threat while the passive shield sits down early on its long cooldown.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- Without a likely lane kill, clear the wave and carry the ultimate to a play elsewhere.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

## Mid lane

- Farm a side lane for gold and experience whenever no fight brews.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- Swap to the open side once bot rotates mid to keep earning without contest.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- Track the minimap, objectives, and enemy jungler under mid pressure, forcing the opposing mid to answer instead of roaming free.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- With R up, hunt isolated or overextended targets; each pick opens Baron or a nearby tower.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- Against late or unsafe enemy-mid arrivals, shove for plates and ping the missing opponent to both sides.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

## Win condition

- Drop R on a lone overstepper, adding Flash plus R to catch exposed targets the team follows.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- Into enemy engages, suppress the closest threat off the backline, or lock the highest-priority target instead.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]
  abilities: [Malzahar.R]

- Peel carries so their damage never stops; losing them early ends winning chances.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

- Value picks and wave-clear over solo-kill chasing, turning each pick into an objective or tower.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar]

## Itemization

- Run Flash and Teleport with Deathfire Touch, Manaflow Band, Transcendence, and Scorch plus Magical Footwear and Cosmic Insight.
  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar-build]

- Order Blackfire Torch first and Sorcerer's Shoes second, then Liandry's, Rylai's, Zhonya's, and Rabadon's in the popular build.
  items: [2503, 3020]

- Slot Shadowflame fourth against healing- and shield-reliant teams.

  sources: [mobalytics-malzahar-build]

- Level E, W, Q first while prioritizing E, Q, and W after; ride the first-component spell frequency plus later cores and R ranks into stronger picks and teamfights.
