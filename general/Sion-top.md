## Key tips

- Keep trades short: land one or two attacks, then back away rather than extending into a long exchange.
  sources: [darkoey]

- Freeze near the turret to make quick trades safer and create a good angle for a charged Decimating Smash from fog of war.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- Proxy when the matchup allows it; dying to clear the wave can break a freeze against opponents Sion cannot beat in a straight duel.
  sources: [darkoey]

## Overview

## Early lane

- After shoving a wave with nothing else to do, look for a Decimating Smash charge from a bush or fogged approach.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- Use Roar of the Slayer to slow enemies before charging Decimating Smash, casting Unstoppable Onslaught, or committing to a passive trade.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.E, Sion.Q, Sion.R]

- Concede extended fights against stronger duelists and use freezes, fog charges, and proxy breaks instead of forcing head-on trades.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

## Mid lane

- Spend mid game in side lanes to collect solo experience and farm rather than grouping without a reason.
  sources: [darkoey]

- Catch side waves consistently, then use the fog of war to threaten a fully charged Decimating Smash pick.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- Tank Sion should group with the team and occupy the frontline when fights are needed.
  sources: [darkoey]

- AD Sion can keep pressure in a side lane and rotate quickly with Unstoppable Onslaught to create a numbers advantage elsewhere.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.R]

## Win condition

- Tank Sion contributes by frontlining for teammates; AD Sion pressures structures and uses fast ultimate rotations to join favorable fights.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.R]

- In Glory in Death, use movement between attacks to reach targets and clear waves while the temporary form is active.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.P]

- Fully charge Decimating Smash when the position is safe; playing from fog gives opponents less warning.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.Q]

- Use Soul Furnace to absorb damage before the shield breaks while still looking for its damage opportunity.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.W]

- Recast Unstoppable Onslaught just before impact to increase its hit area and damage zone.
  sources: [darkoey]
  abilities: [Sion.R]
