## Key tips

- Play the first levels cautiously until trades turn reliable.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- From level 6, shorten trades to lower the opponent, heal back on the passive, and hunt the R execute.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]
  abilities: [Garen.R]

- Peel and absorb on a tank build, or flank from fog for carries on a bruiser build.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hide approaches with lane brush cover when lining up an all-in.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- Trade more often after a few levels or a first component lands.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- Never chase ranged opponents without a flank, since zero ranged engage means getting kited.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- Match the build to team need; full damage risks everything when nobody else frontlines.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

## Mid lane

- Split-push without a developing fight to pull opponents while teammates siege or start plays.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- Duel defenders who answer the split instead of surrendering the lane free.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- At level 9, press the first maxed ability for stronger short trades and easier duels.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- Set picks with Q and R alongside teammates while rotating lanes.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Garen.R]

- Read the first component fork: a tank piece holds lanes and joins fights, a bruiser piece raises duel damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- At level 11, catch exposed opponents from brush with short burst around the second R point.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]
  abilities: [Garen.R]

## Win condition

- Peel for carries and eat pressure on the tank build so they keep dealing damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- Punish an exposed target with Q and R in late fights, then peel for allies instead of diving solo.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]
  abilities: [Garen.Q, Garen.R]

- After poke lands, retreat briefly and let the passive refill health before deciding on recall.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]

- At level 16, hunt a safe catch with teammates near the third R point and its damage and cooldown jump.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]
  abilities: [Garen.R]

## Itemization

- Choose the durable route whenever the team lacks a frontline.
  sources: [mobalytics-garen]
