## Key tips

- Play safely through the opening levels; Veigar needs time, gold, and experience before he can carry fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]

- Keep Event Horizon available to stop a dive or all-in; it is his only self-peel and has a long cooldown.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- After reaching level six, use the full ability kit to set up a finish: poke first, then let Primordial Burst execute the low target.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q, Veigar.W, Veigar.E, Veigar.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Avoid trades unless the lane has a clear advantage; early pressure depends heavily on the support.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]

- Use Baleful Strike to farm and build passive stacks when doing so is safe.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- Use Event Horizon to zone an approach or chain the support's crowd control; its cage also gives the jungler a gank setup.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- After taking the bot outer tower, rotate mid and help take that tower to open the map.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]

## Mid lane

- Farm a side lane and keep collecting passive stacks before opponents begin grouping.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- When the enemy starts grouping, join the team rather than staying isolated.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]

- Look for an Event Horizon catch; after a pick, call for Dragon or Baron while the enemy is down a player.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.E]

- Level nine maxes the first ability and makes Baleful Strike a meaningful chunk of a squishy target.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.Q]

- Two ultimate points at level eleven sharply improve the follow-up damage after the cage or teammates' crowd control.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.R]

## Win condition

- Stay with allies in late game so the enemy cannot pick Veigar off or force a four-versus-five.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]

- Use Event Horizon to create a numbers advantage, then Primordial Burst to remove the caught target before taking a nearby objective or sieging.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.E, Veigar.R]

- If ahead, prioritize an accessible squishy with Primordial Burst to remove damage from the fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.R]

- Use choke points to improve the chance that Event Horizon and Dark Matter control the fight; avoid walking alone into fog because Veigar is immobile.
  sources: [mobalytics-veigar-adc]
  abilities: [Veigar.E, Veigar.W]
