## Key tips

- Farm with Q and autos while protecting health: Zed is item-reliant, so never trade away too much early for a wave that Q can collect.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.Q]

- At level three, poke with W-Q-E or W-E-Q only when the enemy jungler is accounted for and Zed is not pushed into an unsafe position.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.Q, Zed.E]

- Poke opponents toward roughly sixty percent health before level six, then use the W-E-Q just before leveling into the newly available ultimate while the opponent expects the combo to be unavailable.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.E, Zed.Q, Zed.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- When ahead, shove and roam or coordinate a dive with the jungler; when behind, stay mid, ward the center of the lane, clear quickly, and collect plates instead of following every roam.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Track the enemy jungler and use fog of war to keep Zed's location uncertain before choosing each play.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Open Doran's Blade for the standard setup or Doran's Shield into poke, with Pickaxe, Long Sword, and Refillable Potion as the shown first-buy components.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Mid lane

- After either bot-lane tier-one turret falls, swap toward the longer side lane and use the mobility to pressure an opponent with less room to retreat.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Push the side wave before taking nearby enemy jungle camps, gaining experience while denying it to the other team.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Move first or match the opponent's rotation when a play is viable; when it will be over before Zed arrives, stay for wave and plate value instead.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Use deep vision to bait several enemies toward Zed, surviving their response with mobility while teammates take space elsewhere.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- For the next objective, side lane on that objective's side so Zed can pressure and still rotate, letting the top laner with Teleport cover the other lane.
  sources: [aikopanda]

## Win condition

- In late fights, flank for a high-value target and wait for allied engage or crowd control when possible instead of starting the fight alone.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Check defensive answers such as stasis or Guardian Angel before committing the full combo, choosing the target by current threat and value.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R]

- Dodge incoming damage or crowd control with Death Mark's untargetability, then swap back to a shadow when that is the safer exit; a multi-target E shortens Living Shadow's cooldown for the return.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Zed.W, Zed.E]

- When the enemy frontline blocks access, cast R on a frontliner and send W-Q-E onto a squishy behind them to bypass the line.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  abilities: [Zed.R, Zed.W, Zed.Q, Zed.E]

## Itemization

- Run First Strike with Cash Back, Triple Tonic, Jack of All Trades, Legend: Haste, and Cut Down.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Build Voltaic Cyclosword, Bastionbreaker, Gluttonous Greaves, Lord Dominik's Regards, Edge of Night, and Endless Hunger as the main line, with Gluttonous Greaves, Armored Advance, or Chainlaced Crushers for boots, Serylda's Grudge, Lord Dominik's Regards, Mortal Reminder, or Black Cleaver for armor penetration, and Edge of Night, Death's Dance, Maw of Malmortius, Guardian Angel, or Spear of Shojin for late defense by opposing damage.
  sources: [aikopanda]

- Start Doran's Blade by default and Doran's Shield into poke pressure.
  sources: [aikopanda]
