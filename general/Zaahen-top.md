## Key tips

- Protect health at level one: losing too much early can make the lane unplayable, so yield early priority in many lanes and collect safe farm while the second or third wave crashes.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Treat level three as the useful spike and level four as the common all-in timing, especially when the opponent is still level three and the wave allows a chase, but skip any all-in a lost fight would leave pushing away.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Trade safe near your side with W into E and the second Q knock-up, then disengage without growing the wave into an unfavorable long trade; before an apparently easy one-versus-one, confirm where the enemy jungler is.
  sources: [ozneviik]
  abilities: [Zaahen.W, Zaahen.E, Zaahen.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Never commit while the opponent holds a large minion advantage: in the first five levels Zaahen may not have time to build the revive passive during that fight.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Take the level-four all-in when the wave sits on your side, but pass when the wave state turns against the chase.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Land abilities into the level-six all-in with the passive ready, but never fight it without a favorable wave.
  sources: [ozneviik]
  abilities: [Zaahen.R]

- Open Doran's Blade by default, with Doran's Shield for poke and Doran's Helm as the situational start, and run Flash with Ignite.
  items: [1055, 1054, 1120]
  sources: [ozneviik]

## Mid lane

- When ahead, shove toward the tier-two tower and roam mid or into the enemy jungle, but only push where vision covers the move.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- When pushed in and unable to fight, clear waves safely and warn teammates when the opponent leaves lane.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Repeat the push top, roam for a play, push again, then recall while preserving time to catch the next wave.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- For an objective, push first and then roam or Teleport by distance and urgency; after a long fight, reset and catch the wave at the tower.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Note that Stridebreaker makes freezing harder, but use its edge radius to last-hit a single minion without hitting the rest of the wave.
  items: [6631]
  sources: [ozneviik]

## Win condition

- Spike on Stridebreaker first: Zaahen is especially strong with two items and increasingly threatening with a timely third or fourth.
  items: [6631]
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Late game, either push a side wave and Teleport to the fight or group near objectives, preferring the group when the team needs direction.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- In a teamfight, build passive stacks on one exposed enemy before entering the rest of the fight, arriving with the revive available.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- While ahead, skip unnecessary high-risk fights without tracking the enemy team; holding the lead with lower-risk plays beats forcing a one-versus-two or one-versus-three.
  sources: [ozneviik]

## Itemization

- Run Conqueror with Triumph, Legend: Haste, and Last Stand plus Second Wind and Overgrowth, with attack speed, adaptive force, and scaling health shards.
  sources: [ozneviik]

- Build Stridebreaker, Death's Dance, Maw of Malmortius, Endless Hunger, and Overlord's Bloodmail, prioritizing Stridebreaker for movement speed and access, then Death's Dance and Maw for resistances alongside damage; take Plated Steelcaps or Mercury's Treads by game with Cloth Armor, Long Sword, Boots, Refillable Potion, and Dagger as early components.
  items: [6631, 6333, 3156, 2517, 2501, 3047, 3111, 1029, 1036, 2031, 1042]
  sources: [ozneviik]
