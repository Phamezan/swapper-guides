## Key tips

- Keep one plant of each type on a camp when possible, since multiple plants of the same type deal reduced damage to the same target.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.W]

- Auto-attack the intended champion target in fights, because plants otherwise prioritize minions when minions are present.
  sources: [dabloondemon]

- Play the listed teamfight string of E, W, R, Q, W to root, knock up, and amplify plant damage, using it when the root can connect safely and never as the opener into easy hard engage or mobility.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.E, Zyra.W, Zyra.R, Zyra.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open Q at level one for its cooldown and early plant trigger, with the standard max order of Q, W, E; for a poke setup take three points in Q then three in E for root duration before W ranks, or Q, E, W for direct skirmishing.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.Q, Zyra.W, Zyra.E]

- Report the roughly three-minute full clear with clean pathing and plant timing as the pace target for arriving at river early enough to gank or prepare Scuttle.
  sources: [dabloondemon]

- On a Red start, play Q and passive plants on Red, move toward Krugs, place W seeds as Red dies, and smite the large Krug over the wall; on a Blue start, ready plants for the Blue and Gromp sequence, then clear Wolves toward Raptors, letting passive timing place the preparations.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.Q, Zyra.W]

- Drift toward the next camp before the current one dies so a passive seed has a better chance to spawn where it saves time, and clear small raptors or krugs first since W charges refresh faster when large monsters die.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.W]

- Attack the lowest-health small monster when possible to earn the W cooldown refund, and leave camps to plants once they can safely finish the remaining monsters.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.W]

- Hold passive seed generation while sitting in a brush, since passive seeds do not appear there, then leave when a seed will help prepare the next camp or ambush.
  sources: [dabloondemon]

- Scout with W seeds and Q over walls or into brushes; plants also grant temporary vision while attacking.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.W, Zyra.Q]

## Mid lane

- Turn the fast first clear into pressure: push a lane or set plants near the Scuttle approach, and threaten early invades only while healthy.
  sources: [dabloondemon]

- Judge each gank by enemy mobility, allied follow-up, wave direction, and nearby opponents, skipping pushed lanes behind a large enemy minion wave.
  sources: [dabloondemon]

- Predict with E and hold the W charge when the root misses instead of spending it on a low-impact slow; place W only after casting Q or E so the seed never reveals the spell direction early.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.E, Zyra.W, Zyra.Q]

- Catch from fog or behind terrain with E and refresh vision beyond walls with a Q plant.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.E, Zyra.Q]

- Solo most dragons and Rift Herald with prepared plants, while respecting that Hextech dragon splash and Cloud dragon attack speed make plants less reliable there.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.W]

## Win condition

- Past three items, play from behind the team and open with E only when the enemy lacks easy hard engage or mobility to punish Zyra.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
  abilities: [Zyra.E]

## Itemization

- Spend on the tempo identity: clear speed, range, objective pressure, and disengage matter more than dueling stats.
  sources: [dabloondemon]
