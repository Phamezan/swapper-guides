## Key tips

- Harass with Arcanopulse and Eye of Destruction; the Eye of Destruction slow makes it easier to land Arcanopulse and Shocking Orb.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q, Xerath.W, Xerath.E]

- Save Shocking Orb as self-peel rather than spending it carelessly; a missed stun can leave Xerath open to an all-in.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.E]

- When Rite of the Arcane is ready, poke first and then use it to finish a low target from range; cast from a position that cannot be interrupted immediately.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Xerath is immobile and his charged Arcanopulse can be interrupted, so do not channel where enemy crowd control can reach him.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q]

- After bot tower falls, rotate mid, pressure the tower, and use poke to keep the defender away from objectives.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]

- Against a healthy enemy pair, hold Shocking Orb for an approaching diver instead of throwing it into the wave.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.E]

## Mid lane

- Delay a full fight while Arcanopulse poke lowers targets, then join when the enemy is sufficiently softened.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q]

- Track the next major objective and establish vision safely before retreating to the team.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]

- Support Xerath is vulnerable while warding or moving alone, so take a teammate on routes through uncertain areas.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]

- Level six gives long-range Rite of the Arcane access; level nine maxes Arcanopulse and increases poke threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.R, Xerath.Q]

- The next ultimate point at level eleven improves cleanup, and Eye of Destruction reaches max rank at level thirteen for a stronger Arcanopulse follow-up.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.R, Xerath.W, Xerath.Q]

## Win condition

- Ward major objectives and high-traffic routes late to reveal enemy movement.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]

- Do not ward alone without knowing enemy positions; a pick on Xerath leaves the team in a four-versus-five.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]

- Peel for allies with Shocking Orb and slow threats with Eye of Destruction instead of trying to stand in front.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.E, Xerath.W]

- Use range and fog to pressure squishies, but keep a safe position for the ultimate channel.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support]
  abilities: [Xerath.R]

## Itemization

- Take Arcane Comet, Manaflow Band, Transcendence, and Scorch with Coup de Grace and Presence of Mind as the 16.21 rune page.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support-build]

- Build the popular path of Zaz'Zak's Realmspike, Sorcerer's Shoes, Luden's Echo, Shadowflame, and Rabadon's Deathcap.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support-build]

- Start with Health Potion, Amplifying Tome, and Stealth Ward as the displayed early purchases.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support-build]

- Choose Horizon Focus to replace Shadowflame in the fourth slot for longer-range damage or against immobilized targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support-build]

- Max Q, then W, then E, taking Rite of the Arcane at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-xerath-support-build]
  abilities: [Xerath.Q, Xerath.W, Xerath.E, Xerath.R]
