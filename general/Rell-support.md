## Key tips

- Reach level two before the enemy if possible, then use Rell's early all-in pressure to start a favorable trade.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- Preserve health while waiting for cooldowns; step outside auto-attack range when the engage tools are unavailable.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- Shattering Strike is useful against shielding because it can break shields before a follow-up engage.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- If enemy knock-ups or interrupts shut down lane engages, look for a roam or approach from an unwarded bush with Crash Down instead.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.W]

- Rell's level-three spike adds another ability to her all-in pattern and increases her crowd-control threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- Boots improve both roaming pace and the ability to stay on enemies after engaging.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- Start a roam from base or when the carry can farm safely without Rell's presence.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

## Mid lane

- Stay with the team and ward objectives only when enemy positions make the trip safe.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- Use Magnet Storm on grouped enemies to create a multi-target crowd-control window and force an objective or team fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.R]

- Coordinate with the jungler to set up picks in an unwarded enemy-jungle brush.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- After an engage, retreat toward the carry and peel rather than continuing forward without follow-up.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- A second Magnet Storm rank at level eleven shortens its cooldown and creates more mid-game all-in chances.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.R]

## Win condition

- Use Attract and Repel from a frontline ally's engage to extend crowd control onto the enemy team.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.E]

- When behind, avoid forcing a deep ultimate or Crash Down engage without allied damage; Rell can die before the team follows up.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.R, Rell.W]

- Late game, stay grouped and do not leave the carry exposed to an enemy punish.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]

- After using the ultimate in a late fight, fall back to protect carries and control the nearest threat.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.R]

- Look for picks at choke points, where the ultimate's area control can turn a catch into an objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.R]

- At level sixteen, Magnet Storm is fully ranked and its shorter cooldown supports repeated pick attempts.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support]
  abilities: [Rell.R]

## Itemization

- Build Celestial Opposition, Plated Steelcaps, Zeke's Convergence, Locket of the Iron Solari, and Knight's Vow as the 26.21 support core.
  items: [3869, 3047, 3050, 3190, 3109]
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support-build]

- Take Aftershock, Shield Bash, Bone Plating, Unflinching, Cosmic Insight, and Hextech Flashtraption as the rune setup.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support-build]

- Choose Thornmail as a situational fourth-item alternative to Locket against auto-attack-heavy teams or when anti-heal is needed.
  items: [3075]
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support-build]

- Start W, Q, E, max Crash Down first, then Attract and Repel, then Shattering Strike, ranking Magnet Storm at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-rell-support-build]
  abilities: [Rell.W, Rell.Q, Rell.E, Rell.R]
