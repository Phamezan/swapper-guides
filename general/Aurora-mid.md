- Bully early and contest mid 2v2s whenever the jungler can follow.

- Treat roughly 13 to 17 minutes as the strongest phase and keep pressing priority, tracking, and roams.

- Hunt backline angles with mobility and burst, but never pair Flash and R on a low-value target.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Stand ready to help the jungler invade, contest the river, or gank mid, checking first whether Aurora can push first.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Turn waveclear into priority, since enemies who chase a roam without it donate the wave for a worse play.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Spend priority on a roam, a jungle invade, or safer vision rather than idling in lane.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Trigger Electrocute fast with Q on the displayed page of Electrocute, Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, and Ultimate Hunter plus Manaflow Band and Transcendence.
  sources: [auroraaddict]
  abilities: [Aurora.Q]

- Start Doran's Ring with two Health Potions, then recall for Dark Seal, Refillable Potion, and Boots when affordable.
  items: [1056, 1082, 2031]
  sources: [auroraaddict]

## Mid lane

- Push side waves to force a response, then roam, invade, or slide toward an objective with the free time.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- When enemies collapse, burn escape tools to waste their time only if the team converts elsewhere during the distraction.
  sources: [auroraaddict]
  abilities: [Aurora.W]

- Control vision and lane priority late, then punish mispositioning with picks and objective pressure.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

## Win condition

- Link early priority with the jungler, then turn picks into vision and objective control.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Reach squishy backline targets from a safe angle in mid and late fights.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- While ahead, shove sides and force opponents to choose between answering Aurora and contesting the team objective.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

## Itemization

- Run Electrocute, Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, and Ultimate Hunter with Manaflow Band and Transcendence; Ultimate Hunter keeps the teamfight ultimate up more often.
  sources: [auroraaddict]
  abilities: [Aurora.R]

- Swap Celerity in when movement speed matters, especially top lane, pairing it with Boots of Swiftness.
  items: [3009]
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Try Presence of Mind plus Alacrity only for skirmish-heavy games, since the setup can run short on mana.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Take Flash and Teleport standard, Barrier into high-burst assassins, and Cleanse into Zoe bubble.
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Follow the standard page of Stormsurge into Sorcerer's Shoes, Shadowflame, Rabadon's Deathcap, Void Staff, and Zhonya's Hourglass.
  items: [4646, 3020, 4645, 3089, 3135, 3157]
  sources: [auroraaddict]

- Treat the Hextech Gunblade build as the alternate, and the anti-assassin setup as the explicitly weaker option.
