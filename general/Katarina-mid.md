## Key tips

- Guard health at level 1 outside opponent attack and spell range; it stays her weakest stage.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Roam from level 6 into other lanes and the jungle, since pre-6 kill and reset odds run lower.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Enter late fights only after most enemy crowd control is spent, since control shuts her down fast.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Watch for invades pre-lane and note which lane leashes the enemy jungler to predict their path.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Against an early-arriving jungler side, ward it around 2:30 to 3:00 and play toward the other side.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Rush level 2 for E; the level-2 trade works only with a safe way back out.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]
  abilities: [Katarina.E]

- At level 3, Q the enemy or a minion, E to the dagger, auto once, cast W, then E to an allied minion and out.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]
  abilities: [Katarina.Q, Katarina.E, Katarina.W]

- Alternate with E first into W and Q, then E to the Q dagger.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]
  abilities: [Katarina.E, Katarina.W, Katarina.Q]

- Avoid early deaths, since falling behind strangles the snowball.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- When opponents leave, pick freezing, shoving for a crash and plates, or following the roam.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

## Mid lane

- Farm the side lane after bot rotates mid to keep gold and experience flowing instead of sharing mid waves.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Rotate back the moment the team starts fighting; otherwise keep farming sides and building items.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Past six Dark Seal stacks and a first item, upgrade to Mejai's while ahead to sharpen the snowball.
  items: [1082]
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Group at Dragon and other majors, where takedowns feed resets that help close games.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

## Win condition

- Chain fights through the passive, which cuts remaining cooldowns after damaged champions die.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Route through picked-up daggers so each slash damages nearby enemies while repositioning.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Stay patient at objectives and never force an early engage that strands the team in a 4v5.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

## Itemization

- Take Electrocute for short lane trades and burst, Dark Harvest as the alternative, and Conqueror for longer fights and teamfights.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Pair Sudden Impact with dashes, Grisly Mementos for shorter trinket cooldown, and Relentless Hunter for post-takedown lane moves.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Add Triumph and Coup de Grace for survival and low-health finishes through reset chains.
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

- Start Second Wind with Doran's Shield into lane poke, plus Overgrowth for scaling health.
  items: [1054]
  sources: [prestigegalaxy]

  sources: [prestigegalaxy]
