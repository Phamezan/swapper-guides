## Key tips

- Powerfarm by default with occasional ganks and objective plays; never duel champions who dash through Wall of Pain and stick around.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.W]

- Treat the early 1v1 as unreliable against most junglers and contest duels only with range, vision, or teammate help.
  sources: [streetmagiks]

- Zone as a high-damage teamfighter from outside enemy range with Q and W while allied control opens targets.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q, Karthus.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Open Blue unless a strong enemy group contests it, with enemy Raptors into own Blue as the alternate start.
  sources: [streetmagiks]

- Farm camps with single-target Q on big monsters, then finish with the area hit once their health drops.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]

- Run Defile on Blue buff while mana allows, switching it off before it starves the Q casts that finish camps.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.E, Karthus.Q]

- Pull big monsters with max-range Q and drift toward the next camp between casts to cut idle time.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]

- Honor allies who signal off ganks; otherwise announce approaches with a couple of spaced pings.
  sources: [streetmagiks]

## Mid lane

- Draft Karthus with reliable allied control and early-surviving teammates; tanks pin enemies for long-range Q follow-up.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]

- Core Rylai's Crystal Scepter for utility in most builds, since its slow sets up Q against melee champions without dashes.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]

- Target at least 40 ability haste to make Q sequences land more reliably.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]

- Drop Wall of Pain in chokes or after enemy dashes; early walls waste the slow before allies follow.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.W]

- Pair Q into an immediate W to land the next Q, but lead W first when the wall needs exact placement.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q, Karthus.W]

- Move between Q casts to stay outside attack range and keep firing.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]

## Win condition

- Save Wall of Pain for short-range dashes when possible, then rebuild distance after the dash burns.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.W]

- Accept death value through passive damage and positioning only with teammates ready to follow it.
  sources: [streetmagiks]

- Fire an early mid-to-late Requiem to soften squishies for allied engage; hold it when one guaranteed finish beats less than teamwide damage.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.R]

- Steer opponents with edge-targeted Qs that fence safe ground instead of only chasing direct hits.
  sources: [streetmagiks]
  abilities: [Karthus.Q]
