## Key tips

- Accept a weak early phase into most lanes and farm for scaling over repeated mana-heavy trades.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Treat level 6 as the first clear spike, with a loaded stun plus Tibbers threatening kills and ganks.

  abilities: [Annie.R]

- Save Flash for high-value objective and teamfight engages instead of low-impact chases.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep the Pyromania stun ready to punish a spent enemy cooldown, stop an all-in, or set up the jungler.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Throw autos when the opponent mispositions, but respect mid gank angles and ward from a safe spot.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Play the level-10 spike when max-rank W gives enough priority to move first.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]
  abilities: [Annie.W]

- Slow-push ahead of a dive, recall, long roam, or safer lane; freeze to deny farm or hold the wave closer when behind.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Fast-push when priority matters for a dragon fight, a roam, a quick recall, or pinning the opponent under turret.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Use a neutral handshake wave on a centered cannon wave for a safer recall or roam.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Refuse forced roams by weighing lost minions, unspent gold, and target danger before leaving.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Take longer roams after a cannon wave starts pushing out or a crash strands many allied minions under the enemy turret.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

## Mid lane

- After pushing, convert the free time into vision, a roam, an efficient recall, or a jungler play instead of idling.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]

- Turn level-6 stun and Tibbers picks directly into objective pressure.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]
  abilities: [Annie.R]

- Claim priority with W waveclear, then rotate only when the wave state keeps the move cheap.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]
  abilities: [Annie.W]

## Win condition

- Protect farm and levels early, then open picks and mid priority at the level-6 and level-10 windows.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]
  abilities: [Annie.R]

- Drop the loaded stun and Tibbers onto a carry or a cluster, then turn the pick into objective pressure.
  sources: [cyb3r1a]
  abilities: [Annie.R]
