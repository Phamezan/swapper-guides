## Key tips

- Play a weaker lane around gank setup and skirmishes instead of forcing a solo lead.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- After level 6, trade harder on the relatively short ultimate cooldown.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.R]

- Remove priority targets with mobility; even a huge chunk can take them out of the coming fight.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Punish enemy cooldowns with WEQ or QW inside safe trade windows.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W, LeBlanc.E, LeBlanc.Q]

- Before level 6, hold the wave near the middle and stage the lane for a jungler gank.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Treat the strong level-2 QW into level-3 kill sequence as conditional, never automatic.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.Q, LeBlanc.W]

- Without an early lead, focus on not falling behind; she scales better than most players expect.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Clear ranged minions with autos first so W wipes them, then surprise with WEQ off their guard.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W, LeBlanc.E, LeBlanc.Q]

- Clear full waves later by autoing ranged minions once, waiting for low melees, then W-ing across and out.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

## Mid lane

- Run mid-game side lanes for picks and key-target removal in teamfights.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Hold CS even; once Luden's clears waves fast, shove sides before grouping.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Stretch side pressure further than less mobile champions can, while balancing farm against objective timers.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

- Join objective fights over abandoning the team to 4v5; take side waves only when the team can wait for the rotation.
  sources: [shokleblanc]

## Win condition

- Poke ahead with Q or QR when an approach opens.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.Q, LeBlanc.R]

- Bank W against unspent key control; patience stops dashes into lockdown.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.W]

- Peel a teammate with double chain only as the exception; default to deleting squishy targets unless the ADC runs very fed.
  sources: [shokleblanc]
  abilities: [LeBlanc.E]
