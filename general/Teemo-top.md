## Key tips

- Play the weaker early game for farm and wave control toward Liandry's: take one safe auto when an opponent steps up for a minion, then disengage instead of following into their engage range.
  sources: [trickybam]

- Save Q for opponents who rely on basic attacks or the moment they commit, since the blind stops basic attacks rather than most abilities.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.Q]

- Spend one shroom per wave only when it buys a useful recall or rotation; keep charges for escape routes and objectives, and start objective traps about a minute before Dragon or Baron when the team can safely enter.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Collect Dark Harvest stacks only when they cost no death and no missed wave; steady poke matters less than staying alive and on schedule.
  sources: [trickybam]

- Keep the wave near your side when walking up is dangerous, and when safe push fully before recalling, warding ahead of a hard push.
  sources: [trickybam]

- Start Doran's Ring, two Health Potions, and a Stealth Ward; max E first, Q second, and W last, taking R at levels six, eleven, and sixteen, with E-Q-E-W as the example opening.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.E, Teemo.Q, Teemo.W, Teemo.R]

- Maintain distance with W and pre-placed escape-route shrooms before trouble starts rather than reacting after the enemy commits.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.W, Teemo.R]

## Mid lane

- Push a safe wave before rotating, but never push deep near an objective spawn or while several opponents are missing.
  sources: [trickybam]

- Trap river entrances, corners, walls, and likely jungle paths, spreading charges across approaches so one Sweeper clears less, and hold one or two charges for the fight itself.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

- Ward objectives with Control Wards and consider Oracle Lens after lane; shrooms aid vision but never replace real wards.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

- When behind, farm safely and trap your own jungle entrances; never enter fog alone, and let opponents walk through the traps before helping contest after they back off.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

## Win condition

- Scale through safe farm into AP and shroom damage, weakening opponents on their approaches before Dragon or Baron fights rather than forcing plays early.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

- Value staying alive and on schedule over extra Dark Harvest stacks or kills; the shroom field becomes the main threat as items arrive.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

## Itemization

- Run Dark Harvest with Cheap Shot, Grisly Mementos, and Ultimate Hunter plus Absolute Focus and Gathering Storm; take safe stacks since shroom slows trigger Cheap Shot, Grisly Mementos feeds trinket uptime, and Ultimate Hunter means more frequent shrooms.
  sources: [trickybam]
  abilities: [Teemo.R]

- Build Liandry's Torment for max-health burn, Malignance for mana plus more frequent shrooms with magic-resistance reduction in its ground, then Shadowflame for the damage increase against targets below forty percent health, buying Sorcerer's Shoes when recall gold allows.
  sources: [trickybam]

- Into substantial purchased magic resistance follow the core with Void Staff and Rabadon's Deathcap, or Deathcap first without much resistance; take Gluttonous Greaves over Sorcerer's only for sustain games with no enemy Ignite and little other healing reduction.
  sources: [trickybam]
