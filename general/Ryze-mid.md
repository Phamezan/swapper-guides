## Key tips

- Rush Seraph's Embrace instead of Rod of Ages for earlier damage and ability haste with its shield as protection, stacking Tear of the Goddess toward completion around twelve to thirteen minutes.
  items: [6657, 3070]
  sources: [youngryze]

- Respect the weak early levels: avoid treating pre-Seraph's trades as guaranteed wins, push and roam only when the opponent permits it, and coordinate those moves with the jungler around objectives.
  sources: [youngryze]

- After Seraph's and Fimbulwinter, Ryze survives longer and can win some one-versus-two fights against unfed opponents; treat that durability as a build-dependent claim, not a reason to force fights.
  sources: [youngryze]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use E early to build Tear and Manaflow Band stacks while staying safe.
  sources: [youngryze]
  abilities: [Ryze.E]

- Shove and take the pre-level-six bot roam behind Dragon pit with enemy-jungle vision when the lane allows it, rather than sitting mid with no plan.
  sources: [youngryze]

- Treat the level-six Realm Warp unlock as the cross-map spike: after a recall, look for a fast ult to mid or top instead of walking back to lane on autopilot.
  sources: [youngryze]
  abilities: [Ryze.R]

- Open Tear of the Goddess with two Health Potions to start the stacking clock immediately.
  items: [3070]
  sources: [youngryze]

## Mid lane

- Work with the jungler for picks, or take a side lane when the map allows safe collection.
  sources: [youngryze]

- At the Seraph's plus Fimbulwinter durability spike, reported around three thousand health plus the Seraph's shield near eighteen minutes, Ryze can hold riskier positions.
  sources: [youngryze]

## Win condition

- In the late game, choose between side-lane pressure that pulls enemies off your team, grouping mid for picks before objectives, or a flank onto squishy carries.
  sources: [youngryze]

- Use Realm Warp to reach lanes or create an unexpected flank, but staying alive for the late fights matters more than the flashy angle.
  sources: [youngryze]
  abilities: [Ryze.R]

- Treat Actualizer as the late one-versus-two multiplier from the guide's training-dummy comparison of Q-W-E-Q, using it as a power spike rather than an excuse to fight while behind.
  items: [2522]
  sources: [youngryze]
  abilities: [Ryze.Q, Ryze.W, Ryze.E]

## Itemization

- Run Stormraider's Surge with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, Gathering Storm, Cash Back, and Cosmic Insight; open Tear and potions, then prioritize Lucidity boots with Seraph's, Fimbulwinter, Actualizer, and Rabadon's Deathcap.
  items: [2522, 3089]
  sources: [youngryze]

- Take Blackfire Torch as a late option and Warmog's Armor, Frozen Heart, Zhonya's Hourglass, Cryptbloom, or Cosmic Drive for the last slot; the alternate Rod of Ages route exists, though the author prefers Seraph's first for earlier haste and damage.
  items: [2503, 3083, 3110, 3157, 3137, 4629, 6657]
  sources: [youngryze]
