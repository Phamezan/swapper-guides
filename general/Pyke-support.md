## Overview

## Early lane

- Seek frequent trades and kills in lane to establish a health lead that can set up a later kill.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

- Use Ghostwater Dive and Phantom Undertow mobility to escape danger or reposition around an enemy engage.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]
  abilities: [Pyke.W, Pyke.E]

- Treat early boots as a roaming spike; use the movement to pressure mid and return to bot quickly.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

- After taking the bot tower, rotate toward mid and pressure its outer tower to open the map.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

## Mid lane

- Track the next major objective and establish vision around it while there is still time to retreat safely.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

- Look for picks with allied follow-up; use the opponent's death timer to take a tower, an objective, or further kills.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

- Additional Death from Below ranks improve its damage and availability, creating more mid-game chances to secure kills.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]
  abilities: [Pyke.R]

- Level sixteen is a major spike because the ultimate becomes available more often for fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]
  abilities: [Pyke.R]

## Win condition

- Convert early pressure and level advantages into kills, then use roaming and vision control to keep the map unstable for opponents.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

- In late fights, wait for enemy cooldowns or Flash to be spent before committing to a high-value ultimate attempt.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]
  abilities: [Pyke.R]

- Stay with the team late and avoid solo warding or isolated rotations that could leave allies outnumbered.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]

- Peel for allies when needed, while looking for low-health targets whose deaths can reset Death from Below.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]
  abilities: [Pyke.R]

- Pyke's late game is weaker than his mid game, so prioritize safe objective vision and decisive picks over wandering alone.
  sources: [mobalytics-pyke-support]
