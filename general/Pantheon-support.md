## Key tips

- Control the lane brushes so you can surprise opponents with Shield Vault and keep them away from minions.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.W]

- Poke with Comet Spear to build a health lead, then all-in once the opponent is low enough to finish.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.Q]

- Level six is more valuable for roaming than for winning every lane all-in; use Grand Starfall to create plays elsewhere.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- At level one, work from a brush and choose poke or an all-in based on the opening rather than walking into a prepared fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]

- Hold Aegis Assault for a committed trade where blocking damage turns the fight; Pantheon is vulnerable while it is unavailable.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.E]

- Keep the wave even or slightly on your side so you can run the enemy down while staying safer from ganks.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]

- After taking the bot tower or when the lane goes quiet, treat the unlocked ultimate as a roaming tool and look for the first cross-map opening.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

## Mid lane

- Use Grand Starfall for cross-map plays after a recall or when another lane needs help.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Lock down a high-priority target and work with the jungler to pick off the enemy jungler or support.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.W]

- Flank with the ultimate during an ongoing fight so enemies have less time to react to the arrival.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- Use extra roaming speed from boots to create more plays across the map and convert that movement into pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]

- A second ultimate rank mainly reduces its cooldown, so use the more frequent windows to set up picks rather than expecting a larger utility jump.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

## Win condition

- In late game, look for picks instead of forcing a front-to-back five-versus-five; Pantheon's damage falls off as opponents buy defenses.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]

- Stay near neutral objectives and track the enemy jungler so you can contest or protect the objective instead of being caught on the opposite side.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]

- Use fog of war to reach enemy carries from an unexpected angle; if the team needs a frontline, a tankier build can fill that role.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]

- Avoid starting an ultimate engage so far ahead of the team that nobody can follow up; the landing position can leave Pantheon exposed.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

- If Grand Starfall is unavailable, delay a late-game fight or disengage rather than committing without the main engage tool.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support]
  abilities: [Pantheon.R]

## Itemization

- Take Press the Attack, Triumph, Legend: Haste, Coup de Grace, Cheap Shot, and Relentless Hunter as the 26.21 support rune page.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support-build]

- Build Bloodsong, Plated Steelcaps, Umbral Glaive, Eclipse, and Black Cleaver as the 26.21 support core.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support-build]

- Choose Sundered Sky as the alternative to Eclipse when the game calls for its critical strike and healing as a bruiser damage-and-sustain option.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support-build]

- Start W, Q, E, then max Comet Spear first followed by Shield Vault and Aegis Assault, upgrading Grand Starfall at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-pantheon-support-build]
  abilities: [Pantheon.W, Pantheon.Q, Pantheon.E, Pantheon.R]
