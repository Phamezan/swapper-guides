## Overview

## Early lane

- Stand behind minions into hook and engage lanes so they eat the enemy line to Ezreal.
  sources: [moosicel]

- Line up beside the wave when aiming Q so minions intercept fewer shots.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.Q]

- Skip Ezreal into multiple tanks or reliably outscaling compositions; check the draft before accepting a low-pressure lane.
  sources: [moosicel]

- Prefer single- or low-tank enemy teams, while still demanding accurate Q and W hits plus autos for real damage.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.Q, Ezreal.W]

## Mid lane

- Push outer turrets across bot, mid, and top to convert mid-game strength into a lead.
  sources: [moosicel]

- Fire R into nearby and cross-map skirmishes; a good hit sets up dragon and turret takes for teammates.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.R]

- Spend E offensively only for a secured kill, keeping it otherwise as the collapse-proof escape.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.E]

- Blend Q, W, and autos for damage instead of relying on Q alone.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.Q, Ezreal.W]

- Use the ultimate to sway fights away from lane rather than saving it purely for lane duels.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.R]

## Win condition

- Poke ahead of full fights so opponents engage down on health.
  sources: [moosicel]

- Map terrain and escape routes before fights; E crosses walls out of dangerous control chains.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.E]

- Spend E and Flash as separate repositions only when each keeps a safe attack angle; never burn both to start without an exit.
  sources: [moosicel]
  abilities: [Ezreal.E]

- Focus priority targets with accurate Q and W damage, then move before divers arrive.

  abilities: [Ezreal.Q, Ezreal.W]
