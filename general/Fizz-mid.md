## Key tips

- Start E in most lanes and refuse trades until level 3, treating level-2 trades as matchup-only.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Guard E as both damage and the main dodge or escape; casual casts delete all pressure until it returns.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Throw max-range R for the biggest shark with stronger damage, slow, and teamfight disruption even without a kill.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Donate some of the first two waves against heavy poke to keep health for later trades.
  sources: [gladiore]

- At level 1, last-hit melee minions with E while sparing the caster line, then let the opponent push in.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

- Take W at level 2 and take only safe last-hits instead of eating free autos.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.W]

- When wave two crashes under tower, expect wave three back toward center with a chance at first level 3.
  sources: [gladiore]

- Trade at level 3 with Q onto the enemy, auto plus W, keep hitting until they answer, then E away to dodge and slow.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q, Fizz.W, Fizz.E]

- After a good level-3 trade, shove fast for level 4 and crash the wave.
  sources: [gladiore]

- At level 4, check kill range before all-inning with Flash, Q, W, and Ignite.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q, Fizz.W]

- For that level-4 commit, take a second W point over a second E point, then return to maxing E.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.W, Fizz.E]

- On level-4 dives, drop turret aggro with E instead of spending it purely for damage.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.E]

## Mid lane

- Buy Dark Seal early, upgrading to Mejai's only past 10 stacks and when the stacks project to survive.
  sources: [gladiore]

- Keep Zhonya's Hourglass second for safer engages, dives, and side duels.
  sources: [gladiore]

- Q to a minion after E to finish the wave or land in a safer trade spot.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q, Fizz.E]

## Win condition

- Land R on a backline carry to decide fights, accepting the hard execution cost.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.R]

- Hide the cast and stretch its reach with Q through a minion into an immediate R.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q, Fizz.R]

- Q onto a minion after E to apply W or exit safely rather than leaning on Q damage alone.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.Q, Fizz.E, Fizz.W]

## Itemization

- Run Press the Attack with Triumph, Legend: Haste, and Coup de Grace, plus Manaflow Band and Transcendence, with Flash and Ignite.
  sources: [gladiore]

- Recall first for Dark Seal with enough gold, upgrading to Mejai's at 10 stacks only when they project to live.
  sources: [gladiore]

- Hold Zhonya's second and use its active to extend an engage or stall for the next rotation.
  sources: [gladiore]

- Choose Lich Bane for all-in damage and movement speed, or Dusk and Dawn as the preferred teamfight spellblade.
  sources: [gladiore]

- On Dusk and Dawn, trigger W before the first spellblade hit to double its on-hit effect, setting up with W then Q.
  sources: [gladiore]
  abilities: [Fizz.W, Fizz.Q]

- Pair Press the Attack with the Dusk and Dawn route.
  sources: [gladiore]
