## Key tips

- Make plays from level 3, especially with red buff, when nearby lanes bring setup or crowd control.
  sources: [christi21]

- Default to a sequenced full clear in camp order instead of breaking it for low-percentage ganks.
  sources: [christi21]

- Convert the level-6, level-9 max-Q, Dirk, and first-item spikes into a fight, invade, or objective rather than auto-returning to camps.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Run the example clear of red, Krugs, Raptors, Wolves, Gromp, then blue.
  sources: [christi21]

- End clears toward lanes with reliable control or setup, never pathing somewhere merely because it sits close.
  sources: [christi21]

- Track enemy camp order for safe early invades into slower farmers or tank junglers, refusing forced plays into strong control without priority.
  sources: [christi21]

- Gank outside common vision toward allies who can lock targets down, since Graves brings little control himself.
  sources: [christi21]

- Hold W until the target spends a dash or Flash when possible, and save E for the angle or follow-up that holds attack range.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.W, Graves.E]

- Play E around the two-shell reload: it reloads a shell and resets an attack, so an early dash wastes the damage window mid-reload.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.E]

- Dash diagonally to stay in attack range without flying past the target or opening an escape gap.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.E]

- Shorten Q with terrain; its return detonates sooner off a wall hit.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.Q]

## Mid lane

- Prefer Hubris as the snowball first item on the lethality route, keeping Youmuu's Ghostblade as the less-used mobility and lethality alternative.
  items: [6697, 3142]
  sources: [christi21]

- Stretch side-lane plays into towers, objectives, safe enemy camps, and deep vision instead of resetting to only own camps.
  sources: [christi21]

- Carry a control ward for dragon and Herald setups, firing Q through chokes and terrain in small skirmishes for more return hits.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.Q]

- Never face-check even on True Grit stacks, and never dash forward without a secured kill; keep E as the exit near control.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.E]

- Pick between the standard full clear and the Raptors-start variant that lines up a mid gank after Scuttle plus a big recall, by lane setup and invade safety.
  sources: [christi21]

## Win condition

- While ahead, rule the enemy jungle with early dueling and mobility for repeat skirmishes on efficient camp tempo.
  sources: [christi21]

- Flank isolated carries pre-objective on lethality builds, sweeping first and avoiding visible routes.
  sources: [christi21]

- On bruiser builds, hold maximum attack range and kite the nearest frontline instead of forcing one-shots.
  sources: [christi21]

- Finish low targets with R or recoil backward for space, never opening with it and gifting room to escape.
  sources: [christi21]
  abilities: [Graves.R]

## Itemization

- Run Dark Harvest with Precision secondary.
  sources: [christi21]

- Stack lethality through Hubris into The Collector and Lord Dominik's Regards for snowballing squishier targets.
  items: [6697, 6676, 3036]
  sources: [christi21]

- Start Youmuu's Ghostblade and Collector before crit on the alternate path, or Umbral Glaive and Sundered Sky on the bruiser path.
  items: [3142, 3179, 6610]
  sources: [christi21]

- Take the blue pet for speed and reach, red for direct damage and kill pressure, or green for durability into burst and heavy control.
  sources: [christi21]

- Wear Plated Steelcaps into auto-heavy physical teams and Mercury's Treads into high magic damage or control.
  items: [3047, 3111]
  sources: [christi21]

- Prioritize Serrated Dirk plus basic boots on the first recall, leaving finished boots until after the first two cores.
  items: [3134]
  sources: [christi21]
