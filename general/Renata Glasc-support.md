## Key tips

- Poke with Handshake while moving in and out of brush; add an auto attack, then step back to limit return damage.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.E, Renata Glasc.Q]

- Leverage Rumor can grab a minion and send it into an enemy champion for a brief stun, giving another way to start a trade.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.Q]

- Cast Bailout just before an allied carry would die to create a chance for them to turn the fight; the buff resets on takedowns.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Take Leverage Rumor early as trading crowd control for the lane.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.Q]

- Weave autos into the Handshake poke pattern, since Renata's passive rewards alternating attacks with allies.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.E, Renata Glasc.Q]

- Her Hostile Takeover travels slowly, so do not treat the ultimate as a solo initiation tool; use it after landing hard crowd control or to counter an enemy dive.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.R]

- Take Ignite for lane kill pressure, and take Exhaust instead when a dive threat is likely to reach the carries.
  sources: [lathyrus]

- If the enemy support catches you or your partner with crowd control, answer by aiming Handshake and Leverage Rumor at their carry to discourage the follow-up.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.E, Renata Glasc.Q]

## Mid lane

- Bailout can also help a carry chase by granting movement speed toward enemy champions.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.W]

- Self-cast Bailout can buy time during a tower dive when Renata herself is being focused.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.W]

- Save Hostile Takeover for clustered fights around Dragon or Baron, where the confined area makes its slow-moving wave harder to avoid.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.R]

- Treat Hostile Takeover after landing Leverage Rumor as a safer follow-up than casting the ultimate alone.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.Q, Renata Glasc.R]

## Win condition

- Build enough durability to stay alive and keep peeling; the guide favors a tankier support role over maximizing enchanter stats.
  sources: [lathyrus]

- Protect the carry with Handshake shields and the last-second Bailout window, then use Leverage Rumor or Hostile Takeover to disrupt divers.
  sources: [lathyrus]
  abilities: [Renata Glasc.E, Renata Glasc.W, Renata Glasc.Q, Renata Glasc.R]

- Stay alive through team fights so Renata can keep applying peel and crowd control.
  sources: [lathyrus]
