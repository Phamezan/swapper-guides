## Key tips

- Start with the wave close to the turret and let the opponent push; the long top lane gives Yasuo a safer chase if they overextend.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Avoid losing too much health to poke before level three; if the opponent is extended and the wave is set, Yasuo can look for an all-in around that spike.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Do not fight without checking the Steel Tempest tornado and passive shield timing; a trade after the tornado expires leaves Yasuo without knock-up or ultimate setup.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Fake a leash or let the opponent hit Yasuo through the minions to help the wave move toward the turret.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- If Wind Wall is not useful against the lane opponent, use it to block ranged minion attacks and help hold the freeze.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- Contest only farm that does not cost most of the health bar before level three; preserve enough health to threaten the all-in window.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

## Mid lane

- Play the side lane near the next objective; use Teleport from top to affect Baron or Dragon rather than arriving late.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- After pushing a side wave toward the enemy tier-two tower, take nearby camps when safe to build the lead and deny experience.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Use control wards and clear enemy vision while extending into their jungle; back off when the next wave or objective is unsafe.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

## Win condition

- If Yasuo is the main carry, wait for key enemy crowd control to be used before entering and use Wind Wall to block burst.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- Engage only when the team is close enough to follow; Yasuo is better as follow-up than as a blind initiator.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- If even or behind while an ally is carrying, peel for that ally with Wind Wall and knock-ups instead of forcing a solo highlight play.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W, Yasuo.Q]

- Against key projectiles, keep Wind Wall for the enemy ability that would otherwise decide the fight.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.W]

- Build more durability when the team needs a frontline; the guide suggests Randuin's Omen or Spirit Visage based on the opposing damage.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

## Itemization

- Take Lethal Tempo, Absorb Life, Legend: Alacrity, and Cut Down with Second Wind and Overgrowth as the standard page.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Take Flash and Ignite as the usual lane spells; consider Teleport for top-lane objective pressure, Cleanse into heavy hard crowd control, or Exhaust against burst.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Max Q, then E, then W, taking Last Breath at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
  abilities: [Yasuo.Q, Yasuo.E, Yasuo.W, Yasuo.R]

- Build Berserker's Greaves, Blade of the Ruined King, Infinity Edge, Immortal Shieldbow, Death's Dance, and Mercurial Scimitar as the main path.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]

- Swap toward armor or magic resistance when needed, listing Randuin's Omen and Thornmail against physical threats and Spirit Visage or Force of Nature against heavy ability power.
  sources: [wayofthetempesst]
