## Key tips

- Spike at level 2 with W and Q together, then use the early level edge to start a skirmish.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q, Alistar.W]

- Reach level 3 fast and engage once an opponent steps past their safe line.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

- Save R for tower dives and objective fights so its protection covers the whole play.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- At level 2, combine displacement and knock-up the moment the level lead opens.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q, Alistar.W]

- When the lane sits safely, roam elsewhere, with stronger roams once boots land.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

- Aim Q at the most important or mobile target during an engage so they cannot slip the follow-up.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q]

- Before crashing a big wave for a dive, sync with the jungler and keep R to soak tower pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.R]

- At level 6, try riskier bot or mid dives when allies can follow up.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.R]

## Mid lane

- Roam through the enemy jungle, plant deep wards, track their jungler, and back invades.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

- At objectives, hunt a multi-person W and Q knock-up, or zone their jungler from the pit when no angle exists.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q, Alistar.W]

- Find flank paths with vision and displace the enemy carry with Q, diving only with teammates behind.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q]

- Group to create more multi-target W and Q chances, since lower cooldowns also open extra picks.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q, Alistar.W]

- Buy defensive items in this phase to hold the frontline through longer fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

## Win condition

- Open with W and Q only when the team stands close enough to turn the control into a kill or objective.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.Q, Alistar.W]

- Hunt late side picks and flanks through the enemy jungle, including a support stepping out to ward.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

- Dive enemy carries with R active, then drop back to guard allied carries and release tower aggro before running low.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
  abilities: [Alistar.R]

- Before a big neutral fight, set a pick on the enemy jungler with vision, then zone their carries while the team secures it.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

## Itemization

- Buy boots early to unlock stronger roams across the map.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]

- Stack defensive items afterward so Alistar can frontline and survive longer fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-alistar]
