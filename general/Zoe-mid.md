## Key tips

- With Electrocute and Ignite, play for solo-kill pressure after Sleepy Trouble Bubble lands, following with Paddle Star and Ignite.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.E, Zoe.Q]

- Trade Paddle Star into an empowered passive auto into Paddle Star again for Electrocute; the empowered auto keeps the exchange useful even when Q misses.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.Q]

- Fish for Bubble as an opponent steps forward to last-hit, when their movement turns predictable, and after level six clear the back wave with Paddle Star when damage allows to remove minion cover for Bubble attempts.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.E, Zoe.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Extend a forward Bubble angle with Portal Jump only from a safe launch point, since hard engage punishes Zoe hardest.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.E, Zoe.R]

- Conserve mana before Lost Chapter and blue buff, spending nothing on cooldown without a clear trade or wave purpose.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

- On the Unsealed Spellbook and Teleport plan, trade health for the opponent's, then recall and teleport back to make them lose farm.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

- Open Doran's Ring and Health Potions, with Dark Seal, boots, and Lost Chapter among the early buys.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

## Mid lane

- Skip extended side-laning: collect side waves as they reach the tower, pushing them only toward the river rather than committing long.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

- Balance that safe farm with arriving early at dragon or Rift Herald fights, where the long-range pick can decide whether the enemy contests.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

- When first to an objective, hit a high-value target with Bubble or Paddle Star to create the advantage before both teams fully commit.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.E, Zoe.Q]

## Win condition

- Throw Sleepy Trouble Bubble through walls and follow with Paddle Star poke, using terrain for cover so the return engage never finds Zoe.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.E, Zoe.Q]

- Prioritize Bubble and poke on enemy carries when the angle exists; otherwise chip the frontline instead of forcing a risky dive.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.E, Zoe.Q]

- Fire Portal Jump only from a position whose return point stays safe, keeping hard engage answered by distance.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
  abilities: [Zoe.R]

## Itemization

- Run Electrocute with Taste of Blood, Deep Ward, Relentless Hunter, Nimbus Cloak, and Scorch, with Unsealed Spellbook and Teleport as the separate recall-and-return lane plan.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

- Complete Luden's Echo, Sorcerer's Shoes, Shadowflame, Rabadon's Deathcap, Void Staff, and Horizon Focus, choosing Ionian Boots, Sorcerer's Shoes, or Mercury's Treads by game with Mercury's flagged against double AP and crowd control.
  sources: [shok-zoe]

- Keep Mejai's Soulstealer for snowballs, Morellonomicon for healing, Zhonya's or Banshee's for defense, and Lich Bane for damage as situational picks.
  sources: [shok-zoe]
