## Key tips

- Prioritize farming and health through level two, then begin regular trades at level three rather than forcing damage early.
  sources: [deylan]

- Always clear the wave before roaming: the cleared wave is the window for a kill or an objective, and leaving it uncleared wastes the turn.
  sources: [deylan]
  abilities: [Qiyana.Q]

- In teamfights, hunt a multi-target ultimate when enemies line up, with E-Flash-R as the main engage example; use water Q for the stun, grass Q for concealment, and stone Q for the execute hit.
  sources: [deylan, allyooops]
  abilities: [Qiyana.Q, Qiyana.W, Qiyana.E, Qiyana.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Against a melee opponent with a weak level one, start W, dash in, and land an empowered auto to proc Electrocute; take this only when the early matchup allows it.
  sources: [deylan]
  abilities: [Qiyana.W]

- Use the listed openers: E into auto, grass Q, W, water Q, or water Q into W and stone Q; the full all-in adds autos and Ignite around stone Q into W and stone Q.
  sources: [deylan]
  abilities: [Qiyana.Q, Qiyana.W, Qiyana.E]

- Run Electrocute as the lane-pressure setup for early trades and pushing out squishy opponents; skip its defensive costs only when the burst plan fits.
  sources: [allyooops]

- Take Conqueror for the longer level-two trade into opponents who stack health: W in, keep auto-attacking, then Q-W-Q for sustained damage over burst.
  sources: [deylan]
  abilities: [Qiyana.Q, Qiyana.W]

- Chip opponents down and punish their mistakes rather than forcing a full-health one-shot the kit cannot reliably finish.
  sources: [deylan]

## Mid lane

- After clearing mid, turn toward a roam, a kill, or an objective instead of hovering on an empty lane.
  sources: [deylan]

- Match the build path to the team's need: the assassin path for burst targets, the Conqueror cooldown path for extended fights, or the scaling path with Muramana, Profane Hydra, Death's Dance, and Serylda's Grudge into several tanks.
  items: [6698, 6333, 6694]
  sources: [allyooops]

## Win condition

- Enter with E-Flash-R onto a lined-up group, and when no group lines up, remove the enemy carry as the fight-deciding play even without a one-shot.
  sources: [deylan, allyooops]
  abilities: [Qiyana.E, Qiyana.R]

- If a key squishy sits in execute range, prioritize that target over a flashier multi-target angle.
  sources: [deylan]
  abilities: [Qiyana.Q]

- Treat grass Q as the teamfight survival tool: its concealment hides Qiyana through the chaos so she can close and reposition between targets.
  sources: [deylan]
  abilities: [Qiyana.Q, Qiyana.W]

## Itemization

- Choose Electrocute with Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, Ultimate Hunter, Presence of Mind, and Last Stand for lane pressure and burst, or Conqueror for repeated autos and extended fights into health-stacking opponents.
  sources: [allyooops, deylan]

- Start Doran's Blade with a potion and buy Long Sword, Serrated Dirk, boots, a Control Ward, and Oracle Lens at the first recall.
  items: [1055, 1036, 3134, 2055, 3364]
  sources: [deylan]

- Build the assassin core of Profane Hydra, Voltaic Cyclosword, Axiom Arc, and Serylda's Grudge; on the Conqueror path start Hubris into Profane Hydra, with Edge of Night, Guardian Angel, Death's Dance, and The Collector as situational picks.
  items: [6698, 6699, 6696, 6694, 6697, 3814, 3026, 6333, 6676]
  sources: [allyooops, deylan]
