## Key tips

- Compare both full team compositions before loading in and aim the build direction at the likely fights.
  sources: [thespian]

- Treat passive marks as the reason to press nearby river and jungle control, skipping any mark that invites a collapse.
  sources: [thespian]

- Scale on marks and safe range, then deal sustained damage from fight edges with Lamb's Respite banked against decisive burst.

## Overview

## Early lane

- Preview early 1v1 and 2v2 lane outcomes, marking which lanes push first and where kills look plausible.
  sources: [thespian]

- Let likely lane priority and the enemy opener strength decide the first route and contest appetite.
  sources: [thespian]

- Press the tempo window against weaker early junglers with threatening ganks instead of conceding it.
  sources: [thespian]

- Track early duelists who win counterganks and refuse to show on their side first.
  sources: [thespian]

- Run the same matchup-and-priority read on mid and bot before locking any route.
  sources: [thespian]

- Review unclear matchups and early duels after games so future invade calls rest on learned outcomes.
  sources: [thespian]

## Mid lane

- Convert successful picks and invades into a camp lead, vision, or an objective instead of chasing into fog.
  sources: [thespian]

## Win condition

- Check enemy control and ally follow-up before starting any fight; Kindred needs kite room and sustained attacks.
  sources: [thespian]

- Hold Lamb's Respite for the burst that decides fights rather than spending it early.
  sources: [thespian]
  abilities: [Kindred.R]
