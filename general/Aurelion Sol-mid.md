## Key tips

- Rank survival first, then XP, then last-hit gold; skip unsafe Stardust early.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Play for the two-item plus empowered-ultimate spike around 18 to 20 minutes, and stay cautious before it.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Favor Rylai's early in most lanes for health and self-peel; save Blackfire Torch first for safer lanes.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Lean on a stronger level 1 into many opponents, especially melee champions forced to walk through Q, while respecting a weak overall lane.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q]

- Tap Q briefly to stack Manaflow Band, Scorch, and item effects while keeping mana for clearing and defense.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q]

- Before level 9, soften melee minions with autos first, since the short max channel clears poorly; then collect low units with E.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q, Aurelion Sol.E]

- On cannon waves, stand slightly off-center and hold Q on the cannon so the beam covers more of the wave for extra Stardust.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q]

- Cancel Q rather than hold the beam into a Charm, root, or other threat; a few lost stacks beat lost health or death.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q]

- Ward enemy raptors around 1:20 to 1:25 at level 1, then stand away from the side the jungler clears.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Match the first recall buy to the planned core: near 900 gold Fated Ashes, near 1,200 Lost Chapter, near 1,300 Catalyst of Aeons, or Dark Seal plus refillable when short.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Time recalls to return with health and mana before objectives, and concede early objectives rather than fight weak and under-resourced.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Skip roams before level 9 unless the kill, assist, or objective help outweighs the missed mid wave.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

## Mid lane

- Push side waves and stay objective-ready; after the outer bot turret falls, expect bot lane to drift mid.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Pair Astral Flight with Rylai's slow and Falling Star for a far stronger catch.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.E]

- Around 25 minutes, target roughly 300 Stardust with a third item; with Teleport, push opposite the next objective unless a teammate covers it.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- In fights, hit the highest-damage carry when safe, or spend Falling Star on peel and zoning when an ally needs it more.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Chain cleanup with Astral Flight resets, since a takedown shortly after damaging an enemy refunds most of its remaining cooldown; still refuse to fly into danger for a chase.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.E]

- After level 9, clear a full cannon wave with a slightly off-center Q for about eight stacks, using E plus Q to execute minions for Stardust.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q, Aurelion Sol.E]

## Win condition

- Survive the weak early phase while banking safe Stardust, then take over around the two-item and empowered-ultimate windows.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Set up catches or guard the carry with Rylai's, E zone control, and Q range.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q, Aurelion Sol.E]

- Hold a safe angle in teamfights and spend R on the highest-value target, or on peel and zoning into dangerous dive.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.R]

## Itemization

- Take Deathfire Touch for late teamfights and multi-target burns, or Arcane Comet for early Q-tap poke in mid.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Stack Manaflow Band with brief Q taps; pair Absolute Focus plus Scorch for lane pressure, or Transcendence plus Gathering Storm for safer scaling.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
  abilities: [Aurelion Sol.Q]

- Add Precision secondary with Cut Down for riskier damage, Inspiration for sustain and free boots, or Resolve with Bone Plating into burst and Second Wind into poke plus Overgrowth.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Take Flash by default, Teleport for safe lanes and map play, Barrier into high-burst assassins, and Cleanse into Zoe bubble.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Build Rylai's Crystal Scepter first or second with Rod of Ages, Blackfire Torch, or Liandry's Torment, since its slow feeds Q and E while adding health.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Default to Rylai's into Liandry's; recall for Tear or Sapphire Crystal mana, then sell or grow it into Archangel's or Blackfire later.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Reserve health-less Blackfire Torch first for low-threat lanes, and Rod of Ages first for hard or scaling lanes despite its slower stacked payoff.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Add Bloodletter's Curse third or fourth into resistance or AP-heavy teams, Zhonya's into heavy AD and burst, and Void Staff into high MR.
  sources: [eventhorizon]

- Take Banshee's Veil into AP or engage, Shadowflame for squishies, Horizon Focus as cheaper damage and vision when behind, and Riftmaker for long fights into tanky or high-sustain teams.
  sources: [eventhorizon]
