## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep W active while clearing and weave basic attacks, since each hit lowers E cooldown.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]
  abilities: [Amumu.W, Amumu.E]

- Hold Q for gank starts and wall shortcuts rather than spending it inside routine camps.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]
  abilities: [Amumu.Q]

## Mid lane

- Use R to flip a skirmish with a group stun, or press it early as duel pressure onto an isolated squishy.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]
  abilities: [Amumu.R]

- Bring an ally to the first dragon; attempt later solo takes only with vision, and bail or call help when it turns unsafe.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]

- Before Rift Herald, budget its mana cost and ward the river approach first.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]

- After R catches several opponents, follow with Q and full control so the team can finish the fight.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]
  abilities: [Amumu.R, Amumu.Q]

- Hand kills to carries after a pick whenever possible, since Amumu exists to set them up.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]

## Win condition

- Start fights where R can catch multiple enemies, then hold them still long enough for teammates to deal damage.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]
  abilities: [Amumu.R]

- Stay beside the most fed ally late and peel with control instead of diving away from the team.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]

- Punish a lone squishy with Q and the full kit on a safe route, but never turn an engage into a death without follow-up.
  sources: [averagelilliaplayer]
  abilities: [Amumu.Q]
