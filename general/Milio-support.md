## Key tips

- Handle levels 1 to 5 carefully into engage supports, saving health and mana before trading behind Warm Hugs shielding.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.E]

- Save Ultra Mega Fire Kick to break engages and guard the carry; its knockback also stops incoming dashes, so never waste it randomly.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.Q]

- Win by stretching fights and defusing enemy engages so a scaling carry keeps attacking.
  sources: [ssl]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Cast E before damage lands and keep both charges for meaningful trades, since the two shields stack on one ally.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.E]

- Add poke to help the ADC win short trades from outside engage range, never stepping in to force it.
  sources: [ssl]

- Open three ranks in E then max Cozy Campfire, E, and Q as the displayed order.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.E, Milio.W, Milio.Q]

- At level 6, track enemy engage tools and flip them with Breath of Life instead of spending R on the first minor control effect.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.R]

## Mid lane

- Travel with the ADC or the next strongest carry, since Milio peaks while amplifying reliable allied damage.
  sources: [ssl]

- Set W slightly behind the carry pre-fight for forward kiting inside its healing range, retargeting the campfire as they move.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.W]

- Rotate early to objectives beside the enabled carry, contesting space with range and attack-speed buffs from safety.
  sources: [ssl]

- With enemy engage up, hold Q and R ready behind the carry instead of walking up to start fights.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.Q, Milio.R]

## Win condition

- Stand behind and slightly off-axis from the carry, dodging shared area control while staying in shield and cleanse range.
  sources: [ssl]

- Clear disabling and immobilizing chains with R while healing nearby allies, remembering it never removes knockups.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.R]

- Aim Q at divers and engagers to knock them off the carry; a wasted Q invites collapses onto Milio.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.Q]

- Refresh E shields through danger and use W range so the carry holds damage uptime from farther out.
  sources: [ssl]
  abilities: [Milio.E, Milio.W]

- Apply the passive through ally-facing spells before their attacks, granting bonus magic damage on the next attack or ability.
  sources: [ssl]
