## Key tips

- Nasus is weak early, so avoid trading until you have several levels or a clear opening and prioritize safe farming instead.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- Collect Siphoning Strike stacks steadily because they drive his later damage, but do not risk health for a stack when the trade is unsafe.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Level six is the first major dueling spike because Fury of the Sands gives him the tools to run down an overextended lane opponent.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Keep collecting Siphoning Strike stacks without taking bad trades, since falling behind on stacks weakens the whole game plan.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Completing the first item improves damage and survivability, but keep farming stacks rather than treating the purchase as a reason to force every fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- Keep the wave in a safe spot and concede contested last hits early instead of trading health for farm you cannot take for free.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- If bot lane takes its outer tower and rotates top, move around the map to play with the team rather than staying alone in a stale lane.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

## Mid lane

- Keep stacking Siphoning Strike as the game moves toward larger fights so Nasus does not fall behind on damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Rotate to assist teammates when needed, but do not abandon a safe objective or overcommit to a low-value chase.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- Draw opponents to your side of the map while allies siege an objective elsewhere.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- At level eleven, the second rank of Fury of the Sands is a power spike for both durability and kill pressure.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.R]

- Max Siphoning Strike first, then Wither, then Spirit Fire, upgrading Fury of the Sands at levels six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top-build]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q, Nasus.W, Nasus.E, Nasus.R]

## Win condition

- In late game, split push for towers while teammates group elsewhere; pressure on your lane can open the map for them.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- Group when there is no safe objective left to take alone or the team needs Nasus to frontline.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]

- In teamfights, Wither the approaching frontline and focus the nearest enemy while peeling for the carries.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.W]

- High Siphoning Strike stacks let Nasus threaten unprotected carries, so use that threat to force the enemy to respond before the team takes objectives elsewhere.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

- Keep stacking and building rather than forcing early fights, since his strongest carry window is late game.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top]
  abilities: [Nasus.Q]

## Itemization

- Build the popular 26.21 core of Trinity Force, Lucidity boots, Frozen Heart, Spirit Visage, and Thornmail.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top-build]

- Take Fleet Footwork, Triumph, Legend: Haste, Last Stand, Second Wind, and Unflinching as the standard rune page.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top-build]

- Choose Randuin's Omen over Spirit Visage when the enemy has heavy physical damage or critical-strike attackers.
  sources: [mobalytics-nasus-top-build]
