## Key tips

- Land Q first so E finds a marked target, refunds its cooldown, and enables another dash.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.Q, Diana.E]

- Initiate teamfights with R, whose pull also threatens several enemies at once.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.R]

- Hold R until Flash, blink, and dash escapes are spent, since its delay lets those tools dodge it.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Treat passive stacks as the lane and early-fight currency on either the AP or attack-speed build.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Dash through targets with Q, keeping the reset line in mind when other damage kills the target mid-dash.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.Q]

- Soak AP dives with Defiant Dance, which also answers back while charged.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.W]

- Read W as physical damage with less value into heavy armor, and hold it as defense there when needed.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.W]

- Delay the second E blade to stretch the stun setup, and open from out of vision so the catch stays hard to read.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.E]

- After a confirmed E stun, start charging W at once and follow with R near its peak charge.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.E, Diana.W, Diana.R]

- Respect the flagged gank vulnerability before extending for lane pressure.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Never assume free shoves into pressure, since the guide flags weak waveclear against it.
  sources: [diimoonz]

## Mid lane

- Split time between side pressure and grouping by map state, since Diana fits both.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Treat the first one to three core items as the labeled power spike.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Run the displayed AP path from Sorcerer's Shoes and Lich Bane into Shadowflame and Rabadon's Deathcap.
  items: [3020, 3100, 4645, 3089]
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Take the second path of Liandry's Torment, Riftmaker, and Shadowflame into tanks or bruisers.
  items: [6653, 4633, 4645]
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Keep access rolling in skirmishes with Q-hit E resets for repeated dashes.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.Q, Diana.E]

## Win condition

- Let each pulled champion raise Moonfall damage, so multi-target casts pay best.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.R]

- Convert openings with a Q-enabled E reset for follow-up access after the strong side-lane and burst value carries in.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.Q, Diana.E]

- Land skillshots before committing, since the AP build depends on them and escapes run short.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Zone a key enemy with R or catch several, parking the main target where follow-up damage lands.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.R]

- Guard W as a defensive cooldown too, since Diana turns very squishy while it stays down.
  sources: [diimoonz]
  abilities: [Diana.W]

## Itemization

- Run Stormraider's Surge with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, and Gathering Storm plus Shield Bash and Conditioning as the recommended panel.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Take Flash and Ignite as the aggressive summoner setup.
  sources: [diimoonz]

- Hold Zhonya's Hourglass, Void Staff, and Banshee's Veil as situational defense and resistance answers.
