## Key tips

- Accept a weak early lane built on surviving to scale rather than forcing every trade.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Slow-push the first two or three waves, then crash hard on wave three or four for a recall tempo edge.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Treat the W stun as a major teamfight tool by predicting movement; even a miss still banks the shield on entry.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Pick a wave plan early: shove, concede the push, or line up a cheater recall.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Sync wave and reset timing with the jungler route to support their next play.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Roam freely once one Q clears casters around level 7 or 9 without donating the whole wave.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.Q]

- Trade fast with E1, Q, E2, auto; a missed Q makes triggering the passive much harder.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.E, Ekko.Q]

- Shield the exit or stun for a kill with E1, W, E2, Q, auto.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.E, Ekko.W, Ekko.Q]

## Mid lane

- Point roams and resources at the teammate or lane carrying the win condition.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- With bot already ahead, hunt dives and counterplays that stretch its lead.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Arrive early to fights and build plays with the jungler.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Farm sides for picks, slow-pushing first so opponents must answer while the play starts.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Check bushes with W for free vision instead of face-checking.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.W]

- Smooth the cast with E1 into W, which hides part of the throw animation.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.E, Ekko.W]

## Win condition

- Hold R for the most valuable ultimate instead of spending it on the first forced mistake, using W cover and passive footwork to wait.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.R, Ekko.W]

- Respect crowd control as the main vulnerability on an otherwise bursty scaler.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Run E1, Q, E2 for a faster, longer combo at the cost of Q reliability over E1, E2, Q.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.E, Ekko.Q]

- With situational Hextech Rocketbelt, burst E1, Q, E2, Rocketbelt, auto.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
  abilities: [Ekko.E, Ekko.Q]

## Itemization

- Run Hail of Blades with Sudden Impact, Grisly Mementos, and Relentless Hunter, plus Manaflow Band and Scorch.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Take Flash and Teleport as the defensive mid summoners.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Build Lich Bane, Nashor's Tooth, Rabadon's Deathcap, Void Staff, and Dusk and Dawn as the current standard panel.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Start Doran's Ring, buying Dark Seal, Blasting Wand, Aether Wisp, and Sheen early.
  sources: [osgrewynd]

- Rotate situationally through Mejai's Soulstealer, Zhonya's Hourglass, Shadowflame, Banshee's Veil, Morellonomicon, and Hextech Rocketbelt.
  sources: [osgrewynd]
