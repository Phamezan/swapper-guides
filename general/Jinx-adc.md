## Key tips

- Play the first levels safely and fight early only on a clear edge like an engage support.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

- At level 6, finish low-health opponents with R after skirmishes, even far retreating targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]
  abilities: [Jinx.R]

- Stand behind the team near the support late and kite the nearest enemy instead of chasing riskier targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

## Overview

## Early lane

- With a few levels banked, prefer extended trades over repeated short exchanges.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

- Budget mana so Q rockets stay ready for long-range last-hits instead of letting enemies zone Jinx off farm.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]
  abilities: [Jinx.Q]

- Spend the first item component on skirmishing, firing Q rockets into clumps with allied peel around.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]
  abilities: [Jinx.Q]

## Mid lane

- After the bottom tower, rotate mid and open the map through its tower.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

- Stick near the support or another ally all mid game; isolation kills Jinx.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

- Group over split-pushing without a specific reason to leave the team.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

- Set picks with the support or jungler, then weigh Baron on the resulting numbers edge.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]

- At level 9, deal heavier sustained damage with maxed Q under teammate peel.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]
  abilities: [Jinx.Q]

## Win condition

- Trigger the passive movement and attack-speed window with R finishes on low targets to keep damage rolling.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]
  abilities: [Jinx.R]

- Keep allies between Jinx and divers; without E to help escape, all-ins stick hard.
  sources: [mobalytics-jinx]
  abilities: [Jinx.E]

## Itemization

- Reach core items safely on the page plan while managing mana, since it names no item build or rune setup.
