## Key tips

- Press early lane strength for gold and kill pressure from the first waves.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Bend the wave into a freeze or slow push so opponents must walk into Ghost or gank threat.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Stack the passive to five on a reachable target, then execute with R and turn onto the rest of the fight.
  sources: [raideru-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Grow the slow push large; the stacked wave deters ganks and helps turn a 1v2 through passive bonus damage.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Poke with Q while freezing, aiming to miss minions so the freeze survives.
  sources: [raideru-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.Q]

- Save Ghost whenever possible and spend it on real all-ins rather than every trade.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Walk or Ghost up to stack the passive, holding E until the target burns their escape.
  sources: [raideru-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.E]

- Start with E only when the pull makes a true all-in; otherwise keep it for escaping targets.
  sources: [raideru-darius]
  abilities: [Darius.E]

## Mid lane

- Sit in the side lane while major summoners stay down, since Darius barely moves without them.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Keep side pressure rolling to grow the experience and gold lead.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Steal nearby enemy camps when their top laner leaves and the jungler works the opposite side.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Join objective fights only after shoving the side wave, forcing an answer during the setup.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Group around imminent objectives and farm the side lane while nothing brews.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

## Win condition

- Let the fight develop while stacking, then commit deeper the moment the five-stack threshold lands.
  sources: [raideru-darius]

- Keep side farm and level edges high so fights start with an item and experience advantage.
  sources: [raideru-darius]
