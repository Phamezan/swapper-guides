## Key tips

- Play around enemy cooldowns early: Sona is vulnerable with high mana costs and no escape, so avoid getting caught before items and trade only with ADC backup confirmed.
  sources: [neiri]

- Spend W just before incoming damage to shield the hit and heal after, and prioritize the W Power Chord onto whoever deals the most damage to blunt their exchange.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.W, Sona.P]

- Move with the carries rather than far behind them, weaving in and out to apply passive autos and cycle auras while staying protected.
  sources: [neiri]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Trade when the enemy ADC steps up to last-hit, answering with Q and a passive auto rather than spending health without a safe follow-up.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.Q]

- When the opponent burns a bad roam timer, build a large wave and pressure their ADC, calling jungle or mid help before attempting a dive.
  sources: [neiri]

- Run Summon Aery with Manaflow Band, Celerity, Gathering Storm, Presence of Mind, and Legend: Haste, with Flash and Heal for most games.
  sources: [neiri]

## Mid lane

- Choose the Power Chord that best disrupts the current threat instead of spending the passive without a target plan.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.P]

- Keep hunting Flash-R angles, but first check that teammates have the health, resources, and numbers to follow the initiation.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.R]

- Store an E Chord while moving around the map so a slow is ready for the next chase or disengage, chaining another slow after it to hold the target.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.E]

- Roam after pushing the wave, when the ADC is safe, or on a better recall, planning the path and return so the ADC loses no tempo; never leave during a slow push, an enemy freeze, or when the ADC is exposed or holding a large shutdown.
  sources: [neiri]

## Win condition

- Scale into the aura enchanter: stay alive near the carry line, keep applying auras and Power Chord debuffs, and let W, E, and R decide the extended fight.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.W, Sona.E, Sona.R, Sona.P]

- Aim R where allies can follow the stun, W Chord onto the highest damage dealer, and E Chord onto the target your team can catch.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.W, Sona.E, Sona.R]

- Treat every roam as a trade against what the ADC gives up, and at maximum stacks keep casting safely since basic-ability haste plus faster Crescendo rewards continued spell use.
  sources: [neiri]
  abilities: [Sona.R]

## Itemization

- Buy Boots of Swiftness into Echoes of Helia, with the order reversible by game, then add Diadem of Songs, Moonstone Renewer, and Dawncore for the healer line.
  sources: [neiri]

- Situationally take ability-haste boots, Seraph's Embrace, Frozen Heart, Kaenic Rookern, or Dawncore by team damage and survival needs, with Knight's Vow, Shurelya's Battlesong, Mikael's Blessing, and Oblivion Orb as further options.
  sources: [neiri]
