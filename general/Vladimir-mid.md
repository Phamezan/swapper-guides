## Key tips

- Farm safely through the weak early levels and limit trades until level five or six, then play more aggressively once the transition arrives instead of staying passive by default.
  sources: [zamus]

- Trade around empowered Transfusion: landing the empowered Q on a champion also heals Vladimir, and Ghost can be worth spending to secure one for a health lead when the exchange favors it.
  sources: [zamus]
  abilities: [Vladimir.Q]

- Open committed lane fights with Hemoplague so marked targets take amplified damage and heal Vladimir for the damage dealt.
  sources: [zamus]
  abilities: [Vladimir.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Hold a pushing opponent's wave closer to your tower so their jungler has less room to approach, and never let it settle on the opponent's side.
  sources: [zamus]

## Mid lane

- Keep collecting side-lane farm and pressuring side-turret plates without abandoning farming for low-value grouping.
  sources: [zamus]

- Join the team's plans more as the game moves on: when the team prepares for Baron, push the top side lane so Vladimir stays close enough to rotate.
  sources: [zamus]

- Protect the farm and experience that build Vladimir's late-game strength, choosing side-lane pressure that supports the next objective.
  sources: [zamus]

## Win condition

- In late fights, threaten the enemy back line and deal with squishier targets before spending time on tanks.
  sources: [zamus]

- Never walk unwarded jungle routes alone: getting caught by layered crowd control can end the fight and the game.
  sources: [zamus]

- Cast Hemoplague before the rest of a committed damage sequence so allies also benefit from its damage amplification.
  sources: [zamus]
  abilities: [Vladimir.R]

## Itemization

- Build Hextech Rocketbelt, Ionian Boots of Lucidity, Rabadon's Deathcap, Riftmaker, Void Staff, and Zhonya's Hourglass as the standard line, keeping Riftmaker and Void Staff into tankier teams.
  items: [3152, 3158, 3089, 4633, 3135, 3157]
  sources: [zamus]

- Take the high-burst alternative of Stormsurge with Sorcerer's Shoes, Rocketbelt, Void Staff, Deathcap, and Zhonya's; run Stormraider's Surge with Nimbus Cloak, Transcendence, Gathering Storm, Legend: Haste, and Cut Down, with Deathfire Touch plus Ignite as the aggressive melee-lane option.
  items: [4646, 3020, 3135]
  sources: [zamus]

- Swap boots by game: Sorcerer's Shoes for damage into squishy teams, Swifties against repeated slows, Lucidity for haste, and Mercury's Treads reserved for substantial magic damage and crowd control.
  items: [3020, 3111]
  sources: [zamus]
