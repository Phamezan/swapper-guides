## Key tips

- Treat levels 1 to 4 as the weak stretch, especially into longer-range ADCs, and refuse forced early trades.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

- Guard mana so W stays available as an escape; running dry removes both poke and the disengage option.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.W]

- In grouped fights, delay commitment while Q and R chip the enemy, then enter once they soften.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.Q, Corki.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Line Q and R through both the wave and a champion so each cast clears while chipping.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.Q, Corki.R]

- Balance spell poke with last-hitting, since repeated Q and R drain mana before fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.Q, Corki.R]

- Pressure melee supports with range, but stop before trading through a large minion wave.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

- Spend W forward only after the enemy support loses crowd control or teammates can follow, given its long early cooldown.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.W]

- At level 6, use R for extra clearing and poke the opponent toward their tower while pushing.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.R]

- Treat the first finished item as a major poke and clearing spike.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

## Mid lane

- At level 9, clear waves hard with maxed Q to punish opponents who want to roam.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.Q]

- At level 11, sharpen both clearing and pre-fight poke with the second R rank.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.R]

- Without an objective forming, farm a side lane instead of waiting mid with no play.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

- Never get caught alone before an objective, since the death hands the enemy numbers to start or siege with.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

## Win condition

- Lower enemy health with long-range poke before the team commits.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

- In late fights, keep poking while staying close enough to avoid getting picked off.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

- Refuse to split away late when it strands the team in a forced 4v5.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]

- Save W as the safety tool unless the team can follow a forward dash; lone entries invite a clean punish.
  sources: [mobalytics-corki]
  abilities: [Corki.W]
