## Key tips

- At level 1 into melee, walk into their wave, keep swinging, then raise Counter Strike to dodge attacks and boost its return damage.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- At level 6, stack two attacks on minions, leap with Q, then auto plus W to stack the third-hit R passive on the reset.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.Q, Jax.W, Jax.R]

- Split to drag enemies off the team, letting allies spend the numbers edge on objectives.
  sources: [ph45]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Swing at minions before engaging to bank attack-speed passive stacks.
  sources: [ph45]

- Blunt auto trades with Counter Strike, whose reactivation stuns nearby enemies.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.E]

- Attack first and press W right after the hit, since Empower resets the basic attack for a fast trade.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.W]

- Cast W before Q as well so its bonus damage lands with Leap Strike.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.W, Jax.Q]

- Keep a Stealth Ward ready for Q ward-hops into or out of fights.
  items: [3340]
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.Q]

- Default to Grasp for safer lanes, or Lethal Tempo for stat-checks like Darius, Yone, Yasuo, Yorick, or Olaf.
  sources: [ph45]

- Bait movement before Flash plus Q finishes, since the combo reads predictably.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.Q]

## Mid lane

- Rush Trinity Force first for its health, attack speed, Sheen proc, damage, and on-hit movement.
  items: [3078, 3057]
  sources: [ph45]

- Vision the side lane and watch the minimap, backing off the moment unseen enemies can collapse.
  sources: [ph45]

- When enemies leave to fight, push hard only while the team disengages safely; otherwise reset and hold objective vision.
  sources: [ph45]

- Teleport into fights the team can stall until arrival with a surprising flank.
  sources: [ph45]

- Ward-hop over walls to escape multi-enemy side rotations.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.Q]

- Clear rough lanes with Titanic Hydra waves, accepting it sits outside the standard build.
  items: [3748]
  sources: [ph45]

## Win condition

- Convert side pressure into fast towers whenever enemies leave Jax unattended.
  sources: [ph45]

- Split or join by objective state and whether the team survives a 4v5.
  sources: [ph45]

- Skip entries with no route to targets into heavy control and disengage.
  sources: [ph45]

- Hold Q as the ward-hop escape while splitting deep.
  sources: [ph45]
  abilities: [Jax.Q]

## Itemization

- Run Grasp with Demolish, Second Wind or Conditioning, and Overgrowth plus Triple Tonic and Jack of All Trades for safe trades and scaling.
  sources: [ph45]

- Swap Lethal Tempo with Triumph, Legend: Haste, and Last Stand for matchups demanding attack speed in long all-ins.
  sources: [ph45]

- Start Doran's Shield for safety, with Doran's Bow as the other setup.
  items: [1054, 1086]
  sources: [ph45]

- Build Trinity Force usual first, or Dusk and Dawn as the Sheen start for AP-bruiser Jax.
  items: [3078, 2510, 3057]
  sources: [ph45]

- Add Spear of Shojin for health, damage, and haste into more frequent ability use.
  items: [3161]
  sources: [ph45]

- Rush Blade of the Ruined King into Dr. Mundo builds, accepting weaker early health.
  sources: [ph45]

- Pick Ionian Boots for cooldowns, Mercury's Treads into heavy control, Plated Steelcaps while far behind into autos, Swiftness Boots into slows, Wit's End for resistance plus tenacity, Randuin's Omen into crit teams, and Zhonya's Hourglass to stall burst for the Counter Strike return.
  items: [3111, 3047, 3091, 3143, 3157]
