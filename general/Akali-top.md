## Key tips

- Farm safely and protect health until W, E, and R are all online.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.W, Akali.E, Akali.R]

- At level 6, threaten an all-in with R when the opponent overextends.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

- Prefer picks and split fights over a straight 5v5, where grouped enemies and defensive items deny clean kills.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Use Q to hit the wave while keeping the opponent under threat at the same time.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- Check energy before any all-in and track the enemy jungler, since Akali invites early camps before level 6.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

- Treat E as both engage and escape, and never waste it when it may be needed to leave.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- After the first component and a recall, trade more often since Q hits harder.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- At level 3, use the full basic kit for trades without chasing so far that the exchange turns.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

## Mid lane

- Shove with Q, then roam toward an extended lane, using E and R to close the gap.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.Q, Akali.E, Akali.R]

- At neutral objectives, show E so enemy carries respect the follow-up and give ground instead of forcing a dive.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.E]

- Save W for a real fight opening; spending it early lets opponents bait a bad all-in.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

- Pick the longer side lane when safe, since extra space helps commit to an isolated target.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

- At level 9, use maxed Q for faster clearing and stronger side-lane duels.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- At level 11, use the second R rank to catch isolated enemies more often.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

- Turn a mid-game pick into a numbers edge for a Baron call.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

## Win condition

- Snowball isolated squishy kills into further picks with the earned lead.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

- Before committing late, account for nearby backup, since grouped opponents ruin clean assassinations.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]

- Stay patient in teamfights and keep W for the entry or the escape window.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.W]

- At level 16, use the third R rank and its lower cooldown for more late-fight windows.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.R]

## Itemization

- Trade more aggressively after the first component, since it directly raises Q damage.
  sources: [mobalytics-akali]
  abilities: [Akali.Q]

- Scout Zhonya's Hourglass and Guardian Angel on late opponents and hold the commit until their answers are mapped.
