## Key tips

- Run the full-clear default for a top-side start: Raptors, Red, and Krugs, then recall before sweeping the blue-side camps.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Take a second point in Q before that recall so the return path uses its movement speed, and time the blue-side finish to arrive as the river crab spawns.
  sources: [yourdadday]
  abilities: [Nocturne.Q]

- Contest the first Scuttle only with a real duel edge, and only take the second crab as well when the first fight ends safely with a lead.
  sources: [yourdadday]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Prefer the full clear over the earlier invade-first plan; the route is built for pace and a Smite upgrade lead, not constant fighting.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- After both crabs, continue through Krugs, Raptors, and Wolves, then recall and return for Gromp to keep the farm cycle unbroken.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Invade and fight only when the enemy team cannot rotate first; check every collapse route before committing to their side.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Treat the author's dragon call as the payoff for the early route: prioritize dragons over Void Grubs when the path creates that control.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Run Ignite as this plan's dueling edge for the early fights it seeks; it is a matchup choice for this strategy, not a universal summoner setup.
  sources: [yourdadday]

## Mid lane

- In the midgame, keep camps and objectives first and spend the ultimate only where it turns a gank into a clear kill.
  sources: [yourdadday]
  abilities: [Nocturne.R]

- Skip repeated ganks while most camps are still up; holding Nocturne's level and farm pace matters more than forcing a low-percentage play.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Keep seeking controlled invades while Ignite and a level lead make the duel favorable, tracking which lanes can rotate before entering.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Only attempt the author's solo-dragon line when the enemy team cannot contest it; it is a high-risk solo-queue tactic that fails against a collapsing team.
  sources: [yourdadday]

## Win condition

- In the late game, hunt picks with the team and convert the numbers advantage into Baron rather than starting it blind.
  sources: [yourdadday]
  abilities: [Nocturne.R]

- Set up Baron with teammates and map control, and never start it while all five opponents are positioned to burst or steal it; avoid diving the enemy team without a pick-into-objective plan.
  sources: [yourdadday]

## Itemization

- Keep Ignite as the strategy's dueling summoner: it supplies the early-fight advantage the invade and Scuttle contests rely on.
  sources: [yourdadday]

- Build the route around reaching an early Smite upgrade lead, and treat the guide's exact camp-count and Smite-damage comparison as patch-sensitive math to recheck.
