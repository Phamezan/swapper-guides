## Key tips

- Open cautiously unless the matchup runs clearly favorable, taking longer trades once a few levels bank.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]

- At level 6, spend R on the kill window when opponents step too far forward, never forcing it from bad positioning.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.R]

- Start fights with R only beside following allies; its speed helps them keep up, but a split team strands Kled.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.R]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Build all-ins around Q and E, refusing starts with W down since its attack run carries extended trades.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.Q, Kled.E, Kled.W]

- Bank E access against ranged opponents; a miss lets them kite Kled down before contact.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.E]

- Dismounted, skip risky farm into ranged opponents unless Skaarl returns safely; the passive also tanks tower shots for planned dives.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]

- Never assume the remount: enemies who disengage after the dismount can deny Skaarl recovery in time.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]

- Take extended trades around W instead of burning its empowered run on brief exchanges.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled-build]
  abilities: [Kled.W]

## Mid lane

- Split-push to pull a defender and free the team elsewhere, checking the map before spending R on duels or rotations.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.R]

- When an answer arrives, hunt the pick or fight with the advantage in hand.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]

- Flank grouped fights from the side for high-value targets instead of walking through the frontline.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]

## Win condition

- Ride R onto isolated or overextended targets, then remove the backline member that buys a numbers edge.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.R]

- Group for late fights; starting R from too far out removes Kled while the fight gets decided.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
  abilities: [Kled.R]

- Soak with the passive and scramble formations without treating durability as a solo-engage license.
  sources: [mobalytics-kled]
