## Key tips

- Poke with empowered R and Q to soften the enemy duo ahead of an all-in.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
  abilities: [Karma.Q, Karma.R]

- Pressure through lane brushes so Q poke turns harder to answer.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
  abilities: [Karma.Q]

- Keep the team alive late with empowered E as the default over empowered Q.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
  abilities: [Karma.E, Karma.Q]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Position conservatively as a fragile target that dies stepping too far forward.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

- Shield the ADC with lane vision plays that also keep Karma alive for later fights.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
  abilities: [Karma.E]

- Track the E cooldown and refuse fights without it when the enemy lane answers back.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
  abilities: [Karma.E]

## Mid lane

- After the bot tower, rotate with the ADC or cover mid beside the mid laner for the next tower.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

- Shield allies with empowered E in teamfights unless Q or W answers a specific play better.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
  abilities: [Karma.E, Karma.Q, Karma.W]

- Travel with allies and never ward alone beyond teammate help range.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

- At level 11, ride the first maxed ability toward either more burst or stronger carry protection by max order.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

- Spend poke and utility into grouped opponents where several allies share the movement speed and shields.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

## Win condition

- Stay near teammates so no lone catch converts into Baron or another forced fight.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

- Blunt heavy engage with shields and movement speed, then disengage or counterattack behind them.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]

- Make team-wide utility count with vision and grouped positioning around neutral objectives.
  sources: [mobalytics-karma]
