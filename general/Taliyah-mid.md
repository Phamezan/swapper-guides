## Key tips

- Play as the lane stabilizer: thin waves early, scale, and only look to move around the map once waveclear is sufficient, especially after level six.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.Q]

- Into ranged champions play defensively with occasional E poke on the wave-clear advantage; against shorter-range opponents threaten with E, Q, and autos while respecting their all-in range.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.E, Taliyah.Q]

- Hold E for dashes and gap closers, keep the wave closer to your side, and save W to follow allied crowd control so the displacement lands reliably.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.E, Taliyah.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Freeze near your tower when behind or threatened by an assassin, slow-push before a planned Destiny-style roam, and fast-push before a recall or map move.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

- Run the displayed order of W at level one, Q at two, E at three, then max Q, W, E while taking R at six, eleven, and sixteen.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.W, Taliyah.Q, Taliyah.E, Taliyah.R]

- Judge the early phase by high CS, few deaths, and some takedown contribution from skirmishes or a side-lane roam, and treat the level-six Weaver's Wall unlock as the signal to clear quickly and move around the map.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.R]

- Ward river entrances defensively early, and with mid priority ward enemy Raptors or a Red Buff entrance to track the jungler and open safer roams.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

## Mid lane

- Keep gold and experience flowing while attending every major objective fight; never trade the whole wave timer for an empty rotation.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

- Push a side wave only to a safe point, and without Teleport group and collect waves as they reach your tower instead of holding a long side lane.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

- Use the ultimate to join a side-lane play without Teleport when the team fights near mid, but avoid getting stranded across the map.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.R]

- Recall roughly a minute before dragon to spend gold and restore health and mana, and take Krugs or Raptors when they are safe, especially to deny the enemy team.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

## Win condition

- Play front to back behind tanks and damage the frontline instead of stepping forward alone; keep E ready when its displacement protects the team.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.E]

- Fight around chokes and small spaces where E disrupts several opponents, switching onto a mispositioned squishy rather than tunneling the frontline.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
  abilities: [Taliyah.E]

- Account for enemy engage tools before committing, positioning around threats such as a hook that punishes Taliyah's placement.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

## Itemization

- Run Phase Rush with Manaflow Band, Transcendence, Waterwalking, Legend: Haste, and Cut Down; open Doran's Ring and two Health Potions, with Tear and two potions as the greedy start.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

- Build Seraph's Embrace into Sorcerer's Shoes, Liandry's Torment, Rabadon's Deathcap, Cryptbloom, and Zhonya's Hourglass, with Ionian Boots or Mercury's Treads by game, Mercury's specified against double AP and crowd control.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]

- Keep Mejai's Soulstealer, Banshee's Veil, Morellonomicon, Shadowflame, Cosmic Drive, Liandry's Torment, and Bloodletter's Curse as situational picks around lead, survival, and enemy defenses.
  sources: [shok-taliyah]
