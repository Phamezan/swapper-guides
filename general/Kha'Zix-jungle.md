## Key tips

- Hunt early ganks and duels against low or overextended opponents; one kill eases control of everything after.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

- At level 6, evolve to the plan: Q for faster objective damage, or E and R for more gank and escape options.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]
  abilities: [Kha'Zix.Q, Kha'Zix.E, Kha'Zix.R]

- Duel lone side laners through isolation, especially a squishy carry catching a side wave.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

## Overview

## Early lane

- Farm camps between ganks so early aggression never builds an experience or gold hole.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

- Take objectives fast when lanes allow, including without the full team.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

- Spend the first item component on threatening isolated backline targets.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

## Mid lane

- Ambush isolated rotators across the map, then call the nearby objective after each pick.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

- At level 9, burst isolated targets harder with maxed Q while clearing camps faster.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]
  abilities: [Kha'Zix.Q]

- At level 11, strengthen the pick-and-escape plan with the second R rank.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]
  abilities: [Kha'Zix.R]

- Clear enemy vision before isolated-target setups and never chase where their team collapses.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

## Win condition

- Flank late fights and reach priority targets on evolved E without showing the approach early.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]
  abilities: [Kha'Zix.E]

- While ahead, wait on high-traffic routes for an isolated mid, ADC, or support, then spend the death timer on a major objective or the end.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

- Hold the next-objective side and never show opposite shortly before it spawns.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]

- Bank E or R against available control, since getting caught without an escape ends Kha'Zix fast.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]
  abilities: [Kha'Zix.E, Kha'Zix.R]

## Itemization

- Pick the first evolution and item component around the wanted pick or objective plan, since the page names no item or rune setup.
  sources: [mobalytics-khazix]
