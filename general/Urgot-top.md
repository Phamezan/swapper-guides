## Key tips

- Treat levels one to three as pressure, not a mandate to brawl: Urgot scales into a midgame champion, so zone and build toward the level-nine spike instead of forcing every fight.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

- Start E, landing a basic attack before it when surprising an opponent, and align an early E to hit the first three melee minions plus the opponent when they stand low.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

- Reach level nine with Black Cleaver complete: max-rank W then stays active without mana cost or cooldown until toggled off, and the sustained damage jumps sharply.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.W]

## Overview

## Early lane

- At levels one and two, use passive and E pressure to zone and slow-push; ward once the wave is fully under the enemy tower, ideally before the three-minute gank window.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

- At levels four and five, take safe autos when the opponent farms and use E only when they step into range, waiting for its return rather than forcing trades.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.E]

- Plan an early Cull when a recall comes around levels four and five, including a roughly four-hundred-fifty gold recall timing.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

## Mid lane

- Play the level-eleven and level-thirteen passive shotgun cooldowns by circling targets so repeated attacks land harder as Urgot rounds them.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

- Around level thirteen, press the advantage with side-lane fights, then rotate or Teleport to teamfights; the guide calls this midgame window Urgot's strongest phase.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

- On turrets or neutral objectives, weave one basic attack after every three W hits for extra damage.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.W]

- In a teamfight, follow allied crowd control with R, using Flash near the end of the pull to spread the ultimate fear across nearby enemies.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.R]

- Split-push while waiting for Flash or Teleport; with Teleport ready, pressure the side opposite a major objective and join when the fight starts.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

## Win condition

- Build through the level-nine W toggle and Black Cleaver spike, then use the level-eleven to thirteen passive cooldowns to win extended fights and convert them into objectives.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.W]

- Favor teamfights where Urgot follows allied engage and creates an area fear, or flank to expose a backline target for E into R.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.E, Urgot.R]

- Spend the lead before roughly thirty to thirty-five minutes: Urgot's relative strength tapers after that point, so secure map pressure during the midgame peak.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

## Itemization

- Run Press the Attack with Triumph, Legend: Bloodline, and Cut Down plus Second Wind and Overgrowth, with Flash and Ignite and a Doran's Blade start.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]

- Build Black Cleaver, Boots of Swiftness, Sterak's Gage, Dead Man's Plate, Force of Nature, and Overlord's Bloodmail, maxing W first; keep Plated Steelcaps, Gluttonous Greaves, Doran's Shield, Kaenic Rookern, Randuin's Omen, Thornmail, Chempunk Chainsword, Jak'Sho, and Spear of Shojin as situational picks.
  sources: [goliath-urgot]
  abilities: [Urgot.W]
