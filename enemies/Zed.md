## Early lane

- Zed often places Living Shadow before Death Mark so he can swap away from skillshots. Hold your crowd control until he has used the swap.
  sources: [aikopanda]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Zed.W, Zed.R]

## Win condition

- Death Mark always lands Zed behind his target. Pre-aim crowd control and escapes behind you, not toward where he cast it.
  sources: [peng04]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Zed.R]
