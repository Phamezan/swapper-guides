## Early lane

- Double Up bounces off a dying minion into the champion behind it. Never stand on the bounce line behind low-health minions when she steps up to farm.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.Q]

- Make It Rain slows you for her support's short trades. Save mobility for the slow and back out of the trade while it covers you.
  sources: [yunfang-mf]
  patch: "26.21"
  abilities: [Miss Fortune.E]
