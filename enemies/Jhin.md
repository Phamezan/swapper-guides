## Early lane

- Jhin loads his fourth shot on a minion and holds it to zone you off the wave. Trade while it is spent and give ground while it is held.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.19"
  abilities: [Jhin.Q]

- Deadly Flourish is a follow-up for support crowd control, not dry poke. A wasted W is your window to walk up.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.19"
  abilities: [Jhin.W]

## Win condition

- A landed Deadly Flourish sets up a guaranteed first Curtain Call shot when fired instantly. Break vision lines and stay mobile once it connects.
  sources: [omibro]
  patch: "26.19"
  abilities: [Jhin.W, Jhin.R]
